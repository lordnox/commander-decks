# Kitchen-table power

This is an opinion ranking of how hard each stored 99 is to beat at this table: how readily it ends the game, and how much pressure it applies while it gets there. It is the sort order for the [root deck index](README.md).

It is **not** Commander Bracket (see [BRACKET-DEFINITIONS.md](BRACKET-DEFINITIONS.md)), not Jank / Fun / Mean, and not a tournament standing. One recorded game does not reshuffle the list. Folder prefixes stay as assessed.

Edit `power-rankings.json` when a list change actually moves a deck, then refresh the index:

```bash
python3 .agents/skills/tag-deck/scripts/update_deck_tags.py decks/<prefix><slug>
```

New decks go at the bottom until they are placed on purpose. Slugs omit the rating prefix so a bracket rename keeps the same row.

Hot-seat live table. Homer emptied the other three libraries on turn 10 with [Lumra, Bellow of the Woods](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/183/lumra-bellow-of-the-woods) after two missed land drops. ## Order

1. **The Twelfth Doctor — Borrowed Time** — Extra-turn chains take the game away; usual close around seven, with a Sol Ring line that can start chaining on four.
2. **Bartolomé — Graveyard Shift** — Compact aristocrats loop. Usual threat around six, with an unlikely four. Highest combo ceiling in the collection; more pieces than Iname, so it whiffs more.
3. **Iname — Death Aspect** — [Mortal Combat](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/160/mortal-combat) as a usual turn-six win, with a four-turn line. Slightly more telegraphed than Bartolomé, almost as fast.
4. **Ms. Bumbleflower — Tea Party** — Two-spell counters plus [Triumph of the Hordes](https://scryfall.com/card/nph/123/triumph-of-the-hordes). Muse makes extra double-spell turns; Force of Will covers the close. Usual eight to ten, a grown board can die around six or seven.
5. **Homer — Dumpster-Diver Crab** — Self-mill landfall combo. Usual around seven, strong six, unlikely four. Faster than the `3−` folder suggests; mill still dies to hate and to missing the explosion. The 2026-09-08 live table still closed on ten after two missed land drops.
6. **Alania — Divergent Laughter** — Copy-control that holds up answers and dumps with [Jaya's Immolating Inferno](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/415/jayas-immolating-inferno). Usual eight; a reasonable six. The turn-four Tasha chain mills without ending the game.
7. **Sin — Sin-fall** — Land lottery. Portal or Valgavoth can show up as early as five, usual threat around seven. Copies enter tapped and opponents pick the sacrifices, so the high roll is a problem, not a kill.
8. **Indoraptor — Hybrid Theory** — Enrage attrition with a mid-game [Last Laugh](https://scryfall.com/card/tor/68/last-laugh) table kill. Usual eight or nine; a real four-turn kill on one player.
9. **Osgir — Captain of the Dawnsire** — Reconstruction into a large combat or [Arcbond](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/91/arcbond) wipe. Usual seven or eight. Gunships and reconstruct are the engine; the namesake burn did not show in the 2026-09-08 live table.
10. **Nethroi — Backyard Trebuchet** — Toughness catapult. Usual seven or eight, with an unlikely turn-five [Tree of Perdition](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/49/tree-of-perdition) line. Without a fling body the Tree is a wall; Catapult Fodder missed the 2026-09-08 live table.
11. **Hazel — Misty Critters** — Go-wide squirrels. Usual around eight; Triumph is a reasonable six if the swarm is already there.
12. **Umbris — Mental Breakdown** — Exile-theft voltron. First real attack around six or seven, close eight to ten. Theft fills the clock; it is not a combo finish.
13. **Madcap Jester — Tutor Your Deck** — Library theft into a timed dump. Usual eight or nine; Hideous Laughter with the Jester down can dump on six.
14. **Mishra — Racecar Driver** — Artifact value into a late combat. Usual eight or nine, exceptional seven. Loudest Incremental pressure of the 2026-09-08 pod (Wellspring, Paladin, Copter); still not the table favorite to actually win.
15. **Extus — Stop Hitting Yourself** — Aikido that needs attackers. Table win around eight to ten after recycling reflectors; a stuffed-doll draw can take one player on six.
16. **Rocco — Endless Wariness** — Flicker pings. Usual nine to eleven; Torbran can reasonably pull that to eight.
17. **Jon Irenicus — Unwanted Presents** — Donate lock around six to eight, then attrition. Stops one opponent early; it is slow at winning the table.
18. **Jalira — The Polyfisher** — Combat after a polymorph. Usual nine to eleven. Training Grounds plus Elixir can put a monster on four, which is not yet a win.
19. **Sygg — Thousand Cuts** — Symmetric drain. Usual ten to twelve; a stacked wheel with Caress and charged Ascension can reasonably end around eight.
20. **Lady Evangela — Foggy Blood Transfusion** — Draw-go aikido into Coffers drain. Wins around ten or later. Dense fogs and counters, no early kill.
21. **Jhoira — Hot Tub Time Machine** — Suspend cook into free haste creatures. Usual threat around nine or ten; telegraphed, not an extra-turn kill.
