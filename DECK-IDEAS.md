# Deck Ideas

Parking lot for grilled Commander brews that are not a stored 99. Each deck still has its own primer and `DECISIONS.md`. [House rules](DECISIONS.md) still apply if an idea is promoted.

## How to use

- **Queued** — worth grilling or building when someone asks.
- **Currently not viable** — funny or on-theme, but not worth a folder. Read this before proposing the same 99 again.
- Move an idea between sections; delete it when it becomes a deck under `decks/`.

## Queued

### Zur + Pestilence (lifelink sweep)

Parked 2026-08-26. Worth a 99 later: the Oracle line is real, the engine is old-school black, and [Zur, Eternal Schemer](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/228/zur-eternal-schemer) is a working commander lock for the grill, not a stored folder. No brew title yet.

Plan in one sentence: turn [Pestilence](https://scryfall.com/card/6ed/149/pestilence) (or a clone) into a creature, give that source lifelink, pay `{B}` to deal 1 to each creature and each player, gain that much life, then convert the gain into a kill. That activation is not a loop. Each ping costs mana and does not restore itself. Fat-once is a board wipe plus a life swing, not infinite.

Rules that were checked: Zur's `{1}{W}` has no duration, so Pestilence stays an enchantment creature (base P/T = mana value 4) until it leaves or another effect strips the type. Pestilence is the damage source, so lifelink on the permanent applies to the noncombat ping. Zur's static also grants deathtouch, so the first self-hit usually destroys it unless it is indestructible. Hexproof does not stop the ability (no targets). The "sacrifice if no creatures" clause does not fire while Pestilence is itself a creature.

Esper copies of the same machine: [Withering Wisps](https://scryfall.com/card/me2/114/withering-wisps) (snow-Swamp cap), [Screams of the Damned](https://scryfall.com/card/ody/160/screams-of-the-damned), [Festering Evil](https://scryfall.com/card/wth/68/festering-evil), [Last Laugh](https://scryfall.com/card/tor/68/last-laugh). [Noxious Field](https://scryfall.com/card/pcy/70/noxious-field) is an Aura, so Zur cannot target it. Creature pingers ([Crypt Rats](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/84/crypt-rats), [Thrashing Wumpus](https://scryfall.com/card/mmq/166/thrashing-wumpus), [Pestilence Demon](https://scryfall.com/card/ddr/56/pestilence-demon), [Conductor of Cacophony](https://scryfall.com/card/j22/20/conductor-of-cacophony)) need an outside lifelink grant; they do not pick up Zur's static. Safer Zur damage enchantments that miss your own 4/4: [Underworld Dreams](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/121/underworld-dreams), [Hecatomb](https://scryfall.com/card/me1/71/hecatomb), [Lightmine Field](https://scryfall.com/card/roe/32/lightmine-field), [Megrim](https://scryfall.com/card/m10/104/megrim). Life-loss enchantments are not lifelink.

Lifelink without Zur's deathtouch: [Whip of Erebos](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/159/whip-of-erebos) (team anthem, also an enchantment), equipment ([Basilisk Collar](https://scryfall.com/card/wwk/122/basilisk-collar), [Shadowspear](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/236/shadowspear)), [Vizkopa Guildmage](https://scryfall.com/card/ima/211/vizkopa-guildmage), [Heliod, Sun-Crowned](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/29/heliod-sun-crowned), [Scion of Draco](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/234/scion-of-draco) for black creatures. Self-kill fix: [Indestructibility](https://scryfall.com/card/m14/23/indestructibility), [Darksteel Plate](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/304/darksteel-plate), [Shielded by Faith](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/172/shielded-by-faith). Mass-animate: [Opalescence](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/13/opalescence), [Starfield of Nyx](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/840/starfield-of-nyx); pairing those with Zur's deathtouch plus a Pestilence activation eats your own animated enchantments.

Named finishers: [Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/127/vito-thorn-of-the-dusk-rose), [Sanguine Bond](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/153/sanguine-bond), [Enduring Tenacity](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/95/enduring-tenacity) (already an enchantment creature), [Exquisite Blood](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/195/exquisite-blood) plus any of those (one lifelinked ping starts the two-card loop). Doublers and draw: [Boon Reflection](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/10/boon-reflection), [Alhammarret's Archive](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/233/alhammarrets-archive), [Well of Lost Dreams](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/275/well-of-lost-dreams). Recursion: [Replenish](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/15/replenish), [Open the Vaults](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/24/open-the-vaults), [Dance of the Manse](https://scryfall.com/card/nec/136/dance-of-the-manse) at X≥6 (returns already animated). [Heliod, Sun-Crowned](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/29/heliod-sun-crowned) plus [Walking Ballista](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/306/walking-ballista) is a true loop (lifelink ping, gain, +1/+1 counter back). Neither piece is a Game Changer; house rules still prefer not to staple-build unless this deck is excepted.

House rules for a later 99: no Game Changers unless excepted ([Enlightened Tutor](https://scryfall.com/card/mir/14/enlightened-tutor) is one). Prefer the old Pestilence cousins over modern drain staples. Still open: brew title, how much two-card combo vs a sticky Pestilence lock, and whether [Oloro, Ageless Ascetic](https://scryfall.com/card/c13/203/oloro-ageless-ascetic) belongs in the zone instead of Zur. No `decks/` folder yet; [Indoraptor — Hybrid Theory](decks/3_indoraptor-hybrid-theory/README.md) is a different Pestilence-style plan (Jund enrage).

### Gor + Peer Pressure (newt war)

Parked 2026-09-03. Worth a 99 later: Simic type-change plus an Onslaught steal, and [Gor Muldrak, Amphinologist](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/277/gor-muldrak-amphinologist) is the commander lock. No brew title yet.

Plan in one sentence: at each of your end steps, give a 4/3 Salamander to every player tied for fewest creatures, keep protection from those tokens, then turn a slice of the board into Salamanders and resolve [Peer Pressure](https://scryfall.com/card/ons/101/peer-pressure) naming Salamander. That steal is not a loop. Peer Pressure needs you to control strictly more creatures of the chosen type than each other player; the control change lasts indefinitely even after until-end-of-turn type changes wear off.

Rules that were checked: Gor's static also protects Gor. The token ability checks creature counts on resolution, not on trigger. Tied for fewest means each tied player makes a token, so a wide board on your side donates to the table. Protection from Salamanders stops Salamander sources from damaging, enchanting, blocking, or targeting you and your permanents; it does not stop you from attacking with stolen creatures. Type-changers in identity: [Unnatural Selection](https://scryfall.com/card/apc/32/unnatural-selection), [Imagecrafter](https://scryfall.com/card/ons/87/imagecrafter), [Amoeboid Changeling](https://scryfall.com/card/lrw/51/amoeboid-changeling) (grant all types, or strip them). Neither Gor nor Peer Pressure is a Game Changer.

Named finishers: Peer Pressure itself as the swing, then beatdown with the stolen pile. Combat lords and overrun are backup, not the joke. House rules for a later 99: prefer the old type-change toys over modern changeling staples unless the extra types are the line. Still open: brew title, whether you stay creature-light to keep receiving tokens or go wide and only donate, and how much extra steal ([Cultural Exchange](https://scryfall.com/card/ody/79/cultural-exchange) era) belongs next to Peer Pressure. No `decks/` folder yet.

### Atemsis (six mana values)

Parked 2026-09-03. Worth a 99 later: the lose-the-game line is printed on [Atemsis, All-Seeing](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/46/atemsis-all-seeing), and mono-blue is the commander lock. No brew title yet.

Plan in one sentence: keep a six-mana-value hand, connect with Atemsis, reveal, that player loses. The tap ability (`{2}{U}`, draw two then discard one) is the sculpt, not the win. It is not a loop. Each trigger can only eliminate the opponent who took the damage, and only if the reveal still has six different mana values.

Rules that were checked: the trigger is any damage to an opponent, not only combat. Every card has exactly one mana value; no-cost cards and `{X}` in hand are 0, and 0 counts. Split cards use the combined halves (Fire // Ice is 4, not two values). Lands in hand are 0. Game Changers are not required for the line; [Cyclonic Rift](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/40/cyclonic-rift) and [Rhystic Study](https://scryfall.com/card/j22/114/rhystic-study) stay out unless excepted as the joke.

Named finishers: Atemsis connecting is the named win. Path to connect: bounce ([Evacuation](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/156/evacuation), [Aetherize](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/151/aetherize), [Consuming Tide](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/53/consuming-tide)), then a spread of mana values in the 99 so a full hand is six columns without tutors. Hand-size without Reliquary Tower: [Library of Leng](https://scryfall.com/card/me4/211/library-of-leng), [Spellbook](https://scryfall.com/card/m10/220/spellbook). Still open: brew title, how much the 99 is "only connect the Sphinx" versus a backup mill or combat plan, and whether the tap ability is worth building around as card selection or just incidental. No `decks/` folder yet.

## Currently not viable

### Mind Unbound + The Magic Mirror (proliferate)

Parked 2026-08-26. The pairing is a joke: [Mind Unbound](https://scryfall.com/card/m12/68/mind-unbound) and [The Magic Mirror](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/51/the-magic-mirror) are the same engine twice (upkeep: put a lore/knowledge counter, then draw that many). Mirror also drops the hand-size cap and discounts for instants and sorceries in the graveyard. Proliferate is the right verb — it hits both permanents. [Vorel of the Hull Clade](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/294/vorel-of-the-hull-clade) does not; it cannot target the enchantment.

It is not a loop. Proliferate is `+1` of each counter already there. Fat-once is not infinite. Extra-turn [Magistrate's Scepter](https://scryfall.com/card/m19/238/magistrates-scepter) lines, poison, and [Viral Drake](https://scryfall.com/card/nph/49/viral-drake) plus infinite mana sit next to the theme, not inside those two cards.

A real win would still need a named finisher after the upkeep draws: [Psychosis Crawler](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/234/psychosis-crawler) (each draw is a separate drain), [Laboratory Maniac](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/71/laboratory-maniac) / [Jace, Wielder of Mysteries](https://scryfall.com/card/war/54/jace-wielder-of-mysteries) already in play, or a combat payoff like [Chasm Skulker](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/218/chasm-skulker). [Thassa's Oracle](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/73/thassas-oracle) is a Game Changer and is out unless a deck is excepted.

Both cards are 6–7 mana, do nothing the turn they enter, and the first cash-in is the next upkeep. Drawing that many without a replacement effect loses. Blue already has better draw-and-win options than this battlecruiser pair, so it stays here instead of a `decks/` folder.

Commander was never locked ([Tekuthal, Inquiry Dominus](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/78/tekuthal-inquiry-dominus), [Atraxa, Praetors' Voice](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/190/atraxa-praetors-voice), [Ezuri, Stalker of Spheres](https://scryfall.com/card/one/201/ezuri-stalker-of-spheres), [Kilo, Apogee Mind](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/3/kilo-apogee-mind)).
