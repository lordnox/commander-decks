# 8×8 / cube-style packages — working notes

Community heuristic, not an official rule. Repeated in many 2025–2026
"how to build a Commander deck" roundups (for example Spellweave's 2026
guide). Same shape as commandertemplate's "eight cards per theme package."

Snapshot **2026-09-14**.

## The idea

After lands (and usually the commander), pick about **eight named jobs** and
put about **eight cards** in each so a 100-card singleton list actually draws
the job:

Example jobs: ramp, draw, removal, wipes, synergy, finishers, protection,
utility.

8 × 8 = 64 nonlands. Plus ~35 lands and the commander is 100. More lands
means trimming a package.

## Why it helps here

A theme of three cards is a hope. Eight functional copies of a *job* (not
eight copies of one card) is closer to "I will see this plan." That matches
how this repo already wants named packages in `design-deck` / `autobrew-deck`.

## Why it is not a law

Real decks merge jobs onto one card, run 10–16 of a single verb, or skip
wipes on purpose. Use 8×8 to notice a package that is too thin, not to pad
a joke with seven extra lords.
