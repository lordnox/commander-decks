## How the deck works

The deck does not hide behind a pillow fort. It puts creatures on the battlefield that opponents can see and bargain around. [**Maddening Imp**](https://scryfall.com/card/tmp/143/maddening-imp?utm_source=api), [**Nettling Imp**](https://scryfall.com/card/sum/119/nettling-imp?utm_source=api), and [**Rage Nimbus**](https://scryfall.com/card/roe/160/rage-nimbus?utm_source=api) nominate attackers. [**Goblin Spymaster**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/795/goblin-spymaster?utm_source=api), [**Life of the Party**](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/48/life-of-the-party?utm_source=api), [**Rite of the Raging Storm**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/159/rite-of-the-raging-storm?utm_source=api), and [**Angel's Trumpet**](https://scryfall.com/card/ulg/121/angels-trumpet?utm_source=api) ensure combat exists. [**Windshaper Planetar**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/50/windshaper-planetar?utm_source=api) then decides where a declared attack actually lands.

The defense is made of creatures rather than counters. [**Mine Bearer**](https://scryfall.com/card/pcy/16/mine-bearer?utm_source=api), [**Kami of False Hope**](https://scryfall.com/card/bok/10/kami-of-false-hope?utm_source=api), and [**Children of Korlis**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/14/children-of-korlis?utm_source=api) sacrifice themselves. [**Flumph**](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/15/flumph?utm_source=api) turns routed damage into shared cards, [**Souls of the Faultless**](https://scryfall.com/card/gpt/131/souls-of-the-faultless?utm_source=api) drains its attacker, and [**High Priest of Penance**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/848/high-priest-of-penance?utm_source=api) converts a hit into destruction of any nonland permanent. [**Invisible Force Field**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/19/invisible-force-field?utm_source=api) keeps four permanents alive through the resulting disaster and rebounds for a second [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) trigger.

With [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) in play, an instant or sorcery retrieves one nonlegendary creature from the graveyard. Sacrifice a creature to [**Worthy Cause**](https://scryfall.com/card/tmp/53/worthy-cause?utm_source=api), [**Burnt Offering**](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/116/burnt-offering?utm_source=api), [**Skulltap**](https://scryfall.com/card/scg/73/skulltap?utm_source=api), [**Deadly Dispute**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/794/deadly-dispute?utm_source=api), [**Corrupted Conviction**](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/84/corrupted-conviction?utm_source=api), or [**Spontaneous Combustion**](https://scryfall.com/card/cns/194/spontaneous-combustion?utm_source=api); the sacrificed creature is already in the graveyard when magecraft chooses a target, so it comes straight back to hand. The two new draw spells make retrieving and recasting creatures sustainable rather than merely clever.

## Main contraptions

### Sacrifice, trigger, retrieve

Have [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) and any nonlegendary creature in play. Cast [**Deadly Dispute**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/794/deadly-dispute?utm_source=api), sacrificing that creature as the additional cost. After the spell is cast, [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) triggers and can target the creature now in the graveyard. Resolve magecraft to return it, then resolve [Deadly Dispute](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/794/deadly-dispute?utm_source=api) to draw two and create a Treasure that helps recast it. [**Skulltap**](https://scryfall.com/card/scg/73/skulltap?utm_source=api), [**Corrupted Conviction**](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/84/corrupted-conviction?utm_source=api), [**Worthy Cause**](https://scryfall.com/card/tmp/53/worthy-cause?utm_source=api), [**Burnt Offering**](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/116/burnt-offering?utm_source=api), and [**Spontaneous Combustion**](https://scryfall.com/card/cns/194/spontaneous-combustion?utm_source=api) follow the same timing.

[**Body Snatcher**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/75/body-snatcher?utm_source=api) makes the line stranger. Sacrifice it to one of those spells, put its dies trigger on the stack first and [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api)'s trigger above it, then return [Body Snatcher](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/75/body-snatcher?utm_source=api) to hand. Its dies trigger can still return another target creature card from the graveyard to the battlefield.

### Route the punch

Prepare [**Shaman en-Kor**](https://scryfall.com/card/sth/15/shaman-en-kor?utm_source=api) by activating its `{0}` ability once for each point expected, targeting [**Mogg Maniac**](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/STH-94/mogg-maniac?utm_source=api), [**Spitemare**](https://scryfall.com/card/ddh/16/spitemare?utm_source=api), or [**Stuffy Doll**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/875/stuffy-doll?utm_source=api). Its `{1}{W}` ability can route damage aimed at another creature into the Shaman; the prepared one-point shields then send that damage onward into the reflector. [**Blaze of Glory**](https://scryfall.com/card/30a/303/blaze-of-glory?utm_source=api) takes the combat version: make an indestructible [Stuffy Doll](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/875/stuffy-doll?utm_source=api) or [Brash Taunter](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/161/brash-taunter?utm_source=api) block every attacker it legally can.

### Copy the paperwork

Casting [**Fork**](https://scryfall.com/card/30a/445/fork?utm_source=api) triggers [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) once. When [Fork](https://scryfall.com/card/30a/445/fork?utm_source=api) resolves and copies another spell, the created copy triggers [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) again. [**Dualcaster Mage**](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/165/dualcaster-mage?utm_source=api) produces one magecraft trigger from its copy.

### Make one damage event become everyone's problem

Cast [**Arcbond**](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/91/arcbond?utm_source=api) on a creature that is about to be damaged. Each time it is dealt damage that turn, it deals that much damage to every other creature and every player. Reflectors damaged by that spray add their own triggers; a reflector that sends damage back into the Arcbonded creature starts another spray. The chain is not automatically infinite because [Boros Reckoner](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/201/boros-reckoner?utm_source=api), [Spitemare](https://scryfall.com/card/ddh/16/spitemare?utm_source=api), and [Brash Taunter](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/161/brash-taunter?utm_source=api) can choose other targets, but careless choices can kill every remaining player.

[**Pain for All**](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/151/pain-for-all?utm_source=api) is the controlled version. It makes the enchanted creature deal its power to another target on entry, then every later hit to the enchanted creature damages each opponent without also hitting us or the board. Aim the entry punch at a second reflector when available; that reflector can send damage back into the enchanted creature and start [Pain for All](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/151/pain-for-all?utm_source=api).

## Win conditions

1. **An opponent's enormous creature.** [**Mirror Strike**](https://scryfall.com/card/pcy/17/mirror-strike?utm_source=api), [**Reflect Damage**](https://scryfall.com/card/mir/277/reflect-damage?utm_source=api), [**Delirium**](https://scryfall.com/card/mir/260/delirium?utm_source=api), [**Backlash**](https://scryfall.com/card/inv/234/backlash?utm_source=api), [**Deflecting Palm**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/205/deflecting-palm?utm_source=api), and [**Boros Fury-Shield**](https://scryfall.com/card/rav/5/boros-fury-shield?utm_source=api) all turn somebody else's power into the kill.
2. **[Arcbond](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/91/arcbond?utm_source=api) cascade.** Put [Arcbond](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/91/arcbond?utm_source=api) on a creature that will survive or meaningfully distribute the first hit, then damage it with combat, [Volcano Hellion](https://scryfall.com/card/plc/111/volcano-hellion?utm_source=api), [Crypt Rats](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/84/crypt-rats?utm_source=api), or a reflector. Every spray can wake the rest of the reflector board.
3. **Pain for everyone else.** Enchant [Brash Taunter](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/161/brash-taunter?utm_source=api), [Boros Reckoner](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/201/boros-reckoner?utm_source=api), [Spitemare](https://scryfall.com/card/ddh/16/spitemare?utm_source=api), or another creature likely to take damage with [Pain for All](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/151/pain-for-all?utm_source=api). Aim its entry punch at a second reflector when available, then route later damage into the enchanted creature so each opponent takes the same amount.
4. **[Repercussion](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/95/repercussion?utm_source=api) mathematics.** Every creature hit by [Crypt Rats](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/84/crypt-rats?utm_source=api) or [Spontaneous Combustion](https://scryfall.com/card/cns/194/spontaneous-combustion?utm_source=api) sends that much damage to its controller. Count our own creatures before pulling the lever.
5. **[Guilty conscience](https://scryfall.com/card/scg/17/guilty-conscience?utm_source=api).** Enchant [Stuffy Doll](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/875/stuffy-doll?utm_source=api) with [**Guilty Conscience**](https://scryfall.com/card/scg/17/guilty-conscience?utm_source=api), then tap Stuffy to deal 1 to itself. The mandatory chain repeats until the named opponent leaves. [Brash Taunter](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/161/brash-taunter?utm_source=api) creates the same chain after another source deals the first damage to it.
6. **The wide attack.** [Inkshield](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/316/inkshield?utm_source=api) turns combat damage into flying attackers; [Batwing Brume](https://scryfall.com/card/arc/80/batwing-brume?utm_source=api) can drain a player who sends enough creatures, while [Windshaper Planetar](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/50/windshaper-planetar?utm_source=api) can send the whole attack to somebody else.

## Early, mid, and late game

**Turns 1–3.** Develop colored mana and play one visible trap or provocateur. [Flumph](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/15/flumph?utm_source=api), [High Priest of Penance](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/848/high-priest-of-penance?utm_source=api), [Souls of the Faultless](https://scryfall.com/card/gpt/131/souls-of-the-faultless?utm_source=api), and [Mine Bearer](https://scryfall.com/card/pcy/16/mine-bearer?utm_source=api) all change combat while giving [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) a useful creature to retrieve later. Do not deploy a reflector without a reason to expect damage.

**Turns 4–7.** Cast [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) when at least one instant or sorcery is available and a useful creature can reach the graveyard. [Deadly Dispute](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/794/deadly-dispute?utm_source=api) and [Corrupted Conviction](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/84/corrupted-conviction?utm_source=api) are the preferred first engines because they replace themselves while retrieving the sacrifice. Keep deals explicit: the Advocates can save any player, [Duelist's Heritage](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/153/duelists-heritage?utm_source=api) can make an attacker lethal, and [Windshaper Planetar](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/50/windshaper-planetar?utm_source=api) can change every defender after attacks are declared.

**Turn 8 onward.** Assemble whichever unsafe machine the draw supplied. The deck has no deterministic search path, so the table must read the actual permanents: [Arcbond](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/91/arcbond?utm_source=api) routing, [Pain for All](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/151/pain-for-all?utm_source=api), [Repercussion](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/95/repercussion?utm_source=api), [Guilty Conscience](https://scryfall.com/card/scg/17/guilty-conscience?utm_source=api), or an [Inkshield](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/316/inkshield?utm_source=api) counterattack. The correct finish changes every game.

## Mulligan guide

Keep three lands with white and double-black for [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api), preferably with red available too, plus two useful early plays. An ideal hand has one disposable creature and [Deadly Dispute](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/794/deadly-dispute?utm_source=api), [Corrupted Conviction](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/84/corrupted-conviction?utm_source=api), [Skulltap](https://scryfall.com/card/scg/73/skulltap?utm_source=api), or another cheap instant.

- Keep [**Humble Defector**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/170/humble-defector?utm_source=api) with a sacrifice spell: activate it, sacrifice it while the draw ability is on the stack, retrieve it with [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api), then draw without donating it.
- Keep a reflector only when the hand can route or manufacture damage.
- Ship hands that are only five-mana reactions. The deck needs creatures in play before its expensive tricks become credible.
- Ship two-land hands without another draw effect. [Burnt Offering](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/116/burnt-offering?utm_source=api) is not stable ramp.

## Search priorities

There are no card tutors and no land-search spells. Scry decisions prioritize:

1. the missing colors for [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api);
2. a cheap creature that can trade or sacrifice itself;
3. a draw spell or instant that triggers magecraft;
4. a reflector only after damage routing or a damage source is available.

## Important sequencing and rules

- A creature sacrificed or discarded as an additional cost is already in the graveyard when [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api)'s magecraft trigger is put on the stack, so that creature is a legal target.
- [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) returns creatures to hand, not to the battlefield. Budget mana for redeployment.
- [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api)'s trigger is mandatory when a legal nonlegendary creature card exists in the graveyard.
- [**Volcano Hellion**](https://scryfall.com/card/plc/111/volcano-hellion?utm_source=api) deals the chosen amount to both us and its target, and that damage cannot be prevented. Stay above the chosen amount; delayed life gain cannot save a player from lethal damage.
- [Arcbond](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/91/arcbond?utm_source=api)'s delayed trigger only sees damage actually dealt. Prevention stops it, and the Arcbonded creature's controller is among the players hit by every spray.
- [Pain for All](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/151/pain-for-all?utm_source=api) damages each opponent, not us, and does not hit other creatures. It is safer but cannot create [Arcbond](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/91/arcbond?utm_source=api)'s board-wide chain.
- [Blaze of Glory](https://scryfall.com/card/30a/303/blaze-of-glory?utm_source=api) does not remove evasion or attack restrictions; its target blocks only the attackers it legally can.
- A [Big Game Hunter](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/102/big-game-hunter?utm_source=api) or [Muck Drubb](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/126/muck-drubb?utm_source=api) cast for madness moves from discard to exile, so [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) cannot retrieve it from that discard.
- Attack requirements never force a player to pay a cost such as a tax. If an attack requires a payment, that creature may stay home.
- [Awaken the Blood Avatar](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) is not a hidden backup plan. Leave the card front-face up in the command zone.

## Weaknesses

The deck is intentionally inefficient. It still has little conventional ramp and needs both creatures and spells for [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) to generate cards. Graveyard exile interrupts retrieval; exile removal bypasses most creature recycling; enchantment removal disconnects the loudest finishers. [Wear // Tear](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/343/wear-tear?utm_source=api) and [High Priest of Penance](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/848/high-priest-of-penance?utm_source=api) can answer problematic permanents, but neither is guaranteed without tutors.

Protect [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) only when a magecraft turn is ready. Preserve [**Shaman en-Kor**](https://scryfall.com/card/sth/15/shaman-en-kor?utm_source=api) when it is the only routing piece, and avoid exposing [Repercussion](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/95/repercussion?utm_source=api) until its damage can matter immediately. Most importantly, count every player and every creature before resolving a table-wide damage event.
