A Grixis Doctor's-companion list. Cast the first spell each turn from anywhere but your hand so [**The Twelfth Doctor**](https://scryfall.com/card/who/164/the-twelfth-doctor?utm_source=api) demonstrates it: you keep a copy, an opponent gets a copy. The payload is lose-the-game extra turns and pacts. [**Obeka, Brute Chronologist**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/289/obeka-brute-chronologist?utm_source=api), [**Sundial of the Infinite**](https://scryfall.com/card/m12/218/sundial-of-the-infinite?utm_source=api), and [**Discontinuity**](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/48/discontinuity?utm_source=api) exile your own bill off the stack. [**Vislor Turlough**](https://scryfall.com/card/who/74/vislor-turlough?utm_source=api) adds black, draws every end step, and is cheap to empty-hand around.

## How the deck works

The Doctor only demonstrates the first spell you cast from a zone other than your hand each turn. Foretell, plot, flashback, play-from-top, and impulse exile all count. Cast from hand does not.

Develop rocks so a turn-four or turn-five Doctor is realistic, then keep a not-from-hand window open. [**Dream Devourer**](https://scryfall.com/card/khm/90/dream-devourer?utm_source=api) and [**Bohn, Beguiling Balladeer**](https://scryfall.com/card/slx/30/bohn-beguiling-balladeer?utm_source=api) turn the whole hand into foretell. [**The Reality Chip**](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/74/the-reality-chip?utm_source=api), [**Johann, Apprentice Sorcerer**](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/207/johann-apprentice-sorcerer?utm_source=api), [**The Lunar Whale**](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/60/the-lunar-whale?utm_source=api), [**The Belligerent**](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/225/the-belligerent?utm_source=api), and [**Into the Pit**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/20/into-the-pit?utm_source=api) cast from the top. [**Kess, Dissident Mage**](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/344/kess-dissident-mage?utm_source=api), [**Emet-Selch of the Third Seat**](https://scryfall.com/card/fic/81/emet-selch-of-the-third-seat?utm_source=api), [**Vohar, Vodalian Desecrator**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/226/vohar-vodalian-desecrator?utm_source=api), and [**Sphinx of Forgotten Lore**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/51/sphinx-of-forgotten-lore?utm_source=api) recast from the graveyard. [**Prosper, Tome-Bound**](https://scryfall.com/card/afc/2/prosper-tome-bound?utm_source=api), [**Sanar, Innovative First-Year**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/241/sanar-innovative-first-year?utm_source=api), [**Hidetsugu, Devouring Chaos**](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/99/hidetsugu-devouring-chaos?utm_source=api), and the Hidden caves discover or impulse into exile.

Once that window is live, spend it on a doom spell, not on a random cantrip, unless you need the Doctor's +1/+1 counters or a copy of interaction.

## Main lines

### Demonstrate a doom turn

1. Have the Doctor in play and a way to cast from exile, graveyard, or library.
2. Cast [**Final Fortune**](https://scryfall.com/card/7ed/182/final-fortune?utm_source=api), [**Last Chance**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/127/last-chance?utm_source=api), [**Warrior's Oath**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/130/warriors-oath?utm_source=api), or [**Alchemist's Gambit**](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/140/alchemists-gambit?utm_source=api) from that zone.
3. Demonstrate: keep a copy, give a copy to an opponent. Copies resolve before the original, so that opponent's extra turn is next, then yours.
4. The opponent's copy makes them lose at the end of *their* extra turn. Yours does the same to you unless you skip the bill.

[**Glorious End**](https://scryfall.com/card/akh/133/glorious-end?utm_source=api) and [**Sudden Substitution**](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/11/sudden-substitution?utm_source=api) are the same idea on someone else's turn: end a lethal stack, then either eat your own delayed trigger or hand them the spell.

This is not infinite. Each extra-turn card is one extra turn (two if demonstrated). [**Kess, Dissident Mage**](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/344/kess-dissident-mage?utm_source=api) can recast one instant or sorcery from the graveyard on each of your turns, so a resolved extra turn plus Kess can chain, still one card at a time.

### Skip your own bill

When your extra-turn lose trigger is on the stack at the end step, tap [**Obeka, Brute Chronologist**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/289/obeka-brute-chronologist?utm_source=api) or pay `{1}` and tap [**Sundial of the Infinite**](https://scryfall.com/card/m12/218/sundial-of-the-infinite?utm_source=api). Ending the turn exiles that trigger. [**Discontinuity**](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/48/discontinuity?utm_source=api) does the same at instant speed; on your turn it costs `{1}{U}`.

Ending the turn *before* the end step means the trigger never happens. Pact upkeep triggers only go away if you end the turn *during* that upkeep with the trigger on the stack.

[**Nivmagus Elemental**](https://scryfall.com/card/rtr/219/nivmagus-elemental?utm_source=api) and [**Synthesis Pod**](https://scryfall.com/card/onc/23/synthesis-pod?utm_source=api) exile *your* doom spell so it never resolves, which means your delayed trigger is never created. Demonstrate already copied it on cast, so the opponent keeps their copy.

[**Lich's Mastery**](https://scryfall.com/card/dom/98/lichs-mastery?utm_source=api) ("You can't lose the game") turns every pact and extra-turn clause off while it remains. If it leaves, you lose. [**Marina Vendrell's Grimoire**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/64/marina-vendrells-grimoire?utm_source=api) only stops losing at 0 life; it does not stop "you lose the game" from [Final Fortune](https://scryfall.com/card/7ed/182/final-fortune?utm_source=api).

### [Hive Mind](https://scryfall.com/card/m10/54/hive-mind?utm_source=api) pacts

[**Hive Mind**](https://scryfall.com/card/m10/54/hive-mind?utm_source=api) copies every instant or sorcery for each other player. [**Pact of Negation**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/77/pact-of-negation?utm_source=api), [**Slaughter Pact**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/138/slaughter-pact?utm_source=api), and [**Pact of the Titan**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/179/pact-of-the-titan?utm_source=api) are free now and bill their controller on the next upkeep. Opponents who cannot pay lose on their upkeep. You still owe your own pact unless you pay, skip that upkeep, or have [Lich's Mastery](https://scryfall.com/card/dom/98/lichs-mastery?utm_source=api).

[Hive Mind](https://scryfall.com/card/m10/54/hive-mind?utm_source=api) plus an extra-turn spell gives every opponent their own extra turn and their own lose trigger. That is chaotic, not a deterministic table kill, unless they also cannot dodge the trigger.

## Win conditions

- Demonstrated extra-turn spells: one opponent loses at the end of the extra turn they were given.
- Your own extra turns, with Obeka, Sundial, [Discontinuity](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/48/discontinuity?utm_source=api), Nivmagus, or [Lich's Mastery](https://scryfall.com/card/dom/98/lichs-mastery?utm_source=api) skipping the bill, then pacts, combat with a grown Doctor, or another demonstrated doom spell.
- [Hive Mind](https://scryfall.com/card/m10/54/hive-mind?utm_source=api) plus unpaid pacts on opponents' upkeeps.
- [**Ensnared by the Mara**](https://scryfall.com/card/who/84/ensnared-by-the-mara?utm_source=api) and [**Prisoner's Dilemma**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/34/prisoners-dilemma?utm_source=api) as damage backups when the table will not take a gifted extra turn.

## Early, mid, and late game

**Early:** Keep two or three lands and a rock. [**Sol Ring**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/211/sol-ring?utm_source=api), Signets, and Talismans aim at a turn-four Doctor. A [**Dream Devourer**](https://scryfall.com/card/khm/90/dream-devourer?utm_source=api), [**The Reality Chip**](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/74/the-reality-chip?utm_source=api), or [**Sundial of the Infinite**](https://scryfall.com/card/m12/218/sundial-of-the-infinite?utm_source=api) in the opener is worth more than a third doom spell. [**Vislor Turlough**](https://scryfall.com/card/who/74/vislor-turlough?utm_source=api) can come down first if you need black and the extra card; dump the hand with looters or [**One with Nothing**](https://scryfall.com/card/sok/84/one-with-nothing?utm_source=api) before the end-step drain hurts.

**Mid:** Cast the Doctor, open a not-from-hand window, and look for one extra-turn spell plus an end-the-turn piece. Hold [**Pact of Negation**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/77/pact-of-negation?utm_source=api), [**Flusterstorm**](https://scryfall.com/card/ima/55/flusterstorm?utm_source=api), [**Spider-Sense**](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/46/spider-sense?utm_source=api), or [**Double Negative**](https://scryfall.com/card/arb/87/double-negative?utm_source=api) for the turn you demonstrate. [**Scheming Symmetry**](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/113/scheming-symmetry?utm_source=api) puts the missing piece on top, where this deck can cast it.

**Late:** [**Kess, Dissident Mage**](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/344/kess-dissident-mage?utm_source=api) and [**Emet-Selch of the Third Seat**](https://scryfall.com/card/fic/81/emet-selch-of-the-third-seat?utm_source=api) replay spent extra turns. [**Hive Mind**](https://scryfall.com/card/m10/54/hive-mind?utm_source=api) is the six-mana closer. [**Lich's Mastery**](https://scryfall.com/card/dom/98/lichs-mastery?utm_source=api) is the insurance policy if Obeka and Sundial are gone. [**Sea Gate Restoration**](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/76/sea-gate-restoration-sea-gate-reborn?utm_source=api) refills after you have emptied the hand for Vislor.

## Mulligan guide

Keep:

- Two or three lands, a two-mana rock, and either a not-from-hand enabler or Sundial/Obeka plus a doom spell.
- [**Sol Ring**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/211/sol-ring?utm_source=api) with a Signet or Talisman.
- [**Dream Devourer**](https://scryfall.com/card/khm/90/dream-devourer?utm_source=api) or [**The Reality Chip**](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/74/the-reality-chip?utm_source=api) plus lands that make `{U}` or `{B}`.

Ship:

- All doom spells and no way to cast them off-hand or to skip the lose trigger.
- One land, no rock, five-drops.
- A [**Hive Mind**](https://scryfall.com/card/m10/54/hive-mind?utm_source=api) / [**Lich's Mastery**](https://scryfall.com/card/dom/98/lichs-mastery?utm_source=api) pile with no early mana.

## Important sequencing and rules notes

- Demonstrate triggers on **cast**, so copies exist even if you later exile the original with Nivmagus or [Synthesis Pod](https://scryfall.com/card/onc/23/synthesis-pod?utm_source=api).
- Extra-turn copies resolve first: opponent's extra turn, then yours. Each lose trigger stays with its controller.
- On your turn, [**Discontinuity**](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/48/discontinuity?utm_source=api) costs `{1}{U}` after the discount.
- [**Sudden Substitution**](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/11/sudden-substitution?utm_source=api) has split second. Exchange your [Final Fortune](https://scryfall.com/card/7ed/182/final-fortune?utm_source=api) or [Glorious End](https://scryfall.com/card/akh/133/glorious-end?utm_source=api) on the stack for a creature; they become the spell's controller.
- [**Pact of Negation**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/77/pact-of-negation?utm_source=api) cannot be demonstrated if you cast it from hand to protect the combo. Foretell it, or recast it with Kess, if you want the Doctor to copy it.
- [**Alchemist's Gambit**](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/140/alchemists-gambit?utm_source=api) still has the lose clause unless you pay the cleave cost. Demonstrate copies include the uncleaved text.

## Weaknesses and what to protect

The Doctor is five mana and does nothing if every spell is cast from hand. Counterspells, graveyard exile, and "play only from hand" effects shut the engine off. [**Obeka, Brute Chronologist**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/289/obeka-brute-chronologist?utm_source=api) and [**Sundial of the Infinite**](https://scryfall.com/card/m12/218/sundial-of-the-infinite?utm_source=api) are the life-support for extra turns; remove them and the next [Final Fortune](https://scryfall.com/card/7ed/182/final-fortune?utm_source=api) is suicidal. [**Lich's Mastery**](https://scryfall.com/card/dom/98/lichs-mastery?utm_source=api) is a second lose condition if it dies. The list has little board presence before the Doctor grows, so tokens and go-wide decks race it. Mass land denial is not the plan; [**Sundering Eruption**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/248/sundering-eruption-volcanic-fissure?utm_source=api) is a single Strip Mine.

Protect the Doctor on the turn you demonstrate a doom spell. Protect Obeka or Sundial whenever you have an extra turn on the stack. Do not demonstrate [**Hive Mind**](https://scryfall.com/card/m10/54/hive-mind?utm_source=api) copies of extra turns into a player who can end the turn themselves.
