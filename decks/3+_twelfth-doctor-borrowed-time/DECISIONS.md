# Decisions

## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, the primer, and the assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a cut back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated session notes so an argument does not live only in chat.

Every unique card needs a Cards in line. The other headings appear when there is something to record.

## Rules

- **Demonstrate copies of pacts and doom turns** — A copy of Pact of Negation, Slaughter Pact, or Final Fortune that resolves for a player sets up that player's delayed trigger: "you" in the resolved copy refers to its controller. A demonstrated Final Fortune gives the chosen opponent the extra turn *and* the lose-at-end-step trigger; a Hive Mind copy of a pact makes each copying player owe the upkeep payment.
- **Ending the turn erases your bill** — Final Fortune-style triggers ("at the beginning of that turn's end step, you lose") are delayed triggers in that extra turn. Obeka, Sundial of the Infinite, or Discontinuity ending the turn while that trigger is on the stack exiles it (CR 728.1a); ending the turn *before* the end step means the trigger never happens at all. Pact upkeep triggers work the same way only if the turn is ended during your upkeep with the trigger on the stack.
- **Nivmagus Elemental / Synthesis Pod vs. your own spells** — Exiling your own doom spell off the stack (activated cost or ability) means it never resolves, so its delayed lose trigger is never created — but demonstrate already made the copies when the spell was *cast*, so opponents keep theirs.
- **Extra-turn order** — When you demonstrate an extra-turn spell, the copy resolves first (it was created on top of the stack): turn order is your extra turn last, the opponent's copied turn first. Both lose triggers stay with their own turn's controller.
- **The Twelfth Doctor sees zones, not prices** — Foretell (exile), plot (exile), flashback and Kess/Vohar/Emet-Selch (graveyard), Johann/Reality Chip/Lunar Whale/Belligerent/Into the Pit (library), Jeleva/Prosper/Hidetsugu/Sanar/discover (exile), and MDFC spells cast off Bloodsoaked Insight all count as "cast from anywhere other than your hand." Only the *first* such cast each turn demonstrates.

## Talks

### 2026-08-27 — Assessment

- Official sources: [Commander format page](https://magic.wizards.com/en/formats/commander) (Game Changer caps: 0 in Brackets 1–2, up to 3 in Bracket 3, unlimited in 4–5), [Introducing Commander Brackets Beta](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/introducing-commander-brackets-beta) (Bracket 3 extra turns in low quantities, not chained), [October 21, 2025 bracket update](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-october-21-2025) (pace: at least six turns in Bracket 3, four in Bracket 4), [February 9, 2026 update](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-february-9-2026) (intent over checklist; Farewell and Biorhythm added to Game Changers). Scryfall `is:gamechanger` (53 cards) matched **none** of this 100.
- Extra-turn barometer is the reason this is not a middle Bracket 3: Final Fortune, Last Chance, Warrior's Oath, and Alchemist's Gambit, plus Obeka / Sundial / Discontinuity to skip the lose trigger, plus Kess recasts. Hive Mind plus pacts is a second table-ending package. None of that is a two-card infinite.
- Pace is still Bracket 3: five-mana Doctor, no Game Changers, no fast combo. A representative goldfish threatens around turn seven. Sol Ring into Sundial or Obeka can start extra turns on turn four; that line is extra turns, not a table kill, and is the exceptional note in the pregame sentence.
- Not Bracket 4: the list is not built to win as fast as possible, and a typical game still gives everyone six turns. Not Bracket 2: extra-turn density and free pacts are above Core expectations.
- Oracle checks for the primer: demonstrate copies of extra-turn spells give the chosen opponent that turn and that lose trigger; ending the turn with the trigger on the stack exiles it (CR 728.1a); Discontinuity is `{1}{U}` on your turn; Lich's Mastery blanks lose-the-game, Marina Vendrell's Grimoire does not; Nivmagus / Synthesis Pod stop *your* spell from resolving after copies already exist.
