A Mardu masquerade where a crowd of anonymous face-down 2/2s hides four elaborate Extreme Plans. Carl ([Bruse Tarl](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/30/bruse-tarl-boorish-herder?utm_source=api)) makes one crawler lethal for a single swing; Princess Donut ([Falthis](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/126/falthis-shadowcat-familiar?utm_source=api)) makes the commanders themselves unblockable-adjacent killers. The table is goaded into hunting each other while you decide which mask comes off.

## How the deck works

Almost every creature can be cast face down for {3} as a nameless 2/2. That does three things at once: it deploys on curve regardless of colour, it hides which threat you actually drew, and it builds a board that opponents cannot evaluate. A face-down creature has no name, no types, and no abilities, so nothing on the other side of the table knows whether it is a 1/1 Cleric or Akroma.

The deck then unmasks at the moment of maximum damage. Turning a creature face up is not casting it, so it does not use the stack and cannot be countered, and it can happen mid-combat after blockers are declared.

Two cards convert that from a trick into an engine. [Skirk Alarmist](https://scryfall.com/card/lgn/111/skirk-alarmist?utm_source=api) turns a face-down creature face up **for free**, ignoring its morph cost entirely, and [Panoptic Projektor](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/44/panoptic-projektor?utm_source=api) makes every turn-face-up trigger happen **twice**. Together they mean the expensive unmask costs printed on the best morphs never have to be paid, and the payoff is doubled when it lands.

The masks also pay for themselves. A face-down creature spell has mana value 0, so **every** crawler you cast triggers [Bygone Bishop](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/689/bygone-bishop?utm_source=api) for a Clue. Together with Roshan drawing when a permanent is unmasked and [Grim Haruspex](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/754/grim-haruspex?utm_source=api) drawing when a creature dies, the deck draws at all three stages of a mask's life: cast, flipped, killed.

Around that sit the two commanders and the Hunt. [Bruse Tarl](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/30/bruse-tarl-boorish-herder?utm_source=api) grants double strike and lifelink to one creature when he enters or attacks. [Falthis](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/126/falthis-shadowcat-familiar?utm_source=api) gives both commanders menace and deathtouch, which makes them nearly impossible to block profitably. Goad effects push the rest of the table into attacking each other so your masked crowd stays untouched.

## Extreme Plans

Four assembled plans, each five cards. None is an infinite loop; each is a telegraphed, finite line that the table can see coming and interact with.

### Plan 1 — The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook

**Pieces:** [Panoptic Projektor](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/44/panoptic-projektor?utm_source=api) · [Skirk Alarmist](https://scryfall.com/card/lgn/111/skirk-alarmist?utm_source=api) · [Ponyback Brigade](https://scryfall.com/card/ktk/191/ponyback-brigade?utm_source=api) (face down) · [Ashcloud Phoenix](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/147/ashcloud-phoenix?utm_source=api) (face down) · [Goblin Bombardment](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/279/goblin-bombardment?utm_source=api)

Tap [Skirk Alarmist](https://scryfall.com/card/lgn/111/skirk-alarmist?utm_source=api) to flip [Ponyback Brigade](https://scryfall.com/card/ktk/191/ponyback-brigade?utm_source=api) for free. Its turn-face-up trigger makes three Goblins, and the Projektor triggers it a second time for six Goblins total. On a later turn, flip [Ashcloud Phoenix](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/147/ashcloud-phoenix?utm_source=api) the same way: 2 damage to each player, doubled to 4. Then feed the Goblins to [Goblin Bombardment](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/279/goblin-bombardment?utm_source=api), one damage per body, aimed at whoever is closest to dead. The Phoenix returns face down when it dies, so it can be flipped again later.

**Output:** 4 damage to every opponent plus at least 6 damage aimed at one of them, from a board that looked like four anonymous 2/2s.

### Plan 2 — The Mask Comes Off

**Pieces:** [Master of Pearls](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/73/master-of-pearls?utm_source=api) (face down) · [Skirk Alarmist](https://scryfall.com/card/lgn/111/skirk-alarmist?utm_source=api) · [Panoptic Projektor](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/44/panoptic-projektor?utm_source=api) · [Veiled Ascension](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/18/veiled-ascension?utm_source=api) · [Bruse Tarl](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/30/bruse-tarl-boorish-herder?utm_source=api)

[Veiled Ascension](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/18/veiled-ascension?utm_source=api) puts a flying counter on every face-down creature you control, and on each one that enters afterwards, so the anonymous crowd is suddenly evasive. Attack with everything. After blockers are declared, tap [Skirk Alarmist](https://scryfall.com/card/lgn/111/skirk-alarmist?utm_source=api) to flip [Master of Pearls](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/73/master-of-pearls?utm_source=api) for free: creatures you control get +2/+2, doubled by the Projektor to **+4/+4**. [Bruse Tarl](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/30/bruse-tarl-boorish-herder?utm_source=api)'s attack trigger gives one of them double strike and lifelink.

**Output:** A board of 6/6 fliers with a 12/12-equivalent double striker, arriving after blocks. Five face-down creatures is 30 damage in the air.

### Plan 3 — Half, Then Half Again

**Pieces:** [Ebonblade Reaper](https://scryfall.com/card/ons/141/ebonblade-reaper?utm_source=api) · [Bruse Tarl](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/30/bruse-tarl-boorish-herder?utm_source=api) · [Rogue's Passage](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/212/rogues-passage?utm_source=api) · [Master of Pearls](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/73/master-of-pearls?utm_source=api) · [Gift of Doom](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/17/gift-of-doom?utm_source=api)

[Ebonblade Reaper](https://scryfall.com/card/ons/141/ebonblade-reaper?utm_source=api) halves a player's life whenever it deals combat damage to them, rounded up. [Bruse Tarl](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/30/bruse-tarl-boorish-herder?utm_source=api) gives it double strike, which is two separate damage events and therefore **two halvings**: a player at 40 goes to 20, then to 10. [Rogue's Passage](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/212/rogues-passage?utm_source=api) makes it unblockable so the damage cannot be chumped, [Master of Pearls](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/73/master-of-pearls?utm_source=api) keeps it out of range of one-toughness sweepers, and [Gift of Doom](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/17/gift-of-doom?utm_source=api)'s indestructible keeps it alive through a wipe.

**Cost:** Its attack trigger takes half *your* life, rounded up, every single time. This is the plan that hurts you as much as them, which is why it is Carl's.

### Plan 4 — Recruit the Hunters

**Pieces:** [Soul Collector](https://scryfall.com/card/tsb/47/soul-collector?utm_source=api) (face down) · [Basilisk Collar](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/669/basilisk-collar?utm_source=api) · [Bruse Tarl](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/30/bruse-tarl-boorish-herder?utm_source=api) · [Disrupt Decorum](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/151/disrupt-decorum?utm_source=api) · [Kardur, Doomscourge](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/223/kardur-doomscourge?utm_source=api)

Unmask [Soul Collector](https://scryfall.com/card/tsb/47/soul-collector?utm_source=api) and equip [Basilisk Collar](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/669/basilisk-collar?utm_source=api) for deathtouch and lifelink. Any creature it damages that dies that turn returns to the battlefield **under your control**. [Bruse Tarl](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/30/bruse-tarl-boorish-herder?utm_source=api)'s double strike lets it kill in the first-strike step and survive, and with [Disrupt Decorum](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/151/disrupt-decorum?utm_source=api) or Kardur goading the table, opponents' creatures are forced into attacks where [Soul Collector](https://scryfall.com/card/tsb/47/soul-collector?utm_source=api) can block them.

**Output:** The hunters change sides one at a time, and Kardur drains a life for every attacker that dies anywhere on the board.

## Early, mid, and late game

**Turns 1–3.** Play a land, a two-mana rock, and start putting face-down 2/2s on the board from turn three. Do not unmask anything yet. A face-down creature you never flip is still a real attacker and a real blocker, and the information you withhold is worth more than the two extra power.

**Turns 4–6.** Deploy the engine: [Panoptic Projektor](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/44/panoptic-projektor?utm_source=api), [Skirk Alarmist](https://scryfall.com/card/lgn/111/skirk-alarmist?utm_source=api), [Goblin Maskmaker](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/130/goblin-maskmaker?utm_source=api) or [Dream Chisel](https://scryfall.com/card/ons/308/dream-chisel?utm_source=api) to cheapen the crowd, Roshan for menace and draw. Start goading. This is also when you unmask the cheap utility morphs — [Skinthinner](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/129/skinthinner?utm_source=api) to destroy a nonblack creature, [Aphetto Exterminator](https://scryfall.com/card/lgn/59/aphetto-exterminator?utm_source=api) for -3/-3, [Skirk Marauder](https://scryfall.com/card/arc/48/skirk-marauder?utm_source=api) for 2 damage — because those flips are removal you already paid for.

**Turns 7+.** Assemble a plan and swing. The deck wants one big telegraphed turn, not incremental chip damage, so hold the [Master of Pearls](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/73/master-of-pearls?utm_source=api) flip until the attack that actually kills someone.

## Mulligan guide

Keep a hand with three lands and at least one of: a two-mana rock, a face-down body you can cast on turn three, or a tutor. The deck needs three colours by roughly turn four, so a two-land hand with no fixing is a mulligan even if the spells are strong.

Ship hands that are all six-drops, and ship hands with Extreme Plan pieces but no way to find the rest — a single combo piece with no tutor and no draw is a dead card for six turns.

## Sequencing and rules notes

- A face-down creature spell has no mana cost and mana value 0, so [Bygone Bishop](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/689/bygone-bishop?utm_source=api) investigates on every crawler you deploy regardless of what that card costs face up.
- Turning a creature face up with [Skirk Alarmist](https://scryfall.com/card/lgn/111/skirk-alarmist?utm_source=api) does **not** pay the morph cost. That is the entire reason the deck can run [Master of Pearls](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/73/master-of-pearls?utm_source=api) ({3}{W}{W} to unmask) and Akroma ({3}{R}{R}{R}) without ever affording those costs.
- [Skirk Alarmist](https://scryfall.com/card/lgn/111/skirk-alarmist?utm_source=api) sacrifices the creature it flipped at the beginning of the next end step. For [Master of Pearls](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/73/master-of-pearls?utm_source=api) the pump has already resolved and for [Ponyback Brigade](https://scryfall.com/card/ktk/191/ponyback-brigade?utm_source=api) the Goblins remain, so the cost is only the body.
- [Panoptic Projektor](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/44/panoptic-projektor?utm_source=api) doubles the *trigger*, not the flip. It also doubles Roshan's draw and [Ashcloud Phoenix](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/147/ashcloud-phoenix?utm_source=api)'s damage.
- Unmask after blockers are declared, not before. Flipping [Master of Pearls](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/73/master-of-pearls?utm_source=api) in your first main phase tells the table exactly what to block.
- [Ashcloud Phoenix](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/147/ashcloud-phoenix?utm_source=api) returns face down when it dies, and a face-down permanent has no abilities, so it will not return a second time from that state. Treat it as two uses, not a loop.
- [Ebonblade Reaper](https://scryfall.com/card/ons/141/ebonblade-reaper?utm_source=api)'s attack trigger is life *loss*, not damage, so [Fortune Thief](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/130/fortune-thief?utm_source=api) will not protect you from it.
- Goad does not stop a creature from blocking on other players' turns. It only forces attacks.
- [Falthis](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/126/falthis-shadowcat-familiar?utm_source=api) grants menace and deathtouch to commanders only. No other creature in the deck receives it.

## Weaknesses and what to protect

Graveyard exile hurts less than usual here, but **artifact removal is a real problem**: [Panoptic Projektor](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/44/panoptic-projektor?utm_source=api) is the single point of failure for three of the four Extreme Plans, and the deck has no way to recur it. Protect it with [Benevolent Bodyguard](https://scryfall.com/card/ema/4/benevolent-bodyguard?utm_source=api)'s protection grant on the attacker rather than the artifact, and be willing to run a plan at half output rather than waiting for a Projektor that is never coming back.

A sweeper on the turn before your alpha strike is the worst case, because face-down 2/2s die to almost every wipe and you lose the hidden information along with the bodies. [Gift of Doom](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/17/gift-of-doom?utm_source=api)'s indestructible protects exactly one creature; [Fortune Thief](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/130/fortune-thief?utm_source=api) keeps you alive but saves nothing on the board.

The deck is also slow to interact. Outside [Skinthinner](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/129/skinthinner?utm_source=api), [Aphetto Exterminator](https://scryfall.com/card/lgn/59/aphetto-exterminator?utm_source=api), [Terminate](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/189/terminate?utm_source=api), [Vindicate](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/294/vindicate?utm_source=api), and [Chaos Warp](https://scryfall.com/card/mbc/72/chaos-warp?utm_source=api) there is very little that answers a resolved threat at instant speed, so the goad package is doing double duty as defence: a creature forced to attack someone else is a creature that is not attacking you.
