# Bruse Tarl & Falthis — The Butcher's Masquerade: Decision Log

## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, the primer, and the assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a cut back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated session notes so an argument does not live only in chat.

## Cards in

### Commanders

- **Bruse Tarl, Boorish Herder** — Carl. A human who is not the strongest thing on the board but makes one crawler lethal for one swing: double strike and lifelink on enter or attack. Any replacement must grant a combat buff from the command zone without needing to survive.
- **Falthis, Shadowcat Familiar** — Princess Donut. The Cat that makes Carl dangerous rather than being dangerous herself: commanders get menace and deathtouch. A replacement must be a Cat that upgrades the other commander.

### Extreme Plans

- **Panoptic Projektor** — Doubles every turn-face-up trigger from a permanent you control. This is the card that turns unmasking into an Extreme Plan instead of a value blip.
- **Skirk Alarmist** — Turns a face-down creature face up for free, ignoring its morph cost, then sacrifices it at the next end step. The engine that makes expensive morph costs irrelevant.
- **Master of Pearls** — Flip for a team-wide +2/+2, doubled to +4/+4 by the Projektor. The alpha-strike button.
- **Ponyback Brigade** — Flip makes three Goblins, six with the Projektor. Fodder for the Bombardment and bodies for a Pearls swing.
- **Goblin Bombardment** — Free sacrifice outlet that converts crawler corpses into reach. The only way this deck kills a player it cannot attack.
- **Ashcloud Phoenix** — Flip deals 2 to each player, 4 with the Projektor, and it returns face down when it dies, so it can be re-flipped later.
- **Ebonblade Reaper** — Halves a player's life on combat damage, twice with double strike. Costs you half your life per attack, which is exactly Carl's kind of plan.
- **Gift of Doom** — Morph Aura that grants deathtouch and indestructible, flipped by sacrificing another creature. Protection and lethality on the attacker in one card.
- **Soul Collector** — Any creature it damages that dies comes back under your control. Paired with deathtouch, it recruits the hunters who block it.
- **Veiled Ascension** — Gives every face-down creature a flying counter, turning a crowd of anonymous 2/2s into an evasive army.
- **Rogue's Passage** — Makes one attacker unblockable, which is how Ebonblade Reaper connects.

### Masked crawlers

- **Akroma, Angel of Fury** — Uncounterable red finisher that can be deployed face down for {3} and unmasked later when the mana exists.
- **Aphetto Exterminator** — Flip gives a creature -3/-3. Removal disguised as a 2/2.
- **Bane of the Living** — Morph board wipe for {X}{B}{B} whose size you choose after seeing the table.
- **Blistering Firecat** — 7/1 trample haste for {R}{R} out of a face-down body, sacrificed at end of turn. A burst of unexpected damage.
- **Cabal Executioner** — Combat damage forces a sacrifice, which is menace-friendly and gets through wide boards.
- **Dream Chisel** — Face-down creature spells cost {1} less, turning the {3} mask tax into {2} and letting two crawlers land in a turn.
- **Exalted Angel** — Flip for {2}{W}{W} into a 4/5 flying lifelinker that repairs the life Ebonblade Reaper costs.
- **Fortune Thief** — Damage cannot take you below 1 life. A crawler that keeps you alive through a swing you should not have survived.
- **Gathan Raiders** — Morph cost is discarding a card, so it unmasks for free and grows when your hand is empty.
- **Goblin Maskmaker** — Attacking makes face-down spells cheaper, so the mask crowd deploys faster.
- **Grim Haruspex** — Draws a card whenever another nontoken creature you control dies, which is constant while the Bombardment is running.
- **Haunted Cadaver** — Combat damage plus a sacrifice strips three cards from a hand. Disruption from an anonymous body.
- **Proteus Machine** — Morph {0}. A free, repeatable turn-face-up trigger for the Projektor and Roshan.
- **Rockshard Elemental** — Native double strike, so it does not need Bruse's grant to hit twice.
- **Roshan, Hidden Magister** — Face-down creatures have menace, and every turn-face-up draws a card, doubled by the Projektor.
- **Ruthless Ripper** — Deathtouch, and its morph cost is revealing a black card, so unmasking is free.
- **Silent Specter** — Flying plus a two-card discard on damage. A hunter that punishes the player who left the sky open.
- **Skinthinner** — Flip destroys a nonblack creature outright. The deck's cleanest answer to a bomb.
- **Skirk Commando** — Combat damage pings a creature, which clears the way for the next attacker.
- **Skirk Marauder** — Flip deals 2 damage to a creature. Cheap removal wearing a mask.
- **Watcher of the Roost** — Free unmask by revealing a white card, and gains 2 life. A flyer that costs nothing to reveal.
- **Whipcorder** — Taps a blocker for {W} each turn, which is how a lethal swing gets through.
- **Zombie Cutthroat** — Morph cost is 5 life, so a 3/4 arrives for free when the life total can afford it.

### Goad and the Hunt

- **Agitator Ant** — Hands out +1/+1 counters and goads the creatures that took them, which starts fights among the hunters every end step.
- **Disrupt Decorum** — Goads every creature you do not control. The Hunt in one card: the arena turns on itself for a turn.
- **Kardur, Doomscourge** — Enter-the-battlefield goad on the whole table, then drains a life every time an attacking creature dies.
- **Bloodthirsty Blade** — Attaches to an opponent's creature and goads it permanently. A weapon you give to someone else.

### Equipment

- **Basilisk Collar** — Deathtouch plus lifelink for {1}. Turns Soul Collector into a recruiter and any crawler into a deterrent.
- **Loxodon Warhammer** — Trample and lifelink, which converts an overpumped Master of Pearls swing into real damage through chump blockers.
- **Mask of Memory** — Draw two, discard one on combat damage. Named right and it refills the hand this deck empties.
- **Sunforger** — Improvised weapon: unattach to fetch and cast a red or white instant for free.

### Tutors

- **Imperial Recruiter** — Finds a creature with power 2 or less, which is nearly every morph body plus Skirk Alarmist and Goblin Maskmaker.
- **Recruiter of the Guard** — Finds a creature with toughness 2 or less, covering the pieces the Recruiter above misses.
- **Diabolic Intent** — Any card for {1}{B} at the cost of feeding a crawler to the dungeon. Thematically and mechanically the right price.
- **Wishclaw Talisman** — Finds anything, then hands the tutor to an opponent. A sponsorship deal with the audience, and the drawback keeps it honest.
- **Beseech the Queen** — Any card with mana value at or below your land count. Slow, old, and not a Game Changer.

## Rules

- A face-down creature is a 2/2 colourless creature with no name, no creature types, and no abilities. It keeps its own characteristics only once it is turned face up.
- Turning a creature face up with an effect such as **Skirk Alarmist** does not use, and does not require paying, the morph cost. Morph costs only apply when the controller turns it up themselves.
- **Panoptic Projektor** doubles triggered abilities of permanents *you control* that trigger from a face-down permanent being turned face up. **Master of Pearls** therefore gives +2/+2 twice, for +4/+4. It does not double the morph flip itself.
- **Skirk Alarmist** sacrifices the flipped creature at the beginning of the next end step. For **Master of Pearls** the pump has already resolved, and for **Ponyback Brigade** the Goblin tokens stay behind. Losing the body is the intended cost.
- **Ashcloud Phoenix** returns face down when it dies. The returned permanent has no abilities, so sacrificing it again does not return it a second time. This is a two-use card, not a loop.
- Double strike creates two separate combat damage events, so **Ebonblade Reaper** triggers twice: a player at 40 goes to 20, then to 10. Its attack trigger costs you half your life rounded up, and that is life loss rather than damage, so **Fortune Thief** does not prevent it.
- Goad forces a creature to attack each combat if able and to attack a player other than you if able. It does not stop that creature from blocking on other players' turns.
- **Bloodthirsty Blade** attaches to a creature an opponent controls, which is legal because the Equipment's own ability moves it there.
- **Gift of Doom** cast face down is a 2/2 creature, not an Aura. Turning it face up by sacrificing another creature lets you attach it to a creature at that time.
- **Falthis** grants menace and deathtouch to commanders you control, which includes Falthis and Bruse Tarl but no other creature in the deck.
- **Soul Collector** returns a creature that it damaged and that died in the same turn. Deathtouch from **Basilisk Collar** or **Gift of Doom** makes any amount of its damage lethal.
- No card in this deck appears on the cached Game Changers snapshot in `BRACKET-DEFINITIONS.md` dated 2026-09-03. The black tutors that would normally fill these slots — Demonic Tutor, Vampiric Tutor, Imperial Seal — are all on that list and were excluded for that reason.

## Talks

### 2026-09-09 — Character-cast brew, locked with the user

The user's original request had been read as a mechanics prompt and produced a Sefris dungeon-venture deck. That was the wrong fork: the correct reading was to cast the *characters* from Matt Dinniman's books and let the setting be a single represented floor. This deck is that second reading, built alongside rather than instead of the Sefris list.

Commander casting: Carl is **Bruse Tarl, Boorish Herder** (a human who makes one crawler lethal for a swing rather than being a threat himself) and Princess Donut is **Falthis, Shadowcat Familiar** (a Cat whose whole job is upgrading the other commander). Partner makes the pair legal and sets Mardu. Rejected: **Prava of the Steel Legion** as Donut (a genuine Cat with Partner, but her token-buff plan makes the deck about soldier tokens rather than the pair), **Toggo, Goblin Weaponsmith** as Carl (mechanically ideal for improvised weapons, but a Goblin), **Akiri, Line-Slinger** as Carl (Soldier Ally who scales with artifacts, but pushes the deck to equipment-matter).

Floor: the sixth floor, the Hunting Grounds of *The Butcher's Masquerade*. Chosen because Magic already has the mechanics — masks and hidden identity are morph, and the Hunt is goad. Mardu has 75 morph cards and 53 goad cards. Rejected: the Iron Tangle (147 Mardu vehicles and the Crew keyword make it the cleanest mechanical fit, but it turns the cast into support for an artifact deck), and the faction-wars floor (only 17 battles in Mardu, too thin). The user noted the ninth floor as the agent's likely pick and the seventh as their own favourite, then chose the sixth.

Morph over disguise: 75 Mardu morph cards against 28 disguise or cloak, and morph is Onslaught-era, which matches the root `DECISIONS.md` preference for esoteric old cards over modern reprints of the same idea.

### 2026-09-09 — Extreme Plans and tutors

The user asked for two to four five-card combos representing Carl's extreme plans, plus four or five tutors, and required the plans to appear in the primer. Four plans were built; each is written up in the primer with its full sequence.

This request moves the deck off the Exhibition target agreed earlier. Five-card assembly plus five tutors is Core (Bracket 2) texture: still zero Game Changers, still telegraphed on the board, still slow enough to clear the eight-turn floor. Recorded here so the earlier Bracket 1 intent is not treated as the current target.

Tutor selection was constrained by the Game Changers cap. Demonic Tutor, Vampiric Tutor, and Imperial Seal are all on the cached list, so the package is Imperial Recruiter, Recruiter of the Guard, Diabolic Intent, Wishclaw Talisman, and Beseech the Queen. Two of those are creature-only and one hands the tutor to an opponent, which keeps consistency below what an unrestricted black package would give.
