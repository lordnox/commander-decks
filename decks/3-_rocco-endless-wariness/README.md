## How the deck works

1. Ramp into [**Rocco, Cabaretti Caterer**](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/218/rocco-cabaretti-caterer?utm_source=api) for X = 1 and fetch [**Norin the Wary**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsp/171/norin-the-wary?utm_source=api). Four mana is enough; [Rocco](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/218/rocco-cabaretti-caterer?utm_source=api)'s later recasts grab a pinger, [**Torbran, Thane of Red Fell**](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/147/torbran-thane-of-red-fell?utm_source=api), or a draw engine instead.
2. Every spell anyone casts and every creature that attacks exiles [**Norin the Wary**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsp/171/norin-the-wary?utm_source=api), then he returns at the next end step. That is a creature entering on almost every turn of the table.
3. Payoffs fire on those entries: [**Impact Tremors**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/717/impact-tremors?utm_source=api), [**Witty Roastmaster**](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/131/witty-roastmaster?utm_source=api), [**Molten Gatekeeper**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/128/molten-gatekeeper?utm_source=api), [**Agate Instigator**](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/BLC-21/agate-instigator?utm_source=api), and [**Arbaaz Mir**](https://scryfall.com/card/acr/46/arbaaz-mir?utm_source=api) ping the table; [**Mentor of the Meek**](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/170/mentor-of-the-meek?utm_source=api), [**Tocasia's Welcome**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/181/tocasias-welcome?utm_source=api), and [**Welcoming Vampire**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/140/welcoming-vampire?utm_source=api) draw; [**Genesis Chamber**](https://scryfall.com/card/bbd/235/genesis-chamber?utm_source=api), [**Blessed Sanctuary**](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/1/blessed-sanctuary?utm_source=api), and [**Saradoc, Master of Buckland**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/282/saradoc-master-of-buckland?utm_source=api) make tokens that ping again.
4. [**Panharmonicon**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/310/panharmonicon?utm_source=api) and [**Gandalf the White**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/19/gandalf-the-white?utm_source=api) copy the relevant triggers. [**Torbran, Thane of Red Fell**](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/147/torbran-thane-of-red-fell?utm_source=api) turns each red ping into three. The game ends when forty life per opponent has dripped away, or when [**End-Raze Forerunners**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/822/end-raze-forerunners?utm_source=api) swings a token army.

There is no two-card infinite. [**Kodama of the East Tree**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/239/kodama-of-the-east-tree?utm_source=api) plus the bounce lands is extra land drops, not a loop.

## Main synergy patterns

### Norin is a trigger, not a beater

[**Norin the Wary**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsp/171/norin-the-wary?utm_source=api) never stays to block. Cast him, or let [Rocco](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/218/rocco-cabaretti-caterer?utm_source=api) fetch him, then let the table do the work: each spell and each attacker sends him away and brings him back. Stack pingers first so the return actually matters.

### Confusion steals, then Norin comes home

When [**Confusion in the Ranks**](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/87/confusion-in-the-ranks?utm_source=api) is out, [**Norin the Wary**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsp/171/norin-the-wary?utm_source=api) entering makes you exchange him for another player's creature. Norin's own trigger still exiles him on the next spell or attack and returns him to *you*, his owner. You keep their creature.

### [Aura Shards](https://scryfall.com/card/cmd/182/aura-shards?utm_source=api) is the Game Changer

[**Aura Shards**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmd/182/aura-shards?utm_source=api) may destroy an artifact or enchantment whenever a creature you control enters. With Norin flickering through a busy table, that is repeated targeted hate, which is why the deck matchmakes at Bracket 3 even though the clock is slow.

### [Blessed Sanctuary](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/1/blessed-sanctuary?utm_source=api) covers the red wrath

[**Blessed Sanctuary**](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/1/blessed-sanctuary?utm_source=api) prevents noncombat damage to you and your creatures, so [**Blasphemous Act**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/163/blasphemous-act?utm_source=api) can clear the board while your team lives. The Unicorn tokens it makes are extra Tremors triggers.

## Win conditions

- **Ping attrition**: one or two pingers plus Norin deal a few points to each opponent on every spell and attack. [**Torbran, Thane of Red Fell**](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/147/torbran-thane-of-red-fell?utm_source=api) triples the red ones. Forty life per head usually falls across several turn cycles, not in a single spell.
- **Stacked ETB turn**: [**Panharmonicon**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/310/panharmonicon?utm_source=api) or [**Gandalf the White**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/19/gandalf-the-white?utm_source=api) with two pingers and a token maker can take a double-digit chunk off each opponent when Norin (and the tokens) return.
- **Go-wide combat**: [**Champion of Lambholt**](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/293/champion-of-lambholt?utm_source=api) grows until nothing can block, then [**End-Raze Forerunners**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/822/end-raze-forerunners?utm_source=api) gives the tokens trample and haste.

## Game progression

### Early game

Ramp on two and three: [**Rampant Growth**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/265/rampant-growth?utm_source=api), [**Farseek**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/173/farseek?utm_source=api), [**Sakura-Tribe Elder**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/285/sakura-tribe-elder?utm_source=api), a Talisman, or [**Arcane Signet**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/192/arcane-signet?utm_source=api). A cheap pinger or [**Soul Warden**](https://scryfall.com/card/mm3/24/soul-warden?utm_source=api) is fine; do not spend the turn on a six-drop.

### Mid game

Cast [**Rocco, Cabaretti Caterer**](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/218/rocco-cabaretti-caterer?utm_source=api) for X = 1 and get [**Norin the Wary**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsp/171/norin-the-wary?utm_source=api) unless you already have him. Sequence [**Impact Tremors**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/717/impact-tremors?utm_source=api) before the next flicker. Hold a spot removal for a stax piece or a graveyard eater that stops the loop. Recast [Rocco](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/218/rocco-cabaretti-caterer?utm_source=api) for [**Torbran, Thane of Red Fell**](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/147/torbran-thane-of-red-fell?utm_source=api), [**Witty Roastmaster**](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/131/witty-roastmaster?utm_source=api), or [**Mentor of the Meek**](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/170/mentor-of-the-meek?utm_source=api) as needed.

### Late game

[**Panharmonicon**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/310/panharmonicon?utm_source=api) and [**Gandalf the White**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/19/gandalf-the-white?utm_source=api) turn a crowded board into lethal pings. If the table has stabilized behind fogs and lifegain, overrun with [**End-Raze Forerunners**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/822/end-raze-forerunners?utm_source=api) or reset with [**Austere Command**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/121/austere-command?utm_source=api) / [**Dusk // Dawn**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/61/dusk-dawn?utm_source=api) while keeping the enchantment engines.

## Mulligan guide

Keep hands with:

- two or three lands that make green plus a second color by turn three;
- a two-mana ramp spell or rock;
- a pinger, [**Norin the Wary**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsp/171/norin-the-wary?utm_source=api), or a plan to hit four mana for [Rocco](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/218/rocco-cabaretti-caterer?utm_source=api).

Mulligan one-landers, all-sweepers, and five-plus-mana piles with no ramp. [**Fog**](https://scryfall.com/card/ema/167/fog?utm_source=api) alone is not a keep.

## Important sequencing and rules notes

- [**Rocco, Cabaretti Caterer**](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/218/rocco-cabaretti-caterer?utm_source=api) searches only if you *cast* him. A blink or a [**Genesis Chamber**](https://scryfall.com/card/bbd/235/genesis-chamber?utm_source=api) token copy does not tutor.
- [**Norin the Wary**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsp/171/norin-the-wary?utm_source=api) returns at the beginning of the *next* end step after he is exiled, which may be an opponent's end step. Several spells in one turn still exile him only once until he is back.
- [**Panharmonicon**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/310/panharmonicon?utm_source=api) doubles triggered abilities of permanents you control caused by an artifact or creature entering. [**Impact Tremors**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/717/impact-tremors?utm_source=api) is an enchantment, but the *cause* is a creature entering, so Tremors doubles. [**Gandalf the White**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/19/gandalf-the-white?utm_source=api) doubles when the entering or leaving permanent is legendary or an artifact, so Norin (legendary) retriggers Tremors extra; a vanilla Beast token does not.
- [**Arbaaz Mir**](https://scryfall.com/card/acr/46/arbaaz-mir?utm_source=api) cares about nontoken *historic* permanents. Norin is legendary, so each of his returns pings. Myr tokens from [**Genesis Chamber**](https://scryfall.com/card/bbd/235/genesis-chamber?utm_source=api) do not.
- [**Outpost Siege**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/804/outpost-siege?utm_source=api) must be on Dragons to ping; Khans is a loot mode when you are flooding.
- [**Heronblade Elite**](https://scryfall.com/card/mic/26/heronblade-elite?utm_source=api) grows only when another *Human* enters. Norin is a Human; most tokens are not.
- [**Gimli of the Glittering Caves**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/32/gimli-of-the-glittering-caves?utm_source=api) and [**Legolas Greenleaf**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/40/legolas-greenleaf?utm_source=api) grow on other *legendary* creatures. Norin counts; [**Saltskitter**](https://scryfall.com/card/fut/14/saltskitter?utm_source=api) does not.
- [**Confusion in the Ranks**](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/87/confusion-in-the-ranks?utm_source=api) hits artifacts, creatures, and enchantments. Exchange Norin, not your Tremors, unless you mean to.
- [**Blessed Sanctuary**](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/1/blessed-sanctuary?utm_source=api) stops noncombat damage to you, including your own [**Impact Tremors**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/717/impact-tremors?utm_source=api) if something ever redirected it, and it stops [**Blasphemous Act**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/163/blasphemous-act?utm_source=api) to your team.

## Weaknesses and what to protect

Graveyard exile and flicker-hate (Rest in Peace effects, Grafdigger's Cage that stops returns, or a player who just never casts spells) stall Norin. The deck has little protection for [**Impact Tremors**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/717/impact-tremors?utm_source=api) and [**Aura Shards**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmd/182/aura-shards?utm_source=api); recast [Rocco](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/218/rocco-cabaretti-caterer?utm_source=api) for another pinger if the enchantment dies. Commander tax on [Rocco](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/218/rocco-cabaretti-caterer?utm_source=api) hurts more than losing Norin, who is one mana from hand. Mass artifact and enchantment wipes take both the pingers and [**Panharmonicon**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/310/panharmonicon?utm_source=api). Lifegain does not race stacked Torbran pings, but a single pinger without doubling can lose to a 40-life cushion.
