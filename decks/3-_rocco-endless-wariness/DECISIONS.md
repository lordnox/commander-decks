# Decisions

## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, the primer, and the assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a cut back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated session notes so an argument does not live only in chat.

Every unique card needs a Cards in line. The other headings appear when there is something to record.

## Goals

- **Pingers** — The clock is noncombat damage on every Norin return, not a big swing.
- **ETB Effects** — Rocco fetches Norin so Impact Tremors, draw, and tokens retrigger all game.

## Primer

The primer treats Rocco as a repeatable creature tutor and Norin as the engine, not as a combat commander. Win-turn claims assume ping attrition, not a compact combo. Kodama plus bounce lands is described as value only.

## Rules

- Rocco's search requires casting him; it does not trigger if he is put onto the battlefield.
- Norin returns under his owner's control, which is why Confusion in the Ranks is a steal rather than a donation.
- Panharmonicon cares about the entering permanent type (artifact or creature), not the source of the trigger, so Impact Tremors doubles on creature ETBs.
- Gandalf the White extra-triggers only when the entering or leaving permanent is legendary or an artifact.
- Blessed Sanctuary prevents noncombat damage to you and creatures you control, so Blasphemous Act does not kill your team.

## Talks

### 2026-08-27 — Bracket assessment

Official sources: [Commander format page](https://magic.wizards.com/en/formats/commander) (Game Changers: 0 in Brackets 1–2, up to 3 in Bracket 3, unlimited in 4–5; intent over checklist), [February 9, 2026 brackets update](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/commander-brackets-beta-update-february-9-2026) (Farewell and Biorhythm added to Game Changers; list otherwise stable), and Scryfall `is:gamechanger` (53 cards). October 21, 2025 update still supplies the turn barometers (Core: at least eight turns; Upgraded: at least six).

**Aura Shards** is the only Game Changer in the 100. That is a hard floor of Bracket 3. The rest of the list is incremental, telegraphed ping attrition with no two-card infinite, no extra turns, no mass land denial, and no fast mana. Rocco is a commander-zone tutor, which is more consistency than a typical Core Norin pile, but the repeatable clock still wants several turn cycles of spells and attacks after Norin is online (usually turn four with ramp).

Position **3−**: matchmake as Upgraded because of Aura Shards, sit at the low edge because the normal goldfish is closer to Core pace.

Win turns (untested goldfish, not a simulation):

- Credible high roll: ramp into turn-four Rocco for Norin with a pinger already down, Torbran around turn six, lethal pings around turn eight.
- Normal: engines online turns five to seven, table dead turns nine to eleven.
- Interactive: Norin or Tremors answered, commander tax on Rocco, turns twelve and later.

Not Bracket 2: Game Changer present. Not Bracket 3 mid/plus or 4: no compact combo, no early two-card win, slow mana, one GC used as a value engine.
