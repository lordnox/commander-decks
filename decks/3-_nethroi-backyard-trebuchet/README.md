An Abzan deck that builds a pile of high-toughness, low-power creatures, then either mutates [**Nethroi, Apex of Death**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/163/nethroi-apex-of-death?utm_source=api) over a non-Human to dump the pile from the graveyard, or loads [**Catapult Captain**](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/99/catapult-fodder-catapult-captain?utm_source=api) and [**Tree of Perdition**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/49/tree-of-perdition?utm_source=api) so one sacrifice sets an opponent's life to their own former total.

**Commander:** [Nethroi, Apex of Death](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/163/nethroi-apex-of-death)  
**Deck size:** 100 cards  
**Primary plan:** transform [Catapult Fodder](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/99/catapult-fodder-catapult-captain?utm_source=api) and fling a Tree or a pumped Wall  
**Backup plan:** [Nethroi](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/163/nethroi-apex-of-death?utm_source=api) mutate, [Eerie Ultimatum](https://scryfall.com/card/iko/184/eerie-ultimatum?utm_source=api), and [Vito](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/127/vito-thorn-of-the-dusk-rose?utm_source=api)-style lifegain drain

## How the deck works

1. Deploy defender mana — [**Overgrown Battlement**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/264/overgrown-battlement?utm_source=api), [**Axebane Guardian**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/248/axebane-guardian?utm_source=api), [**Bloom Tender**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/166/bloom-tender?utm_source=api), [**Faeburrow Elder**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/53/faeburrow-elder?utm_source=api) — and keep those creatures untapping with [**Instill Energy**](https://scryfall.com/card/30a/495/instill-energy?utm_source=api), [**Nature's Chosen**](https://scryfall.com/card/all/97/natures-chosen?utm_source=api), [**Tyvar, Jubilant Brawler**](https://scryfall.com/card/one/218/tyvar-jubilant-brawler?utm_source=api), and [**Thousand-Year Elixir**](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/271/thousand-year-elixir?utm_source=api).
2. Mill the Trees, Walls, and mutate targets with [**Stitcher's Supplier**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/196/stitchers-supplier?utm_source=api), [**Ripples of Undeath**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/107/ripples-of-undeath?utm_source=api), [**Old Stickfingers**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/227/old-stickfingers?utm_source=api), [**Underrealm Lich**](https://scryfall.com/card/clu/215/underrealm-lich?utm_source=api), and [**Lumra, Bellow of the Woods**](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/183/lumra-bellow-of-the-woods?utm_source=api).
3. Put [**Catapult Fodder**](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/99/catapult-fodder-catapult-captain?utm_source=api) into play. At the beginning of combat, if you control three creatures with toughness greater than power, it becomes [**Catapult Captain**](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/99/catapult-fodder-catapult-captain?utm_source=api).
4. Cast or reanimate [**Tree of Perdition**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/49/tree-of-perdition?utm_source=api), tap it to set one opponent to 13 (or whatever its toughness is), then have Captain sacrifice the now-huge Tree so that player loses life equal to their old total.
5. If the yard is full and you have seven mana, mutate [**Nethroi**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/163/nethroi-apex-of-death?utm_source=api) onto a non-Human and return any number of creatures with total power 10 or less — Walls, Trees, [Doran](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/201/doran-the-siege-tower?utm_source=api), and most of the mana dorks all qualify.

There is no infinite loop. The trebuchet is a one-shot per opponent unless you recur the Tree.

## Main synergy patterns

### The backyard trebuchet

[**Catapult Fodder**](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/99/catapult-fodder-catapult-captain?utm_source=api) transforms at the beginning of combat if you control three creatures with toughness greater than power. Most defenders already satisfy that; [**Doran**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/201/doran-the-siege-tower?utm_source=api) and [**Assault Formation**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/246/assault-formation?utm_source=api) change combat math but do not change printed power, so 0/N walls still count. [**Catapult Captain**](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/99/catapult-fodder-catapult-captain?utm_source=api) then pays `{2}{B}`, taps, and sacrifices another creature to make a player lose life equal to that creature's toughness.

[**Tree of Perdition**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/49/tree-of-perdition?utm_source=api) is the payload. Tap it to exchange an opponent's life total with its toughness (13). The Tree is now as tough as that player was (often 40). Sacrifice it to Captain: they lose 40 life. [**Wall of Blood**](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/82/wall-of-blood?utm_source=api) is the backup payload: pay life into it, then fling the new toughness; [**Children of Korlis**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/14/children-of-korlis?utm_source=api) refunds the life you paid this turn.

Haste and extra untaps matter. [**Crashing Drawbridge**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/316/crashing-drawbridge?utm_source=api), [**Tyvar**](https://scryfall.com/card/one/218/tyvar-jubilant-brawler?utm_source=api), [**Thousand-Year Elixir**](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/271/thousand-year-elixir?utm_source=api), and [**Instill Energy**](https://scryfall.com/card/30a/495/instill-energy?utm_source=api) let the Tree tap the turn it enters. [**Nature's Chosen**](https://scryfall.com/card/all/97/natures-chosen?utm_source=api) on a white creature can untap the Tree once on your turn.

### [Nethroi](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/163/nethroi-apex-of-death?utm_source=api) as a reload, not a combo

Mutate cost is `{4}{G/W}{B}{B}`. The trigger returns creatures with **total power** 10 or less, so pile in 0-power Trees and Walls plus a few 1- and 2-power dorks. You cannot return [**Betor**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/172/betor-kin-to-all?utm_source=api) or [**Nethroi**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/163/nethroi-apex-of-death?utm_source=api) itself this way. Mutate onto a non-Human you own; Humans such as [**Children of Korlis**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/14/children-of-korlis?utm_source=api) and [**Axebane Guardian**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/248/axebane-guardian?utm_source=api) are illegal hosts.

[**Eerie Ultimatum**](https://scryfall.com/card/iko/184/eerie-ultimatum?utm_source=api) is the other dump: every differently named permanent in the yard comes back, including lands after a [**Lumra**](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/183/lumra-bellow-of-the-woods?utm_source=api) or [**Aftermath Analyst**](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/91/aftermath-analyst?utm_source=api) mill.

### Life as a resource

[**Angel of Suffering**](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/67/angel-of-suffering?utm_source=api) turns incoming damage into mill. [**Greed**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/274/greed?utm_source=api) and [**Wall of Blood**](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/82/wall-of-blood?utm_source=api) spend life. [**Tree of Redemption**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/97/tree-of-redemption?utm_source=api) sets your life to 13 (or its current toughness). [**Willowdusk, Essence Seer**](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/6/willowdusk-essence-seer?utm_source=api) and [**Cradle of Vitality**](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/98/cradle-of-vitality?utm_source=api) turn that swing into +1/+1 counters. [**Mister Negative**](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/135/mister-negative?utm_source=api) exchanges life totals with an opponent and draws if you went down. [**Vito**](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/127/vito-thorn-of-the-dusk-rose?utm_source=api) and [**Enduring Tenacity**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/95/enduring-tenacity?utm_source=api) make any gain you get afterward lethal.

[**Betor, Kin to All**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/172/betor-kin-to-all?utm_source=api) is a late checkpoint: 10 toughness draws, 20 untaps the team (another Tree tap, another Captain shot), 40 halves each opponent.

## Win conditions

- **Trebuchet**: Captain plus [Tree of Perdition](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/49/tree-of-perdition?utm_source=api) or a pumped [Wall of Blood](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/82/wall-of-blood?utm_source=api).
- **[Nethroi](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/163/nethroi-apex-of-death?utm_source=api) combat**: mutate, then attack with [Doran](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/201/doran-the-siege-tower?utm_source=api)/[Assault Formation](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/246/assault-formation?utm_source=api) and Ikra lifegain into [Vito](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/127/vito-thorn-of-the-dusk-rose?utm_source=api).
- **Life-swap**: [Mister Negative](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/135/mister-negative?utm_source=api), [Tree of Redemption](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/97/tree-of-redemption?utm_source=api) plus drain, or [Scourge of the Skyclaves](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/122/scourge-of-the-skyclaves?utm_source=api) after totals are already low.
- **[Betor](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/172/betor-kin-to-all?utm_source=api) / [Wound Reflection](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/113/wound-reflection?utm_source=api) / Bloodletter**: one large life-loss turn that the rest of the table also pays for.

## Game progression

### Early game

Ramp and walls. Keep [**Bloom Tender**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/166/bloom-tender?utm_source=api), [**Overgrown Battlement**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/264/overgrown-battlement?utm_source=api), or [**Axebane Guardian**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/248/axebane-guardian?utm_source=api) plus an untap Aura. Mill if the hand already has mana; do not mutate [Nethroi](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/163/nethroi-apex-of-death?utm_source=api) into an empty yard.

### Mid game

Get three T>P bodies and [Catapult Fodder](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/99/catapult-fodder-catapult-captain?utm_source=api) transforming. Play a Tree with haste if you can tap it immediately. Otherwise mutate [Nethroi](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/163/nethroi-apex-of-death?utm_source=api) to put the Trees into play already, then tap and fling on the same turn if Captain is ready.

### Late game

[**Eerie Ultimatum**](https://scryfall.com/card/iko/184/eerie-ultimatum?utm_source=api) or [**Shifting Woodland**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/228/shifting-woodland?utm_source=api) copying a Tree. [**Wound Reflection**](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/113/wound-reflection?utm_source=api) on a Perdition or [Mister Negative](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/135/mister-negative?utm_source=api) turn usually ends the table. Recast [Nethroi](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/163/nethroi-apex-of-death?utm_source=api) only if the yard still has low-power meat; commander tax on a seven-mana mutate gets expensive.

## Mulligan guide

Keep hands with:

- two or three lands that produce green by turn two;
- a defender mana dork or [Bloom Tender](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/166/bloom-tender?utm_source=api);
- mill **or** a Tree/Catapult piece, not a hand of only finishers.

Mulligan all-five-drops, no green, or a mutate-only hand with no mill and no ramp.

## Important sequencing and rules notes

- [Catapult Fodder](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/99/catapult-fodder-catapult-captain?utm_source=api) checks **at the beginning of combat**, and the three creatures must have toughness greater than power **at that moment**. [**Faeburrow Elder**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/53/faeburrow-elder?utm_source=api) has equal power and toughness and does not help transform.
- [**Tree of Perdition**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/49/tree-of-perdition?utm_source=api) exchanges with **toughness**, not power. After the exchange the Tree's toughness is the opponent's old life; its power stays 0.
- [**Catapult Captain**](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/99/catapult-fodder-catapult-captain?utm_source=api) makes the opponent **lose life** equal to toughness; it is not combat damage and does not use [Doran](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/201/doran-the-siege-tower?utm_source=api).
- [Nethroi](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/163/nethroi-apex-of-death?utm_source=api) counts **power** on cards in the graveyard, not toughness. A 0/13 Tree costs 0 of the 10 budget.
- Mutate needs a **non-Human** you own. [**Doran**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/201/doran-the-siege-tower?utm_source=api), [**Bloom Tender**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/166/bloom-tender?utm_source=api), the Trees, [**Overgrown Battlement**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/264/overgrown-battlement?utm_source=api), and [**Catapult Fodder**](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/99/catapult-fodder-catapult-captain?utm_source=api) are legal; [**Axebane Guardian**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/248/axebane-guardian?utm_source=api) is not.
- [**Instill Energy**](https://scryfall.com/card/30a/495/instill-energy?utm_source=api) and [**Nature's Chosen**](https://scryfall.com/card/all/97/natures-chosen?utm_source=api) untap only **once each turn**, and [Nature's Chosen](https://scryfall.com/card/all/97/natures-chosen?utm_source=api)'s second ability needs the enchanted creature to be white.
- [**Mikaeus, the Unhallowed**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/173/mikaeus-the-unhallowed?utm_source=api) gives undying to other non-Humans. It is recursion insurance, not a closed loop unless a free sacrifice outlet is already draining the table.
- [**Scourge of the Skyclaves**](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/122/scourge-of-the-skyclaves?utm_source=api) is `20 - highest life total`. After Perdition sets someone to 13 it is still small if you are at 40; use it after totals are compressed.
- [**Lumra**](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/183/lumra-bellow-of-the-woods?utm_source=api) returns **all** lands from the graveyard, including fetches you cracked and bounce lands you used.

- [**Choose Your Weapon**](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/175/choose-your-weapon?utm_source=api), [**Unnatural Growth**](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/223/unnatural-growth?utm_source=api), [**Zopandrel, Hunger Dominus**](https://scryfall.com/card/one/195/zopandrel-hunger-dominus?utm_source=api) — combat doubling when the trebuchet is not coming online.

## Weaknesses and what to protect

Graveyard hate blanks [Nethroi](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/163/nethroi-apex-of-death?utm_source=api) and the Woodland copy; keep [**Six**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/169/six?utm_source=api) retrace and [**Altar of Bone**](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/281/altar-of-bone?utm_source=api) as ways to play from the library instead. [**Tree of Perdition**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/49/tree-of-perdition?utm_source=api) and [**Catapult Captain**](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/99/catapult-fodder-catapult-captain?utm_source=api) die to ordinary removal before the tap, so hold [**Ephemerate**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/7/ephemerate?utm_source=api), [**Djeru's Resolve**](https://scryfall.com/card/akh/11/djerus-resolve?utm_source=api), [**Legolas's Quick Reflexes**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/493/legolass-quick-reflexes?utm_source=api), or [**Golgari Charm**](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/201/golgari-charm?utm_source=api) regenerate for the payload turn. The list has almost no counterspells and few sweepers; [**Crystal Barricade**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/7/crystal-barricade?utm_source=api) hexproof on you and [**Teyo, Geometric Tactician**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/725/teyo-geometric-tactician?utm_source=api) are the main pillows. Commander tax on mutate is the late-game failure mode — win with the Trees still in play rather than recasting [Nethroi](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/163/nethroi-apex-of-death?utm_source=api) three times.
