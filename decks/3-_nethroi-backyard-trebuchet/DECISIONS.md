# Decisions

## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, the primer, and the assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a cut back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated session notes so an argument does not live only in chat.

Every unique card needs a Cards in line. The other headings appear when there is something to record.

## Cards in

### Commander and trebuchet

- **Nethroi, Apex of Death** — Mutate commander that returns total power 10 or less; the yard dump that puts Trees and walls onto the battlefield.
- **Catapult Fodder // Catapult Captain** — Transforms if three creatures have toughness greater than power, then flings toughness as life loss; the namesake win.
- **Tree of Perdition** — Sets an opponent to 13, becomes 0/40, and is the primary Captain payload.
- **Tree of Redemption** — Sets your life to its toughness so drain engines and Willowdusk have a number to work with; also a 0-power Nethroi target.
- **Wall of Blood** — Pay life into toughness, then fling or feed Willowdusk; Children of Korlis refunds the payment.
- **Children of Korlis** — Gains back all life lost this turn after Wall of Blood or Greed payments.
- **Doran, the Siege Tower** — Makes every creature deal damage equal to toughness so walls can attack.
- **Assault Formation** — Same toughness combat plus a way for defenders to attack.

## Primer

The trebuchet line is Catapult Captain sacrificing Tree of Perdition after the life exchange, not a death-trigger transform. Catapult Fodder transforms at the beginning of combat if three creatures have toughness greater than power. Nethroi is the reload, not a two-card infinite.

## Rules

- Tree of Perdition exchanges toughness with an opponent's life total; power stays 0.
- Catapult Captain causes life loss equal to the sacrificed creature's toughness, which is not combat damage and ignores Doran.
- Nethroi mutate returns total **power** 10 or less and must target a non-Human you own.
- Instill Energy and Nature's Chosen each untap only once per turn.
- Scourge of the Skyclaves is 20 minus the highest life total among players.

## Talks

### 2026-08-27 — Import and assessment

Imported the Archidekt-style list as **Nethroi — Backyard Trebuchet**. Official Commander page (https://magic.wizards.com/en/formats/commander) still gates Game Changers at 0 / 0 / 3 / unlimited by bracket, with two-card infinites, extra turns, and mass land denial as barometers rather than a hard checklist; February 9, 2026 update added Farewell and Biorhythm to Game Changers. Scryfall `is:gamechanger` matched **zero** cards in this 99.

Maybeboard (`{noDeck}`) stays out of Cards in: Archfiend of Despair, Deathbloom Ritualist, Saryth, the Viper's Fang, Choose Your Weapon, Unnatural Growth, Zopandrel, Hunger Dominus, Perpetual Timepiece.

Assigned **Bracket 3−**:

- No two-card infinite, no extra turns, no mass land denial.
- Repeatable pace is a mid-game one-turn kill after mill and defender mana, which matches Bracket 3's "deploy a large turn after resources accumulate" more than Bracket 2's incremental board race.
- Not middle Bracket 3: commander mutate is seven mana, interaction is mostly creature-based, and the trebuchet is telegraphed.
- Credible high roll is a haste Tree of Perdition around turn five, not a table-win on turn four.
- Normal goldfish: threaten around turn seven or eight (mutate dump or Tree plus Captain).
- Interactive game: commander tax and graveyard hate push wins toward turn nine or later.
