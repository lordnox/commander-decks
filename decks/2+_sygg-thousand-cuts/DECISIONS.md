# Decisions

## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, the primer, and the assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a cut back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated session notes so an argument does not live only in chat.

Every unique card needs a Cards in line. The other headings appear when there is something to record.

## Cards in

- **Sygg, River Cutthroat** — Two-mana commander that draws a card each end step where any opponent lost 3+ life; the entire punisher shell exists to make that check free.
- **Aclazotz, Deepest Betrayal // Temple of the Dead** — Evasive lifelink discard engine that strips a card from every opponent per attack, feeds Liliana's Caress, and returns as a land when killed.
- **Aetherize** — Fog-plus-tempo against the one alpha strike goad politics cannot deflect; the deck has no other mass answer to attackers.
- **Anvil of Bogardan** — Symmetric extra draw plus a forced discard each draw step: fuels the hand punishers, triggers the discard payoffs, and lifts your own hand-size cap for Venser's Journal and Ivory Tower.
- **Arcane Signet** — Two-mana any-color rock; the deck wants turn-three punishers a turn early.
- **Baleful Strix** — Deathtouch flyer that replaces itself; the front line of the "don't attack me" wall.
- **Bloodchief Ascension** — Charges itself off the ambient two-life-a-turn bleeding, then drains two per card hitting any opponent's graveyard — wheels become massacres.
- **Bloodthirsty Blade** — Repeatable one-mana goad that stays on the battlefield; points the biggest creature away from you every turn.
- **Bloodthirsty Conqueror** — Converts every point of opponent life loss into lifegain on a relevant flying deathtouch body; second copy of the Exquisite Blood effect.
- **Breathstealer's Crypt** — Taxes every draw: reveal, and creatures cost 3 life or get discarded — draw punishment and discard fuel in one esoteric enchantment.
- **Charcoal Diamond** — Budget black rock; enters tapped but keeps the punisher curve on schedule.
- **Choked Estuary** — Dual that usually enters untapped off the deck's heavy Island/Swamp count.
- **Clearwater Pathway // Murkwater Pathway** — Untapped modal dual; picks the color the hand needs.
- **Command Tower** — Best land in any two-color commander deck.
- **Countersquall** — Counters the noncreature answer aimed at your engines and drains 2, which alone can flip a Sygg check.
- **Coveted Peacock** — Repeatable goad every combat on an evasive body; keeps opponents' boards pointed at each other.
- **Curse of Verbosity** — Cursing the archenemy pays you (and their attackers) cards, redirects aggression, and feeds Sygg with the resulting combat.
- **Darkwater Catacombs** — Filter land that fixes double-pip costs like Painful Quandary and Whispering Madness.
- **Dimir Signet** — Two-mana fixing rock; the deck's costs are pip-heavy.
- **Drowned Catacomb** — Check land that is nearly always untapped here.
- **Exquisite Blood** — Turns the whole punisher suite into a life engine; every opponent life loss becomes your gain, funding shocks, fetches, and Reanimate.
- **Eye of Nidhogg** — Goad aura that also shrinks the threat to a 4/2 and gives it deathtouch so it trades; returns to hand when the creature dies, so it re-goads the next problem.
- **Faerie Mastermind** — Flash flyer that taxes opponents' extra draws into your cards; group draw makes it trigger constantly, and its activated ability forces a table-wide draw for the hand punishers.
- **Fathom Feeder** — Deathtouch deterrent that ingests on damage and later converts mana into draw plus exile; feeds Nighthawk Scavenger's card-type count.
- **Feed the Swarm** — Black's only clean enchantment removal; the life cost is trivial in a deck gaining constantly.
- **Fellwar Stone** — Two-mana rock that fixes off opponents' lands.
- **Foulmire Knight // Profane Insight** — One-mana deathtouch blocker early or a flash cantrip late; both halves fit the wall-and-draw plan.
- **Geth's Grimoire** — Converts every forced discard into a card for you; a wheel with this out draws you a fresh grip beyond your own seven.
- **Ghoulish Impetus** — Goad aura granting deathtouch, so the goaded creature kills what it hits; recurs itself when the creature dies.
- **Gixian Puppeteer** — Drains the whole table two on your second draw each turn — trivial with Sygg — and reanimates a small creature when it dies.
- **Grazilaxx, Illithid Scholar** — Draws on any combat damage from your evasive deathtouchers and rescues blocked attackers; keeps the card flow going when punishers are slow.
- **Howling Mine** — Fills opponents' hands past four so Iron Maiden and Viseling bite; the extra card you draw feeds Oneirophage and Gixian Puppeteer.
- **Iron Maiden** — Punishes every hand above four cards each upkeep; the namesake drip that turns group draw hostile.
- **Island** — Nine basics keep Fellwar Stone-proof mana and feed Choked Estuary, Drowned Catacomb, and Tainted Isle checks.
- **Isolation Cell** — Two-life-or-two-mana tax on every creature spell; slows aggro to Sygg's pace and bleeds the creature decks.
- **Ivory Tower** — Pays you life for the big hand the Mine effects give you; mirrors Iron Maiden in your favor.
- **Likeness Looter** — Loot away fatties or spent value creatures, then become the best creature in your graveyard at the right mana value; reanimation on an evasive faerie body.
- **Liliana's Caress** — Two life per forced discard; the core discard payoff that turns Windfall and Aclazotz attacks into table-wide drain.
- **Long River's Pull** — Counters the key creature; the gift mode fills the opponent's hand for the punishers while upgrading it to counter anything.
- **Memory Plunder** — Casts the best instant or sorcery from an opponent's graveyard — the wheels and discard stock the shelf; hybrid cost is easy on this mana base.
- **Mistvault Bridge** — Indestructible tapped dual; safe fixing that dodges land destruction.
- **Morphic Pool** — Untapped dual in every multiplayer game, which is the only place this deck plays.
- **Nighthawk Scavenger** — Flying deathtouch lifelink wall that scales with opponents' graveyards; a late-game clock through Rogue's Passage.
- **Oneirophage** — Grows a counter per card you draw; with Sygg plus group draw it becomes a real threat from an innocuous squid.
- **Otawara, Soaring City** — A land slot that bounces the problem permanent; primary answer to resolved artifact/enchantment hate.
- **Painful Quandary** — Five life or a card for every spell; the single strongest engine — nearly every opponent action flips Sygg and feeds the discard payoffs.
- **Parasitic Impetus** — Goad aura that drains its controller two per forced attack and gains you the same; goad plus repeating Sygg fuel.
- **Polluted Delta** — Fetch that fixes perfectly and hands you an on-demand life loss when you need to charge Bloodchief Ascension.
- **Psychic Impetus** — Goad aura that pays you scry 2 on every forced attack; keeps your draws live in the long game.
- **Qarsi Revenant** — Flying deathtouch lifelink deterrent that later exiles itself from the graveyard to move its keywords onto a bigger body.
- **Rankle, Master of Pranks** — Hasty evasive discard machine: every hit forces table-wide discard, life loss, or sacrifice — all three modes feed this deck.
- **Ravenous Chupacabra** — Unconditional creature kill on a body; also a fine reanimation target.
- **Reanimate** — One-mana rebuy of your best dead creature — or someone else's from graveyards this deck fills; the life cost is covered by the blood engines.
- **Renegade Silent** — Goads a creature every end step, grows, then phases out so it cannot be punished; a maintenance-free goad engine.
- **Rise of the Dark Realms** — The late-game haymaker: after ten turns of punisher attrition, all graveyards belong to you.
- **Rogue's Passage** — Makes Nighthawk Scavenger or a ciphered carrier unblockable; a win-condition land at no card cost.
- **Rug of Smothering** — One life per spell cast that turn, scaling against the decks that chain spells; a flying blocker in the meantime.
- **Scrawling Crawler** — Group draw stapled to draw punishment: every opponent draw drains one, doubling with the Mine effects.
- **Sky Diamond** — Budget blue rock; enters tapped but keeps the two-color curve honest.
- **Soot Imp** — One life per nonblack spell; a flying drip that taxes almost everything three opponents do.
- **Sunken Hollow** — Typed dual the check and tainted lands see as Island Swamp; usually untapped by the mid game.
- **Sunken Ruins** — The best filter land for double-pip Dimir costs.
- **Swamp** — Fourteen basics; black is the deck's primary color and the checks want them.
- **Syphon Mind** — Each opponent discards, you draw three; card advantage that triggers Liliana's Caress and Geth's Grimoire three times.
- **Tainted Isle** — Painless dual once any Swamp is down, which is nearly always.
- **Talion, the Kindly Lord** — Names the table's most common mana value and taxes every such spell two life and a card; a punisher and draw engine in one flyer.
- **Temple of Deceit** — Tapped dual with scry; sequencing land for slow turns.
- **The Soul Stone** — Indestructible black rock that later becomes a recurring reanimation engine once harnessed; the exile cost eats a spent token or Foulmire Knight.
- **This Is How It Ends** — Shuffle-away removal that dodges death triggers; the villainous choice drains five or eats a second creature — both halves feed Sygg.
- **Tidehollow Strix** — Two-mana flying deathtouch wall; the artifact body dodges some sweepers.
- **Titan Hunter** — Four damage at each quiet end step; on goaded tables it flickers off, but against passive decks it is the fastest clock here, and it sacrifices for four life at instant speed.
- **Undermine** — Hard counter that drains three; protection for the engines that also flips the Sygg check itself.
- **Urza's Guilt** — Everyone draws two, discards three, loses four: a one-card Sygg trigger, triple Caress trigger, and hand-punisher refill.
- **Venser's Journal** — Gains life per card in your overstuffed hand and removes your maximum hand size; the personal payoff for the group-draw engine.
- **Vile Consumption** — One life per creature per upkeep; crushes token boards while your handful of deathtouchers pay pennies.
- **Viseling** — Second Iron Maiden on a 3/3 construct body; redundancy for the core hand-size punishment.
- **Watery Grave** — Untapped typed dual; the two-life shock even feeds your own Sygg-adjacent bookkeeping (opponents' copies feed his check).
- **Whispering Madness** — Wheel with cipher: repeatable table-wide discard off an unblockable creature, detonating Liliana's Caress, Geth's Grimoire, and Bloodchief Ascension every combat.
- **Windfall** — The classic one-shot wheel; with the discard payoffs down it is the deck's biggest single-turn life swing.

## Talks

### 2026-08-27 — Assessment: Bracket 2+

Checked against the live Wizards Commander page (Brackets beta: 0 Game Changers in Brackets 1–2, up to 3 in Bracket 3) and a fresh Scryfall `is:gamechanger` query: the 100 contains zero Game Changers, no two-card infinite combo, no extra turns, and no mass land denial. Exquisite Blood and Bloodthirsty Conqueror were read carefully — both trigger on *opponents losing life*, so they stack but cannot loop with each other; the deck has no Sanguine Bond half. The win is incremental, telegraphed, board-based drain that dies to enchantment/artifact sweepers, which is Core behavior, but the engine density, the continuous Sygg draw, and the wheel burst (Windfall or Whispering Madness with Liliana's Caress plus charged Bloodchief Ascension drains roughly 8–14 per opponent in one turn) put it at the top of the bracket. Estimated pace: credible high roll ~turn 8, normal goldfish ~turn 10–12, interactive games later, often via Rise of the Dark Realms. Not Bracket 3: no tutors, no fast mana, and the deck cannot reliably present a win by turn six; the one-large-turn wheel line needs three assembled pieces and a full table of hands.
