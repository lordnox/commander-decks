# [Sygg, River Cutthroat](https://scryfall.com/card/znc/103/sygg-river-cutthroat?utm_source=api) — Thousand Cuts

> Bracket 2+ Dimir group-slug punisher deck with no Game Changers. A Dimir punisher deck where nobody takes a big hit, but everybody bleeds. Passive drip engines — hand-size punishment, discard taxes, cast taxes, and goaded combat — make every opponent lose life on every turn of the table, and [**Sygg, River Cutthroat**](https://scryfall.com/card/znc/103/sygg-river-cutthroat?utm_source=api) converts each end step where any opponent lost three or more life into a card. The life that leaves the table comes back to you through drain and lifegain, and the game ends by attrition.

## How the deck works

1. Land [**Sygg, River Cutthroat**](https://scryfall.com/card/znc/103/sygg-river-cutthroat?utm_source=api) on turn two and keep him around; he asks for no mana afterward and only needs someone, anyone, to have lost three life by each end step.
2. Deploy passive punishers that make that life loss happen without you spending cards: [**Iron Maiden**](https://scryfall.com/card/ulg/127/iron-maiden?utm_source=api) and [**Viseling**](https://scryfall.com/card/c13/269/viseling?utm_source=api) tax full hands, [**Soot Imp**](https://scryfall.com/card/dvd/37/soot-imp?utm_source=api) and [**Rug of Smothering**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/336/rug-of-smothering?utm_source=api) tax every spell, and [**Titan Hunter**](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/48/titan-hunter?utm_source=api) hits each player's end step outright.
3. Feed opponents cards so the taxes hurt more: [**Howling Mine**](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/257/howling-mine?utm_source=api), [**Anvil of Bogardan**](https://scryfall.com/card/vis/141/anvil-of-bogardan?utm_source=api), and [**Scrawling Crawler**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/132/scrawling-crawler?utm_source=api) fill hands for the hand-size punishers while the Crawler drains each draw directly.
4. Layer discard punishment on top: [**Painful Quandary**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/179/painful-quandary?utm_source=api) makes every spell cost five life or a card, and each discarded card triggers [**Liliana's Caress**](https://scryfall.com/card/m11/103/lilianas-caress?utm_source=api) and [**Geth's Grimoire**](https://scryfall.com/card/dst/123/geths-grimoire?utm_source=api).
5. Goad the biggest creatures at the table so opponents beat each other bloody while your deathtouch wall keeps you safe. Every point they trade fuels [Sygg](https://scryfall.com/card/znc/103/sygg-river-cutthroat?utm_source=api).
6. Convert the bleeding into your own life total with [**Exquisite Blood**](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/195/exquisite-blood?utm_source=api), [**Bloodthirsty Conqueror**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/58/bloodthirsty-conqueror?utm_source=api), and [**Bloodchief Ascension**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/139/bloodchief-ascension?utm_source=api), then win the attrition war you started.

There is no infinite combo; the deck wins because everyone else's life total has been paying rent since turn two.

## Main synergy patterns

### [Sygg](https://scryfall.com/card/znc/103/sygg-river-cutthroat?utm_source=api) only needs one bleeder

[**Sygg, River Cutthroat**](https://scryfall.com/card/znc/103/sygg-river-cutthroat?utm_source=api) checks each end step — including every opponent's — whether **any** opponent lost three or more life that turn. Damage counts, life payments count, and drain effects count. With one [**Viseling**](https://scryfall.com/card/c13/269/viseling?utm_source=api) plus a fetch land crack or a goaded attack somewhere at the table, that is a card on nearly every turn cycle, which quickly outdraws the extra card [**Howling Mine**](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/257/howling-mine?utm_source=api) hands your opponents.

### Overfilled hands are a life tax

[**Howling Mine**](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/257/howling-mine?utm_source=api), [**Anvil of Bogardan**](https://scryfall.com/card/vis/141/anvil-of-bogardan?utm_source=api), and [**Scrawling Crawler**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/132/scrawling-crawler?utm_source=api) look generous until [**Iron Maiden**](https://scryfall.com/card/ulg/127/iron-maiden?utm_source=api) and [**Viseling**](https://scryfall.com/card/c13/269/viseling?utm_source=api) start charging each opponent for every card above four in their upkeep, and [**Painful Quandary**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/179/painful-quandary?utm_source=api) charges them again for casting their way out. You are exempt from your own half of the pain: [**Ivory Tower**](https://scryfall.com/card/brr/86z/ivory-tower?utm_source=api) and [**Venser's Journal**](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/273/vensers-journal?utm_source=api) pay you for the same big hand, and the Journal plus [**Anvil of Bogardan**](https://scryfall.com/card/vis/141/anvil-of-bogardan?utm_source=api) removes your maximum hand size.

### Wheels as burst damage

With [**Liliana's Caress**](https://scryfall.com/card/m11/103/lilianas-caress?utm_source=api) out, [**Windfall**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/123/windfall?utm_source=api) or [**Whispering Madness**](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/229/whispering-madness?utm_source=api) drains each opponent two life per card they discard — often eight to twelve life across the table in one spell — while [**Geth's Grimoire**](https://scryfall.com/card/dst/123/geths-grimoire?utm_source=api) draws you a card per discarded card. With [**Bloodchief Ascension**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/139/bloodchief-ascension?utm_source=api) at three counters, every one of those discarded cards drains another two on its way to the graveyard, and mass discard refills you to full. Cipher [**Whispering Madness**](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/229/whispering-madness?utm_source=api) onto a flyer to do it again.

### Goad is removal that pays you

The goad auras double as removal-by-redirection: [**Ghoulish Impetus**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/214/ghoulish-impetus?utm_source=api) gives the problem creature deathtouch so it trades with whatever blocks it, [**Parasitic Impetus**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/222/parasitic-impetus?utm_source=api) drains its controller on every forced attack, and [**Bloodthirsty Blade**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/225/bloodthirsty-blade?utm_source=api) can re-goad a new threat every turn for one mana. Combat between opponents is life loss you did not pay for, and [**Curse of Verbosity**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/717/curse-of-verbosity?utm_source=api) on the archenemy pays you a card whenever the table piles on.

### The blood engines

[**Exquisite Blood**](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/195/exquisite-blood?utm_source=api) and [**Bloodthirsty Conqueror**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/58/bloodthirsty-conqueror?utm_source=api) each convert every point of opponent life loss into your life, so together they double it. They do not form a loop with each other — both trigger on the same event — but with the whole punisher suite ticking they routinely gain you ten or more life per turn cycle. [**Bloodchief Ascension**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/139/bloodchief-ascension?utm_source=api) charges itself off the ambient bleeding (an opponent losing two life in a turn is the norm here) and then punishes every discard, mill, fetch crack, and dying creature for two more.

## Win conditions

- **Attrition drain**: the passive engines deal two to eight life per opponent per turn cycle once three or four punishers are down. Forty life per head erodes in a handful of turn cycles while your own total climbs.
- **Wheel turns**: [**Windfall**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/123/windfall?utm_source=api) or [**Whispering Madness**](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/229/whispering-madness?utm_source=api) with [**Liliana's Caress**](https://scryfall.com/card/m11/103/lilianas-caress?utm_source=api) and [**Bloodchief Ascension**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/139/bloodchief-ascension?utm_source=api) online is the closest thing to a finisher and can take twenty-plus life off the table at once.
- **[Rise of the Dark Realms](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/183/rise-of-the-dark-realms?utm_source=api)**: after a long punisher game the graveyards are full; stealing every creature at once usually ends it on the spot.
- **Incidental combat**: [**Nighthawk Scavenger**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/144/nighthawk-scavenger?utm_source=api) grows large fast, and [**Rogue's Passage**](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/212/rogues-passage?utm_source=api) pushes it through for chunks of lifelinked damage.

## Game progression

### Early game

Turn two [Sygg](https://scryfall.com/card/znc/103/sygg-river-cutthroat?utm_source=api), then rocks: [**Arcane Signet**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/192/arcane-signet?utm_source=api), [**Dimir Signet**](https://scryfall.com/card/clu/221/dimir-signet?utm_source=api), [**Fellwar Stone**](https://scryfall.com/card/mbc/74/fellwar-stone?utm_source=api), [**Charcoal Diamond**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/305/charcoal-diamond?utm_source=api), [**Sky Diamond**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/337/sky-diamond?utm_source=api), [**The Soul Stone**](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/66/the-soul-stone?utm_source=api). A turn-three [**Baleful Strix**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/215/baleful-strix?utm_source=api) or [**Tidehollow Strix**](https://scryfall.com/card/c13/222/tidehollow-strix?utm_source=api) tells early aggro to go elsewhere.

### Mid game

Sequence punishers by what the table is doing: hand-size taxes against draw-heavy decks, [**Isolation Cell**](https://scryfall.com/card/nph/141/isolation-cell?utm_source=api) and [**Vile Consumption**](https://scryfall.com/card/inv/285/vile-consumption?utm_source=api) against creature swarms, [**Soot Imp**](https://scryfall.com/card/dvd/37/soot-imp?utm_source=api) and [**Rug of Smothering**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/336/rug-of-smothering?utm_source=api) against storm-adjacent value piles. Start goading the biggest creature every turn. Hold up [**Undermine**](https://scryfall.com/card/ddh/62/undermine?utm_source=api) or [**Countersquall**](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/198/countersquall?utm_source=api) for enchantment removal aimed at your engines.

### Late game

[**Painful Quandary**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/179/painful-quandary?utm_source=api) plus a wheel empties the last resistance. [**Aclazotz, Deepest Betrayal**](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/88/aclazotz-deepest-betrayal-temple-of-the-dead?utm_source=api) attacks strip the last cards from hands, gains life in the air, and comes back as a land even if killed — flip it back once someone is hellbent. Close with a big wheel turn or [**Rise of the Dark Realms**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/183/rise-of-the-dark-realms?utm_source=api).

## Mulligan guide

Keep hands with:

- two or three lands producing both colors by turn three;
- [Sygg](https://scryfall.com/card/znc/103/sygg-river-cutthroat?utm_source=api) on curve plus at least one punisher or draw engine costing four or less;
- a rock or an early blocker against aggressive seats.

Mulligan hands that are all five-drops, all interaction with no engine, or one land. The deck draws a lot once it starts; it just needs to survive to that point.

## Important sequencing and rules notes

- [**Sygg, River Cutthroat**](https://scryfall.com/card/znc/103/sygg-river-cutthroat?utm_source=api) triggers at **each** end step, not only yours, and checks the whole turn's life loss from any single opponent. Life *paid* (fetch lands, [**Watery Grave**](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/306/watery-grave?utm_source=api) shocks, [**Breathstealer's Crypt**](https://scryfall.com/card/vis/127/breathstealers-crypt?utm_source=api) payments) counts as life loss.
- [**Exquisite Blood**](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/195/exquisite-blood?utm_source=api) and [**Bloodthirsty Conqueror**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/58/bloodthirsty-conqueror?utm_source=api) both trigger on opponents losing life; they stack but do **not** loop with each other. Neither triggers the other.
- [**Iron Maiden**](https://scryfall.com/card/ulg/127/iron-maiden?utm_source=api) and [**Viseling**](https://scryfall.com/card/c13/269/viseling?utm_source=api) deal damage on the opponent's upkeep — before their draw step. [**Anvil of Bogardan**](https://scryfall.com/card/vis/141/anvil-of-bogardan?utm_source=api) and [**Howling Mine**](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/257/howling-mine?utm_source=api) refill them on the draw step that follows, keeping the count high for the next cycle.
- [**Vile Consumption**](https://scryfall.com/card/inv/285/vile-consumption?utm_source=api) hits your creatures too. Deploy it when your board is small deathtouchers and theirs is wide, and remember each upkeep payment is life loss that feeds [Sygg](https://scryfall.com/card/znc/103/sygg-river-cutthroat?utm_source=api) and [**Bloodchief Ascension**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/139/bloodchief-ascension?utm_source=api).
- [**Titan Hunter**](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/48/titan-hunter?utm_source=api) checks *each player's* end step and only fires if no creature died that turn; goad-heavy turns with combat trades switch it off, quiet turns turn it on. Its sacrifice ability can gain you four life at instant speed in response to removal.
- [**Aclazotz, Deepest Betrayal**](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/88/aclazotz-deepest-betrayal-temple-of-the-dead?utm_source=api) returns transformed as [Temple of the Dead](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/88/aclazotz-deepest-betrayal-temple-of-the-dead?utm_source=api) when it dies; you can flip it back to the creature side for {2}{B} only when a player has one or fewer cards in hand — easy to reach after a [**Windfall**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/123/windfall?utm_source=api) someone answered with an empty grip.
- [**Memory Plunder**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/849/memory-plunder?utm_source=api) casts from an **opponent's** graveyard; the wheels and forced discard stock it. It is four hybrid mana, so any mix of blue and black pays for it.
- [**Whispering Madness**](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/229/whispering-madness?utm_source=api) cipher triggers on your creature's combat damage; an unblockable route via [**Rogue's Passage**](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/212/rogues-passage?utm_source=api) makes the wheel repeatable.
- [**Likeness Looter**](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/208/likeness-looter?utm_source=api) copies a creature card in **your** graveyard at sorcery speed; loot your best creature away early and wear it later. The [Bat](https://scryfall.com/card/tblb/10/bat?utm_source=api) tokens in the box are for [**Aclazotz, Deepest Betrayal**](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/88/aclazotz-deepest-betrayal-temple-of-the-dead?utm_source=api), whose trigger makes a [Bat](https://scryfall.com/card/tblb/10/bat?utm_source=api) whenever an opponent discards a **land** card.
- [**Oneirophage**](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/162/oneirophage?utm_source=api) grows on *your* draws — [Sygg](https://scryfall.com/card/znc/103/sygg-river-cutthroat?utm_source=api), [**Faerie Mastermind**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/114/faerie-mastermind?utm_source=api), and group draw all feed it into a real threat.

## Weaknesses and what to protect

Enchantment and artifact sweepers are the nightmare: the whole tax base is [**Painful Quandary**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/179/painful-quandary?utm_source=api), [**Liliana's Caress**](https://scryfall.com/card/m11/103/lilianas-caress?utm_source=api), and a shelf of artifacts, so hold [**Undermine**](https://scryfall.com/card/ddh/62/undermine?utm_source=api), [**Countersquall**](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/198/countersquall?utm_source=api), and [**Otawara, Soaring City**](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/271/otawara-soaring-city?utm_source=api) for the wipe, not the creature. The deck has no board wipe of its own — [**Aetherize**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/151/aetherize?utm_source=api), [**Vile Consumption**](https://scryfall.com/card/inv/285/vile-consumption?utm_source=api), and the goad suite are the crowd control, so a fast wide board that ignores goad politics can race you. Lifegain hate ([**Exquisite Blood**](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/195/exquisite-blood?utm_source=api) being named by removal, or an opponent's Tainted Remedy effect) removes your cushion but not the clock. [Sygg](https://scryfall.com/card/znc/103/sygg-river-cutthroat?utm_source=api) himself is cheap to recast; protect the enchantment engines first.
