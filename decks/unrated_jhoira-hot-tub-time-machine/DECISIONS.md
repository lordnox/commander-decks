# Decisions

## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, the primer, and the assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a card back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated session notes so an argument does not live only in chat.

Every unique card needs a Cards in line. The other headings appear when there is something to record.

## Goals

- **Suspend** — Cards cook in exile with time counters and resolve for free; the 99 is a hot-tub timer, not a storm kill.
- **Redo** — Rebound, flashback, buyback, and extra upkeeps make the same action happen again without chaining extra turns.

## Rules

- Jhoira's ability is not suspend-from-hand cost: you pay `{2}`, exile a nonland you already have, and it gains suspend with exactly four counters regardless of mana value.
- Suspended creatures cast without paying mana cost have haste from the suspend reminder, including cards Jhoira suspended.
- Removing the last time counter is a trigger; the cast is optional. Opponents can respond to the trigger before you cast.
- Paradox Haze on yourself gives an additional upkeep after the first; each suspended card loses a counter in each of those upkeeps.
- The Ninth Doctor's extra upkeep triggers when it becomes untapped during your untap step, not when you untap it with an effect at another time.
- Delay counters a spell and suspends it for its owner; when the last counter leaves, that player may cast it. It is not a hard exile forever.
- Greater Gargadon's sacrifice ability only works while it is suspended.
- This is not infinite: each timer hack removes a finite number of counters. Fat-once is one free cast per card.

## Talks

### 2026-09-08 — Autobrew: Hot Tub Time Machine

**Game plan.** Exile nonlands with Jhoira (or native suspend) so they cook with time counters; accelerate timers and extra upkeeps; convert free haste creatures and suspended value into combat, with Inevitable Betrayal and Niv-Mizzet as backups. Backup route: rebound stack (Cast Through Time, Faithless Salvaging, Recurring Insight) without the commander.

**Target bracket.** Incremental Core (Bracket 2). Normal win turn 9–10; high roll ~8; commander turn 3–4; turn-five milestone = Jhoira online and at least one card cooking or a native suspend ticking. No Game Changers, no permanent fast mana, no chained extra turns, no two-card infinites. Extra turns are not the plan (Twelfth Doctor — Borrowed Time owns that joke).

**Commander shortlist.** Winner Jhoira of the Ghitu. Lost: Alaundo (no red heat), Obeka Splitter (combat-upkeep, Grixis overlap with Borrowed Time's Obeka lore), Taigam (rebound needs attack, Azorius), Tenth Doctor (Doctor Who overlap with stored deck).

**Packages.** Tub loader (Jhoira + draw/filter); timer hacks (Paradox Haze, Clockspinning, Timecrafting, Fury Charm, Timebender, Timebug, Sand-Mage, Rift Elemental, Suspend, Ninth Doctor); native suspend spells; suspend creatures; rebound redo; Capsize/Delay interaction; 37 lands + 7 gentle ramp.

**Mana budget (v1).** 37 true lands, 0 modal land backs. Commander `{1}{U}{R}` aims for turn 3 with an untapped dual or rock. Curve leans on 1–2 mana cantrips and hacks; bombs mostly enter via suspend rather than hardcast. Opening: 2–3 lands including U, keep hands that can cast Jhoira by turn 4 even if turn 3 is a stretch.
