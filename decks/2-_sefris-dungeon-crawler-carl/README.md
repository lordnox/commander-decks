## How the deck works

[**Sefris of the Hidden Ways**](https://scryfall.com/card/afc/3/sefris-of-the-hidden-ways?utm_source=api) ventures once per turn when a creature card hits the graveyard, then returns a creature whenever you complete a dungeon. The 99 is built to make that once-a-turn trigger easy (loot, mill, sacrifice) and to venture extra times with other crawlers.

Default first floor is [**Lost Mine of Phandelver**](https://scryfall.com/card/tafr/21/lost-mine-of-phandelver?utm_source=api): four rooms on a short path, tokens and a card at the end. [**Tomb of Annihilation**](https://scryfall.com/card/tafr/22/tomb-of-annihilation?utm_source=api) is the painful shortcut when you already have a fat target to reanimate. [**Dungeon of the Mad Mage**](https://scryfall.com/card/tafr/20/dungeon-of-the-mad-mage?utm_source=api) is the long loot dungeon after you can afford the extra rooms.

If Sefris is gone, [**White Plume Adventurer**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/49/white-plume-adventurer?utm_source=api) and friends take the initiative and race Undercity instead.

## Win conditions

1. Complete floors, reanimate [**Grave Titan**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/74/grave-titan?utm_source=api) / [**Radiant Solar**](https://scryfall.com/card/afc/9/radiant-solar?utm_source=api) / [**Sun Titan**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/178/sun-titan?utm_source=api), and attack behind [**Midnight Pathlighter**](https://scryfall.com/card/afc/52/midnight-pathlighter?utm_source=api).
2. [**Gray Merchant of Asphodel**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/142/gray-merchant-of-asphodel?utm_source=api) drains after devotion is on the board; Sefris can return it after another floor.
3. [**Felidar Sovereign**](https://scryfall.com/card/bfz/26/felidar-sovereign?utm_source=api) if life from dungeons, Solar, and Merchant crosses 40 — it has to survive an upkeep.
4. Initiative combat if the table is fighting over Undercity and Sefris never sticks.

None of these is a two-card infinite. [**Altar of Dementia**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/218/altar-of-dementia?utm_source=api) milling opponents is a slow clock after you already have creatures to feed it.

## Early, mid, and late

**Turns 1–3.** Land, a diamond or Bauble, a cheap cat or looter. Cast Sefris on three when you have all three colors. Do not tap out into a wipe if you have [**Selfless Spirit**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/167/selfless-spirit?utm_source=api) or [**Mother of Runes**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/702/mother-of-runes?utm_source=api).

**Turns 4–6.** Trigger Sefris every turn. Add a second venture source. Aim to complete Lost Mine and return the best creature in the yard. [**Hama Pashar, Ruin Seeker**](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/224/hama-pashar-ruin-seeker?utm_source=api) is the upgrade that makes each remaining room huge.

**Turns 7+.** [Radiant Solar](https://scryfall.com/card/afc/9/radiant-solar?utm_source=api) turns every creature into a room. Keep completing dungeons. Present lethal over a turn cycle, not from an empty board.

## Mulligan

Keep: two or three lands that can produce two colors immediately, a ramp rock or Knight, and either yard fuel or an extra-venture creature. A one-land hand is a mulligan. A five-land hand with no cheap play is a mulligan. Do not keep a pile of six-drops.

Bottom cards that do nothing before Sefris: extra wipes, [Felidar Sovereign](https://scryfall.com/card/bfz/26/felidar-sovereign?utm_source=api), [Grave Titan](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/74/grave-titan?utm_source=api), [Deep Analysis](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/149/deep-analysis?utm_source=api) if you are already flooding.

## Tutor and [Buried Alive](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/273/buried-alive?utm_source=api) order

[**Buried Alive**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/273/buried-alive?utm_source=api) is the only real tutor. Typical pile: [**Karmic Guide**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/151/karmic-guide?utm_source=api) + a closer + [**Nine-Lives Familiar**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/66/nine-lives-familiar?utm_source=api) or [**Nekrataal**](https://scryfall.com/card/ema/99/nekrataal?utm_source=api). If Sefris is in play, the three cards are one venture. Leave a target for Create Undead when the floor is about to end.

## Sequencing

- Trigger Sefris on *your* turn if you can, so you choose the next room before combat.
- Tokens dying do not venture Sefris; sacrifice an actual creature card.
- Complete the dungeon with Sefris's trigger on the stack only if the last room is already the one you are entering — completing happens as you advance into the last room's ability, then Create Undead.
- [**Nine-Lives Familiar**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/66/nine-lives-familiar?utm_source=api) only has revival counters if you *cast* it. A Sefris return is a 1/1 that dies for good.
- [**Immovable Rod**](https://scryfall.com/card/afc/7/immovable-rod?utm_source=api): tap to shut a permanent off, leave it tapped; when you finally let it untap, you venture.
- Recast Sefris after two removals; the deck still crawls with Nadaar, maps, and initiative.

## Weaknesses

Graveyard hate blanks Sefris loot and [Buried Alive](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/273/buried-alive?utm_source=api). Rest in Peace also stops the venture trigger. The mana base has a lot of taplands; three tapped lands on the play often miss the turn-three commander. The deck is slow if you never complete a dungeon and never take the initiative. Wipes after a completed-floor reanimate cost a turn of loot.

Protect Sefris when you are one room from a complete floor.
