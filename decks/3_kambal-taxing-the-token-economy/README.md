## How the deck works

Build a draw engine and a sacrifice outlet, then deploy [Kambal](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/211/kambal-profiteering-mayor?utm_source=api) before giving opponents tokens. His copy trigger works once each turn, so spread gifts across turns when possible. His drain trigger has no once-per-turn limit, but drains once per token-entry event rather than once per token.

## Synergies and win conditions

[Tombstone Stairwell](https://scryfall.com/card/mir/149/tombstone-stairwell?utm_source=api) creates all players' Tombspawn simultaneously at each upkeep. If [Kambal](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/211/kambal-profiteering-mayor?utm_source=api)'s copy ability has not triggered that turn, it copies the entire opposing batch. Your original batch and [Kambal](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/211/kambal-profiteering-mayor?utm_source=api)'s later copied batch each trigger his drain once when nonempty. The original Tombspawn are destroyed at each end step; [Kambal](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/211/kambal-profiteering-mayor?utm_source=api)'s copies were created by [Kambal](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/211/kambal-profiteering-mayor?utm_source=api) and are not among the tokens created by the Stairwell. Creature graveyards determine the size of the original batches.

[Alliance of Arms](https://scryfall.com/card/cmd/4/alliance-of-arms?utm_source=api) rewards a stocked mana pool. [Kambal](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/211/kambal-profiteering-mayor?utm_source=api) copies all opposing tokens in its single simultaneous entry event, then drains from the copied batch. [Mondrak, Glory Dominus](https://scryfall.com/card/one/23/mondrak-glory-dominus?utm_source=api) doubles tokens created under your control, including [Kambal](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/211/kambal-profiteering-mayor?utm_source=api)'s copies, but doubling one batch does not double the number of [Kambal](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/211/kambal-profiteering-mayor?utm_source=api) drain triggers.

[Massacre Wurm](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/754/massacre-wurm?utm_source=api) punishes opposing creature deaths and can clear small donated tokens. [Blood Artist](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/209/blood-artist?utm_source=api) sees creatures die on every side; most of the other death payoffs require your own creatures. [Mirkwood Bats](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/95/mirkwood-bats?utm_source=api) counts each token created or sacrificed, including Treasures; [Marionette Apprentice](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/100/marionette-apprentice?utm_source=api) sees your artifacts reach the graveyard too.

Combat is a backup finish: [Thalisse, Reverent Medium](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/306/thalisse-reverent-medium?utm_source=api) builds flying Spirits, [Caretaker's Talent](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/6/caretakers-talent?utm_source=api) can boost tokens, and [Champions from Beyond](https://scryfall.com/card/fic/11/champions-from-beyond?utm_source=api) rewards a wide attack. [Halo Fountain](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/15/halo-fountain?utm_source=api) wins with fifteen tapped creatures, five white mana, and an untapped Fountain. [Kambal](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/211/kambal-profiteering-mayor?utm_source=api)'s copies enter tapped and can pay its untap costs immediately; the creatures' summoning sickness does not stop paying that cost.


[Bojuka Bog](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/363/bojuka-bog?utm_source=api) clears one player's graveyard when played as a land. [Boggart Trawler](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/243/boggart-trawler-boggart-bog?utm_source=api) offers the same reset on a three-mana creature, which can then be sacrificed, copied, or returned through recursion. Its [Boggart Bog](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/243/boggart-trawler-boggart-bog?utm_source=api) land face produces black and can enter untapped for three life, but provides no graveyard hate. Save these resets for dangerous recursion or graveyard engines; harmless opposing creatures in graveyards remain useful [Tombstone Stairwell](https://scryfall.com/card/mir/149/tombstone-stairwell?utm_source=api) material.

## Early, mid, and late game

Early: develop lands and a mana rock, then establish draw or a cheap sacrifice outlet. Respect the many lands that enter tapped when planning the third turn.

Mid: cast [Kambal](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/211/kambal-profiteering-mayor?utm_source=api) before a token gift and layer a drain payoff. Use removal to keep dangerous attackers in check; token-granting removal can also supply [Kambal](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/211/kambal-profiteering-mayor?utm_source=api)'s first copy event of the turn.

Late: combine repeated token batches with sacrifice and drain, rebuild creatures through recursion, or threaten a wide attack or [Halo Fountain](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/15/halo-fountain?utm_source=api). Keep enough resources to recast [Kambal](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/211/kambal-profiteering-mayor?utm_source=api) rather than spending every token on a single burst of mana.

## Mulligan guide

Prefer three lands with access to both colors and an early draw, ramp, or engine card. A two-land hand needs reliable early draw or acceleration and a clear path through tapped lands. Avoid hands full of expensive payoffs without setup; [Kambal](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/211/kambal-profiteering-mayor?utm_source=api) alone does not keep cards flowing.

## Sequencing and rules notes

- [Ophiomancer](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/118/ophiomancer?utm_source=api) checks for no Snakes both when its upkeep trigger would trigger and when it resolves. Sacrifice an existing Snake before the next upkeep if you want another.
- [Caretaker's Talent](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/6/caretakers-talent?utm_source=api) and [Enduring Innocence](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/6/enduring-innocence?utm_source=api) each draw at most once per turn. [Belladonna Took](https://scryfall.com/card/hob/4/belladonna-took?utm_source=api) instead rewards the first three token-entry events that resolve each turn.
- [Wedding Ring](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/32/wedding-ring?utm_source=api) creates its opposing copy only if cast. [Saw in Half](https://scryfall.com/card/unf/88/saw-in-half?utm_source=api) creates creature copies; it does not cast those creatures.
- [Silverquill Lecturer](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/M3C-96/silverquill-lecturer?utm_source=api)'s demonstrate gives an opponent a creature-spell copy as well as your own extra copy. Choose the opponent with the consequences of that creature's abilities in mind.
- [Thalisse, Reverent Medium](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/306/thalisse-reverent-medium?utm_source=api) counts tokens you created earlier that turn even if they have already left the battlefield.

## Weaknesses and what to protect

Protect [Kambal](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/211/kambal-profiteering-mayor?utm_source=api) when a gift is about to resolve and protect draw engines when rebuilding. Creature wipes can cash in death triggers but also dismantle the engine. Opponent gifts can become attackers or sacrifice resources, and several effects provide value to the whole table. Graveyard disruption weakens recursion and [Tombstone Stairwell](https://scryfall.com/card/mir/149/tombstone-stairwell?utm_source=api). A tapped-land-heavy opening can delay setup, while [Halo Fountain](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/15/halo-fountain?utm_source=api) asks for substantial white production.
