Exile-theft control replaces familiar resources with other players' cards. [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) turns draws into opposing-library choices, [Knowledge Pool](https://scryfall.com/card/mbs/111/knowledge-pool?utm_source=api) exchanges spells cast from hand, and every opponent-owned card left in exile makes [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) larger. The deck steals only what it needs; unused cards remain a power bank for combat or direct damage.

## How the deck works

The first turns establish Dimir mana plus access to off-colour mana. [Fellwar Stone](https://scryfall.com/card/mbc/74/fellwar-stone?utm_source=api), [Spectral Searchlight](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/411/spectral-searchlight?utm_source=api), [Darksteel Ingot](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/256/darksteel-ingot?utm_source=api), [Prismatic Lens](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/264/prismatic-lens?utm_source=api), and the rainbow lands matter because older theft cards do not automatically fix the costs of stolen spells.

The middle game begins stocking exile. Small engines such as [Circu, Dimir Lobotomist](https://scryfall.com/card/rav/196/circu-dimir-lobotomist?utm_source=api), [Grimoire Thief](https://scryfall.com/card/mor/35/grimoire-thief?utm_source=api), [Knacksaw Clique](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/42/knacksaw-clique?utm_source=api), [Nightveil Specter](https://scryfall.com/card/gtc/222/nightveil-specter?utm_source=api), and [Ornate Kanzashi](https://scryfall.com/card/bok/157/ornate-kanzashi?utm_source=api) add cards incrementally. [Tasha's Hideous Laughter](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/78/tashas-hideous-laughter?utm_source=api), [Raven Guild Master](https://scryfall.com/card/scg/47/raven-guild-master?utm_source=api), [Author of Shadows](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/35/author-of-shadows?utm_source=api), [Curse of the Swine](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/192/curse-of-the-swine?utm_source=api), and [Perilous Vault](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/968/perilous-vault?utm_source=api) create large jumps.

Opposing graveyards are a second bank that nobody defends. [Agent of Erebos](https://scryfall.com/card/jou/59/agent-of-erebos?utm_source=api) and [Canoptek Scarab Swarm](https://scryfall.com/card/40k/150/canoptek-scarab-swarm?utm_source=api) empty one whole yard into exile, and the Swarm converts its artifacts and lands into flying bodies at the same time.

[Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) changes every future draw into an opposing-library card. [Knowledge Pool](https://scryfall.com/card/mbs/111/knowledge-pool?utm_source=api) starts with three cards from every library, then exchanges spells cast from hand. Neither is a hard lock: players continue playing Magic, but their usual hands and libraries stop behaving normally.

[Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) turns all opponent-owned cards still in exile into a single oversized threat. Casting a stolen card spends one point from that bank, so take lands, removal, protection, and decisive threats while leaving marginal cards untouched.

## Key patterns

### [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api)

While it is out, this deck never draws its own cards again. [Moonlight Bargain](https://scryfall.com/card/c18/114/moonlight-bargain?utm_source=api), [Petals of Insight](https://scryfall.com/card/mma/60/petals-of-insight?utm_source=api), and [Silent Hallcreeper](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/72/silent-hallcreeper?utm_source=api) put cards into hand without drawing, so they still work underneath it. Deploy [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) once the hand already holds evasion or protection rather than while relying on future draws for them.

Opponents choose which opponent supplies each [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) draw. A card owned by any opponent counts for [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) even when a different opponent exiled it. Cards taken from this deck do not count.

### [Knowledge Pool](https://scryfall.com/card/mbs/111/knowledge-pool?utm_source=api)

[Knowledge Pool](https://scryfall.com/card/mbs/111/knowledge-pool?utm_source=api) exiles three cards from each library as it enters, normally giving [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) nine power at a four-player table before anyone casts another spell. Its exchange trigger only sees spells cast from hand. Spells cast from [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) or another exile zone bypass the Pool.

Do not cast an expendable spell into the Pool without checking what every player can take. The next player may select any other eligible card, not merely one owned by the player who triggered it.

### Nonlegendary [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) copies

[Spark Double](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/62/spark-double?utm_source=api) and [Irenicus's Vile Duplication](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/78/irenicuss-vile-duplication?utm_source=api) create persistent nonlegendary copies. [Auton Soldier](https://scryfall.com/card/who/36/auton-soldier?utm_source=api) also becomes a nonlegendary [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) and gives that copy myriad. Its myriad tokens enter as additional Umbrises, triggering each [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) before combat damage and attacking the other opponents. Those copies deal ordinary damage, not commander damage.

[Nanogene Conversion](https://scryfall.com/card/who/49/nanogene-conversion?utm_source=api) does not cause entry triggers. Instead, it turns every other established creature into a nonlegendary [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) until end of turn, giving each the same exile-based power for one combat.

## Win conditions

1. **Commander damage:** make [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) lethal, then use [Dauthi Embrace](https://scryfall.com/card/tmp/120/dauthi-embrace?utm_source=api), [Shizo, Death's Storehouse](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/233/shizo-deaths-storehouse?utm_source=api), [Rogue's Passage](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/212/rogues-passage?utm_source=api), or [Ebony Fly](https://scryfall.com/card/afc/60/ebony-fly?utm_source=api) to connect. Keep track of commander damage separately; copied Umbrises do not contribute to it.
2. **Multiple Umbrises:** [Spark Double](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/62/spark-double?utm_source=api), [Irenicus's Vile Duplication](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/78/irenicuss-vile-duplication?utm_source=api), or [Auton Soldier](https://scryfall.com/card/who/36/auton-soldier?utm_source=api) creates enough full-sized attackers to pressure several players.
3. **Power shot:** [Surestrike Trident](https://scryfall.com/card/dst/147/surestrike-trident?utm_source=api) and [Essence Harvest](https://scryfall.com/card/avr/100/essence-harvest?utm_source=api) use the greatest power directly. [Rite of Consumption](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/76/rite-of-consumption?utm_source=api) sacrifices its creature, so reserve it for a final player or a commander that would otherwise be removed.
4. **Borrowed win:** [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api), [Arvinox, the Mind Flail](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/130/arvinox-the-mind-flail?utm_source=api), [Mind's Dilation](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/101/minds-dilation?utm_source=api), and the smaller theft engines can find an opponent's finisher, but the deck never depends on a particular opposing list.

[Tasha's Hideous Laughter](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/78/tashas-hideous-laughter?utm_source=api) is setup, not a win by itself. It exiles until each opponent has exiled cards with total mana value twenty or greater; lands contribute zero, and the spell does not cause a player to lose merely because their library becomes small.

## Game plan

### Early game

Keep hands with three mana sources or two lands plus a ramp piece. Prioritize access to both core colours, then establish one source capable of producing an opponent's colours. Deploy a small exile engine only when it can activate or attack safely.

### Mid game

Cast [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) when one exile deposit is ready or protection is available. A naked five-mana 1/1 advertises the plan without applying pressure. [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) is strongest after the hand contains usable mana and at least one way to protect or deliver [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api).

[Knowledge Pool](https://scryfall.com/card/mbs/111/knowledge-pool?utm_source=api) should enter when the table's hands are less useful than the cards likely to be exchanged. If [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) is already active, cards played from its exile avoid the Pool, giving this deck a predictable lane through the chaos.

### Late game

Count opponent-owned cards in every exile zone before committing. Lead with the large deposit, then create nonlegendary copies or grant evasion. Leave mana for [Vanishing](https://scryfall.com/card/vis/48/vanishing?utm_source=api), [Spellskite](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/873/spellskite?utm_source=api), or an exile counter whenever possible. Spend stolen cards only when their effect is worth shrinking every [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api).

## Mulligan guide

Keep:

- three lands with both core colours;
- two lands plus [Fellwar Stone](https://scryfall.com/card/mbc/74/fellwar-stone?utm_source=api), [Prismatic Lens](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/264/prismatic-lens?utm_source=api), or [Ebony Fly](https://scryfall.com/card/afc/60/ebony-fly?utm_source=api);
- early fixing plus [Grimoire Thief](https://scryfall.com/card/mor/35/grimoire-thief?utm_source=api), [Circu, Dimir Lobotomist](https://scryfall.com/card/rav/196/circu-dimir-lobotomist?utm_source=api), [Nightveil Specter](https://scryfall.com/card/gtc/222/nightveil-specter?utm_source=api), or [Ornate Kanzashi](https://scryfall.com/card/bok/157/ornate-kanzashi?utm_source=api);
- a five-mana route and either [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) protection or a large exile deposit.

Mulligan:

- hands whose only acceleration costs three or more and miss a third land;
- off-colour fixing without reliable blue and black;
- multiple six- and seven-mana theft engines;
- finishers without a way to stock opposing exile.

[Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) is not an automatic keep. Without mana development it gives opponents the first useful turn under its replacement effect while this deck remains unable to cast what it finds.

## Sequencing and rules

- Cast [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) after a large exile effect when possible; its power updates continuously and does not require it to witness the exile.
- A stolen spell stops contributing to [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) as soon as it moves from exile to the stack.
- Face-down opponent-owned cards count even when this deck cannot look at them.
- If [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) leaves, its cards remain exiled but nobody retains its permission to look at or play them.
- Ordinary clones retain legendary. [Silent Hallcreeper](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/72/silent-hallcreeper?utm_source=api) may copy another value creature, but copying [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) forces the legend rule.
- [Perilous Vault](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/968/perilous-vault?utm_source=api) exiles itself along with all nonland permanents. [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) will not survive, although the opponent-owned permanents remain banked for a later recast.
- [Vanishing](https://scryfall.com/card/vis/48/vanishing?utm_source=api) phases out itself with [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api). The Aura returns attached when [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) phases in.
- [Scrib Nibblers](https://scryfall.com/card/wwk/66/scrib-nibblers?utm_source=api) untaps only when an exiled card is a land. Its own activation may repeat only while it continues hitting lands.
- [Abyssal Harvester](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/54/abyssal-harvester?utm_source=api) can only take a creature card put into a graveyard this turn, and each activation exiles the previous Nightmare token. Use it before [Agent of Erebos](https://scryfall.com/card/jou/59/agent-of-erebos?utm_source=api) or [Canoptek Scarab Swarm](https://scryfall.com/card/40k/150/canoptek-scarab-swarm?utm_source=api) empties the yard it wants.
- The [Abyssal Harvester](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/54/abyssal-harvester?utm_source=api) token is a Nightmare, so it triggers every [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) as it enters.

## Weaknesses

[Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) is vulnerable before exile accumulates and becomes expensive after repeated removal. Enchantment removal restores normal draws by removing [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api), while artifact removal can collapse [Knowledge Pool](https://scryfall.com/card/mbs/111/knowledge-pool?utm_source=api) or the slow theft engines. The deck also relies on opposing libraries for card quality, so disciplined colour fixing is essential.

Graveyard decks can benefit from cards discarded or milled by [Thief of Sanity](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/243/thief-of-sanity?utm_source=api) and [King Narfi's Betrayal](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/272/king-narfis-betrayal?utm_source=api), so hold [Agent of Erebos](https://scryfall.com/card/jou/59/agent-of-erebos?utm_source=api) or [Canoptek Scarab Swarm](https://scryfall.com/card/40k/150/canoptek-scarab-swarm?utm_source=api) for the yard that is actually being used rather than the first one available. Fast go-wide pressure can arrive before the expensive engines matter; preserve [Curse of the Swine](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/192/curse-of-the-swine?utm_source=api) and [Perilous Vault](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/968/perilous-vault?utm_source=api) rather than spending them on isolated threats.
