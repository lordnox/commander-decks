> Bracket 3+ combo deck with no Game Changers. Usually threatens a win around turn six, but has an unlikely turn-four line.

A mono-black sacrifice deck built around repeatedly killing and recasting its commander. [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) turns itself into Jumblebones, Jumblebones returns [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api), and the deck converts that rotation into mana, cards, and life loss.

**Commander:** [Ozox, the Clattering King](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king)  
**Deck size:** 100 cards  
**Primary plan:** loop [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) and Jumblebones through sacrifice outlets  
**Backup plan:** grind with recursive creatures, sacrifice-based draw, and reanimation

## How the deck works

When [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) dies, it creates **Jumblebones**, a legendary 2/1 Skeleton token. When Jumblebones leaves the battlefield, it returns [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) from the graveyard to your hand.

That creates the deck's basic rotation:

1. Cast [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api).
2. Sacrifice [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api).
3. Create Jumblebones.
4. Sacrifice Jumblebones.
5. Return [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) to your hand.
6. Cast [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) again.

The raw loop costs mana, so the deck combines sacrifice outlets, cost reducers, and mana engines to make each rotation cheap or self-sustaining. Death and cast payoffs then turn the loop into a win.

[Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) is useful even without a full combo. Each trip through the rotation supplies two sacrifice events, two deaths, and two creatures entering the battlefield.

## Main combo lines

### [Bontu's Monument](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/179/bontus-monument?utm_source=api) + [Phyrexian Altar](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/311/phyrexian-altar?utm_source=api)

This is the cleanest win.

1. [Bontu's Monument](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/179/bontus-monument?utm_source=api) reduces [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) from {2}{B} to {1}{B}.
2. Cast [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api); [Bontu's Monument](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/179/bontus-monument?utm_source=api) drains each opponent and gains 1 life.
3. Sacrifice [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) to [Phyrexian Altar](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/311/phyrexian-altar?utm_source=api) for {B}; create Jumblebones.
4. Sacrifice Jumblebones to [Phyrexian Altar](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/311/phyrexian-altar?utm_source=api) for {B}; return [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) to your hand.
5. Spend the two mana to recast [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api).
6. Repeat.

The loop pays for itself, and [Bontu's Monument](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/179/bontus-monument?utm_source=api) wins through repeated cast triggers.

### [Phyrexian Altar](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/311/phyrexian-altar?utm_source=api) + any cost reducer + death payoff

[**Jet Medallion**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/292/jet-medallion?utm_source=api) is sufficient because it reduces [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) to {1}{B}. [**Heartless Summoning**](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/117/heartless-summoning?utm_source=api), [**Urza's Incubator**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/297/urzas-incubator?utm_source=api) naming Skeleton, and [**The Sibsig Ceremony**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/91/the-sibsig-ceremony?utm_source=api) reduce it even further.

Sacrificing [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) and Jumblebones to [Phyrexian Altar](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/311/phyrexian-altar?utm_source=api) produces the two mana needed for a {1}{B} [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api). Repeat the rotation with one of these in play:

- [Bastion of Remembrance](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/160/bastion-of-remembrance?utm_source=api)
- [Funeral Room](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/100/funeral-room-awakening-hall?utm_source=api)
- [Ayara, First of Locthwain](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/75/ayara-first-of-locthwain?utm_source=api)
- transformed Sephiroth's emblem

Note that [**Bastion of Remembrance**](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/160/bastion-of-remembrance?utm_source=api) triggers for [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api), but not for token Jumblebones. [**Funeral Room**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/100/funeral-room-awakening-hall?utm_source=api) and Sephiroth's emblem see both deaths.

### [Heartless Summoning](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/117/heartless-summoning?utm_source=api) + [Phyrexian Altar](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/311/phyrexian-altar?utm_source=api)

[Heartless Summoning](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/117/heartless-summoning?utm_source=api) reduces [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) to {B} and gives creatures -1/-1.

1. Sacrifice [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) to [Phyrexian Altar](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/311/phyrexian-altar?utm_source=api) for {B}.
2. Jumblebones enters as a 1/0 and dies immediately.
3. Jumblebones returns [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) to your hand.
4. Spend {B} to recast [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api).
5. Repeat.

This needs a death or enter-the-battlefield payoff to win. It does not require a second sacrifice activation for Jumblebones.

### [Ashnod's Altar](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/368/ashnods-altar?utm_source=api) + [Bog Initiate](https://scryfall.com/card/inv/95/bog-initiate?utm_source=api)

This line creates unlimited colorless mana.

1. Sacrifice [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) and Jumblebones to [Ashnod's Altar](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/368/ashnods-altar?utm_source=api) for four colorless mana total.
2. Spend one colorless through [Bog Initiate](https://scryfall.com/card/inv/95/bog-initiate?utm_source=api) to make {B}.
3. Spend {2}{B} to recast [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api).
4. Keep the remaining one colorless mana.
5. Repeat.

Each rotation adds one colorless mana and repeats the deck's death triggers. Add a death payoff to end the game.

## Value engines

### Reusable creatures

[**Reassembling Skeleton**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/195/reassembling-skeleton?utm_source=api) and [**Forsaken Miner**](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/88/forsaken-miner?utm_source=api) keep returning for further sacrifices. They turn conditional draw spells, Braids, and removal such as [Eaten Alive](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/106/eaten-alive?utm_source=api) into repeatable value.

[**Nine-Lives Familiar**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/66/nine-lives-familiar?utm_source=api) can die and return repeatedly across end steps. It is not an immediate infinite loop, but it provides a large reserve of sacrifice triggers without consuming more cards.

### Sacrifice-based draw

[Corrupted Conviction](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/84/corrupted-conviction?utm_source=api), [Costly Plunder](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/80/costly-plunder?utm_source=api), [Deadly Dispute](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/794/deadly-dispute?utm_source=api), [Eviscerator's Insight](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/93/eviscerators-insight?utm_source=api), [Nasty End](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/99/nasty-end?utm_source=api), [Reckoner's Bargain](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/120/reckoners-bargain?utm_source=api), and [Village Rites](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/137/village-rites?utm_source=api) turn a creature that was going to die anyway into cards.

[Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) is a particularly good additional cost: sacrificing it creates Jumblebones, and sacrificing Jumblebones later returns [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api).

Permanent draw engines reward longer chains:

- [Dark Prophecy](https://scryfall.com/card/m14/93/dark-prophecy?utm_source=api)
- [Grim Haruspex](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/754/grim-haruspex?utm_source=api)
- [Midnight Reaper](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/609/midnight-reaper?utm_source=api)
- [Erebos, Bleak-Hearted](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/868/erebos-bleak-hearted?utm_source=api)
- [Into the Pit](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/20/into-the-pit?utm_source=api)
- [Braids, Arisen Nightmare](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/82/braids-arisen-nightmare?utm_source=api)

Be careful during a loop: mandatory draw triggers can empty your library before opponents die.

### Sephiroth

[Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) and Jumblebones make transforming [**Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER**](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/115/sephiroth-fabled-soldier-sephiroth-one-winged-angel?utm_source=api) straightforward. Once transformed, his emblem remains for the rest of the game and turns every future creature death into life drain.

Sephiroth's attack trigger also converts spare recursive bodies into cards.

### [The Sibsig Ceremony](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/91/the-sibsig-ceremony?utm_source=api)

The Ceremony makes [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) cost only {B}, then destroys [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) after it enters and creates a 2/2 Zombie Druid. [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) still dies normally and creates Jumblebones.

This supplies bodies quickly and removes the need for an outlet to kill [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api), but Jumblebones still needs to leave the battlefield before [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) returns to hand.

## Winning the game

### 1. Repeat [Bontu's Monument](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/179/bontus-monument?utm_source=api)

The preferred deterministic finish is [Bontu's Monument](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/179/bontus-monument?utm_source=api), [Phyrexian Altar](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/311/phyrexian-altar?utm_source=api), and the [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) rotation.

### 2. Repeat death triggers

Use a self-sustaining [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) loop with [Funeral Room](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/100/funeral-room-awakening-hall?utm_source=api), [Bastion of Remembrance](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/160/bastion-of-remembrance?utm_source=api), Ayara, or Sephiroth's emblem.

### 3. Grind out the table

If no loop is available:

- sacrifice recursive creatures for cards and mana;
- drain opponents through normal creature deaths;
- recover key creatures with [Chthonian Nightmare](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/83/chthonian-nightmare?utm_source=api), [Reanimate](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/155/reanimate?utm_source=api), [Victimize](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/198/victimize?utm_source=api), or [Vat of Rebirth](https://scryfall.com/card/one/113/vat-of-rebirth?utm_source=api);
- unlock [Awakening Hall](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/100/funeral-room-awakening-hall?utm_source=api) late to rebuild the entire board.

## Playing the deck

### Early game

- Keep two or three lands plus ramp or cost reduction.
- Establish a sacrifice outlet before committing the valuable payoff.
- Cast [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) when you can sacrifice it or profit from its death.
- Use cheap draw to find one card from each engine role.

### Midgame

- Assemble [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api), a sacrifice outlet, and a cost reducer.
- Use recursive creatures to feed draw and removal without exhausting your hand.
- Transform Sephiroth when convenient.
- Hold removal for graveyard hate or a permanent that prevents sacrifice.

### Endgame

- Find the missing mana engine or payoff.
- Demonstrate one complete [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api)/Jumblebones cycle before repeating it.
- Watch mandatory draw and life-loss triggers while resolving a loop.
- If the combo is interrupted, recur the hardest-to-replace creature and rebuild.

## Mulligan guide

A good opening hand normally has:

- two or three lands;
- a mana rock, reducer, or mana-producing sacrifice outlet;
- a sacrifice outlet;
- draw or a reusable creature.

Do not keep a hand containing only payoffs and expensive recursion. [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) supplies the fodder, but the hand still needs a way to sacrifice him and reuse the resulting rotation.

## Important sequencing

- Sacrifice [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) before Jumblebones; Jumblebones can only return an [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) already in the graveyard.
- If [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) would be exiled, sacrifice it first so the rotation remains available.
- Jumblebones must leave the battlefield, not necessarily die. Sacrifice is simply the deck's most useful method.
- Commander tax applies only when casting [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) from the command zone. After Jumblebones returns it from the graveyard to your hand, normal cost reducers apply without additional tax.
- With [Heartless Summoning](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/117/heartless-summoning?utm_source=api), Jumblebones dies as a state-based action before anyone receives priority.
- Use optional draw outlets carefully once a loop is assembled; mandatory draw engines can become a liability.
- Graveyard exile can break the rotation while [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api)'s death trigger or Jumblebones's return trigger is pending.

## What to protect

Priority usually is:

1. [Phyrexian Altar](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/311/phyrexian-altar?utm_source=api) or the mana engine that sustains the loop.
2. The payoff that makes the loop lethal.
3. A cost reducer when the mana math depends on it.
4. [Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) only when exile or commander tax would prevent the next rotation.

[Ozox](https://scryfall.com/card/j25/44/ozox-the-clattering-king?utm_source=api) is designed to die. Protect the machinery around him more aggressively than the commander himself.
