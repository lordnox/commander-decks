Azorius portal/gate blink-control built from literal doors and creatures depicted crossing or guarding them. The commander is the door: `{1}{W}{U}` sends any other creature or artifact you control through and returns it at the next end step, which retriggers enters-the-battlefield abilities, dodges targeted removal at instant speed, and untaps every `{T}` portal artifact for a second activation.

**Commander:** [Abuelo, Ancestral Echo](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/219/abuelo-ancestral-echo?utm_source=api)  
**Deck size:** 100 cards  
**Primary plan:** blink portal-art ETB creatures and tap-ability portal artifacts every turn until selection, removal, and recursion become a combat-winning board  
**Backup plan:** land a giant portal such as [Portal to Phyrexia](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/240/portal-to-phyrexia?utm_source=api) or [Planar Bridge](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/313/planar-bridge?utm_source=api) and grind behind it

## How the deck works

The early turns establish a gate guard or a card engine and get to three mana. [Wall of Tears](https://scryfall.com/card/sth/50/wall-of-tears?utm_source=api), [Resolute Watchdog](https://scryfall.com/card/rna/19/resolute-watchdog?utm_source=api), and a face-down [Unyielding Gatekeeper](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/35/unyielding-gatekeeper?utm_source=api) deter attacks, while [Jacob Hauken, Inspector](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/65/jacob-hauken-inspector-haukens-insight?utm_source=api), [Aether Spellbomb](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/456/aether-spellbomb?utm_source=api), and [Archaeomancer's Map](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/136/archaeomancers-map?utm_source=api) keep cards and lands moving.

[Abuelo, Ancestral Echo](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/219/abuelo-ancestral-echo?utm_source=api) is the whole midgame. Every turn he can send one other creature or artifact through the door for `{1}{W}{U}`, and the deck is built so that every legal target does something on the way back. Because the ability is an instant-speed activation, it doubles as protection: point it at whatever an opponent just targeted and the removal spell fizzles.

The two things people forget are that [Abuelo](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/219/abuelo-ancestral-echo?utm_source=api) hits **artifacts**, and that permanents come back **untapped**. Blinking a used [Phyrexian Portal](https://scryfall.com/card/all/126/phyrexian-portal?utm_source=api), [Planar Portal](https://scryfall.com/card/8ed/311/planar-portal?utm_source=api), or [Prototype Portal](https://scryfall.com/card/c18/216/prototype-portal?utm_source=api) buys a second activation in the same turn cycle, and blinking during an opponent's turn returns the permanent on *their* end step, ready for yours.

[Heliod, the Radiant Dawn](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/17/heliod-the-radiant-dawn-heliod-the-warped-eclipse?utm_source=api) now lives in the 99, which is where he is actually good. Send him through the door to buy back [Journey to Nowhere](https://scryfall.com/card/cmd/17/journey-to-nowhere?utm_source=api), [Planar Disruption](https://scryfall.com/card/one/28/planar-disruption?utm_source=api), [Teleportation Circle](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/39/teleportation-circle?utm_source=api), or [Extraordinary Journey](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/48/extraordinary-journey?utm_source=api) as often as you can pay. Only transform him if you intend to keep him flipped, because a blink returns him front face up.

## Main patterns

- **[Abuelo](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/219/abuelo-ancestral-echo?utm_source=api) plus any ETB creature** is the baseline engine: `{1}{W}{U}` a turn for a scry 3, a card, a bounce, or an exile. With [Panharmonicon](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/310/panharmonicon?utm_source=api) out, each of those happens twice.
- **[Abuelo](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/219/abuelo-ancestral-echo?utm_source=api) plus a tapped portal artifact** gets a second activation out of [Phyrexian Portal](https://scryfall.com/card/all/126/phyrexian-portal?utm_source=api), [Planar Portal](https://scryfall.com/card/8ed/311/planar-portal?utm_source=api), or [Prototype Portal](https://scryfall.com/card/c18/216/prototype-portal?utm_source=api) in one turn cycle.
- **[Abuelo](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/219/abuelo-ancestral-echo?utm_source=api) as a counterspell.** Hold `{1}{W}{U}`. When a removal spell or edict targets your engine piece, blink it and the spell has no legal target.
- **Blink Heliod for the enchantment doors.** Each crossing returns a spent [Journey to Nowhere](https://scryfall.com/card/cmd/17/journey-to-nowhere?utm_source=api), [Planar Disruption](https://scryfall.com/card/one/28/planar-disruption?utm_source=api), or [Teleportation Circle](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/39/teleportation-circle?utm_source=api) to hand.
- **[Planar Guide](https://scryfall.com/card/lgn/18/planar-guide?utm_source=api) as a fog that also pays you.** Exile every creature in response to a lethal attack: opposing Auras, counters, and equipment fall off, and your whole ETB board retriggers at the end step.
- **[Floodgate](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/49/floodgate?utm_source=api) as a repeatable sweeper.** Its damage triggers whenever it leaves, so blink it before or after combat to clear nonblue ground creatures.
- **[Mirror of Life Trapping](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/326/mirror-of-life-trapping?utm_source=api) as one-sided stax.** It only exiles creatures that were *cast*, so opponents' creatures cycle through it while every creature this deck blinks back is invisible to it.
- **[Chrome Host Seedshark](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/68/chrome-host-seedshark?utm_source=api)** turns every bounce spell and portal artifact into incubator material while you set up.
- **[Matzalantli, the Great Door](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/256/matzalantli-the-great-door-the-core?utm_source=api) and [Azor's Gateway](https://scryfall.com/card/rix/176/azors-gateway-sanctum-of-the-sun?utm_source=api)** turn spare cards into selection first and oversized portal mana later.

## Win conditions

Most games end by board presence, not combo. Reused ETB creatures, copied bodies, and portal guards eventually become enough pressure that [Runed Arch](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/334/runed-arch?utm_source=api), [Another Round](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/1/another-round?utm_source=api), or [Venser, the Sojourner](https://scryfall.com/card/ddi/1/venser-the-sojourner?utm_source=api) opens a lethal attack.

The other finishing line is landing a portal the table cannot claw back from. [Portal to Phyrexia](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/240/portal-to-phyrexia?utm_source=api) empties boards, [Planar Bridge](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/313/planar-bridge?utm_source=api) turns mana into the best permanent every turn, and [Prototype Portal](https://scryfall.com/card/c18/216/prototype-portal?utm_source=api) can bury slower tables under repeated artifact copies.

## Early, mid, and late game

Early game means lands, a guard, and getting to three mana for the commander. [Wall of Tears](https://scryfall.com/card/sth/50/wall-of-tears?utm_source=api), [Resolute Watchdog](https://scryfall.com/card/rna/19/resolute-watchdog?utm_source=api), [Jacob Hauken, Inspector](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/65/jacob-hauken-inspector-haukens-insight?utm_source=api), and [Aether Channeler](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/42/aether-channeler?utm_source=api) are the preferred starts.

Midgame is where the deck wants to live. Land [Abuelo](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/219/abuelo-ancestral-echo?utm_source=api), hold up his activation, and only spend it when it either answers a removal spell or converts into a real card. A second door — [Teleportation Circle](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/39/teleportation-circle?utm_source=api) or [Conjurer's Closet](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/259/conjurers-closet?utm_source=api) — makes the crossings free and lets [Abuelo](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/219/abuelo-ancestral-echo?utm_source=api)'s mana go to spells instead.

Late game belongs to the giant artifacts. With a door online you can pass with interaction available every turn and still commit a meaningful board at the last possible moment.

## Mulligan guide

Keep hands with three lands or two lands plus a cheap mana piece, especially if one spell either buys time or reaches the commander on curve. One portal card plus one defensive spell is better than a hand full of sixes. Hands that only contain giant artifacts and no early interaction should go back.

The best keeps usually contain one of [Wall of Tears](https://scryfall.com/card/sth/50/wall-of-tears?utm_source=api), [Jacob Hauken, Inspector](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/65/jacob-hauken-inspector-haukens-insight?utm_source=api), [Aether Channeler](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/42/aether-channeler?utm_source=api), [Archaeomancer's Map](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/136/archaeomancers-map?utm_source=api), or a door paired with an ETB creature.

## Important sequencing and rules notes

- **[Abuelo](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/219/abuelo-ancestral-echo?utm_source=api) cannot blink himself.** The ability reads "another target creature or artifact you control." Ward 2 is his only self-protection, so a second door is worth holding for him.
- **[Abuelo](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/219/abuelo-ancestral-echo?utm_source=api)'s return is a delayed trigger at the next end step.** Activating on an opponent's turn returns the permanent that same turn, so it is untapped and usable on yours.
- **[Panharmonicon](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/310/panharmonicon?utm_source=api) doubles only "enters" triggers.** [Floodgate](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/49/floodgate?utm_source=api), [Wall of Tears](https://scryfall.com/card/sth/50/wall-of-tears?utm_source=api), and Inga's death trigger stay single.
- **[Planar Guide](https://scryfall.com/card/lgn/18/planar-guide?utm_source=api) exiles itself as a cost** and hits every creature on the table including yours. It is a reset, not a value blink.
- **[Unyielding Gatekeeper](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/35/unyielding-gatekeeper?utm_source=api) triggers on being turned face up, not on entering.** Cast it face down for `{3}` and save the `{1}{W}` flip; blinking it wastes the trigger.
- **Do not blink [Jacob Hauken, Inspector](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/65/jacob-hauken-inspector-haukens-insight?utm_source=api).** A returned Jacob is a new object and loses access to the cards exiled with the old one. The same applies to a transformed [Heliod](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/17/heliod-the-radiant-dawn-heliod-the-warped-eclipse?utm_source=api).
- **[Portal Manipulator](https://scryfall.com/card/clu/40/portal-manipulator?utm_source=api) only matters in the declare attackers step.** Leave mana up if you want that line.
- **[Aether Gale](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/186/aether-gale?utm_source=api) needs six targets.** Use your own ETB creatures or portal permanents when opponents do not supply all six.
- **[Another Round](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/1/another-round?utm_source=api) is a giant value spell, not a deterministic combo.**

## Weaknesses and what to protect

The list is slow and intentionally clunky. The commander carries a lot of the deck, so a pod that answers a three-mana 2/2 through ward 2 will slow you down badly — that is what [Justiciar's Portal](https://scryfall.com/card/rna/13/justiciars-portal?utm_source=api), [Resolute Watchdog](https://scryfall.com/card/rna/19/resolute-watchdog?utm_source=api), and the redundant doors are for. Graveyard exile weakens [Halo-Charged Skaab](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/60/halo-charged-skaab?utm_source=api) and [Salvager of Secrets](https://scryfall.com/card/gn2/25/salvager-of-secrets?utm_source=api), artifact hate removes the repeatable doors, and opposing [Containment Priest](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/13/containment-priest?utm_source=api)-style effects shut the engine off outright. Protect the first door paired with a real ETB target.
