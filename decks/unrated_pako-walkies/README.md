## How the deck works

Cast cheap ramp, then [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api). Haste means the first fetch trigger can happen the turn he enters. Keep a protection spell or equipment for that attack: [Gaea's Gift](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/182/gaeas-gift?utm_source=api), [Legolas's Quick Reflexes](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/493/legolass-quick-reflexes?utm_source=api), [Swiftfoot Boots](https://scryfall.com/card/mbc/77/swiftfoot-boots?utm_source=api), [Winged Boots](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/124/winged-boots?utm_source=api), [Canopy Cover](https://scryfall.com/card/wwk/98/canopy-cover?utm_source=api), [Deflecting Swat](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/214/deflecting-swat?utm_source=api), or [Fierce Guardianship](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/94/fierce-guardianship?utm_source=api).

[Haldan](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/2/haldan-avid-arcanist?utm_source=api) can wait a turn. Partner lets you cast either first; [Haldan](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/2/haldan-avid-arcanist?utm_source=api)'s enters trigger can put [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api) into a hand if he is still in the library, which he will not be if he started in the command zone. Play [Haldan](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/2/haldan-avid-arcanist?utm_source=api) when the exile pile already has lands and noncreature spells you want to cast as any color.

Combat mana pays for that dump without skipping the extra combat: [Bear Umbra](https://scryfall.com/card/nec/113/bear-umbra?utm_source=api) and [Sword of Feast and Famine](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/296/sword-of-feast-and-famine?utm_source=api) untap lands, [Mark of Sakiko](https://scryfall.com/card/bok/135/mark-of-sakiko?utm_source=api) and [The Reaver Cleaver](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/8/the-reaver-cleaver?utm_source=api) turn damage into mana or Treasures, [Guild Artisan](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/179/guild-artisan?utm_source=api) makes Treasures if you attack the player with the most (tied) life, and [Leyline Immersion](https://scryfall.com/card/mat/21/leyline-immersion?utm_source=api) taps [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api) for five spell-only mana after the attack if you still have him.

Extra combats multiply fetch counters and +1/+1 counters. [Relentless Assault](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/416/relentless-assault?utm_source=api), [Seize the Day](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/166/seize-the-day?utm_source=api), [Savage Beating](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/252/savage-beating?utm_source=api), [Overpowering Attack](https://scryfall.com/card/acr/37/overpowering-attack?utm_source=api), and [Moraug, Fury of Akoum](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/89/moraug-fury-of-akoum?utm_source=api) are the walks. [Irenicus's Vile Duplication](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/78/irenicuss-vile-duplication?utm_source=api) and [Quantum Misalignment](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/152/quantum-misalignment?utm_source=api) make a nonlegendary [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api) so two attack triggers fire.

## Main patterns

**Walk, then dump.** Attack, put fetch counters on four cards, then use combat mana in the following main (or the extra combat's main) to play [Haldan](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/2/haldan-avid-arcanist?utm_source=api)'s pile. Lands from exile also trigger [The Lost and the Damned](https://scryfall.com/card/40k/129/the-lost-and-the-damned?utm_source=api) and [Moraug](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/89/moraug-fury-of-akoum?utm_source=api).

**Archaeologist pings.** [Passionate Archaeologist](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/656/passionate-archaeologist?utm_source=api) gives each of your commanders "whenever you cast a spell from exile, deal that mana value to an opponent." [Haldan](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/2/haldan-avid-arcanist?utm_source=api) casts, [Quantum Misalignment](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/152/quantum-misalignment?utm_source=api) rebound, and [Delayed Blast Fireball](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/676/delayed-blast-fireball?utm_source=api) foretell all count. [Underworld Breach](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/161/underworld-breach?utm_source=api) escape does not: those spells come from the graveyard.

**Two dogs.** A nonlegendary copy of [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api) attacks alongside the real one. Each trigger exiles a card from each player. [Haldan](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/2/haldan-avid-arcanist?utm_source=api) can play every fetch-countered card you own in exile, including those the copy marked.

**Song turns.** [Song of Creation](https://scryfall.com/card/iko/210/song-of-creation?utm_source=api) draws two per spell and discards the hand at end of turn. Empty it with extra combats, [Haldan](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/2/haldan-avid-arcanist?utm_source=api) casts, and [Exploration](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/159/exploration?utm_source=api) / [Summer Bloom](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/160/summer-bloom?utm_source=api) land drops before the end step.

## Win conditions

- Commander damage from a double-striking or extra-combat [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api) with trample or flying.
- [Chandra's Ignition](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/220/chandras-ignition?utm_source=api) using [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api)'s power.
- [Soul Immolation](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/156/soul-immolation?utm_source=api) blighting [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api)'s toughness into table-wide damage.
- [Passionate Archaeologist](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/656/passionate-archaeologist?utm_source=api) plus a large [Haldan](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/2/haldan-avid-arcanist?utm_source=api) dump.

## Early, mid, and late game

**Early.** Dorks, auras, Talismans, [Sol Ring](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/211/sol-ring?utm_source=api), [Tinder Wall](https://scryfall.com/card/mb2/215/tinder-wall?utm_source=api). Keep interaction for the first [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api).

**Mid.** [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api) connects once or twice. Equip or aura up, land [Haldan](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/2/haldan-avid-arcanist?utm_source=api), start spending exile. Hold extra combats until a protection piece is up.

**Late.** Copy [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api), chain extra combats, Ignition or Immolation. [Wilderness Reclamation](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/205/wilderness-reclamation?utm_source=api) leaves lands up after a dump.

## Mulligan

Keep three lands (or two plus a one-mana dork) and a path to [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api) by turn four. A protection spell or [Bear Umbra](https://scryfall.com/card/nec/113/bear-umbra?utm_source=api) is worth a keep. Extra combats without [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api) or ramp are a mulligan. [Haldan](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/2/haldan-avid-arcanist?utm_source=api) without [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api) is a slow keep only if the rest of the hand ramps.

## Sequencing and rules

- [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api)'s trigger goes on the stack when he attacks, before blockers. Grow happens as the trigger resolves, so he is bigger for the rest of combat.
- Fetch counters sit on the exiled cards. Removing [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api) does not remove those counters; you still need [Haldan](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/2/haldan-avid-arcanist?utm_source=api) to cast them.
- [Overpowering Attack](https://scryfall.com/card/acr/37/overpowering-attack?utm_source=api) freeruns after commander combat damage this turn. Cast it in the first main after [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api) has already hit, or after the extra combat you already took.
- [The Last Agni Kai](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/144/the-last-agni-kai?utm_source=api) is a fight that can add floating red from excess damage. It is not an extra combat.
- [Leyline Immersion](https://scryfall.com/card/mat/21/leyline-immersion?utm_source=api) is tap-for-five with ward, spell-mana only.

## Weaknesses

The deck does little if [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api) never connects. Fog, blockers with deathtouch, and repeated commander tax hurt more than a missing [Haldan](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/2/haldan-avid-arcanist?utm_source=api). Creature-heavy opposing decks feed [Pako](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/13/pako-arcane-retriever?utm_source=api) fewer +1/+1 counters. Graveyard exile from the table blanks [Underworld Breach](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/161/underworld-breach?utm_source=api) and [Noxious Revival](https://scryfall.com/card/nph/118/noxious-revival?utm_source=api).
