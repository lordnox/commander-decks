## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, the primer, and the assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a cut back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated session notes so an argument does not live only in chat.

Every unique card needs a Cards in line. The other headings appear when there is something to record.

## Primer

- **Walkies** is a brew title for extra combats as extra Pako attacks, not the Moxfield name `pako bracket 3`.
- Primer is unrated: the source list is imported as-is, including Sol Ring, free commander counters, and Underworld Breach.

## Rules

- Haldan only plays cards **you** exiled that have fetch counters. Pako's attack trigger is the usual source; a nonlegendary Pako copy's trigger also marks those exiles.
- Passionate Archaeologist triggers on commander creatures you own when **you** cast a spell from exile, including Haldan casts, foretell, rebound, and escape does **not** count (graveyard, not exile).
- The Last Agni Kai is a fight plus floating red from excess damage, not an extra combat.
- Leyline Immersion taps for five mana that can only be spent on spells; it does not grant extra combats.
