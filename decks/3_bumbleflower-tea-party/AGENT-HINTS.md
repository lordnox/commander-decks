# Tea Party — agent hints

Primer owns the plan. This file is the seat checklist.

## Always ask

1. Which cheap spell is being saved for after Ms. Bumbleflower resolves, and
   can this turn cast two spells?
2. Did every Tea Party draw grow Chasm Skulker, including the normal draw
   step? Did an opponent's second draw make Gleaming Splendor trigger?
3. Is there a land drop left? A Temple in hand still enters, scries, and keeps
   future turns on curve.
4. Which untapped creatures have a free attack? Ms. Bumbleflower has
   vigilance and stays available after attacking.

## Sequencing

- Ms. Bumbleflower does not trigger for her own cast. She must already be on
  the battlefield when a later spell is cast.
- Hold Wizard Class or another cheap spell for the post-Bumbleflower turn
  when the opening hand already has the land and creature sequence. Do not
  spend the one-mana spell early merely for level 1.
- Cast Freestrider Lookout before the cheap follow-up. The crime from
  Bumbleflower targeting an opponent triggers Lookout only if Lookout has
  already resolved; Lookout cannot see the cast that puts itself onto the
  battlefield.
- Resolve a second Bumbleflower trigger completely: opponent draw, creature
  counter and flying, then Tea Party draws two. Those draws each trigger
  Chasm Skulker and may cross a Gleaming Splendor second-draw threshold.
- A Bumbleflower trigger is one crime even though it targets both an opponent
  and a creature.

## Combat

- Apply all Chasm Skulker counters before combat. Send it and Freestrider
  Lookout when no profitable block or protection deadline competes.
- Attack with Ms. Bumbleflower whenever the 1 damage is free. Vigilance means
  combat does not spend her as a blocker.

## Closing

Gleaming Splendor Treasures are mana, not decoration. Count every opponent's
normal draw plus gifts and combat-damage draws, then spend the Treasures on
two-spell turns or the Triumph close.
