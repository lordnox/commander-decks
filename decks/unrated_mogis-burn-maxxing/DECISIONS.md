# Mogis — Burn MaXXing: Decision Log

## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, primer, and assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated design and assessment notes.

## Goals

- **Creatureless** — the 99 contains no creature cards; Mogis is the only creature card and usually remains an enchantment on the battlefield.
- **Board Control** — creatures entering, attacking, surviving, or reaching upkeep should carry a cost.
- **Big Mana Burn** — artifact mana and Doubling Cube turn a controlled game into one oversized X-spell.

## Rankings

Initial score: Jank 8, Fun 7, Mean 8, Creatureless 10, Board Control 9, Big Mana Burn 8. The Cube math and old enchantments are unusual and sequencing-heavy, but the visible creature denial is deliberately harsh.

## Cards in

### Commander and creature locks

- **Mogis, God of Slaughter** — Commander and indestructible upkeep clock; gives Rakdos identity while usually remaining a noncreature enchantment.
- **Aether Flash** — Namesake entrance punishment that kills small creatures and combines with Death Pits of Rath.
- **Caltrops** — Colorless attack tax that turns every attacker into a damaged creature for Death Pits.
- **Death Match** — Every creature entering points a temporary -3/-3 at a creature, making development consume the table's board.
- **Death Pits of Rath** — Converts any damage from Flash, Caltrops, or a sweeper into destruction.
- **Lethal Vapors** — Destroys every entering creature unless an opponent spends a turn to remove it.
- **Marchesa's Decree** — Monarch supplies cards while attacking the crown costs life.
- **Mudslide** — Makes nonflying creatures pay during upkeep to untap, extending the upkeep-tax theme.
- **Night of Souls' Betrayal** — Shrinks every creature and lets Aether Flash reach three-toughness bodies.
- **No Mercy** — Makes combat damage to this deck cost the attacker its creature.
- **Smoke** — Limits each player to one creature untap while leaving this creatureless board unaffected.
- **Spreading Plague** — New creatures sweep older creatures sharing their color.
- **Tainted Aether** — Every creature entering costs its controller a creature or land.
- **The Tabernacle at Pendrell Vale** — The deck's one Game Changer; every creature requires an upkeep payment while the creatureless 99 breaks parity.

## Rules

- **Doubling Cube** — Scryfall's ruling explicitly identifies its activated ability as a mana ability, so Rings of Brighthearth cannot copy it. Each resolution doubles each type of unspent mana present at that resolution.
- **Mogis devotion** — Mogis contributes one black and one red devotion. If total devotion reaches seven, Mogis becomes a creature without entering, so Aether Flash does not trigger. Mogis remains indestructible.
- **Damage and life loss** — Furnace and Fiery Emancipation modify damage, not Torment of Hailfire's life loss. Wound Reflection and Warlock Class care about life lost, including damage.
- **Repercussion** — The creature is the damaged object, then Repercussion separately deals that much damage to its controller. Damage multipliers can modify both applicable events.
- **Unwinding Clock** — Untapping Cube on opposing turns does not preserve mana by itself; it chiefly refreshes rocks and ensures Cube is ready on the next turn.

## Talks

### 2026-09-08 — Autonomous build target

Target Bracket 3 (Upgraded): normal win around turns eight to ten, earliest credible turn six or seven, Mogis or a creature lock by turn four or five, no infinite combo, no permanent fast mana, and one thematic Game Changer. The experience is a visible creature-hostile board followed by one oversized burn spell, not a hidden combo.

Commander comparison: Mogis beat Sifa Grent (convention-only), Jeska plus an uncast Tevesh Szat (awkward identity and token text), and a never-cast Rakdos legend (no contribution). Blue was rejected because Cube, Keys, Rings, and red spell copying already support the mana plan; Power Artifact and Pendrell Mists were not worth weakening the identity.

Rules correction: an early discussion treated Doubling Cube as a non-mana ability. Its 2004-12-01 ruling says it is a mana ability. Rings therefore copies an untap ability rather than Cube itself.
