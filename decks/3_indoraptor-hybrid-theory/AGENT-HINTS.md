# Hybrid Theory — agent hints

Primer owns the plan. This file is the seat checklist.

## Always ask

1. Can Drover of the Mighty deal free damage before it is needed for mana and
   turn on a larger bloodthirst Indoraptor?
2. Is a Dinosaur under this seat's control? If so, Drover is 3/3, not 1/1.
3. Did a legendary creature deal combat damage to a player while Vraska
   Joins Up is present? That draw is mandatory.
4. Can Withering Wisps damage Ranging Raptors without killing Indoraptor or
   another needed creature?

## Sequencing

- Attack with Drover or another safe body before casting Indoraptor when that
  damage increases bloodthirst. It untaps next turn; saving it without a mana
  payment or defensive deadline misses damage.
- Vraska Joins Up draws a card whenever Indoraptor or another legendary
  creature deals combat damage to a player. Resolve the draw after combat
  damage and announce any opponent second-draw trigger it causes.
- Play Snow-Covered Swamp before Withering Wisps when it increases both black
  mana and the activation cap.
- With Withering Wisps and Ranging Raptors, activate once per snow Swamp only
  while the damage leaves the creatures needed for the plan alive. Each
  activation that damages Raptors gets one tapped basic. Recalculate after
  Eternal Thirst counters or deaths change toughness.
- Count Drover as a mana source only when it stays untapped. If combat spends
  it, the postcombat worksheet loses that mana.

## Politics

- Boggart Trawler can exile Sin-fall's whole public graveyard. Before casting
  it, name the copy or recursion line it stops and ask for an observable
  concession or short nonaggression period.
- If the deal is refused or Sin can fire before the next priority window,
  cast the Trawler targeting Sin-fall. Use Boggart Bog only when the land is
  worth more than the graveyard answer.

## Closing

Commander damage is the default clock, but do not tunnel on it while a
castable Wisps/Raptors engine adds lands or Trawler removes the table's other
winning engine. Take the free attack, then spend the postcombat mana.
