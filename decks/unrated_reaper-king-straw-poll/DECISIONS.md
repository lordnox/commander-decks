# Decisions

## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, the primer, and the assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a cut back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated session notes so an argument does not live only in chat.

Every unique card needs a Cards in line. The other headings appear when there is something to record.

## Goals

- **Scarecrow Tribal** — the creature type is the engine, not decoration. A card earns its slot by being a Scarecrow, making Scarecrows, or making other creatures count as Scarecrows.
- **Permanent Destruction** — the clock is a Vindicate stapled to a body. Every scarecrow that enters should be answering something on the table.

## Rules

- **Reaper King's cost is the mana base's job.** `{2/W}{2/U}{2/B}{2/R}{2/G}` lets each symbol be paid with the colour or with two generic. Five colours available means five mana; no colours means ten. Every rainbow land and every filter Scarecrow is therefore cost reduction, not just fixing. His mana value stays 10 regardless, which is what One with the Machine cares about.
- **Cauldron of Souls is not a persist loop.** It grants persist *until end of turn*. A creature that dies and returns is a new object and no longer has the granted ability, so it cannot persist again from the same tap. Tatterkite returns without a -1/-1 counter because it cannot have counters put on it, but that only makes it eligible for a *future* tap, not an infinite loop. Wingrattle Scarecrow's persist is printed and static, so it survives the return, but it comes back carrying a -1/-1 counter and will not persist again until Chainbreaker or Grim Poppet removes it.
- **Pili-Pala plus Grand Architect is infinite.** Grand Architect's recolour costs `{U}`, not a tap. Make Pili-Pala blue, tap it as an untapped blue creature for `{C}{C}`, then pay `{2}` and `{Q}` to untap Pili-Pala and add one mana of any colour. Architect's restricted mana legally pays for Pili-Pala's ability because Pili-Pala is an artifact. Each lap nets one unrestricted coloured mana and restores the starting state.
- **Grindstone taps.** Painter's Servant makes every card in a library share a colour, so one activation mills that player out entirely, but the `{T}` in the cost caps it at one opponent per turn without an untapper. Infinite mana does not change that. Milling also does not kill until the player would draw.
- **Conspiracy replaces types; Xenograft and Arcane Adaptation add them.** Conspiracy naming Scarecrow strips Shapeshifter from the changelings and the chosen type from Bloodline Pretender's trigger. Prefer Maskwood Nexus or Xenograft when a changeling's other types matter.
- **Panharmonicon and Elesh Norn stack additively.** Each says the ability triggers "an additional time", so both out means three destroy triggers per Scarecrow entering, not four.
- **Reaper King only counts Scarecrows you control.** Genesis Chamber gives every player a Myr, but opposing Myr do not trigger him, and Tribe Changers only affect creatures you control.

## Talks

### 2026-09-02 — Picking the commander and the shell

`t:scarecrow is:commander` returns exactly four cards: Reaper King, Rendmaw Creaking Nest, The Reaper King No More, and The Swarmweaver. The Swarmweaver was rejected immediately — its text is about Insects and Spiders, so the Scarecrow type is decoration and it gives the deck nothing in the command zone. Rendmaw is a politics and goad card, which is a real deck but not a Scarecrow deck; its trigger cares about card types, not the tribe. The Reaper, King No More is a genuinely good Jund -1/-1 theft commander and it stays in the 99 as a second legendary body, but its once-per-turn steal caps how explosive it can be.

Reaper King was locked because he is the only one of the four whose text makes the tribe itself the engine, and because the hybrid cost creates a real deckbuilding constraint: the greedy rainbow mana base is not a tax, it is what turns a ten-mana commander into a five-mana one.

The shell then wrote itself in three layers. Native Scarecrows from Shadowmoor and Eventide are the cheap bodies. Changelings are Scarecrows for free with no support. Tribe Changers (Maskwood Nexus, Xenograft, Arcane Adaptation, Conspiracy) fold every token maker in the deck into the same trigger.

Deliberately kept out of the 99: the rest of the Shadowmoor colour-check cycle (Watchwing, Thornwatch, Blazethorn, Rattleblaze) and the vanilla Scarecrows (Wicker Witch, Field Creeper, Jousting Dummy). They are on-theme but they are worse Vindicate carriers than a one-mana Heap Doll or Jawbone Skulkin, and the deck already runs enough three-and-four-mana do-nothing bodies. Wickerwing Effigy was wanted for its cast-from-top text but it is an Arena-only printing and not Commander legal; Realmwalker naming Scarecrow does the same job legally.

Combos were kept to lines that contain a Scarecrow. Pili-Pala with Grand Architect is infinite mana into Mirror Entity. Painter's Servant with Grindstone decks a player per turn. No Ashnod's Altar plus Nim Deathmantle package was added: it wins without touching the tribe, which is exactly the kind of generic best-tool inclusion the house rules push back on.
