Every straw man on the table gets a vote, and every vote destroys a permanent. This is five-colour Scarecrow kindred built around one line of text: *whenever another Scarecrow you control enters, destroy target permanent*. Twenty-five cards in the 99 are printed Scarecrows, ten more are changelings that already count, and four type hacks fold every token the deck makes into the same trigger.

## How the deck works

The commander's cost is the first thing to understand. [**Reaper King**](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/260/reaper-king?utm_source=api) is `{2/W}{2/U}{2/B}{2/R}{2/G}`, and each hybrid symbol can be paid with its colour *or* with two generic. With all five colours available he costs five mana. With none he costs ten. That is why the mana base is a greedy pile of rainbow lands and why seven of the Scarecrows are colour filters: [**Scarecrow Guide**](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/250/scarecrow-guide?utm_source=api) and [**Shire Scarecrow**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/249/shire-scarecrow?utm_source=api) do not ramp, they turn one generic mana into one coloured pip, and each pip they cover saves a full mana off the King.

Once he lands, the deck stops being a pile of bad 2/2s and becomes a machine gun. A one-mana [**Heap Doll**](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/228/heap-doll?utm_source=api) is a one-mana Vindicate. [**Prototype Portal**](https://scryfall.com/card/c18/216/prototype-portal?utm_source=api) imprinted on [**Jawbone Skulkin**](https://scryfall.com/card/eve/170/jawbone-skulkin?utm_source=api) destroys a permanent every turn for `{1}`. [**Mirrorworks**](https://scryfall.com/card/c18/211/mirrorworks?utm_source=api) lets every artifact you cast pay `{2}` for a token copy, which is a second body and a second trigger.

The deck is deliberately built so the trigger has three independent sources of bodies, and you rarely need all three:

| Layer | Cards | What it gives you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Printed Scarecrows | 25, from [**Heap Doll**](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/228/heap-doll?utm_source=api) at one mana to [**Grim Poppet**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/142/grim-poppet?utm_source=api) at seven | Triggers with zero setup |
| Changelings | [**Universal Automaton**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/235/universal-automaton?utm_source=api), [**Changeling Outcast**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/128/changeling-outcast?utm_source=api), [**Mirror Entity**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/83/mirror-entity?utm_source=api), [**Irregular Cohort**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/696/irregular-cohort?utm_source=api) and six more | Scarecrows in every colour, no support required |
| Type hacks | [**Maskwood Nexus**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/865/maskwood-nexus?utm_source=api), [**Xenograft**](https://scryfall.com/card/nph/51/xenograft?utm_source=api), [**Arcane Adaptation**](https://scryfall.com/card/xln/46/arcane-adaptation?utm_source=api), [**Conspiracy**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsb/39/conspiracy?utm_source=api) | Every token and every artifact creature becomes a trigger |

The third layer is what turns filler into an engine. [**Bitterblossom**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/69/bitterblossom?utm_source=api), [**Kher Keep**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/270/kher-keep?utm_source=api), and [**Genesis Chamber**](https://scryfall.com/card/bbd/235/genesis-chamber?utm_source=api) are unremarkable on their own; under [**Maskwood Nexus**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/865/maskwood-nexus?utm_source=api) they each destroy a permanent per turn cycle.

[**Panharmonicon**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/310/panharmonicon?utm_source=api) and [**Elesh Norn, Mother of Machines**](https://scryfall.com/card/one/10/elesh-norn-mother-of-machines?utm_source=api) each say the ability triggers "an additional time". Both out means three destroyed permanents per Scarecrow.

## Combos

[**Pili-Pala**](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/258/pili-pala?utm_source=api) plus [**Grand Architect**](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/53/grand-architect?utm_source=api) is the deck's only true infinite. Spend `{U}` on Architect's ability to make [Pili-Pala](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/258/pili-pala?utm_source=api) blue. Tap [Pili-Pala](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/258/pili-pala?utm_source=api) as an untapped blue creature for `{C}{C}`, then pay `{2}` and `{Q}` to untap [Pili-Pala](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/258/pili-pala?utm_source=api) and add one mana of any colour. Architect's mana is restricted to artifact spells and artifact abilities, and [Pili-Pala](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/258/pili-pala?utm_source=api) is an artifact, so it legally pays its own cost. Each lap nets one unrestricted coloured mana and returns to the starting state. [**Painter's Servant**](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/257/painters-servant?utm_source=api) naming blue, or [**Scuttlemutt**](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/484/scuttlemutt?utm_source=api)'s recolour both cover the `{U}` if blue is awkward.

[**Painter's Servant**](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/257/painters-servant?utm_source=api) plus [**Grindstone**](https://scryfall.com/card/otp/79/grindstone?utm_source=api) makes every card in a library share a colour, so one activation mills that opponent out completely. [Grindstone](https://scryfall.com/card/otp/79/grindstone?utm_source=api) taps, so this is one opponent per turn even with infinite mana, and a decked player does not lose until they would draw.

[**Cauldron of Souls**](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/180/cauldron-of-souls?utm_source=api) is the big value turn, and it is worth being precise about what it is not. It grants persist *until end of turn*. A creature that dies and comes back is a new object that no longer has the granted ability, so it cannot persist again off the same tap. What you get is one extra life for your whole board, which after a wrath or a sacrifice turn means every Scarecrow re-enters and every re-entry destroys a permanent. [**Tatterkite**](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/264/tatterkite?utm_source=api) comes back with no counter because it cannot have counters put on it, which makes it eligible for a future tap rather than a loop.

## Win conditions

1. [**Mirror Entity**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/83/mirror-entity?utm_source=api) with infinite mana. Set the team to an arbitrary size and swing. This is the clean one-turn kill.
2. [**Reckless Fireweaver**](https://scryfall.com/card/kld/126/reckless-fireweaver?utm_source=api) as a clock. Every artifact that enters pings each opponent, and this deck enters a lot of artifacts.
3. Combat under the anthem. [Reaper King](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/260/reaper-king?utm_source=api) is a 6/6 who makes every other Scarecrow bigger, and by the mid game the board is wide.
4. [**Grindstone**](https://scryfall.com/card/otp/79/grindstone?utm_source=api) with [**Painter's Servant**](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/257/painters-servant?utm_source=api), one opponent per turn, as the slow inevitability when the board has stalled.

## Playing it

**Early.** Lead on cheap Scarecrows even though they do nothing yet — [**Universal Automaton**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/235/universal-automaton?utm_source=api), [**Heap Doll**](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/228/heap-doll?utm_source=api), [**Jawbone Skulkin**](https://scryfall.com/card/eve/170/jawbone-skulkin?utm_source=api), [**Wreckage Wickerfolk**](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/110/wreckage-wickerfolk?utm_source=api). They are the colour filters and the future triggers. Prioritise getting a fifth colour online over deploying a fourth two-drop, because the fifth colour is worth two mana on the commander.

**Mid.** Cast [Reaper King](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/260/reaper-king?utm_source=api) as early as turn four or five off the filters. From there every land drop and every cheap body answers something. Do not hold triggers for the perfect target; a Signet or a mana rock is a fine kill when the alternative is letting the trigger fizzle.

**Late.** Find a repeatable body source. [**Prototype Portal**](https://scryfall.com/card/c18/216/prototype-portal?utm_source=api), [**Mirrorworks**](https://scryfall.com/card/c18/211/mirrorworks?utm_source=api), [**Bitterblossom**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/69/bitterblossom?utm_source=api) under a type hack, or [**Scarecrone**](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/482/scarecrone?utm_source=api) reanimating an artifact creature every turn. At that point the deck destroys a permanent per turn without spending cards.

**Mulligan.** Keep hands with two or more colours of production and at least one Scarecrow under three mana. A hand of expensive artifacts with two lands is a mulligan even if the artifacts are good, because nothing in the deck catches you up on mana except [**Solemn Simulacrum**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/215/solemn-simulacrum?utm_source=api) and [**Burnished Hart**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/250/burnished-hart?utm_source=api).

**Tutors.** [**Inventors' Fair**](https://scryfall.com/card/kld/247/inventors-fair?utm_source=api) is the only real one. Priorities in order: [**Maskwood Nexus**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/865/maskwood-nexus?utm_source=api) when you already have token makers down, [**Cauldron of Souls**](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/180/cauldron-of-souls?utm_source=api) when the board is wide and someone is holding a wrath, [**Grindstone**](https://scryfall.com/card/otp/79/grindstone?utm_source=api) when [**Painter's Servant**](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/257/painters-servant?utm_source=api) is already out, [**Prototype Portal**](https://scryfall.com/card/c18/216/prototype-portal?utm_source=api) otherwise.

## Sequencing and rules notes

- [**Conspiracy**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsb/39/conspiracy?utm_source=api) *replaces* creature types; [**Xenograft**](https://scryfall.com/card/nph/51/xenograft?utm_source=api) and [**Arcane Adaptation**](https://scryfall.com/card/xln/46/arcane-adaptation?utm_source=api) add them. Naming Scarecrow with [Conspiracy](https://scryfall.com/card/tsb/39/conspiracy?utm_source=api) strips Shapeshifter off the changelings and turns off [**Bloodline Pretender**](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/350/bloodline-pretender?utm_source=api)'s chosen-type trigger. Prefer [**Maskwood Nexus**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/865/maskwood-nexus?utm_source=api) when other types matter.
- [**Reaper King**](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/260/reaper-king?utm_source=api) only counts Scarecrows *you control*. [**Genesis Chamber**](https://scryfall.com/card/bbd/235/genesis-chamber?utm_source=api) hands every player a Myr; theirs do not trigger him, and the type hacks only apply to your creatures.
- [Reaper King](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/260/reaper-king?utm_source=api)'s mana value stays 10 no matter how you pay for him, which is what [**One with the Machine**](https://scryfall.com/card/m19/66/one-with-the-machine?utm_source=api) reads. With him on the battlefield it draws ten.
- [**Cavern of Souls**](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/269/cavern-of-souls?utm_source=api) naming Scarecrow makes the commander uncounterable. [**Unclaimed Territory**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/275/unclaimed-territory?utm_source=api) and [**Secluded Courtyard**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/265/secluded-courtyard?utm_source=api) also pay for him, because he is a creature spell.
- [**Sculpting Steel**](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/374/sculpting-steel?utm_source=api) copying [Reaper King](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/260/reaper-king?utm_source=api) triggers the original before the legend rule kills one of them, so it is a three-mana Vindicate in a pinch.
- [**Grim Poppet**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/142/grim-poppet?utm_source=api)'s counter removal is free and untapped, so it can machine-gun three small creatures at instant speed; those deaths also feed [**The Reaper, King No More**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/4/the-reaper-king-no-more?utm_source=api) and [**Dread Tiller**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/20/dread-tiller?utm_source=api).

## Weaknesses

The deck is a commander-dependent engine wearing a tribal costume. Without [Reaper King](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/260/reaper-king?utm_source=api) on the battlefield, a third of the 99 is vanilla artifact creatures with no text. Expect to recast him, and expect the recast tax to hurt more than usual because he is already the most expensive card in the deck.

The mana base pays real life. [**City of Brass**](https://scryfall.com/card/tmc/62/city-of-brass?utm_source=api), [**Mana Confluence**](https://scryfall.com/card/jou/163/mana-confluence?utm_source=api), and [**Grand Coliseum**](https://scryfall.com/card/tmc/68/grand-coliseum?utm_source=api) all hurt, and [**Bitterblossom**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/69/bitterblossom?utm_source=api) and [**Dismember**](https://scryfall.com/card/mm2/79/dismember?utm_source=api) add to it. Against an aggressive table that life adds up.

There is very little instant-speed interaction — [**Beast Within**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/169/beast-within?utm_source=api), [**Chaos Warp**](https://scryfall.com/card/mbc/72/chaos-warp?utm_source=api), [**Dismember**](https://scryfall.com/card/mm2/79/dismember?utm_source=api), and [**Nameless Inversion**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/113/nameless-inversion?utm_source=api). Everything else answers permanents on your own turn, at sorcery speed, one at a time. A combo deck that wins on turn six does not care how many Scarecrows are in play.

Graveyard recursion is thin, so a well-timed exile wrath is close to game over. [**Cauldron of Souls**](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/180/cauldron-of-souls?utm_source=api) is the main insurance and it wants to be untapped before the wrath resolves.
