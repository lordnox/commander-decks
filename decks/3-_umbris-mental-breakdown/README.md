# [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) — Mental Breakdown

> Bracket 3− Dimir exile-theft voltron-control deck with no Game Changers. Usually presents its first protected or evasive lethal attack around turn six to seven and closes around turn eight to ten; an untapped two-mana rock into [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) on turn four, then [Tasha's Hideous Laughter](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/78/tashas-hideous-laughter?utm_source=api) and [Shadow Rift](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/TMP-86/shadow-rift?utm_source=api), can produce an unlikely turn-five single-player lethal attack.

Exile-theft control replaces familiar resources with other players' cards. [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) turns draws into opposing-library choices, [Knowledge Pool](https://scryfall.com/card/mbs/111/knowledge-pool?utm_source=api) exchanges spells cast from hand, [Rod of Absorption](https://scryfall.com/card/afc/19/rod-of-absorption?utm_source=api) banks resolved instants and sorceries, and every opponent-owned card left in exile makes [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) larger. The deck steals only what it needs; unused cards remain a power bank for combat or direct damage.

## How the deck works

The first turns establish Dimir mana. Seven ramp or reduction pieces can be deployed for two mana: [Arcane Signet](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/192/arcane-signet?utm_source=api), [Everflowing Chalice](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/137/everflowing-chalice), [Fellwar Stone](https://scryfall.com/card/mbc/74/fellwar-stone?utm_source=api), [Jet Medallion](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/292/jet-medallion), [Mind Stone](https://scryfall.com/card/mbc/76/mind-stone?utm_source=api), [Sapphire Medallion](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/296/sapphire-medallion), and [Talisman of Dominance](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/219/talisman-of-dominance?utm_source=api). [Honored Heirloom](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/257/honored-heirloom?utm_source=api) and [Midnight Clock](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/100/midnight-clock) add utility at three. [Ancient Cellarspawn](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/16/ancient-cellarspawn) further reduces the creature package and turns mana saved into life loss. With no burst rock in the list, a turn-four [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) needs any two- or three-mana rock plus an untapped fourth land.

The middle game steals resources and stocks exile. [Thada Adel, Acquisitor](https://scryfall.com/card/wwk/40/thada-adel-acquisitor), [Thieving Skydiver](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/118/thieving-skydiver?utm_source=api), [Faceless Butcher](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/84/faceless-butcher), and [Coffin Queen](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/TPR-87/coffin-queen?utm_source=api) take permanents directly. [Intellect Devourer](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/132/intellect-devourer?utm_source=api) triggers [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) as a Horror while each opponent puts a hand card into a temporary theft bank. [Aboleth Spawn](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/662/aboleth-spawn) copies opposing creature-entry triggers, while [Gonti, Night Minister](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/87/gonti-night-minister) pays Treasures for stolen casts and lets any connecting creature exile a playable card. [Lobelia, Defender of Bag End](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/27/lobelia-defender-of-bag-end?utm_source=api) immediately banks one card from every opposing library and can sacrifice a spare rock or Treasure to cast one for free. [Dream-Thief's Bandana](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/38/dream-thiefs-bandana) does the same job on a single equipped attacker, including [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api). [Tasha's Hideous Laughter](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/78/tashas-hideous-laughter?utm_source=api), [Outrageous Robbery](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/97/outrageous-robbery), [Raven Guild Master](https://scryfall.com/card/scg/47/raven-guild-master?utm_source=api), and [Author of Shadows](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/35/author-of-shadows?utm_source=api) create large jumps.

Opposing graveyards are a second bank. [Maddening Cacophony](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/67/maddening-cacophony) deliberately stocks all three, then [Canoptek Scarab Swarm](https://scryfall.com/card/40k/150/canoptek-scarab-swarm?utm_source=api), [Nautiloid Ship](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/328/nautiloid-ship?utm_source=api), [Strategic Betrayal](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/94/strategic-betrayal?utm_source=api), [Espers to Magicite](https://scryfall.com/card/fic/43/espers-to-magicite?utm_source=api), or [Dauntless Scrapbot](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/237/dauntless-scrapbot?utm_source=api) converts a deposit into [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) power. The Scrapbot clears all three opposing graveyards and leaves a Lander for delayed ramp. [Coffin Queen](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/TPR-87/coffin-queen?utm_source=api) and [Uchuulon](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/673/uchuulon?utm_source=api) instead extract useful creatures before the rest are exiled.

[Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) changes every future draw into an opposing-library card. [Knowledge Pool](https://scryfall.com/card/mbs/111/knowledge-pool?utm_source=api) starts with three cards from every library, then exchanges spells cast from hand. [Rod of Absorption](https://scryfall.com/card/afc/19/rod-of-absorption?utm_source=api) lets those instants and sorceries resolve, then banks them in exile until this deck recasts the pile. None of the three is a hard lock: players continue playing Magic, but their usual hands, libraries, and graveyards stop behaving normally.

Three theft effects do not cast for free, move a permanent directly, or supply their own fixing: [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api), [Praetor's Grasp](https://scryfall.com/card/nph/71/praetors-grasp?utm_source=api), and [Thada Adel, Acquisitor](https://scryfall.com/card/wwk/40/thada-adel-acquisitor). Thada and [Praetor's Grasp](https://scryfall.com/card/nph/71/praetors-grasp?utm_source=api) choose their targets, leaving only [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) as a blind off-colour hit. [Gonti, Night Minister](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/87/gonti-night-minister) and [Predators' Hour](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/147/predators-hour) fix colour; [Bismuth Mindrender](https://scryfall.com/card/m3c/51/bismuth-mindrender) pays life instead of mana. [Exotic Orchard](https://scryfall.com/card/mbc/79/exotic-orchard?utm_source=api), [Mana Confluence](https://scryfall.com/card/jou/163/mana-confluence?utm_source=api), [City of Brass](https://scryfall.com/card/tmc/62/city-of-brass?utm_source=api), [Fellwar Stone](https://scryfall.com/card/mbc/74/fellwar-stone?utm_source=api), [Honored Heirloom](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/257/honored-heirloom?utm_source=api), and stolen lands cover the remaining exceptions.

[Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) turns all opponent-owned cards still in exile into a single oversized threat. Casting a stolen card spends one point from that bank, so take lands, removal, protection, and decisive threats while leaving marginal cards untouched.

## Key patterns

### [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api)

While it is out, this deck never draws its own cards again. [Moonlight Bargain](https://scryfall.com/card/c18/114/moonlight-bargain?utm_source=api) puts selected cards directly into hand without drawing, so it still works underneath the replacement effect; ordinary draw abilities, including [Silent Hallcreeper](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/72/silent-hallcreeper?utm_source=api), instead take cards from opposing libraries. Deploy [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) once the hand already holds evasion or protection rather than while relying on future draws for them.

Opponents choose which opponent supplies each [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) draw. A card owned by any opponent counts for [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) even when a different opponent exiled it. Cards taken from this deck do not count.

### [Knowledge Pool](https://scryfall.com/card/mbs/111/knowledge-pool?utm_source=api)

[Knowledge Pool](https://scryfall.com/card/mbs/111/knowledge-pool?utm_source=api) exiles three cards from each library as it enters, normally giving [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) nine power at a four-player table before anyone casts another spell. Its exchange trigger only sees spells cast from hand. Spells cast from [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) or another exile zone bypass the Pool. With [Gonti, Night Minister](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/87/gonti-night-minister) in play, casting a Pool card you do not own still makes a Treasure.

Do not cast an expendable spell into the Pool without checking what every player can take. The next player may select any other eligible card, not merely one owned by the player who triggered it.

### [Rod of Absorption](https://scryfall.com/card/afc/19/rod-of-absorption?utm_source=api)

[Rod of Absorption](https://scryfall.com/card/afc/19/rod-of-absorption?utm_source=api) does not counter the spell. Instants and sorceries still resolve, then move under the Rod instead of the graveyard. Opponent-owned cards there count for [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) until they are recast. This deck's own answers are also banked, so leave the Rod in play while interaction is still needed, then pay `{X}` and sacrifice it to recast any number of the piled cards with total mana value X or less for free. The Rod is already gone, so those recasts are not intercepted again. With [Gonti, Night Minister](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/87/gonti-night-minister) in play, recasting an opponent's card still makes a Treasure.

### Nonlegendary [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) copies

[Spark Double](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/62/spark-double?utm_source=api) and [Irenicus's Vile Duplication](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/78/irenicuss-vile-duplication?utm_source=api) create persistent nonlegendary copies. Each new copy triggers itself and every established [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api), and each uses the same opponent-owned exile count. Those copies deal ordinary damage, not commander damage.

[Endless Evil](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/665/endless-evil) creates a legendary 1/1-base copy instead. Both Umbrises trigger from the token's entry before the legend rule removes one, so an attached Aura adds two until-land exile triggers at each upkeep even though it does not leave a second attacker.

## Win conditions

1. **Commander damage:** make [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) lethal, then use [Cover of Darkness](https://scryfall.com/card/ons/133/cover-of-darkness), [Dauthi Embrace](https://scryfall.com/card/tmp/120/dauthi-embrace?utm_source=api), [Mindleecher](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/44/mindleecher?utm_source=api), [Shadow Rift](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/TMP-86/shadow-rift?utm_source=api), [Shizo, Death's Storehouse](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/233/shizo-deaths-storehouse?utm_source=api), or [Rogue's Passage](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/212/rogues-passage?utm_source=api) to connect. [Falthis, Shadowcat Familiar](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/126/falthis-shadowcat-familiar?utm_source=api) instead demands two blockers and trades with both through deathtouch. Keep track of commander damage separately; copied Umbrises do not contribute to it.
2. **Multiple Umbrises:** [Spark Double](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/62/spark-double?utm_source=api) and [Irenicus's Vile Duplication](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/78/irenicuss-vile-duplication?utm_source=api) create persistent full-sized attackers. [Endless Evil](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/665/endless-evil) instead compounds exile triggers every upkeep.
3. **Power shot:** [Essence Harvest](https://scryfall.com/card/avr/100/essence-harvest?utm_source=api) uses the greatest power directly without sacrificing the protected creature.
4. **Borrowed win:** [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api), [Thada Adel, Acquisitor](https://scryfall.com/card/wwk/40/thada-adel-acquisitor), [Coffin Queen](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/TPR-87/coffin-queen?utm_source=api), and the other theft engines can supply opposing finishers, but the deck never depends on a particular opposing list.

[Tasha's Hideous Laughter](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/78/tashas-hideous-laughter?utm_source=api) is setup, not a win by itself. It exiles until each opponent has exiled cards with total mana value twenty or greater; lands contribute zero, and the spell does not cause a player to lose merely because their library becomes small.

## Game plan

### Early game

Keep hands with three mana sources or two lands plus a ramp piece. Prioritize access to both core colours; flexible colour production matters only after finding a stolen spell that needs it. Deploy a small theft engine only when it can activate or attack safely.

### Mid game

Cast [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) when one exile deposit is ready or protection is available. A naked five-mana 1/1 advertises the plan without applying pressure. [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) is strongest after the hand contains usable mana and at least one way to protect or deliver [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api).

[Knowledge Pool](https://scryfall.com/card/mbs/111/knowledge-pool?utm_source=api) should enter when the table's hands are less useful than the cards likely to be exchanged. If [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) is already active, cards played from its exile avoid the Pool, giving this deck a predictable lane through the chaos.

### Late game

Count opponent-owned cards in every exile zone before committing. Lead with the large deposit, then create nonlegendary copies or grant evasion. Leave mana for [Vanishing](https://scryfall.com/card/vis/48/vanishing?utm_source=api), [Spellskite](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/873/spellskite?utm_source=api), or [Imp's Mischief](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/167/imps-mischief) whenever possible; a seven-power [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) also turns [Not of This World](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/807/not-of-this-world) into a free answer to targeted interaction. Spend stolen cards only when their effect is worth shrinking every [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api).

## Mulligan guide

Keep:

- three lands with both core colours;
- two lands plus any two-mana rock, especially one that supplies the missing core colour;
- early fixing plus [Maddening Cacophony](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/67/maddening-cacophony), [Thada Adel, Acquisitor](https://scryfall.com/card/wwk/40/thada-adel-acquisitor), or [Gonti, Night Minister](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/87/gonti-night-minister);
- a five-mana route and either [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) protection or a large exile deposit.

Mulligan:

- hands whose only acceleration costs three or more and miss a third land;
- off-colour fixing without reliable blue and black;
- hands crowded with the six- and seven-mana finishers;
- finishers without a way to stock opposing exile.

[Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) is not an automatic keep. Without mana development it gives opponents the first useful turn under its replacement effect while this deck remains unable to cast what it finds.

## Sequencing and rules

- Cast [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) after a large exile effect when possible; its power updates continuously and does not require it to witness the exile.
- A stolen spell stops contributing to [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) as soon as it moves from exile to the stack.
- Face-down opponent-owned cards count even when this deck cannot look at them.
- If [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) leaves, its cards remain exiled but nobody retains its permission to look at or play them.
- Ordinary clones retain legendary. [Silent Hallcreeper](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/72/silent-hallcreeper?utm_source=api) may copy another value creature, but copying [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) forces the legend rule.
- [Endless Evil](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/665/endless-evil) also creates a legendary [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api). Both copies trigger from the token entering before one is removed, and those triggers resolve normally.
- [Sapphire Medallion](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/296/sapphire-medallion) and [Jet Medallion](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/292/jet-medallion) reduce generic costs but do not produce mana. With [Ancient Cellarspawn](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/16/ancient-cellarspawn), every point not spent below a spell's mana value becomes one life lost by each opponent.
- [Maddening Cacophony](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/67/maddening-cacophony) does not grow [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) by itself. Follow it with graveyard exile, and resolve [Dauntless Scrapbot](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/237/dauntless-scrapbot?utm_source=api) only after extracting any opposing creature worth stealing.
- Mutate [Mindleecher](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/44/mindleecher?utm_source=api) under [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api). The pile remains the commander and keeps [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api)'s combat damage while gaining flying and the three-card exile-theft trigger.
- [Perilous Vault](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/968/perilous-vault?utm_source=api) exiles itself along with all nonland permanents. [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) will not survive, although the opponent-owned permanents remain banked for a later recast.
- [Vanishing](https://scryfall.com/card/vis/48/vanishing?utm_source=api) phases out itself with [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api). The Aura returns attached when [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) phases in.
- Name Nightmare with [Cover of Darkness](https://scryfall.com/card/ons/133/cover-of-darkness). [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) and copies of [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) retain that type, so the enchantment gives the commander and its nonlegendary copies fear.
- [Shadow Rift](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/TMP-86/shadow-rift?utm_source=api) grants shadow before drawing. Under [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api), that draw is replaced by exiling a card from an opponent's library.
- [Not of This World](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/807/not-of-this-world) is free only when the spell or ability it targets is itself targeting a creature you control with power seven or greater. It does not counter sweepers.
- [Imp's Mischief](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/167/imps-mischief) needs another legal target and does not answer untargeted interaction.
- [Dream-Thief's Bandana](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/38/dream-thiefs-bandana) exiles the looked-at card face down. It counts for [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) until it is played, and any mana type may be spent to cast it.
- [Faceless Butcher](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/84/faceless-butcher) keeps the exiled creature only while it remains on the battlefield. Removing the Butcher returns that creature to its owner.
- [Gonti, Night Minister](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/87/gonti-night-minister) makes a Treasure for whoever casts a spell they do not own. Its combat trigger rewards damage to *your* opponents, including when an opponent hits another opponent.
- [Bismuth Mindrender](https://scryfall.com/card/m3c/51/bismuth-mindrender) leaves exiled lands in the [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) bank. Paying life to cast the nonland spends that one card.
- [Coffin Queen](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/TPR-87/coffin-queen?utm_source=api) exiles its stolen creature when the Queen untaps. Extract the best creature before a whole-graveyard effect removes the remaining cards.
- [Thada Adel, Acquisitor](https://scryfall.com/card/wwk/40/thada-adel-acquisitor) permits the artifact to be played only during the turn it is found; artifacts with coloured costs still need appropriate mana.
- Each opponent chooses the card that [Intellect Devourer](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/132/intellect-devourer?utm_source=api) exiles. Play the useful cards before Devourer leaves; the rest return to their owners' hands.
- Use [Honored Heirloom](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/257/honored-heirloom?utm_source=api) as mana while developing, then turn spare mana into one opponent-owned graveyard card—and one [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) power—at a time.
- Each creature that connects during [Predators' Hour](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/147/predators-hour) exiles a separate card. The cards remain playable and fix their own casting costs.
- [Midnight Clock](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/100/midnight-clock) first shuffles the hand and graveyard into the library. With [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api) in play, its seven draws then become seven face-down cards from opposing libraries.
- [Rod of Absorption](https://scryfall.com/card/afc/19/rod-of-absorption?utm_source=api) also intercepts this deck's resolved instants and sorceries. Keep it on the board while answers are still needed, then dump the pile in one activation.
- [Abyssal Harvester](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/54/abyssal-harvester?utm_source=api) can only take a creature card put into a graveyard this turn, and each activation exiles the previous Nightmare token. Use it before [Canoptek Scarab Swarm](https://scryfall.com/card/40k/150/canoptek-scarab-swarm?utm_source=api) empties the yard it wants.
- The [Abyssal Harvester](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/54/abyssal-harvester?utm_source=api) token is a Nightmare, so it triggers every [Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) as it enters.
- Let [Abyssal Harvester](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/54/abyssal-harvester?utm_source=api), [Uchuulon](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/673/uchuulon?utm_source=api), or [Nautiloid Ship](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/328/nautiloid-ship?utm_source=api) consume the best creature before a whole-graveyard effect removes the remaining fuel.
- [Espers to Magicite](https://scryfall.com/card/fic/43/espers-to-magicite?utm_source=api) makes an artifact token that is not a creature. It keeps the copied card's other copiable abilities, but cannot attack, block, or use abilities that require a creature object.

## Weaknesses

[Umbris](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/38/umbris-fear-manifest?utm_source=api) is vulnerable before exile accumulates and becomes expensive after repeated removal. Enchantment removal restores normal draws by removing [Shared Fate](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/49/shared-fate?utm_source=api), while artifact removal can collapse [Knowledge Pool](https://scryfall.com/card/mbs/111/knowledge-pool?utm_source=api), [Rod of Absorption](https://scryfall.com/card/afc/19/rod-of-absorption?utm_source=api), or the slow theft engines. The deck also relies on opposing libraries for card quality, so disciplined colour fixing is essential.

Graveyard decks can benefit from cards discarded or milled by [Thief of Sanity](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/243/thief-of-sanity?utm_source=api) and [Maddening Cacophony](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/67/maddening-cacophony). Let [Coffin Queen](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/TPR-87/coffin-queen?utm_source=api), [Abyssal Harvester](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/54/abyssal-harvester?utm_source=api), or [Uchuulon](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/673/uchuulon?utm_source=api) take the best creature before [Canoptek Scarab Swarm](https://scryfall.com/card/40k/150/canoptek-scarab-swarm?utm_source=api) or another whole-yard effect removes the rest. Fast go-wide pressure can arrive before the theft engines matter; preserve [Curse of the Swine](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/192/curse-of-the-swine?utm_source=api) and [Perilous Vault](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/968/perilous-vault?utm_source=api) rather than spending them on isolated threats.
