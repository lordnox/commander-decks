Azorius portal/gate blink-control built from literal doors and creatures depicted crossing or guarding them. Portal artifacts open and close ETB creatures for cards, removal, recursion, and tempo; [Containment Priest](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/13/containment-priest?utm_source=api) temporarily turns those same passages against opponents.

**Commander:** [Heliod, the Radiant Dawn](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/17/heliod-the-radiant-dawn-heliod-the-warped-eclipse?utm_source=api)  
**Deck size:** 100 cards  
**Primary plan:** repeatedly blink portal-art ETB creatures until selection, removal, and recursion become a combat-winning board  
**Backup plan:** close creature entry with Mirror plus Priest, or land a giant portal such as [Portal to Phyrexia](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/240/portal-to-phyrexia?utm_source=api)

## How the deck works

The early turns establish a gate guard or a card engine. [Wall of Tears](https://scryfall.com/card/sth/50/wall-of-tears?utm_source=api) and [Resolute Watchdog](https://scryfall.com/card/rna/19/resolute-watchdog?utm_source=api) deter attacks, while [Jacob Hauken, Inspector](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/65/jacob-hauken-inspector-haukens-insight?utm_source=api), [Aether Spellbomb](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/456/aether-spellbomb?utm_source=api), and [Archaeomancer's Map](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/136/archaeomancers-map?utm_source=api) keep cards and lands moving.

The midgame starts once a portal engine sticks. [Teleportation Circle](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/39/teleportation-circle?utm_source=api), [Conjurer's Closet](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/259/conjurers-closet?utm_source=api), [Planar Incision](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/72/planar-incision?utm_source=api), and [Venser, the Sojourner](https://scryfall.com/card/ddi/1/venser-the-sojourner?utm_source=api) reuse [Aether Channeler](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/42/aether-channeler?utm_source=api), [Inga Rune-Eyes](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/100/inga-rune-eyes?utm_source=api), [Curator of Destinies](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/34/curator-of-destinies?utm_source=api), [Salvager of Secrets](https://scryfall.com/card/gn2/25/salvager-of-secrets?utm_source=api), and [Alabaster Host Intercessor](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/3/alabaster-host-intercessor?utm_source=api).

[Heliod](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/17/heliod-the-radiant-dawn-heliod-the-warped-eclipse?utm_source=api) is the hinge. Before transforming, blink him to recover [Journey to Nowhere](https://scryfall.com/card/cmd/17/journey-to-nowhere?utm_source=api), [Planar Disruption](https://scryfall.com/card/one/28/planar-disruption?utm_source=api), or [Extraordinary Journey](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/48/extraordinary-journey?utm_source=api). After transforming, stop blinking him: he returns front face up and loses the flash engine.

## Main patterns

- [Chrome Host Seedshark](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/68/chrome-host-seedshark?utm_source=api) plus cheap interaction turns every bounce spell and portal artifact into incubator material.
- [Containment Priest](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/13/containment-priest?utm_source=api) turns [Planar Incision](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/72/planar-incision?utm_source=api) into permanent exile against an opposing nontoken creature. Bounce Priest before opening your own portals.
- [Mirror of Life Trapping](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/326/mirror-of-life-trapping?utm_source=api) delays cast creatures. Priest can strand its attempted returns, while creature tokens and portal-cheated creatures never trigger Mirror.
- [Floodgate](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/49/floodgate?utm_source=api) deals its [Island](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/319/island?utm_source=api)-scaled damage whenever it leaves. Blink it after attacks or before combat to sweep nonblue ground creatures.
- [Prosperous Thief](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/73/prosperous-thief?utm_source=api) uses ninjutsu to return an unblocked ETB creature to hand, then turns later connections into Treasure.
- [Whitemane Lion](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/35/whitemane-lion?utm_source=api), [Teleportation Circle](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/39/teleportation-circle?utm_source=api), and [Conjurer's Closet](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/259/conjurers-closet?utm_source=api) let the list grind through long games without spending cards from hand.
- [Matzalantli, the Great Door](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/256/matzalantli-the-great-door-the-core?utm_source=api) and [Azor's Gateway](https://scryfall.com/card/rix/176/azors-gateway-sanctum-of-the-sun?utm_source=api) turn spare cards into selection first and oversized portal mana later.
- [Step Between Worlds](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/70/step-between-worlds?utm_source=api) reloads the table and makes transformed [Heliod](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/17/heliod-the-radiant-dawn-heliod-the-warped-eclipse?utm_source=api) discount the next wave of spells.

## Win conditions

Most games end by board presence, not combo. Reused ETB creatures, copied bodies, and portal guards eventually become enough pressure that [Runed Arch](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/334/runed-arch?utm_source=api), [Another Round](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/1/another-round?utm_source=api), or [Venser, the Sojourner](https://scryfall.com/card/ddi/1/venser-the-sojourner?utm_source=api) opens a lethal attack.

The other finishing line is simply landing a portal that the table cannot claw back from. [Portal to Phyrexia](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/240/portal-to-phyrexia?utm_source=api) empties boards, [Planar Bridge](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/313/planar-bridge?utm_source=api) turns mana into the best permanent every turn, and [Prototype Portal](https://scryfall.com/card/c18/216/prototype-portal?utm_source=api) can bury slower tables under repeated artifact copies.

## Early, mid, and late game

Early game means lands, a guard, and a cheap portal or selection engine. [Wall of Tears](https://scryfall.com/card/sth/50/wall-of-tears?utm_source=api), [Resolute Watchdog](https://scryfall.com/card/rna/19/resolute-watchdog?utm_source=api), [Jacob Hauken, Inspector](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/65/jacob-hauken-inspector-haukens-insight?utm_source=api), and [Aether Channeler](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/42/aether-channeler?utm_source=api) are the preferred starts.

Midgame is where the deck wants to live. Pair one repeatable portal with Inga, Channeler, Skaab, Salvager, Intercessor, or [Floodgate](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/49/floodgate?utm_source=api). Do not deploy [Containment Priest](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/13/containment-priest?utm_source=api) until it either stops an opponent's non-cast entry or converts your temporary exile into a permanent answer.

Late game belongs to [Heliod](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/17/heliod-the-radiant-dawn-heliod-the-warped-eclipse?utm_source=api) and the giant artifacts. Once the back face is online, the deck can pass with interaction available and still commit a meaningful board at the last possible moment.

## Mulligan guide

Keep hands with three lands or two lands plus a cheap mana piece, especially if one spell either buys time or advances a portal engine. One portal card plus one defensive spell is better than a hand full of sixes. Hands that only contain giant artifacts and no early bounce should go back.

The best keeps usually contain one of [Wall of Tears](https://scryfall.com/card/sth/50/wall-of-tears?utm_source=api), [Jacob Hauken, Inspector](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/65/jacob-hauken-inspector-haukens-insight?utm_source=api), [Aether Channeler](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/42/aether-channeler?utm_source=api), [Archaeomancer's Map](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/136/archaeomancers-map?utm_source=api), or a blink engine paired with an ETB creature.

## Important sequencing and rules notes

- [Portal Manipulator](https://scryfall.com/card/clu/40/portal-manipulator?utm_source=api) only matters in the declare attackers step. Leave mana up if you want that line.
- [Portal of Sanctuary](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/71/portal-of-sanctuary?utm_source=api) returns attached Auras with the creature, which is why cheap Auras are worth the slot here.
- [Another Round](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/1/another-round?utm_source=api) is a giant value spell, not a deterministic combo by itself.
- [Aether Gale](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/186/aether-gale?utm_source=api) needs six targets. Use your own ETB creatures or portal permanents when opponents do not supply all six.
- [Containment Priest](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/13/containment-priest?utm_source=api) also exiles your own non-cast creature entries. Bounce it before resolving blink, Mirror returns, [Portal to Phyrexia](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/240/portal-to-phyrexia?utm_source=api) reanimation, or creature-cheat effects.
- Blinking [Jacob Hauken, Inspector](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/65/jacob-hauken-inspector-haukens-insight?utm_source=api) or transformed [Heliod](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/17/heliod-the-radiant-dawn-heliod-the-warped-eclipse?utm_source=api) resets them and abandons their transformed state.

## Weaknesses and what to protect

The list is slow and intentionally clunky. Graveyard exile weakens Skaab and Salvager, artifact hate removes the repeatable doors, and a badly timed [Containment Priest](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/13/containment-priest?utm_source=api) strands your own board. Protect the first portal paired with a real ETB target. Keep a Priest-bounce outlet available whenever your own creatures need to cross back.
