Azorius portal control built around named portals, gates, and aether permanents. The deck spends the early game banking mana, bouncing the most relevant permanent, and sneaking value through odd portal artifacts until [Heliod, the Warped Eclipse](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/17/heliod-the-radiant-dawn-heliod-the-warped-eclipse?utm_source=api) lets the whole hand arrive at flash speed.

**Commander:** [Heliod, the Radiant Dawn](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/17/heliod-the-radiant-dawn-heliod-the-warped-eclipse?utm_source=api)  
**Deck size:** 100 cards  
**Primary plan:** open repeated portal engines, turn noncreature spells into incubators and token bodies, then burst through one large flash turn  
**Backup plan:** land a single giant portal permanent like [Portal to Phyrexia](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/240/portal-to-phyrexia?utm_source=api), [Planar Bridge](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/313/planar-bridge?utm_source=api), or [Gate to the Aether](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/174/gate-to-the-aether?utm_source=api) and let the table drown in tempo

## How the deck works

The early turns are about surviving without looking like a normal Azorius control deck. Cheap bounce like [Boomerang](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/MIR-56/boomerang?utm_source=api), [Snap](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/66/snap?utm_source=api), [Depart the Realm](https://scryfall.com/card/khm/53/depart-the-realm?utm_source=api), and [Ephara's Dispersal](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/103/epharas-dispersal?utm_source=api) buys time while the mana base keeps making land drops. The deck would rather turn one threat sideways or back to hand than answer everything forever.

The midgame starts once a portal permanent sticks. [Erratic Portal](https://scryfall.com/card/mb2/220/erratic-portal?utm_source=api), [Portal of Sanctuary](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/71/portal-of-sanctuary?utm_source=api), [Conjurer's Closet](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/259/conjurers-closet?utm_source=api), and [Mirror of Life Trapping](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/326/mirror-of-life-trapping?utm_source=api) keep reusing creatures while [Chrome Host Seedshark](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/68/chrome-host-seedshark?utm_source=api), [Essence of Orthodoxy](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/323/essence-of-orthodoxy?utm_source=api), [Tiller of Flesh](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/44/tiller-of-flesh?utm_source=api), and [Xerex Strobe-Knight](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/85/xerex-strobe-knight?utm_source=api) quietly build a board.

[Heliod](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/17/heliod-the-radiant-dawn-heliod-the-warped-eclipse?utm_source=api) is the hinge. The front face buys back enchantments like [Journey to Nowhere](https://scryfall.com/card/cmd/17/journey-to-nowhere?utm_source=api), [Aether Tunnel](https://scryfall.com/card/m19/43/aether-tunnel?utm_source=api), [Abduction](https://scryfall.com/card/6ed/55/abduction?utm_source=api), and [Extraordinary Journey](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/48/extraordinary-journey?utm_source=api). Once transformed, [Heliod, the Warped Eclipse](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/17/heliod-the-radiant-dawn-heliod-the-warped-eclipse?utm_source=api) turns everybody's extra draw into a discount and lets the deck pass with mana up before opening several doors at end step.

## Main patterns

- [Chrome Host Seedshark](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/68/chrome-host-seedshark?utm_source=api) plus cheap interaction turns every bounce spell and portal artifact into incubator material.
- [Portal of Sanctuary](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/71/portal-of-sanctuary?utm_source=api) with [Abduction](https://scryfall.com/card/6ed/55/abduction?utm_source=api) or [Aether Tunnel](https://scryfall.com/card/m19/43/aether-tunnel?utm_source=api) lets the deck pick the Aura back up instead of losing it to spot removal.
- [Whitemane Lion](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/35/whitemane-lion?utm_source=api), [Distinguished Conjurer](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/264/distinguished-conjurer?utm_source=api), and [Conjurer's Closet](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/259/conjurers-closet?utm_source=api) let the list grind through long games without spending cards from hand.
- [Day's Undoing](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/85/days-undoing?utm_source=api) and [Step Between Worlds](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/70/step-between-worlds?utm_source=api) are not pure reloads here. They also make transformed [Heliod](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/17/heliod-the-radiant-dawn-heliod-the-warped-eclipse?utm_source=api) discount the next wave of spells.

## Win conditions

Most games end by board presence, not combo. Incubators, Knights, copied artifacts, and the occasional giant Zombie Army eventually turn into enough pressure that one [Runed Arch](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/334/runed-arch?utm_source=api), [Another Round](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/1/another-round?utm_source=api), or anthem side of [Invasion of Belenon](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/20/invasion-of-belenon-belenon-war-anthem?utm_source=api) pushes lethal.

The other finishing line is simply landing a portal that the table cannot claw back from. [Portal to Phyrexia](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/240/portal-to-phyrexia?utm_source=api) empties boards, [Planar Bridge](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/313/planar-bridge?utm_source=api) turns mana into the best permanent every turn, and [Prototype Portal](https://scryfall.com/card/c18/216/prototype-portal?utm_source=api) can bury slower tables under repeated artifact copies.

## Early, mid, and late game

Early game means lands, one cheap portal or bounce spell, and not getting buried. [Archaeomancer's Map](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/136/archaeomancers-map?utm_source=api), [Astral Cornucopia](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/342/astral-cornucopia?utm_source=api), [Extraplanar Lens](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/381/extraplanar-lens?utm_source=api), [Cloud of Faeries](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/219/cloud-of-faeries?utm_source=api), and [Interdimensional Web Watch](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/165/interdimensional-web-watch?utm_source=api) help bridge to the five- and six-mana plays.

Midgame is where the deck wants to live. Repeated bounce and blink keeps the best opposing permanent from staying put, battles become easier to flip, and small token makers start giving every end-step portal more value than a fair deck is supposed to get.

Late game belongs to [Heliod](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/17/heliod-the-radiant-dawn-heliod-the-warped-eclipse?utm_source=api) and the giant artifacts. Once the back face is online, the deck can pass with interaction available and still commit a meaningful board at the last possible moment.

## Mulligan guide

Keep hands with three lands or two lands plus a cheap mana piece, especially if one spell either buys time or advances a portal engine. One portal card plus one defensive spell is better than a hand full of sixes. Hands that only contain giant artifacts and no early bounce should go back.

The best keeps usually contain one of [Erratic Portal](https://scryfall.com/card/mb2/220/erratic-portal?utm_source=api), [Portal of Sanctuary](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/71/portal-of-sanctuary?utm_source=api), [Archaeomancer's Map](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/136/archaeomancers-map?utm_source=api), [Chrome Host Seedshark](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/68/chrome-host-seedshark?utm_source=api), or [Firemane Commando](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/145/firemane-commando?utm_source=api).

## Important sequencing and rules notes

- [Day's Undoing](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/85/days-undoing?utm_source=api) ends the turn if cast on your turn, so use it as a reset or to set up a future [Heliod](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/17/heliod-the-radiant-dawn-heliod-the-warped-eclipse?utm_source=api) turn, not as a mid-combat trick.
- [Portal Manipulator](https://scryfall.com/card/clu/40/portal-manipulator?utm_source=api) only matters in the declare attackers step. Leave mana up if you want that line.
- [Portal of Sanctuary](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/71/portal-of-sanctuary?utm_source=api) returns attached Auras with the creature, which is why cheap Auras are worth the slot here.
- [Another Round](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/1/another-round?utm_source=api) is a giant value spell, not a deterministic combo by itself.

## Weaknesses and what to protect

The list is slow and intentionally clunky. Fast combo decks, repeated artifact hate, and opponents who refuse to overextend into bounce all create problems. Protect the first real engine piece, not the first random token maker. If [Heliod, the Warped Eclipse](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/17/heliod-the-radiant-dawn-heliod-the-warped-eclipse?utm_source=api) is about to let you untap into a burst turn, that is the permanent worth spending [Light the Way](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/24/light-the-way?utm_source=api) or [Justiciar's Portal](https://scryfall.com/card/rna/13/justiciars-portal?utm_source=api) on. [Dolmen Gate](https://scryfall.com/card/lrw/256/dolmen-gate?utm_source=api) is the card that makes the token attack actually connect.
