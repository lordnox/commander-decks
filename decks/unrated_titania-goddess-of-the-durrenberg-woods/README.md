## How the woods awaken

Build five mana while putting a sacrificial land or land-recursion card in place. [Titania](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/111/titania-protector-of-argoth?utm_source=api) returns one land when she enters. After she resolves, crack a fetch land, cast [Harrow](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/98/harrow?utm_source=api), or feed land to [Zuran Orb](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/300/zuran-orb?utm_source=api) or [Sylvan Safekeeper](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/287/sylvan-safekeeper?utm_source=api): each land that reaches the graveyard creates a 5/3 Elemental.

The deck wants small, repeated rituals rather than one explosive sacrifice. [Life from the Loam](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/96/life-from-the-loam?utm_source=api), [Crucible of Worlds](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/303/crucible-of-worlds?utm_source=api), [Ramunap Excavator](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/202/ramunap-excavator?utm_source=api), [Groundskeeper](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/97/groundskeeper?utm_source=api), and [Six](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/169/six?utm_source=api) let the same earth serve again. [Elemental Bond](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/254/elemental-bond?utm_source=api) and [Garruk's Packleader](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/168/garruks-packleader?utm_source=api) turn each spirit into a card; [Greater Good](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/174/greater-good?utm_source=api) converts an Elemental threatened by removal into five fresh cards.

## Finite synergies

### [Titania](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/111/titania-protector-of-argoth?utm_source=api) and a sacrifice land

With [Titania](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/111/titania-protector-of-argoth?utm_source=api) in play, sacrifice [Evolving Wilds](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/240/evolving-wilds?utm_source=api). [Titania](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/111/titania-protector-of-argoth?utm_source=api) triggers and the fetch ability resolves independently: make a 5/3 and find a [Forest](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/325/forest?utm_source=api). Replaying Wilds with Crucible or Ramunap repeats this on later turns, but the land-drop limit keeps it finite.

### [Titania](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/111/titania-protector-of-argoth?utm_source=api) and [Lotus Field](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/385/lotus-field?utm_source=api)

[Lotus Field](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/385/lotus-field?utm_source=api) enters, then its trigger sacrifices two lands. [Titania](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/111/titania-protector-of-argoth?utm_source=api) creates two 5/3s. [Lotus Field](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/385/lotus-field?utm_source=api) remains as a three-mana land. This is a single burst, not a loop.

### Ashaya and the offering

Ashaya makes each nontoken creature a [Forest](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/325/forest?utm_source=api) land. [Zuran Orb](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/300/zuran-orb?utm_source=api) or [Sylvan Safekeeper](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/287/sylvan-safekeeper?utm_source=api) may therefore sacrifice one of those creatures as a land, triggering [Titania](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/111/titania-protector-of-argoth?utm_source=api). Tokens remain only creatures and cannot use this line.

### [Elemental Bond](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/254/elemental-bond?utm_source=api) and [Greater Good](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/174/greater-good?utm_source=api)

[Titania](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/111/titania-protector-of-argoth?utm_source=api)'s 5/3 entering draws through [Elemental Bond](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/254/elemental-bond?utm_source=api). If removal or a wipe threatens it, [Greater Good](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/174/greater-good?utm_source=api) sacrifices it to draw five and discard three. Elemental tokens do not trigger [Titania](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/111/titania-protector-of-argoth?utm_source=api) when sacrificed unless another effect has made them lands.

## Win conditions

1. Attack with a growing procession of 5/3 Elementals.
2. Use [Baru, Fist of Krosa](https://scryfall.com/card/fut/142/baru-fist-of-krosa?utm_source=api) or [Kamahl, Fist of Krosa](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/166/kamahl-fist-of-krosa?utm_source=api) to grant the woodland trample and extra power.
3. Cast [Rude Awakening](https://scryfall.com/card/gvl/22/rude-awakening?utm_source=api) to animate every land; entwine it when nine mana safely untaps the domain first.
4. Let [Liege of the Tangle](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/174/liege-of-the-tangle?utm_source=api) connect and awaken lands as permanent 8/8 Elementals.
5. Return [Multani, Yavimaya's Avatar](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/100/multani-yavimayas-avatar?utm_source=api) repeatedly as the large reach-and-trample backup.

## Early, mid, and late

**Early:** Keep making land drops. A one-mana druid or [Wild Growth](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/195/wild-growth?utm_source=api) aims [Titania](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/111/titania-protector-of-argoth?utm_source=api) at turn four; otherwise develop a fetch land and recursion.

**Mid:** Cast [Titania](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/111/titania-protector-of-argoth?utm_source=api) when one land can die soon afterward. Do not expose [Zuran Orb](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/300/zuran-orb?utm_source=api) and sacrifice the whole domain without a lethal attack or [Splendid Reclamation](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/108/splendid-reclamation?utm_source=api). Keep protection mana available once several Elementals are visible.

**Late:** Rebuild through [World Shaper](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/214/world-shaper?utm_source=api) or [Splendid Reclamation](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/108/splendid-reclamation?utm_source=api). Animate the forest only when combat is favorable; a creature wipe after [Rude Awakening](https://scryfall.com/card/gvl/22/rude-awakening?utm_source=api) can destroy the lands too.

## Mulligan guide

Keep three lands and early ramp, or two lands with a one-mana accelerator plus a land finder. Prefer a fetch land or sacrifice outlet and one recursion piece. Ship hands whose first meaningful play costs four, and hands that cannot reasonably produce five mana by turn five.

## Sequencing and rules

- [Titania](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/111/titania-protector-of-argoth?utm_source=api) must already be on the battlefield when a land reaches the graveyard.
- Returning or bouncing a land does not trigger [Titania](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/111/titania-protector-of-argoth?utm_source=api).
- [Lotus Field](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/385/lotus-field?utm_source=api) sacrifices through an enters-the-battlefield trigger, after it is already on the battlefield.
- [Dryad Arbor](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/273/dryad-arbor?utm_source=api) has summoning sickness and can be replayed from the graveyard as the turn's land.
- Ashaya affects nontoken creatures only.
- Animating lands exposes them to creature removal and wipes; choose the combat turn carefully.

## Weaknesses

[Titania](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/111/titania-protector-of-argoth?utm_source=api) costs seven after one removal and nine after two. Graveyard hate shuts down the regrowth package. Exile-based creature wipes bypass [Wrap in Vigor](https://scryfall.com/card/cns/183/wrap-in-vigor?utm_source=api). Land-animation finishers risk losing the mana base to a creature wipe, while fogs can blank the one large combat turn.
