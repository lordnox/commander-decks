## Goals

- **Nature goddess** — Titania should feel like the embodied guardian of the Dürrenberg woods: druids, animals, old natural magic, and land spirits.
- **Living woods** — Lands must die, return, become creatures, and finally attack; this is not generic mono-green good stuff.

## Rankings

First pass: Jank 8, Fun 8, Mean 4, Nature goddess 9, Living woods 9.

## Rules

- Titania triggers only when a land moves from the battlefield to a graveyard. Discarding, milling, cycling, or channeling a land does not trigger her.
- A fetch land sacrificed before Titania enters cannot retroactively create an Elemental. Titania may return that land with her entry trigger.
- Lotus Field enters first, then its triggered ability sacrifices two lands. If Titania is present, both sacrifices trigger her. Hexproof does not protect the sacrifice trigger from being countered.
- Ashaya makes nontoken creatures Forest lands in addition to their other types. Sacrificing one to Zuran Orb or Sylvan Safekeeper triggers Titania. Tokens are explicitly excluded.
- Dryad Arbor is a land card and creature card. It can be replayed from the graveyard with Crucible or Ramunap Excavator, but it has summoning sickness.
- Greater Good sacrificing a 5/3 draws five and discards three. The sacrificed Elemental token does not trigger Titania because it is not a land.
- Multani's return ability bounces two lands to hand; it does not put them in the graveyard and does not trigger Titania.
- Rude Awakening does not untap creatures that Ashaya makes into lands unless entwined; the first mode untaps all lands, including those creatures.

## Talks

### 2026-09-11 — Design lock

The user requested a second version of the Bad Dürrenberg shaman on the existing branch and delegated all remaining choices. Titania represents the shaman as the nature goddess of the Dürrenberg woods.

**Plan:** Sacrifice or crack one land at a time, recur it, and let Titania manifest each death as a 5/3 forest spirit; card-draw enchantments sustain the procession, then Baru, Kamahl, Rude Awakening, Multani, or Liege turns the visible woodland into lethal combat.

**Target:** Bracket 2+ Incremental Core, matching Three Offerings. Expected Titania on turn four or five, first land-spirit procession by turn five or six, normal combat threat around turn eight or nine. Earliest credible high roll is turn seven after Titania plus Zuran Orb or Lotus Field creates several Elementals. Turn-five milestone: five mana available or Titania cast, plus one sacrifice land or recursion piece.

**Boundaries:** No Game Changers from the 2026-09-03 cache, no Sol Ring or permanent fast mana, no infinite loops, no extra turns, and no plan of destroying opposing lands. Land tutors are Elvish Reclaimer and ordinary land fetch; Crop Rotation is excluded because it is a Game Changer and would make Lotus Field too routine.

**Interaction:** Board protection for Titania and the Elemental army; targeted artifact/enchantment answers; Lignify and Song of the Dryads for creatures or commanders; Bind for a dangerous activated ability; Ezuri's Predation as the creature wipe; Constant Mists as life-total protection. Most answers are visible woodland permanents or old green spells rather than generic staples.

**Package map:** 39 true lands plus Bala Ged Recovery; twelve lands sacrifice themselves. Zuran Orb, Claws of Gix, Sylvan Safekeeper, Lotus Field, Harrow, Roiling Regrowth, Edge of Autumn, and Springbloom Druid provide further sacrifice. Loam, Crucible, Ramunap, Groundskeeper, Six, World Shaper, Splendid Reclamation, Petrified Field, and Drownyard Temple regrow the domain. Elemental Bond, Garruk's Packleader, Greater Good, Tracker, and Shamanic Revelation keep cards moving.

**Name:** **Titania — Goddess of the Dürrenberg Woods**. Directory transliterates the title as `titania-goddess-of-the-durrenberg-woods`.

**Rejected directions:** Mono-green landfall staples without a sacrifice role; mass land destruction; Scapeshift-style one-turn dumps; Field of the Dead, Crop Rotation, Seedborn Muse, and other cached Game Changers; Ashaya untap infinites; a generic Elf-ball shell.
