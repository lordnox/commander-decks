## How the deck works

Make a creature token, then copy it. The copy is the repeating action. [**Trostani, Selesnya's Voice**](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/204/trostani-selesnyas-voice?utm_source=api) does that as a tap rite for `{1}{G}{W}` and pays life equal to toughness whenever another creature enters. [**Growing Ranks**](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/193/growing-ranks?utm_source=api), [**Nesting Dovehawk**](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/17/nesting-dovehawk?utm_source=api), and the populate instants keep the rite going when she is taxed.

Populate only copies a *creature token*. Cast [**Advent of the Wurm**](https://scryfall.com/card/mm3/147/advent-of-the-wurm?utm_source=api), [**Trostani's Summoner**](https://scryfall.com/card/mic/156/trostanis-summoner?utm_source=api), [**Hornet Queen**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/184/hornet-queen?utm_source=api), or crack [**Grove of the Guardian**](https://scryfall.com/card/rtr/240/grove-of-the-guardian?utm_source=api) before the copy. One-mana [**Wake the Reflections**](https://scryfall.com/card/mm3/28/wake-the-reflections?utm_source=api) on an empty board does nothing.

If [Trostani](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/204/trostani-selesnyas-voice?utm_source=api) never sticks, [**Emmara, Soul of the Accord**](https://scryfall.com/card/grn/168/emmara-soul-of-the-accord?utm_source=api), [**Rhys the Redeemed**](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/213/rhys-the-redeemed?utm_source=api), [**Sprout Swarm**](https://scryfall.com/card/fut/138/sprout-swarm?utm_source=api), [**Squirrel Nest**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/177/squirrel-nest?utm_source=api), and the shrine lands still mint a flock. Combat with [**Overrun**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/230/overrun?utm_source=api) or [**True Conviction**](https://scryfall.com/card/c14/93/true-conviction?utm_source=api) is the usual close. [**Test of Endurance**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/32/test-of-endurance?utm_source=api) is the ordeal win once life from [Trostani](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/204/trostani-selesnyas-voice?utm_source=api) is already on the table: 50 life, survive your next upkeep.

## Win conditions

1. Populate a large token (Wurm, Rhino, Adephage, Grove 8/8, [Hornet Queen](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/184/hornet-queen?utm_source=api)) and attack with [**Overrun**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/230/overrun?utm_source=api), [**True Conviction**](https://scryfall.com/card/c14/93/true-conviction?utm_source=api), or [**Beastmaster Ascension**](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/283/beastmaster-ascension?utm_source=api).
2. [**Test of Endurance**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/32/test-of-endurance?utm_source=api) after [Trostani](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/204/trostani-selesnyas-voice?utm_source=api) has already stacked life. It is an upkeep trigger, not an instant kill.
3. [**Giant Adephage**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/179/giant-adephage?utm_source=api) combat copies plus populate on the 7/7.

There is no infinite populate loop. [**Rhys the Redeemed**](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/213/rhys-the-redeemed?utm_source=api) copies each token once per expensive activation.

## Early, mid, and late

**Early.** Hit lands of both colors. Dorks, [**Wild Growth**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/195/wild-growth?utm_source=api), [**Selesnya Signet**](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/RVR-267/selesnya-signet?utm_source=api), [**Nature's Lore**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/177/natures-lore?utm_source=api) / [**Three Visits**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/181/three-visits?utm_source=api). A cheap token ([**Raise the Alarm**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/41/raise-the-alarm?utm_source=api), [**Khalni Garden**](https://scryfall.com/card/c18/262/khalni-garden?utm_source=api)) is worth more than a populate spell with no target.

**Mid.** Cast [Trostani](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/204/trostani-selesnyas-voice?utm_source=api) on four or five. Hold `{1}{G}{W}` after she untaps. Sequence a 4/4+ token before the first populate. [**Rootborn Defenses**](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/RVR-26/rootborn-defenses?utm_source=api) is the wipe you expect.

**Late.** Copy [Hornet Queen](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/184/hornet-queen?utm_source=api) or Grove. [**Hour of Reckoning**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/120/hour-of-reckoning?utm_source=api) clears nontokens. If life is already high, resolve [Test of Endurance](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/32/test-of-endurance?utm_source=api) and protect the upkeep.

## Mulligan

Keep two lands that can make green and a path to white by turn three, or two lands plus a dork/Lore that finds the missing color. Keep a cheap token source. Ship a hand of only populate spells, only four-drops, or all [Plains](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/317/plains?utm_source=api) with no green. London once for [Trostani](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/204/trostani-selesnyas-voice?utm_source=api) colors; twice only if the seven cannot make `{G}{G}{W}{W}` by turn five even with the draw step.

## Sequencing

- [Trostani](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/204/trostani-selesnyas-voice?utm_source=api) has summoning sickness. Populate is the following turn unless you have another populate card.
- Populate the *best* token, not the newest 1/1. A [Hornet Queen](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/184/hornet-queen?utm_source=api) copy is usually better than a Hornet.
- [**Scion of Vitu-Ghazi**](https://scryfall.com/card/dgm/7/scion-of-vitu-ghazi?utm_source=api) makes a Bird and populates only if you *cast it from hand*. Copies are just 4/4s.
- [**Hour of Reckoning**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/120/hour-of-reckoning?utm_source=api) kills [Trostani](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/204/trostani-selesnyas-voice?utm_source=api). Rootborn or Wrap first if she must stay.
- [**Sacred Mesa**](https://scryfall.com/card/c14/86/sacred-mesa?utm_source=api) needs `{W}` each upkeep.
- [**Kjeldoran Outpost**](https://scryfall.com/card/wc97/jk139sb/kjeldoran-outpost?utm_source=api) needs another creature already in play.

## Weaknesses

Commander tax after two removals. Grave hate does little; exile wipes and [Farewell](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/64/farewell)-shaped effects (not in this 99) delete the flock. Empty-board populate is dead. `{G}{G}{W}{W}` is color-hungry if the duals arrive tapped. [Test of Endurance](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/32/test-of-endurance?utm_source=api) dies to any damage before the upkeep.
