# Decisions

## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, the primer, and the assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a cut back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated session notes so an argument does not live only in chat.

Every unique card needs a Cards in line. The other headings appear when there is something to record.

## Goals

- **Congregation** — The repeating action is making a flock and copying it (populate / tap rites), not generic Selesnya anthems or token doubling.
- **Blessing** — Life from Trostani and the Test of Endurance ordeal should read as a priestess blessing the shrine, not a Felidar/Aetherflux dump.

## Primer

Win-turn lines in the primer are goldfish setup (Trostani or a populate engine plus a token by turn five) plus Incremental Core texture: combat and Test of Endurance, not a from-hand dump. No token doublers, no Craterhoof, no Game Changers from the 2026-09-03 cache.

## Rules

- Populate makes a token copy of a *creature token* you control. A nontoken Trostani, Adephage, or Scion is not a legal populate target. Copy Hornet Queen, a Hornet, a Wurm, Grove's 8/8, or a Saproling.
- Trostani's populate is `{1}{G}{W}`, tap, so it needs a full turn after she resolves unless something untaps her. Summoning sickness applies.
- Trostani's life trigger uses toughness of the entering creature, including tokens. A 5/5 Wurm is 5 life; four 1/1 Hornets are 4 life in four triggers.
- Scion of Vitu-Ghazi's Bird-then-populate only happens if it was *cast from hand*. Populate copies of Scion are 4/4s without that clause firing.
- Hour of Reckoning destroys only nontoken creatures. Tokens (and Trostani if you convoke her away from being destroyed—she is nontoken) still die if she is a creature. Hold Rootborn or Wrap if you need her.
- Rhys's second ability copies each creature token once per activation. It is not infinite.
- Sacred Mesa asks for `{W}` each upkeep or you sacrifice it.
- Kjeldoran Outpost makes a Soldier only if you control a creature.
- Test of Endurance is an upkeep trigger you control: if you have 50 or more life, you win. It must survive a turn cycle after it is in play. Damage and life loss still answer it. Teferi's Protection is not in the list.
- Giant Adephage's copy trigger is combat damage to a player; a populated Adephage token has the same trigger.
