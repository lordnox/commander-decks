## How the deck works

1. Make land drops and develop ordinary artifact ramp.
2. Deploy the creature tax that best fits the table: entrance damage, upkeep mana, sacrifice, or combat punishment.
3. Cast [Mogis](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/89/mogis-god-of-slaughter?utm_source=api) once the board is controlled. Keep pressure on life totals without exposing a normal creature commander.
4. Assemble enough mana for [Doubling Cube](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/321/doubling-cube?utm_source=api) to produce a real profit, using Keys or [Clock of Omens](https://scryfall.com/card/m13/202/clock-of-omens?utm_source=api) for extra activations.
5. End the game with a table-wide X-spell or amplified damage before opponents rebuild.

## Creature-control patterns

### Entrance punishment

[**Aether Flash**](https://scryfall.com/card/7ed/172/aether-flash?utm_source=api) deals 2 damage to every creature that enters. [**Night of Souls' Betrayal**](https://scryfall.com/card/ima/99/night-of-souls-betrayal?utm_source=api) lets that kill creatures that would normally have 3 toughness. [**Engineered Plague**](https://scryfall.com/card/7ed/133/engineered-plague?utm_source=api) adds another −1/−1 against the chosen type, and [**Curse of Death's Hold**](https://scryfall.com/card/isd/94/curse-of-deaths-hold?utm_source=api) applies that shrink to one player. [**Death Pits of Rath**](https://scryfall.com/card/9ed/123%E2%98%85/death-pits-of-rath?utm_source=api) upgrades any damage to destruction, so Flash kills creatures of any toughness that can be destroyed.

[**Lethal Vapors**](https://scryfall.com/card/scg/68/lethal-vapors?utm_source=api) is the hardest version: every entering creature is destroyed. An opponent can remove it for `{0}`, but that player skips their next turn. [**Tainted Aether**](https://scryfall.com/card/7ed/167/tainted-aether?utm_source=api) instead forces the entering creature's controller to sacrifice a creature or land.

### Upkeep and combat

[Mogis](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/89/mogis-god-of-slaughter?utm_source=api) asks each opponent to sacrifice a creature or take 2 damage during that opponent's upkeep. [**Shivan Gorge**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/297/shivan-gorge?utm_source=api) adds a repeatable one damage to each opponent that the damage amplifiers can scale.

[**Death Pits of Rath**](https://scryfall.com/card/9ed/123%E2%98%85/death-pits-of-rath?utm_source=api) plus Gorge, Flash, or a red sweeper destroys any damaged creature that can be destroyed. [**Mudslide**](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/204/mudslide?utm_source=api) constrains how many nonflying survivors can untap without consuming mana.

### Sweeper conversion

[**Repercussion**](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/95/repercussion?utm_source=api) turns damage to creatures into damage to their controllers. It makes [**Blasphemous Act**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/163/blasphemous-act?utm_source=api), [**Chain Reaction**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/121/chain-reaction?utm_source=api), and especially [**Star of Extinction**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/259/star-of-extinction?utm_source=api) potential finishers rather than only resets. Count every creature and both applicable damage-replacement effects before committing: symmetrical [**Furnace of Rath**](https://scryfall.com/card/hop/55/furnace-of-rath?utm_source=api) can make this lethal to the whole table.

## Big-mana engine

[**Doubling Cube**](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/321/doubling-cube?utm_source=api) costs `{3}` and taps to double each type of unspent mana. If the pool contains `M` mana before paying, one activation leaves `2 × (M − 3)`. It only increases the pool when `M` is at least 7.

Cube's ability is a mana ability. [**Mirage Mirror**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/206/mirage-mirror?utm_source=api) is not: paying `{2}` lets it become Cube, [**Cabal Coffers**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/301/cabal-coffers?utm_source=api), a lock enchantment, or an opponent's best permanent until end of turn, after which it can tap for that copy's abilities. [**Sculpting Steel**](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/374/sculpting-steel?utm_source=api) enters as a lasting copy of an artifact already in play. [**Clock of Omens**](https://scryfall.com/card/m13/202/clock-of-omens?utm_source=api) trades two spare untapped artifacts for another Cube activation. Extra Cube activations only help when enough mana remains to pay the additional `{3}` each time.

[**Cabal Coffers**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/301/cabal-coffers?utm_source=api) plus [**Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/287/urborg-tomb-of-yawgmoth?utm_source=api), [**Bubbling Muck**](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/54/bubbling-muck?utm_source=api), creature deaths stored by [**Black Market**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/739/black-market?utm_source=api), and [**Mana Geyser**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/247/mana-geyser?utm_source=api) are the best ways to reach Cube's profitable range. [**Mana Flare**](https://scryfall.com/card/mb2/196/mana-flare?utm_source=api) multiplies land production. [**Nyx Lotus**](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/235/nyx-lotus?utm_source=api) rewards a pip-heavy board and repeated artifact untaps. [**Urza's Cave**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/234/urzas-cave?utm_source=api) finds Coffers, Urborg, or [**Deserted Temple**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/301/deserted-temple?utm_source=api). Cube scales those engines rather than trying to rescue a small mana pool.

## Win conditions

- [**Torment of Hailfire**](https://scryfall.com/card/hou/77/torment-of-hailfire?utm_source=api) and [**Exsanguinate**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/173/exsanguinate?utm_source=api) affect every opponent and are the cleanest black-mana finishes.
- [**Crackle with Power**](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/95/crackle-with-power?utm_source=api) is expensive but scales fast enough to remove three players after Cube.
- [**Comet Storm**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/411/comet-storm?utm_source=api) divides instant-speed damage among opponents.
- [**Cut // Ribbons**](https://scryfall.com/card/akh/223/cut-ribbons?utm_source=api) is useful interaction early and a graveyard finisher late.
- [**Reiterate**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/182/reiterate?utm_source=api) copies an efficient table-wide finisher and can return to hand with buyback when mana is abundant.

[**Fiery Emancipation**](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/143/fiery-emancipation?utm_source=api) triples this deck's damage, including [Mogis](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/89/mogis-god-of-slaughter?utm_source=api) and red burn. It does not modify Torment or [Exsanguinate](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/173/exsanguinate?utm_source=api) because those cause life loss rather than damage. [**Wound Reflection**](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/113/wound-reflection?utm_source=api) and level-three [**Warlock Class**](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/125/warlock-class?utm_source=api) do care about that life loss and repeat it at the end step.

[**Dictate of the Twin Gods**](https://scryfall.com/card/jou/93/dictate-of-the-twin-gods?utm_source=api) and [**Furnace of Rath**](https://scryfall.com/card/hop/55/furnace-of-rath?utm_source=api) are symmetrical. Cast or flash them in only when this deck uses the multiplier first or can survive the return attack.

[**The Rollercrusher Ride**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/155/the-rollercrusher-ride?utm_source=api) doubles noncombat damage once four card types are in the graveyard and can clear several creatures when it enters.

## Early, mid, and late game

### Early

Prioritize three lands, both colors, and a two- or three-mana ramp piece. Play [**Night's Whisper**](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/189/nights-whisper?utm_source=api), [**Read the Bones**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/154/read-the-bones?utm_source=api), or [**Mazemind Tome**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/676/mazemind-tome?utm_source=api) before holding expensive control. Do not keep a hand whose first meaningful play costs four.

### Midgame

Choose one lock rather than emptying every enchantment into removal. Cast [Mogis](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/89/mogis-god-of-slaughter?utm_source=api) when opponents have creatures to sacrifice or when the damage clock matters. Hold flexible removal for planeswalkers, enchantments, and artifacts because the permanent locks already cover creatures.

### Late

Count mana before exposing Cube. Generate mana with Coffers, [Black Market](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/739/black-market?utm_source=api), Geyser, or rocks; activate Cube only after the `{3}` cost still leaves a meaningful pool. Keep enough mana for a Key untap only when the second Cube activation produces more than it consumes. Cast the finisher in the same main phase before the pool empties.

## Mulligan guide

Keep hands with:

- three lands, including access to both black and red;
- one early ramp or draw spell;
- one creature-control piece appropriate for turns three to five.

Mulligan hands with only colorless utility lands, multiple seven-plus-mana cards, or Cube without a route to seven available mana.

## Important sequencing and rules

- [Mogis](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/89/mogis-god-of-slaughter?utm_source=api) contributes one black and one red devotion. At devotion seven, [Mogis](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/89/mogis-god-of-slaughter?utm_source=api) becomes a creature without entering, so [Aether Flash](https://scryfall.com/card/7ed/172/aether-flash?utm_source=api) does not trigger.
- Cube is a mana ability. [Mirage Mirror](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/206/mirage-mirror?utm_source=api) copying Cube still uses Cube's tap ability that turn.
- [Liquimetal Torque](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/228/liquimetal-torque?utm_source=api) can make an opposing enchantment or planeswalker an artifact until end of turn for [Vandalblast](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/168/vandalblast?utm_source=api) or [Rakdos Charm](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/125/rakdos-charm?utm_source=api).
- Furnace and Emancipation modify damage, not life loss.
- Death Pits destroys a creature after it is dealt damage; it does not give the original source deathtouch.
- [Vandalblast](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/168/vandalblast?utm_source=api) is asymmetrical when overloaded, which matters because this deck depends heavily on artifacts.
- [Endless Atlas](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/947/endless-atlas?utm_source=api) needs three lands with the same name; the [Mountain](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/323/mountain?utm_source=api) and [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api) counts are the usual enablers.

## Weaknesses

The deck is much better at killing creatures than answering enchantments, planeswalkers, or stack interaction. Artifact removal can collapse both ramp and the Cube plan, while mass enchantment removal clears the creature lock. The deck also draws attention: visible symmetrical punishers can make opponents attack before the full lock arrives. Recover artifacts with [**Buried Ruin**](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/150/buried-ruin?utm_source=api) and enchantments with [**Crystal Chimes**](https://scryfall.com/card/c15/250/crystal-chimes?utm_source=api). Draw through disruption with [**Chandra, Fire Artisan**](https://scryfall.com/card/war/119/chandra-fire-artisan?utm_source=api), [**Endless Atlas**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/947/endless-atlas?utm_source=api), and [**Commune with Lava**](https://scryfall.com/card/afc/118/commune-with-lava?utm_source=api).
