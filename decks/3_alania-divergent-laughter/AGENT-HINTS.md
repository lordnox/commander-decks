# Divergent Laughter — agent hints

Primer owns the plan. This file is the seat checklist.

## Always ask

1. Is the first instant and first sorcery of **this** turn still unused?
2. Can this seat pay Alania plus tax from **tappable** permanents right
   now, with colored pips counted separately from generic?
3. Who is actually winning the table, and does a split spell need a deal
   before it resolves?
4. Is Otawara still in hand, and is there a Mountain or other red source
   that can take the land drop instead?

## Sequencing

- Do not play Otawara as a land while a basic or tapland can keep the
  Channel in hand.
- Recast Alania only when the snapshot pays `{5}` plus tax. Five tapped
  lands do not pay seven.
- On a control turn, spend the first instant on the last opponent's end
  step (Quick Study, Consider, Frantic Search) rather than skipping the
  turn.
- Cast Arcane Signet and other rocks instead of passing with six unused
  mana because "Alania costs too much."
- **Volcanic Offering** destroys a land **and** a creature, then an
  opponent does the same. Name both types. If Hazel is the archenemy,
  talk first and ask another seat to point both halves at that board.

## Combat

Alania is the copy engine. Do not chump her for a 1/1 unless lethal is
coming through. She enters **untapped** and can block the turn she is
cast.

## Closing

Preserve the first instant for Repeated Reverberation and the first
sorcery for the finisher. Count colored mana after reductions. Tasha
setup is not always a table kill; switch to Jaya or Crackle when the
libraries will survive six laughs.
