## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, the primer, and the assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a cut back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated session notes so an argument does not live only in chat.

Every unique card needs a Cards in line. The other headings appear when there is something to record.

## Rules

- Madcap's second trigger needs a spell or ability this player controls to be the cause of the library exile. Replacement effects that exile as part of that spell or ability still count.
- Face-down exile is uncastable unless another effect lets this player look at the card. Jester's Scepter allows looking; Pyxis of Pandemonium does not, and is not in the list.
- Timing is normal: lands only as a land drop on this player's turn, instants whenever this player has priority in the window (until end of next turn).
- Memory Vessel and Uba Mask do not cancel Madcap. Both players may have permission; on this player's turn, priority lets this seat stack instants first.
- Chaos Wand and Wand of Wonder exile cards from libraries even when they are not the chosen instant or sorcery; Madcap can play those extras.
- Grinning Totem already grants a play window until the next upkeep; Madcap's window is until end of next turn and applies in addition, not instead.
- Extract, Jester's Cap, Sadistic Sacrament, Lobotomy, and Bitter Ordeal do not themselves grant play permission; they need Madcap in play (or another theft overlay) to convert.
- Tasha's Hideous Laughter itself does not let anyone play the exiled cards. Without Madcap it is only mill-shaped exile.

### 2026-09-07 — Autobrew from the Madcap Jester short

Source: [YouTube Short](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/1a1aL2T46ak). User constraint: brew the video's commander and the "exile from their library, then play those cards / tutor their deck" joke. No stored Madcap folder; [Umbris — Mental Breakdown](../3-_umbris-mental-breakdown/README.md) is a different Dimir exile-voltron list. Nothing matching in `DECK-IDEAS.md`.

**Game plan:** Repeatably exile cards from opponents' libraries (attack trigger, search-exile, mill-exile), play those cards until the end of the next turn with any mana, and convert the stolen permanents and spells into a board that can kill. Backup: combat with evasive Jester plus mill from Consuming Aberration if the theft window is empty. Experience: annoying jester that tutors *their* deck, not a cEDH Ad Nauseam stand-in.

**Target bracket (design constraint, not a rating):** Upgraded Bracket 3. Expected normal win around turn 8–9 by spending a stolen pile over a turn or two. Earliest credible high roll: turn 6 if the Jester is down on 4 and Hideous Laughter or Memory Vessel resolves on 5–6. Commander on turn 4. Turn-five milestone: Jester in play (attacking if possible) or a mass-exile spell resolved with mana left to start casting, and at least four lands. Interaction buckets: board protection and evasion for a 3/2, targeted removal, one fog-shaped bounce, stack redirection, library exile as proactive disruption. Boundaries: 0 Game Changers from the 2026-09-03 snapshot, no extra turns, no two-card infinites, no permanent fast mana, no Demonic Tutor / Jeska's Will / Rhystic Study / Cyclonic Rift. Opposition Agent is the GC that *is* the joke and was left out so the advertised cap stays clean.

**Commander shortlist (plan first, then colours):** Madcap is locked by the video. Peers that convert opponent-library exile without that lock:

**Package map:** Tutor-their-deck (Cap, Scepter, Extract, Thief, Totem, Sacrament, Earwig, Thada, Lobotomy, Psychic Surgery, Bitter Ordeal). Mass exile (Tasha's, Vessel, Uba Mask, Knowledge Pool, Sire, Sower, Ensnared, Wands). Curve: many 1–3 enablers, commander at 4, Hideous Laughter at 3, Vessel/Knowledge Pool/Sire in the 5–6 band.
