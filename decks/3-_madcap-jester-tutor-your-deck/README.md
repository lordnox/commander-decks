## How the deck works

Cast the Jester on turn four, then attack. Each opponent loses the top card of their library, and those cards join the play window. Every other library-exile this deck controls — search, mill, imprint, or combat — feeds the same window until the end of the next turn. Spend any colours of mana to cast those spells. Lands still use the normal land drop.

Without the Jester, keep running engines that already grant play permission: [Stolen Strategy](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/807/stolen-strategy?utm_source=api), [Court of Locthwain](https://scryfall.com/card/woc/23/court-of-locthwain?utm_source=api), [Gonti, Night Minister](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/87/gonti-night-minister?utm_source=api), [Thief of Sanity](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/243/thief-of-sanity?utm_source=api), [Fallen Shinobi](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/226/fallen-shinobi?utm_source=api), [Grinning Totem](https://scryfall.com/card/tsb/110/grinning-totem?utm_source=api), [Thada Adel, Acquisitor](https://scryfall.com/card/wwk/40/thada-adel-acquisitor?utm_source=api), and [Etali, Primal Storm](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/194/etali-primal-storm?utm_source=api). Search-exile such as Cap, [Extract](https://scryfall.com/card/ody/85/extract?utm_source=api), [Sadistic Sacrament](https://scryfall.com/card/zen/110/sadistic-sacrament?utm_source=api), and [Lobotomy](https://scryfall.com/card/inv/255/lobotomy?utm_source=api) is dead without an overlay; hold those until the Jester is back.

## Main patterns

**Cap the library.** Resolve [Jester's Cap](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/227/jesters-cap?utm_source=api), [Extract](https://scryfall.com/card/ody/85/extract?utm_source=api), or [Grinning Totem](https://scryfall.com/card/tsb/110/grinning-totem?utm_source=api) while Madcap is in play. Name the cards that actually win their deck, exile them, then spend the window casting those same cards. [Psychic Surgery](https://scryfall.com/card/nph/44/psychic-surgery?utm_source=api) peels an extra card whenever they shuffle afterward.

**Laugh, then spend.** [Tasha's Hideous Laughter](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/78/tashas-hideous-laughter?utm_source=api) does not grant play permission. With Madcap, each opponent's twenty mana of exile is a pile of spells and lands this seat may play until the end of next turn. Do not fire it into an empty mana pool.

**Vessel race.** [Memory Vessel](https://scryfall.com/card/big/13/memory-vessel?utm_source=api) lets every player play the seven they own until this player's next turn. Madcap also lets this player play the twenty-one cards that came from opponents. On this turn, hold priority and stack instants from their piles before passing.

**[Uba Mask](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/272/uba-mask?utm_source=api) draws.** Opponents exile their draws face up. Madcap can cast those cards. Their own Mask permission still exists; instants on this turn go to this seat first.

**[Jester's Scepter](https://scryfall.com/card/csp/137/jesters-scepter?utm_source=api) window.** The Scepter exiles five face down, but its controller may look. Cast the useful ones with Madcap before parking leftovers as named counters.

## Win conditions

1. Cast opponents' threats, removal, and finishers out of exile until the table cannot recover.
2. Connect with evasive Jester plus stolen creatures.
3. [Consuming Aberration](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/283/consuming-aberration?utm_source=api) as a mill closer if libraries are already thin from Hideous Laughter and [Sire of Stagnation](https://scryfall.com/card/bfz/206/sire-of-stagnation?utm_source=api).

There is no two-card loop. Hideous Laughter is fat-once exile, not infinite.

## Early, mid, and late

**Early.** Land, rock, loot. [Extract](https://scryfall.com/card/ody/85/extract?utm_source=api) can sit in hand until Madcap is down. Keep hands that make `{U}{B}{R}` by turn four.

**Mid.** Deploy Madcap with a Cloak, Stealthsuit, or [Aqueous Form](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/56/aqueous-form?utm_source=api) if the table has pointed removal. Attack. Follow with a small exile (Prisoners, Wand) before the first Tasha's.

**Late.** [Memory Vessel](https://scryfall.com/card/big/13/memory-vessel?utm_source=api) or [Knowledge Pool](https://scryfall.com/card/mbs/111/knowledge-pool?utm_source=api) with leftover mana. Recast Madcap from Takenuma or [Malakir Rebirth](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/111/malakir-rebirth-malakir-mire?utm_source=api) if it died. Aberration only after libraries are already damaged.

## Mulligan

Keep: three lands including two of U/B/R, a two-mana rock or [Wayfarer's Bauble](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/211/wayfarers-bauble?utm_source=api), and either Madcap-enabler density (loot, [Extract](https://scryfall.com/card/ody/85/extract?utm_source=api), Thief) or a draw spell.

Ship: no coloured sources toward `{U}{B}{R}`, all six-drops, or a hand of search-exile with no overlay and no ramp.

After one London mulligan, keep two lands and a rock if the third land is in the six.

## Tutor priorities

This list tutors *their* decks, not this one. When a search-exile resolves with Madcap up:

1. The card that wins or breaks their game plan this turn.
2. A permanent this seat can cast in the window.
3. Interaction that answers the most immediate board.
4. Lands only if this seat still needs a drop.

## Sequencing and rules

- Madcap's play permission lasts until the end of this player's next turn. Plan Tasha's on this turn to spend through next turn.
- Lands from exile still cost the land drop and only on this player's turn.
- Face-down cards are illegal to cast unless another effect lets this player look (Scepter yes).
- [Chaos Wand](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/254/chaos-wand?utm_source=api) and [Wand of Wonder](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/204/wand-of-wonder?utm_source=api) exile every card they mill through; Madcap can play the non-chosen cards too.
- Commander tax on a 3/2 is real. Hold [Imp's Mischief](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/167/imps-mischief?utm_source=api), [Bolt Bend](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/163/bolt-bend?utm_source=api), or [Malakir Rebirth](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/111/malakir-rebirth-malakir-mire?utm_source=api) for the second copy.

## Weaknesses

A 3/2 dies to any removal. Search-exile is blank without Madcap or another overlay. Hideous Laughter into three land-heavy libraries can exile twenty mana of nothing useful. [Knowledge Pool](https://scryfall.com/card/mbs/111/knowledge-pool?utm_source=api) and [Uba Mask](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/272/uba-mask?utm_source=api) are symmetrical if this seat is tapped out. Graveyard-based decks lose less to library exile than combo decks do.
