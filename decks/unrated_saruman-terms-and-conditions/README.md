Esper draw-go control: hold mana for useful interaction, cast a cheap setup spell before a discounted high-mana-value instant, and turn the second spell into opposing graveyard spells. Small nonlegendary clone and spell-copy packages multiply that value. Five dedicated finishing cards convert the same casts, draws, and mills into a win.

The list contains **38 lands, seven two-mana rocks, two creature-based mana engines plus [**Gleaming Splendor**](https://scryfall.com/card/hob/15/gleaming-splendor?utm_source=api), three dedicated clones, and five dedicated finishers**. It uses no extra turns, mass land denial, unrestricted tutors, or permanent fast mana. The list has no Game Changers in the repository’s 2026-09-03 snapshot and remains formally unassessed.

## How the deck works

Land drops and repeatable draw come first. Saruman costs six and improves an existing shell; he is not the opening-hand engine. Usually pass with an answer and an end-step draw option. When a threat warrants a response, a cheap cantrip can be cast above it before the expensive-looking answer, making that answer your second spell. Do not spend protection or pitch two cards merely to trigger Saruman.

Only the **second** spell matters for second-spell abilities. Saruman's stolen spell is normally third or later, so it does not retrigger the same Saruman. It does feed cast-based draw, magecraft, and mill engines. After two casts, prioritize actual interaction rather than forcing further spells for nonexistent second-spell triggers.

## Interaction: wait for the window that matters

- **Stack threats:** [**Arcane Denial**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/147/arcane-denial?utm_source=api) is the inexpensive broad counter; [**Mystic Confluence**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/108/mystic-confluence?utm_source=api) combines a mana-payment counter with bounce or draw. [**Keep Safe**](https://scryfall.com/card/iko/56/keep-safe?utm_source=api) covers spells targeting our permanents. [**Commandeer**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/80/commandeer?utm_source=api) steals noncreature spells and [**Misdirection**](https://scryfall.com/card/ddt/15/misdirection?utm_source=api) changes a single-target spell's target. These are not interchangeable answers: the list has three true counterspells, so preserve broad coverage for a spell that actually threatens to end the game.
- **Protect the engine:** [**Keep Safe**](https://scryfall.com/card/iko/56/keep-safe?utm_source=api) draws while countering a spell that targets your permanent; it cannot stop an activated ability or an untargeted wipe. [**Shelter**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/56/shelter?utm_source=api) protects one creature and replaces itself. [**Snap**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/66/snap?utm_source=api) and [**Soul Partition**](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/26/soul-partition?utm_source=api) can rescue your own creatures/permanents; Partition still makes you pay to recast yours.
- **Combat traps:** [**Aetherize**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/151/aetherize?utm_source=api) bounces attackers; [**Aetherspouts**](https://scryfall.com/card/mic/97/aetherspouts?utm_source=api) sends them to their owners' chosen library positions. [**Sudden Spoiling**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/144/sudden-spoiling?utm_source=api) strips one player's creatures of abilities and makes their base size 0/2. It does not remove counters or destroy creatures by itself. Pair it with blocks or another answer.
- **Change the board's rules:** [**Mirrorweave**](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/162/mirrorweave?utm_source=api) copies a nonlegendary creature until end of turn. Choose a harmless small creature to shrink threats, or a nonlegendary copy of Saruman to make your creature board generate a burst of theft triggers. For that burst, cast [**Mirrorweave**](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/162/mirrorweave?utm_source=api) as your first spell and another spell as your second; casting it second does not retroactively trigger the newly copied abilities. It affects opponents' creatures too. [**Reins of Power**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/734/reins-of-power?utm_source=api) exchanges and untaps your creatures with one opponent's; stealing that player's attacking creatures removes those creatures from combat. Your own engines temporarily belong to them, so plan the exchange's duration.
- **Permanent answers:** [**Abolish**](https://scryfall.com/card/ddf/29/abolish?utm_source=api) removes an artifact or enchantment, potentially by discarding a [**Plains**](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/317/plains?utm_source=api) card. [**Soul Partition**](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/26/soul-partition?utm_source=api) temporarily exiles any nonland permanent. [**Otawara, Soaring City**](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/271/otawara-soaring-city?utm_source=api) is an ability-based bounce option; [**Bojuka Bog**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/363/bojuka-bog?utm_source=api) is a targeted graveyard reset when enabling an opponent is too dangerous.
- **Redirect combat:** [**Illusionist's Gambit**](https://scryfall.com/card/c13/47/illusionists-gambit?utm_source=api) can only be cast during the declare-blockers step on an opponent's turn. It removes and untaps all attackers, then forces them to attack in an additional combat if able, excluding you and your planeswalkers as defenders. It does not permanently remove creatures or reset a nonattacking board. [**Aetherize**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/151/aetherize?utm_source=api) and [**Aetherspouts**](https://scryfall.com/card/mic/97/aetherspouts?utm_source=api) supply the other combat answers.

## Equipment and reactive mana

[**Wizard's Staff**](https://scryfall.com/card/hob/59/wizards-staff?utm_source=api) grants prowess and an additional instance of the equipped creature's triggered abilities. Saruman is a Wizard, so equipping costs one. Alphinaud, Archmage, or a nonlegendary Saruman copy can also benefit. Staff amplifies triggers, not replacement effects, so Bruvac is not a trigger-doubling target. Saruman’s reflexive theft ability also triggers an additional time after each successful mill trigger; choose distinct legal graveyard targets rather than expect two abilities to exile the same card. Resolve first-spell prowess before announcing the second spell when the extra power will matter to a discount.

[**Glamdring, Foe-hammer**](https://scryfall.com/card/hob/174/glamdring-foe-hammer-gleam-of-death?utm_source=api) discounts instants and sorceries by the equipped creature's power. On Saruman, five generic mana can disappear from [**Into the Story**](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/50/into-the-story?utm_source=api) or [**Dig Through Time**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/195/dig-through-time?utm_source=api) while their printed mana values remain seven and eight. Colored pips still have to be paid. Its [**Gleam of Death**](https://scryfall.com/card/hob/174/glamdring-foe-hammer-gleam-of-death?utm_source=api) adventure mills six and puts the instant/sorcery cards among them into your hand; the equipment can later be cast from exile, triggering Vega. It does not itself grant flash.

[**Gleaming Splendor**](https://scryfall.com/card/hob/15/gleaming-splendor?utm_source=api) produces a Treasure whenever an opponent draws their second card each turn. Its `{2}{W}` ability makes two distinct target players each draw one card: usually you and an opponent. On that opponent's turn, their normal draw plus this extra draw can generate a Treasure, partially offsetting the payment. The activation is not a spell and does not advance the second-spell count. Opponents also receive cards, so select the beneficiary deliberately.


## Win conditions

### 1. [**Aligned Heart**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/12/aligned-heart?utm_source=api): accumulate a board while interacting

[**Aligned Heart**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/12/aligned-heart?utm_source=api) creates one prowess Monk on its first trigger, two on its second, then three, and so on. Four second-spell triggers across a rotation create ten tokens in total. This requires actually casting twice on each of those turns; it is not passive production. Surviving Monks attack on your next turn and grow with noncreature spells. Deploy the enchantment in a safe window, then keep mana open to protect the board.

### 2. [**Professor Onyx**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/153/professor-onyx?utm_source=api): drain from spells and copies

[**Professor Onyx**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/153/professor-onyx?utm_source=api) drains each opponent for two whenever you cast or copy an instant or sorcery **spell**. Your hand-cast spells, Gearhulk's graveyard casts, and Saruman's cast copies count. Storm and Taigam copying an instant or sorcery on the stack also count. Saruman copying a **card in exile** does not itself trigger magecraft; casting that copy does.

An Onyx plus a nonlegendary Onyx copy drains four per relevant event. Ten such events remove 40 life from each opponent. That is a resource-limited finish across turns, not an infinite loop. Onyx's plus ability also finds cards; her minus ability buys time against large creatures.

### 3. [**Duskmantle Guildmage**](https://scryfall.com/card/gtc/158/duskmantle-guildmage?utm_source=api): convert a mill burst into life loss

Activate [**Duskmantle Guildmage**](https://scryfall.com/card/gtc/158/duskmantle-guildmage?utm_source=api)'s first ability **before** the mills resolve. For the rest of that turn each card entering an opponent's graveyard makes that player lose one life; a second activation adds a second trigger per card.

With [**Bruvac the Grandiloquent**](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/35/bruvac-the-grandiloquent?utm_source=api) on board, two first-ability activations cost six mana. [**Archive Trap**](https://scryfall.com/card/zen/41/archive-trap?utm_source=api) then mills its target 26 cards and causes 52 life loss if 26 cards reach that graveyard. Trap can cost zero if any opponent searched their library that turn. Choose a target with enough library cards and account for replacement effects. One Trap kills one opponent, not the table; additional mills and Saruman instances can pressure the others. Graveyard replacement effects can prevent the life-loss triggers.

### 4. [**Shark Typhoon**](https://scryfall.com/card/frc/44/shark-typhoon?utm_source=api): large flying threats from discounted spells

[**Shark Typhoon**](https://scryfall.com/card/frc/44/shark-typhoon?utm_source=api) creates a flying Shark equal to the mana value of each noncreature spell you cast. A free [**Commandeer**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/80/commandeer?utm_source=api) creates a 7/7; a discounted [**Into the Story**](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/50/into-the-story?utm_source=api) creates a 7/7; [**Dig Through Time**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/195/dig-through-time?utm_source=api) creates an 8/8. Saruman's cast copies also create Sharks, while ordinary stack copies do not. This converts the same draw-go exchanges into an evasive combat finish rather than requiring a separate creature package.

Cycling is an instant-speed fallback: pay `{X}{1}{U}`, draw a card, and create an X/X flying Shark. Cycling is an activated ability, not casting a spell, so it does not advance the second-spell count. Cast the enchantment when the engine can be protected; cycle it when a blocker or immediate draw is more important.

### 5. [**Bloodchief Ascension**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/139/bloodchief-ascension?utm_source=api): a cheap mill-to-life-loss finish

[**Bloodchief Ascension**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/139/bloodchief-ascension?utm_source=api) needs three quest counters before its graveyard trigger works. An opponent must lose at least two life during a turn for that turn's end step to add a counter. Combat, an opponent's fetch-plus-shock payment, or Onyx's two-life drains can provide that loss. Counters arrive at end step; earlier mills do not retroactively cause life loss.

Once charged, each card entering an opponent's graveyard can make that player lose two life and gain you two. [**Archive Trap**](https://scryfall.com/card/zen/41/archive-trap?utm_source=api) with Bruvac can mill 26 and cause 52 life loss to one opponent without a Guildmage activation payment. Ascension and Guildmage can convert the same Saruman, Tidecaller, and [**Archive Trap**](https://scryfall.com/card/zen/41/archive-trap?utm_source=api) mills into life loss. Both need cards to actually reach opponents’ graveyards; the finish is staged and disruptable rather than a loop.

## Second-spell sequencing

1. **End-step draw:** a cheap instant followed by discounted [**Into the Story**](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/50/into-the-story?utm_source=api) opens a mana-value-seven Saruman trigger. Delve lets [**Dig Through Time**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/195/dig-through-time?utm_source=api) retain mana value eight while costing as little as two blue mana.
2. **Gearhulk first:** cast [**Torrential Gearhulk**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/128/torrential-gearhulk?utm_source=api) as your first spell. Its enter trigger casts a graveyard instant as your second, using that instant's mana value for Saruman. If Gearhulk itself was second, its graveyard spell is third.
3. **Storm before commander:** with [**Storm of Saruman**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/72/storm-of-saruman?utm_source=api) already on board, cast Saruman as your second spell. Storm produces a nonlegendary Saruman token. Neither entering Saruman sees its own cast.
4. **Multiple Sarumans:** each triggers separately. Resolve the first mill-and-theft sequence, then the next; each can choose from the graveyard as it exists at its own selection window. An exiled card cannot be selected again from that graveyard.
5. **Taigam:** when it and other engines trigger on your second spell, resolve Saruman's theft sequences and Storm's copy before Taigam exiles the original. The stack copy resolves normally; the original returns through suspend later. Legendary permanent copies still obey the legend rule unless an effect explicitly changes that.
6. **[**Spelltwine**](https://scryfall.com/card/c17/94/spelltwine?utm_source=api):** choose an instant/sorcery in your graveyard and one in an opponent’s graveyard. Both targets are required to cast it. Its copied cards are cast from exile, feeding Vega and instant/sorcery cast payoffs. A suitable draw or mill spell makes this a value-to-finisher bridge.
7. **Cast versus copy:** Vega draws for a Saruman copy that you cast outside your hand. She does not draw for a Storm stack copy. [**Archmage Emeritus**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/188/archmage-emeritus?utm_source=api) and Onyx see both instant/sorcery casts and stack copies.
8. **Mill enhancers:** Tidecaller's ten-card mode checks **mana spent**, not mana value; a free high-value spell mills three before enhancers. When Bruvac and Water Crystal both apply, the affected opponent chooses their order: a two-card mill becomes eight or twelve, not automatically twelve. Multiple Bruvacs multiply; opponents still order applicable replacements.
9. **Split second:** second-spell triggers can trigger from casting [**Sudden Spoiling**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/144/sudden-spoiling?utm_source=api), but while it remains on the stack split second prevents casting Saruman's copied spells. Do not rely on Spoiling as your second spell to obtain a free theft cast; use it first when practical or accept the mill-only value.

## Early, mid, and late game

- **Early:** prioritize blue access, land drops, a two-mana rock or Lotho, and a draw engine. Keep a cheap answer when the board demands it. Use cantrips to find land rather than waiting for Saruman.
- **Mid:** deploy one engine at a time. [**Scout's Warning**](https://scryfall.com/card/fut/16/scouts-warning?utm_source=api) can make Saruman or a clone the second spell at instant speed; [**Borne Upon a Wind**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/44/borne-upon-a-wind?utm_source=api) opens an entire turn for otherwise sorcery-speed development. Casting Saruman with no answer left is a deliberate risk, not the default.
- **Late:** multiply the engine that matters: Saruman for theft choices, Alphinaud for draw, or Onyx for drain. Count the cards and mana required to close before spending another turn on value.

## Mulligans

Keep three or four lands with workable blue access and either early mana, a draw engine, or cheap selection plus interaction. Two lands need cheap selection and a credible route to the third land; do not count a conditional free answer as mana. Ship one-land hands, landless hands, and expensive engines with no development. Bottom late finishers and redundant clones before the early draw/mana package.

Fetch basic [**Island**](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/319/island?utm_source=api) when fixing permits it. Fetch a [**Swamp**](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api)-typed dual to enable [**Snuff Out**](https://scryfall.com/card/gvl/53/snuff-out?utm_source=api); retain an actual [**Plains**](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/317/plains?utm_source=api) card in hand when [**Abolish**](https://scryfall.com/card/ddf/29/abolish?utm_source=api)'s alternate cost matters. Source quality matters more than maximizing the number of turn-one plays.

## Mana and recovery

The mana base favors untapped fixing and blue. Nine conventional fetch lands find the typed Esper duals, while [**Prismatic Vista**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/244/prismatic-vista?utm_source=api) finds basics. [**Raffine's Tower**](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/254/raffines-tower?utm_source=api) is a typed early fixing/cycling slot. [**Mystic Sanctuary**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/388/mystic-sanctuary?utm_source=api) trades tempo for instant/sorcery recovery once three other Islands are established; count actual [**Island**](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/319/island?utm_source=api) types, not every blue source. [**Bojuka Bog**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/363/bojuka-bog?utm_source=api) supplies necessary graveyard disruption. These three slots pay tempo for specific functions. Check lands can also enter tapped until a qualifying basic land type is established.

Lavinia is not two mana immediately after entering: summoning sickness applies to her tap ability. Signets consume one input mana and produce two, adding **one net mana**. Pitch spells trade cards for mana; [**Dig Through Time**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/195/dig-through-time?utm_source=api) and [**Spelltwine**](https://scryfall.com/card/c17/94/spelltwine?utm_source=api) compete for graveyard cards. Do not delve away the instant you plan to recover.

When Saruman is removed, continue with independent draw, second-spell payoffs, and finishers rather than immediately paying eight or ten mana. Resolve targeted theft before clearing graveyards unless an opposing recursion threat demands the reset. Nonlegendary Saruman copies can carry the engine while the original remains in the command zone.

## Weaknesses and what to protect

The deck needs actual cards and mana to cast twice across a rotation. Pitch spells and mandatory trigger greed can empty the hand. Mill supplies opponents as well as Saruman; protect against recursion and avoid helping a graveyard deck without a reason. Graveyard hate suppresses theft and Gearhulk but does not shut off Heart, Onyx, or the independent draw engines.

Untargeted enchantment removal can dismantle the draw/mill finish; [**Keep Safe**](https://scryfall.com/card/iko/56/keep-safe?utm_source=api) cannot stop it. The list's broad noncreature answers are mainly on the stack, [**Abolish**](https://scryfall.com/card/ddf/29/abolish?utm_source=api), and temporary exile/bounce, so save those for the permanent that invalidates the plan. Creature-heavy boards can demand an instant reset before committing another six-mana engine.
