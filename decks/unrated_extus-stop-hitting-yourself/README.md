Creature-go Mardu aikido. The battlefield advertises a collection of bad blocks, sacrificial traps, and damage reflectors; the instant suite turns each creature that dies into another card through [**Extus, Oriq Overlord**](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api). The deck runs no card tutors and no Game Changers. [**Awaken the Blood Avatar**](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) is deliberately treated as blank.

**Deck size:** 100 cards<br>
**Primary plan:** force or invite attacks, then route the supplied damage back through creatures and reflective spells<br>
**Backup plan:** manufacture the damage with [**Volcano Hellion**](https://scryfall.com/card/plc/111/volcano-hellion?utm_source=api), [**Crypt Rats**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/84/crypt-rats?utm_source=api), or [**Spontaneous Combustion**](https://scryfall.com/card/cns/194/spontaneous-combustion?utm_source=api)

## How the deck works

The deck does not hide behind a pillow fort. It puts creatures on the battlefield that opponents can see and bargain around. [**Maddening Imp**](https://scryfall.com/card/tmp/143/maddening-imp?utm_source=api), [**Nettling Imp**](https://scryfall.com/card/sum/119/nettling-imp?utm_source=api), [**Norritt**](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/155/norritt?utm_source=api), and [**Rage Nimbus**](https://scryfall.com/card/roe/160/rage-nimbus?utm_source=api) nominate attackers. [**Goblin Spymaster**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/795/goblin-spymaster?utm_source=api), [**Life of the Party**](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/48/life-of-the-party?utm_source=api), [**Rite of the Raging Storm**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/159/rite-of-the-raging-storm?utm_source=api), and [**Angel's Trumpet**](https://scryfall.com/card/ulg/121/angels-trumpet?utm_source=api) make sure combat exists even when the table would prefer not to supply it.

The defense is made of creatures rather than counters. [**Mine Bearer**](https://scryfall.com/card/pcy/16/mine-bearer?utm_source=api), [**Resistance Fighter**](https://scryfall.com/card/6ed/38/resistance-fighter?utm_source=api), [**Kami of False Hope**](https://scryfall.com/card/bok/10/kami-of-false-hope?utm_source=api), and [**Children of Korlis**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/14/children-of-korlis?utm_source=api) sacrifice themselves. [**Spurnmage Advocate**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmd/33/spurnmage-advocate?utm_source=api) and [**Shieldmage Advocate**](https://scryfall.com/card/jud/22/shieldmage-advocate?utm_source=api) offer opponents cards in exchange for survival. [**Standard Bearer**](https://scryfall.com/card/apc/18/standard-bearer?utm_source=api), [**Muck Drubb**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/126/muck-drubb?utm_source=api), and [**Frontline Strategist**](https://scryfall.com/card/scg/15/frontline-strategist?utm_source=api) make targeted interaction and combat math unreliable.

With [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) in play, an instant or sorcery retrieves one nonlegendary creature from the graveyard. The spells with creature costs are the cleanest engine: sacrifice a creature to [**Worthy Cause**](https://scryfall.com/card/tmp/53/worthy-cause?utm_source=api), [**Burnt Offering**](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/116/burnt-offering?utm_source=api), [**Skulltap**](https://scryfall.com/card/scg/73/skulltap?utm_source=api), or [**Spontaneous Combustion**](https://scryfall.com/card/cns/194/spontaneous-combustion?utm_source=api); the sacrificed creature is already in the graveyard when magecraft chooses a target, so it comes straight back to hand. [**Thrill of Possibility**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/210/thrill-of-possibility?utm_source=api) does the same with a discarded creature.

## Main contraptions

### Sacrifice, trigger, retrieve

Have [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) and any nonlegendary creature in play. Cast [**Skulltap**](https://scryfall.com/card/scg/73/skulltap?utm_source=api), sacrificing that creature as the additional cost. After the spell is cast, [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) triggers and can target the creature now in the graveyard. Resolve magecraft to return it, then resolve [Skulltap](https://scryfall.com/card/scg/73/skulltap?utm_source=api) to draw two. [**Worthy Cause**](https://scryfall.com/card/tmp/53/worthy-cause?utm_source=api), [**Burnt Offering**](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/116/burnt-offering?utm_source=api), and [**Spontaneous Combustion**](https://scryfall.com/card/cns/194/spontaneous-combustion?utm_source=api) follow the same timing.

[**Body Snatcher**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/75/body-snatcher?utm_source=api) makes the line stranger. Sacrifice it to one of those spells, put its dies trigger on the stack first and [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api)'s trigger above it, then return [Body Snatcher](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/75/body-snatcher?utm_source=api) to hand. Its dies trigger can still return another target creature card from the graveyard to the battlefield.

### Route the punch

Prepare [**Shaman en-Kor**](https://scryfall.com/card/sth/15/shaman-en-kor?utm_source=api) by activating its `{0}` ability once for each point expected, targeting [**Mogg Maniac**](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/STH-94/mogg-maniac?utm_source=api), [**Spitemare**](https://scryfall.com/card/ddh/16/spitemare?utm_source=api), or [**Stuffy Doll**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/875/stuffy-doll?utm_source=api). Its `{1}{W}` ability can route damage aimed at another creature into the Shaman; the prepared one-point shields then send that damage onward into the reflector. [**Martyrdom**](https://scryfall.com/card/all/10a/martyrdom?utm_source=api) temporarily gives the same zero-cost routing ability to any creature.

### Copy the paperwork

Casting [**Fork**](https://scryfall.com/card/30a/445/fork?utm_source=api) triggers [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) once. When [Fork](https://scryfall.com/card/30a/445/fork?utm_source=api) resolves and copies another spell, the created copy triggers [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) again. [**Dualcaster Mage**](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/165/dualcaster-mage?utm_source=api) produces one magecraft trigger from its copy. Conspiring [**Traitor's Roar**](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/200/traitors-roar?utm_source=api) produces one trigger for the cast and one for the conspire copy; target two different untapped creatures because the first resolving Roar taps its target.

## Win conditions

1. **An opponent's enormous creature.** [**Mirror Strike**](https://scryfall.com/card/pcy/17/mirror-strike?utm_source=api), [**Reflect Damage**](https://scryfall.com/card/mir/277/reflect-damage?utm_source=api), [**Delirium**](https://scryfall.com/card/mir/260/delirium?utm_source=api), [**Backlash**](https://scryfall.com/card/inv/234/backlash?utm_source=api), [**Deflecting Palm**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/205/deflecting-palm?utm_source=api), and [**Boros Fury-Shield**](https://scryfall.com/card/rav/5/boros-fury-shield?utm_source=api) all turn somebody else's power into the kill.
2. **Reflector plus manufactured damage.** Aim [**Volcano Hellion**](https://scryfall.com/card/plc/111/volcano-hellion?utm_source=api) at [Stuffy Doll](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/875/stuffy-doll?utm_source=api) or another reflector. [**Crypt Rats**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/84/crypt-rats?utm_source=api) and [**Spontaneous Combustion**](https://scryfall.com/card/cns/194/spontaneous-combustion?utm_source=api) spread smaller damage over several reflectors.
3. **[Repercussion](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/95/repercussion?utm_source=api) mathematics.** With [**Repercussion**](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/95/repercussion?utm_source=api) in play, every creature hit by [Crypt Rats](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/84/crypt-rats?utm_source=api) or [Spontaneous Combustion](https://scryfall.com/card/cns/194/spontaneous-combustion?utm_source=api) sends that much damage to its controller. Count our own creatures before pulling the lever.
4. **Stuffy conscience.** Enchant [Stuffy Doll](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/875/stuffy-doll?utm_source=api) with [**Guilty Conscience**](https://scryfall.com/card/scg/17/guilty-conscience?utm_source=api), then tap [Stuffy Doll](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/875/stuffy-doll?utm_source=api) to deal 1 to itself. Stuffy damages the chosen player, [Guilty Conscience](https://scryfall.com/card/scg/17/guilty-conscience?utm_source=api) damages Stuffy again, and the mandatory chain repeats until that opponent leaves the game or the chain is interrupted.
5. **The wide attack.** [**Inkshield**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/316/inkshield?utm_source=api) turns combat damage into flying attackers; [Batwing Brume](https://scryfall.com/card/arc/80/batwing-brume?utm_source=api) can simply drain a player who sends enough creatures.

## Early, mid, and late game

**Turns 1–3.** Develop colored mana and play one visible trap or provocateur. The deck would rather show [**Resistance Fighter**](https://scryfall.com/card/6ed/38/resistance-fighter?utm_source=api) than spend a premium instant on a small attack. Do not deploy a reflector without a reason to expect damage.

**Turns 4–7.** Cast [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) when at least one instant or sorcery is available and a useful creature can reach the graveyard. Start forcing combat between opponents, trade disposable creatures, then use magecraft to recover the best one. Keep deals explicit: the Advocates can save any player, while [**Duelist's Heritage**](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/153/duelists-heritage?utm_source=api) can make any player's attacker lethal.

**Turn 8 onward.** Assemble whichever unsafe machine the draw supplied. The deck has no deterministic search path, so the table must read the actual permanents: damage routing, [Repercussion](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/95/repercussion?utm_source=api), Stuffy Conscience, or an [Inkshield](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/316/inkshield?utm_source=api) counterattack. The correct finish changes every game.

## Mulligan guide

Keep three lands with access to black and white, plus two useful early plays. An ideal hand has one forced-combat creature or disposable defender and one cheap instant or sorcery.

- Keep [**Humble Defector**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/170/humble-defector?utm_source=api) with a sacrifice spell: activate it, sacrifice it while the draw ability is on the stack, retrieve it with [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api), then draw without donating it.
- Keep a reflector only when the hand can route or manufacture damage.
- Ship hands that are only five-mana reactions. The deck needs creatures in play before its expensive tricks become credible.
- Ship two-land hands without another draw effect. [Burnt Offering](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/116/burnt-offering?utm_source=api) is not stable ramp.

## Search priorities

There are no card tutors and no land-search spells. Scry decisions prioritize:

1. the missing colors for [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api);
2. a cheap creature that can trade or sacrifice itself;
3. an instant or sorcery that triggers magecraft;
4. a reflector only after damage routing or a damage source is available.

## Important sequencing and rules

- A creature sacrificed or discarded as an additional cost is already in the graveyard when [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api)'s magecraft trigger is put on the stack, so that creature is a legal target.
- [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) returns creatures to hand, not to the battlefield. Budget mana for redeployment.
- [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api)'s trigger is mandatory when a legal nonlegendary creature card exists in the graveyard.
- [**Volcano Hellion**](https://scryfall.com/card/plc/111/volcano-hellion?utm_source=api) deals the chosen amount to both us and its target, and that damage cannot be prevented. Stay above the chosen amount; delayed life gain cannot save a player from lethal damage.
- [**Eye for an Eye**](https://scryfall.com/card/5ed/30/eye-for-an-eye?utm_source=api) does not prevent damage. Both players can lose.
- [**Coalhauler Swine**](https://scryfall.com/card/rav/119/coalhauler-swine?utm_source=api) damages every player, including us. [**Soul Link**](https://scryfall.com/card/apc/120/soul-link?utm_source=api) triggers after damage and cannot rescue anyone already at lethal.
- Attack requirements never force a player to pay a cost such as a tax. If an attack requires a payment, that creature may stay home.
- [Awaken the Blood Avatar](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) is not a hidden backup plan. Leave the card front-face up in the command zone.

## Weaknesses

The deck is intentionally inefficient. It has little conventional ramp, only a few true draw effects, and needs both creatures and spells for [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) to generate cards. Graveyard exile interrupts retrieval; exile removal bypasses most creature recycling; enchantment removal disconnects the loudest finishers.

Protect [Extus](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/149/extus-oriq-overlord-awaken-the-blood-avatar?utm_source=api) only when a magecraft turn is ready. Preserve [**Shaman en-Kor**](https://scryfall.com/card/sth/15/shaman-en-kor?utm_source=api) when it is the only routing piece, and avoid exposing [Repercussion](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/95/repercussion?utm_source=api) until its damage can matter immediately. Most importantly, count every player and every creature before resolving a table-wide damage event.
