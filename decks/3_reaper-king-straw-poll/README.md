## How the deck works

The commander's cost is the first thing to understand. [**Reaper King**](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/260/reaper-king?utm_source=api) is `{2/W}{2/U}{2/B}{2/R}{2/G}`. With all five colours available he costs five mana; after two removals he costs nine. Thirty-six lands, twelve ramp cards, the five typed triomes, and [**The World Tree**](https://scryfall.com/card/khm/275/the-world-tree?utm_source=api) make the first cast reliable and later casts possible.

Do not cast him into open removal and pass. First establish [**Lightning Greaves**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/202/lightning-greaves?utm_source=api), [**Swiftfoot Boots**](https://scryfall.com/card/mbc/77/swiftfoot-boots?utm_source=api), or enough mana to hold [**Tamiyo's Safekeeping**](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/211/tamiyos-safekeeping?utm_source=api), [**Rebuff the Wicked**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/35/rebuff-the-wicked?utm_source=api), [**Heroic Intervention**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/295/heroic-intervention?utm_source=api), or [**Deflecting Swat**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/214/deflecting-swat?utm_source=api). Then resolve him and cast a cheap Scarecrow in the same turn. A one-mana [**Heap Doll**](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/228/heap-doll?utm_source=api) is now a one-mana Vindicate before the table untaps.

The engine has enough native density without fragile type-hack enchantments:

| Layer | Cards | What it gives you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Printed Scarecrows | Thirteen in the 99, including [**Heap Doll**](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/228/heap-doll?utm_source=api), [**Scarecrone**](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/482/scarecrone?utm_source=api), and [**Grim Poppet**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/142/grim-poppet?utm_source=api) | Triggers with no setup |
| Changeling creatures | [**Universal Automaton**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/235/universal-automaton?utm_source=api), [**Changeling Outcast**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/128/changeling-outcast?utm_source=api), [**Mirror Entity**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/83/mirror-entity?utm_source=api), and seven more | Ten additional Scarecrows with useful bodies |
| Artifact engines | [**Jhoira, Weatherlight Captain**](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/118/jhoira-weatherlight-captain?utm_source=api), [**Mystic Forge**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/964/mystic-forge?utm_source=api), [**Vedalken Archmage**](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/187/vedalken-archmage?utm_source=api), and [**Thought Monitor**](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/79/thought-monitor?utm_source=api) | Keep playing when the King is absent |

[**Maskwood Nexus**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/865/maskwood-nexus?utm_source=api) remains as the one type changer because it is an artifact, makes its own bodies, and works from hand, library, and graveyard. [**Panharmonicon**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/310/panharmonicon?utm_source=api) and [**Elesh Norn, Mother of Machines**](https://scryfall.com/card/one/10/elesh-norn-mother-of-machines?utm_source=api) each add one extra King trigger; both together produce three, not four.

## Combos

[**Pili-Pala**](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/258/pili-pala?utm_source=api) plus [**Grand Architect**](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/53/grand-architect?utm_source=api) is the deck's only true infinite. Spend `{U}` on Architect's ability to make [Pili-Pala](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/258/pili-pala?utm_source=api) blue. Tap [Pili-Pala](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/258/pili-pala?utm_source=api) as an untapped blue creature for `{C}{C}`, then pay `{2}` and `{Q}` to untap [Pili-Pala](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/258/pili-pala?utm_source=api) and add one mana of any colour. Architect's mana is restricted to artifact spells and artifact abilities, and [Pili-Pala](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/258/pili-pala?utm_source=api) is an artifact, so it legally pays its own cost. Each lap nets one unrestricted coloured mana and returns to the starting state. [**Painter's Servant**](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/257/painters-servant?utm_source=api) naming blue, or [**Scuttlemutt**](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/484/scuttlemutt?utm_source=api)'s recolour both cover the `{U}` if blue is awkward.

[**Cauldron of Souls**](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/180/cauldron-of-souls?utm_source=api) is the board-wipe insurance. It grants persist until end of turn; each creature can return once from that grant. Returned Scarecrows re-enter and trigger the King, but this is a recovery wave, not a loop.

## Win conditions

1. [**Mirror Entity**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/83/mirror-entity?utm_source=api) with infinite mana. Set the team to an arbitrary size and swing. This is the clean one-turn kill.
2. [**Reckless Fireweaver**](https://scryfall.com/card/kld/126/reckless-fireweaver?utm_source=api) as a clock. Every artifact that enters pings each opponent, and this deck enters a lot of artifacts.
3. Combat under the anthem. [Reaper King](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/260/reaper-king?utm_source=api) is a 6/6 who makes every other Scarecrow bigger, and by the mid game the board is wide.

## Playing it

**Early.** Ramp and fix first. [**Farseek**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/173/farseek?utm_source=api) can find any typed triome; [**Nature's Lore**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/177/natures-lore?utm_source=api) finds the three with [Forest](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/325/forest?utm_source=api). Develop [**Herald's Horn**](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/304/heralds-horn?utm_source=api), [**Mystic Forge**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/964/mystic-forge?utm_source=api), or protection before exposing the King. Cheap Scarecrows are better held when they can enter after him.

**Mid.** The ideal turn is protection permanent, [Reaper King](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/260/reaper-king?utm_source=api), then a one-mana Scarecrow. If you only have an instant shield, keep its colour open and represent it. [**Deflecting Swat**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/214/deflecting-swat?utm_source=api) is free while the King is in play, but [**Boros Charm**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/721/boros-charm?utm_source=api) and [**Mithril Coat**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/245/mithril-coat?utm_source=api) stop destroy effects rather than exile or bounce.

**After removal.** One removal makes the King cost seven; two make him cost nine. [**Command Beacon**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/352/command-beacon?utm_source=api) gives one tax-free cast from hand, while [**Plaza of Heroes**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/255/plaza-of-heroes?utm_source=api) protects him without occupying a spell slot. Do not recast at nine just to pass. Continue through Jhoira, Forge, Archmage, Discovery, or the Architect combo until one turn can produce the King and immediate triggers.

**Mulligan.** Keep three lands or two lands plus two-mana ramp, access to at least three colours, and either draw or protection. A cheap Scarecrow is a bonus, not a reason to keep a mana-poor hand. A five-colour hand without green is acceptable if it has [**Arcane Signet**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/192/arcane-signet?utm_source=api) or [**Chromatic Lantern**](https://scryfall.com/card/mbc/73/chromatic-lantern?utm_source=api).

**Tutor.** [**Inventors' Fair**](https://scryfall.com/card/kld/247/inventors-fair?utm_source=api) usually finds [**Lightning Greaves**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/202/lightning-greaves?utm_source=api) before the King, [**Cauldron of Souls**](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/180/cauldron-of-souls?utm_source=api) against a wrath, [**Maskwood Nexus**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/865/maskwood-nexus?utm_source=api) when non-Scarecrow creatures are already in hand, or the missing half of [Grand Architect](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/53/grand-architect?utm_source=api) plus [Pili-Pala](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/258/pili-pala?utm_source=api).

## Sequencing and rules notes

- [Reaper King](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/260/reaper-king?utm_source=api)'s mana value stays 10 no matter how you pay for him, which is what [**One with the Machine**](https://scryfall.com/card/m19/66/one-with-the-machine?utm_source=api) reads. With him on the battlefield it draws ten.
- [**Cavern of Souls**](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/269/cavern-of-souls?utm_source=api) naming Scarecrow makes the commander uncounterable. [**Unclaimed Territory**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/275/unclaimed-territory?utm_source=api), [**Secluded Courtyard**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/265/secluded-courtyard?utm_source=api), and [**Plaza of Heroes**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/255/plaza-of-heroes?utm_source=api) also cast him.
- [**Lightning Greaves**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/202/lightning-greaves?utm_source=api) gives shroud, including from your own spells. Move it off the King before targeting him with [**Mithril Coat**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/245/mithril-coat?utm_source=api), [**Tamiyo's Safekeeping**](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/211/tamiyos-safekeeping?utm_source=api), or [**Slip Out the Back**](https://scryfall.com/card/clu/99/slip-out-the-back?utm_source=api).
- Phasing the King with Slip keeps attached Equipment phased with him. While phased out, new Scarecrows do not see him and produce no destroy triggers.
- [**Deflecting Swat**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/214/deflecting-swat?utm_source=api) can redirect a targeted exile spell while the King is present; it does not counter a nontargeted board wipe.
- [**Sculpting Steel**](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/374/sculpting-steel?utm_source=api) copying [Reaper King](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/260/reaper-king?utm_source=api) triggers the original before the legend rule kills one of them, so it is a three-mana Vindicate in a pinch.
- [**Kindred Discovery**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/150/kindred-discovery?utm_source=api) naming Scarecrow draws on each Scarecrow entering and attacking. It is not optional; watch the library if a large loop develops.

## Weaknesses

The deck remains commander-dependent for permanent destruction. The improvement is that eleven draw engines and the Architect combo still function while the King is absent; the deck no longer becomes a hand of type-hack enchantments and mana-neutral filters.

Exile and bounce still beat [**Mithril Coat**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/245/mithril-coat?utm_source=api), [**Boros Charm**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/721/boros-charm?utm_source=api), and [**Cauldron of Souls**](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/180/cauldron-of-souls?utm_source=api). Use Greaves, Boots, Safekeeping, Intervention, Rebuff, Swat, Slip, or Plaza for those.

The five triomes enter tapped, and the filter-land suite can still produce awkward openers. [**Farseek**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/173/farseek?utm_source=api) and [**Nature's Lore**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/177/natures-lore?utm_source=api) are the cleanest fixes; mulligan hands whose first two lands cannot cast either ramp or draw.

There is still no dense tutor package and no stack-control suite. The deck is built to survive fair removal and wraths, not to race or police a fast combo table.
