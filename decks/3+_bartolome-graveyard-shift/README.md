# Bartolomé — Graveyard Shift

> Bracket 3+ combo deck. Usually threatens a win around turn six, but has an unlikely turn-four line.

An Orzhov graveyard-combo deck built around Bartolomé's free sacrifice outlet, with the Ozox/Jumblebones rotation and Aura-recursion loops serving as overlapping engines.

**Commander:** [Bartolomé del Presidio](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/224/bartolom%C3%A9-del-presidio)  
**Deck size:** 100 cards  
**Primary plan:** assemble one of several repeatable death loops  
**Backup plan:** grind with recursive creatures, self-mill, reanimation, and a growing Bartolomé

## The two engines

### Ozox rotation

1. Sacrifice Ozox to Bartolomé.
2. Ozox creates Jumblebones.
3. Sacrifice Jumblebones to Bartolomé.
4. Jumblebones returns Ozox from the graveyard to your hand.
5. Recast Ozox and repeat when the mana engine allows it.

Bartolomé supplies the free outlet, so this package concentrates on paying for repeated Ozox casts.

### Aura-recursion loop

1. Attach a return Aura to [Sun Titan](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/178/sun-titan?utm_source=api) or another compatible recursion creature.
2. Sacrifice that creature to Bartolomé.
3. The Aura returns the creature.
4. Its enter-the-battlefield ability recovers the Aura.
5. Repeat.

[Angelic Renewal](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/10/angelic-renewal?utm_source=api) can fill the same role when the returning creature can immediately recover it.

## Main wins

### [Bontu's Monument](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/179/bontus-monument?utm_source=api) + [Phyrexian Altar](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/311/phyrexian-altar?utm_source=api) + Ozox

[Bontu's Monument](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/179/bontus-monument?utm_source=api) reduces Ozox to {1}{B}. Sacrificing Ozox and Jumblebones to [Phyrexian Altar](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/311/phyrexian-altar?utm_source=api) produces the two mana needed to recast Ozox. Every cast drains the table through [Bontu's Monument](https://scryfall.com/card/dmc/179/bontus-monument?utm_source=api).

Bartolomé is not required for this line, which gives the deck a combo that survives commander removal.

### [Ashnod's Altar](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/368/ashnods-altar?utm_source=api) + [Bog Initiate](https://scryfall.com/card/inv/95/bog-initiate?utm_source=api) + Ozox

Sacrificing Ozox and Jumblebones produces four colorless mana. [Bog Initiate](https://scryfall.com/card/inv/95/bog-initiate?utm_source=api) converts one into black; recasting Ozox costs {2}{B}, leaving one additional colorless mana each cycle.

This produces unlimited colorless mana and unlimited death triggers. A death payoff converts the loop into a win.

### Ozox + [Phyrexian Altar](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/311/phyrexian-altar?utm_source=api) + [Ratadrabik of Urborg](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/213/ratadrabik-of-urborg?utm_source=api)

1. Sacrifice Ozox to [Phyrexian Altar](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/311/phyrexian-altar?utm_source=api).
2. Ozox creates legendary Jumblebones, while [Ratadrabik of Urborg](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/213/ratadrabik-of-urborg?utm_source=api) creates a nonlegendary 2/2 Zombie copy of Ozox.
3. Sacrifice Jumblebones and the Ozox copy to [Phyrexian Altar](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/311/phyrexian-altar?utm_source=api).
4. Jumblebones returns the original Ozox to hand; [Ratadrabik of Urborg](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/213/ratadrabik-of-urborg?utm_source=api) copies Jumblebones; the Ozox copy creates a new legendary Jumblebones.
5. Spend the three generated mana to recast Ozox.

Each cycle restores the starting pieces and adds a nonlegendary Jumblebones copy. Repeating produces unlimited tokens, death and enter triggers, storm count, and ultimately unlimited mana. A death payoff wins immediately.

### [Leonin Relic-Warder](https://scryfall.com/card/c17/65/leonin-relic-warder?utm_source=api) + [Animate Dead](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/207/animate-dead?utm_source=api)

1. Cast [Animate Dead](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/207/animate-dead?utm_source=api) targeting [Leonin Relic-Warder](https://scryfall.com/card/c17/65/leonin-relic-warder?utm_source=api) in the graveyard.
2. Leonin enters and exiles [Animate Dead](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/207/animate-dead?utm_source=api).
3. [Animate Dead](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/207/animate-dead?utm_source=api) leaving makes you sacrifice Leonin.
4. Leonin leaving returns [Animate Dead](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/207/animate-dead?utm_source=api), which returns Leonin again.

Repeat for unlimited enter, leave, sacrifice, and death triggers. Begin this mandatory loop only with a payoff or another legal choice that can stop it.

### Bartolomé + [Enduring Renewal](https://scryfall.com/card/tsb/7/enduring-renewal?utm_source=api) + [Greedy Freebooter](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/109/greedy-freebooter?utm_source=api)

1. Sacrifice [Greedy Freebooter](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/109/greedy-freebooter?utm_source=api) to Bartolomé.
2. Freebooter creates a Treasure and scries 1; [Enduring Renewal](https://scryfall.com/card/tsb/7/enduring-renewal?utm_source=api) returns it to hand.
3. Sacrifice the Treasure for {B} and recast Freebooter.
4. Repeat.

The loop is mana-neutral and produces unlimited death, enter, sacrifice, scry, and storm triggers while putting unlimited counters on Bartolomé. A death payoff converts it into a win.

### Recursion creature + return effect + Bartolomé

[Sun Titan](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/178/sun-titan?utm_source=api) is the clearest example:

1. Enchant [Sun Titan](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/178/sun-titan?utm_source=api) with [Fungal Fortitude](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/106/fungal-fortitude?utm_source=api), [Kaya's Ghostform](https://scryfall.com/card/war/94/kayas-ghostform?utm_source=api), [Minion's Return](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/104/minions-return?utm_source=api), or [Necrogen Communion](https://scryfall.com/card/one/99/necrogen-communion?utm_source=api).
2. Sacrifice [Sun Titan](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/178/sun-titan?utm_source=api) to Bartolomé.
3. Return [Sun Titan](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/178/sun-titan?utm_source=api) through the Aura.
4. Return the Aura through [Sun Titan](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/178/sun-titan?utm_source=api).
5. Repeat with a death payoff.

## Playing the deck

### Early game

- Develop both colors and a mana rock.
- Use self-mill to stock the graveyard.
- Cast Bartolomé once there is something useful to sacrifice.
- Keep a payoff concealed until an engine is close.

### Midgame

- Follow whichever engine your opening pieces support.
- Use Ozox as renewable fodder even when the rotation is not mana-neutral.
- Recur removal creatures and utility permanents.
- Tutor for the missing role rather than forcing one particular combo.

### Endgame

- Resolve a death payoff.
- Demonstrate one complete loop.
- If graveyard hate prevents a combo, grow Bartolomé and use [Rogue's Passage](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/212/rogues-passage?utm_source=api).
- [Awakening Hall](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/100/funeral-room-awakening-hall?utm_source=api) can rebuild the board after a failed attempt.

## Mulligan guide

Keep hands with:

- two or three lands and access to both colors;
- ramp or a cost reducer;
- self-mill, draw, or a tutor;
- at least one engine piece.

Avoid hands containing several expensive recursion creatures without ramp or graveyard setup.

## Important sequencing

- Sacrifice Ozox before Jumblebones so Ozox is present in the graveyard when Jumblebones leaves.
- Commander tax does not apply when Jumblebones returns Ozox to your hand.
- Protect the mana engine or recursion creature before protecting Bartolomé.
- [**Elas il-Kor**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/198/elas-il-kor-sadistic-pilgrim?utm_source=api) pays off creatures dying; [**Corpse Knight**](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/321/corpse-knight?utm_source=api) pays off creatures entering. Most loops trigger both sides.
- [**Lurrus**](https://scryfall.com/card/iko/226/lurrus-of-the-dream-den?utm_source=api) casts only one permanent spell from the graveyard during each of your turns and cannot play lands from the graveyard.
- Once [**Syr Vondam**](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/231/syr-vondam-sunstar-exemplar?utm_source=api) has reached 4 power, sacrificing it to Bartolomé turns it into a removal spell and makes opponents think twice about removing it themselves.
- [Corpse Dance](https://scryfall.com/card/tpr/89/corpse-dance?utm_source=api) returns only the top creature card of your graveyard. Preserve graveyard order, and sacrifice the returned creature before the next end step so it goes back to the graveyard instead of being exiled.
- The [Animate Dead](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/207/animate-dead?utm_source=api) and [Leonin Relic-Warder](https://scryfall.com/card/c17/65/leonin-relic-warder?utm_source=api) loop can be mandatory; establish a payoff or an exit before starting it.
- [Enduring Renewal](https://scryfall.com/card/tsb/7/enduring-renewal?utm_source=api) reveals your hand and puts creature cards you would draw into your graveyard.
- Tokens die before ceasing to exist, so [Bastion of Remembrance](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/160/bastion-of-remembrance?utm_source=api) and the other listed death payoffs trigger from Jumblebones tokens.
- Mandatory draw engines such as [Dark Prophecy](https://scryfall.com/card/m14/93/dark-prophecy?utm_source=api) can empty the library during an unlimited loop.
- Graveyard exile is the deck's most important threat; reserve flexible removal for it.

## Deck identity

Graveyard Shift is a Bartolomé graveyard-combo deck with two overlapping engines. Ozox provides renewable bodies and compact loops, while the Aura-recursion package repeatedly returns useful creatures. Most support cards contribute to both plans, so the deck can pivot naturally between setup, attrition, and a combo finish.
