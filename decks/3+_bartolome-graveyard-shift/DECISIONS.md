# Bartolomé — Graveyard Shift: Decision Log

This log records why every card entered the list. Future changes should append a dated entry identifying the card added, the card removed, and the reason.

## 2026-08-23 — Backfill

### Commander and engines

- **Bartolomé del Presidio** — Free sacrifice outlet in the command zone; every loop is built around feeding him without paying extra mana.
- **Ozox, the Clattering King** — Partner engine: sacrifice Ozox and Jumblebones to Bartolomé, recast Ozox, and convert death triggers into a win.
- **Phyrexian Altar** — Colored mana from the Ozox rotation so the loop survives even if Bartolomé is removed.
- **Ashnod's Altar** — Four colorless per Ozox cycle; pairs with Bog Initiate to recast Ozox.
- **Bog Initiate** — Turns Ashnod mana into black for the Ozox recast.
- **Bontu's Monument** — Makes Ozox cost {1}{B} and drains the table on every recast.
- **Urza's Incubator** — Skeleton discount for Ozox and other tribal pieces.
- **Jet Medallion** — Generic black discount for recasts and tutors.
- **Carnival of Souls** — Black mana on creature ETB to fuel the same recast loops.
- **Pitiless Plunderer** — Treasure on every death, turning loops into mana.
- **Reassembling Skeleton** — Repeatable fodder for Bartolomé while waiting for Ozox.
- **Enduring Renewal** — Returns sacrificed creatures to hand; with Greedy Freebooter it is a closed loop.
- **Chthonian Nightmare** — Instant-speed reanimation that also acts as a sacrifice outlet.
- **Funeral Room // Awakening Hall** — Drain plus a late mass-reanimate finish.
- **Ratadrabik of Urborg** — Nonlegendary Ozox copies make the Altar loop explode.
- **Lurrus of the Dream-Den** — Recasts cheap permanents from the yard after loops stall.

### Recursion creatures and Auras

- **Sun Titan** — Recurs the return Aura after Bartolomé eats him, closing the Titan loop.
- **Angel of Indemnity** — Another ETB-reanimate body for the same Aura loop.
- **Brotherhood Outcast** — Recursion creature on theme.
- **Danitha, Benalia's Hope** — Recurs Auras and Equipment onto herself after dying.
- **Redemption Choir** — Convoke reanimator that also is a recursion body.
- **Boonweaver Giant** — Tutors an Aura onto itself to start the loop.
- **Angelic Renewal** — Cheap return enchantment that a Titan can pick up.
- **Fungal Fortitude** — Return Aura with ward, the primary Titan sticker.
- **Kaya's Ghostform** — One-mana return that ignores timing.
- **Minion's Return** — Another return Aura for redundancy.
- **Necrogen Communion** — Toxic return Aura that still completes the loop.
- **Changing Loyalty** — Unusual return effect kept for redundancy and flavor.
- **Animate Dead** — Reanimation Aura and the Leonin Relic-Warder lock.
- **Reanimate** — Cheap one-shot reanimation.
- **Victimize** — Two-for-one reanimation that feeds Bartolomé a sacrifice.

### Death payoffs

- **Zulaport Cutthroat** — Table drain on every loop death.
- **Bastion of Remembrance** — Enchantment drain that survives creature wipes.
- **Marionette Apprentice** — Withering death payoff.
- **Agent of the Iron Throne** — Treasure or drain from the background.
- **Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim** — Lifegain and drain on ETB/death.
- **Corpse Knight** — Drain on creature ETB, including tokens.
- **Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER // Sephiroth, One-Winged Angel** — Death trigger that flips into a second drain engine.
- **Dark Prophecy** — Cards off deaths when the loop is not yet lethal.
- **Village Rites** — Instant draw attached to a sacrifice already required by Bartolomé.
- **Deadly Dispute** — Treasure plus cards for sacrificing a creature or artifact.
- **Braids, Arisen Nightmare** — Forces sacrifices and draws or drains.
- **Erebos, Bleak-Hearted** — Draw outlet and extra sacrifice.

- **Disenchant** — Cheap artifact/enchantment answer.

- **Toxic Deluge** — Efficient wipe that the recursion plan rebuilds from.
