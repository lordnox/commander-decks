# Graveyard Shift — agent hints

Primer owns the plan. This file is the seat checklist.

## Always ask

1. Which loop piece, sacrifice outlet, death payoff, and recursion effect are
   available, and what exact piece is still missing?
2. Can a creature or token attack before it is sacrificed without weakening a
   needed block or changing the loop?
3. Is a wipe resolving cleanly, or can visible mana and known protection
   phase, blink, counter, or recur the current leader's board?
4. Which seat is the current leader, and does protecting a laggard for one
   rotation improve Graveyard Shift's chance to assemble a loop?
5. Is Bartolomé completing a line now? Commander tax counts prior command-zone
   casts, not returns to the command zone; do not spend the turn on a bare
   sacrifice outlet when a setup creature or held answer advances more.

## Sequencing

- Attack with disposable creatures before feeding them to Bartolomé or an
  Altar unless blocking, summoning sickness, or the combo line needs them
  immediately.
- Walk the Ozox and Aura-recursion loops card by card. A sacrifice engine
  without its recursion or payoff is setup, not a win.
- Before paying a large amount of life to Toxic Deluge, identify the
  protection window and what survives. If the leader can phase or blink its
  board, negotiate for that protection first or wait for a cleaner window.
- Preserve the mana and body required for the next loop step when helping
  another seat. Political assistance should also remove leader resources or
  buy a named setup turn.
- Compare Bartolomé with every setup creature before casting. Emeritus of Woe
  enters prepared and threatens a Demonic Tutor copy; if it draws removal,
  Bartolomé is safer, and if it survives, it finds the missing combo piece.
- Dimir House Guard is both a body and Transmute `{1}{B}{B}` for any mana-value
  four card. Enumerate Enduring Renewal, Pitiless Plunderer, Ratadrabik,
  Emeritus of Woe, and the other legal targets before choosing to cast it.
  With Ashnod's Altar already in play, Enduring Renewal is usually the first
  engine to compare.
- Preserve Disenchant mana against a live Portal to Phyrexia or comparable
  permanent engine. Casting Dimir House Guard while leaving `{W}` open is
  stronger than tapping out for a commander that has nothing profitable to
  sacrifice.

## Politics

Use removal and sacrifice timing as leverage before committing it. Prefer
short, observable agreements such as one rotation of nonaggression or a named
attack target. Reassess after every turn; avoid permanent `never` or `always`
terms when the leader can change.

## Closing

Count every death trigger and life payment, confirm the intended recursion
target remains in the graveyard, and check whether an attack should happen
before the next sacrifice.
