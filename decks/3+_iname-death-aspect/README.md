Mono-black Spirit graveyard deck: dump the legend suite with [**Iname, Death Aspect**](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api), then either win on upkeep with [**Mortal Combat**](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/160/mortal-combat?utm_source=api) or reanimate the pile.

**Commander:** [Iname, Death Aspect](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api)  
**Deck size:** 100 cards (plus token extras)  
**Primary plan:** mill every Spirit, resolve [Mortal Combat](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/160/mortal-combat?utm_source=api), win on the next upkeep  
**Backup plan:** [Living Death](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/185/living-death?utm_source=api) / [Patriarch's Bidding](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/275/patriarchs-bidding?utm_source=api), [The Capitoline Triad](https://scryfall.com/card/acr/1/the-capitoline-triad?utm_source=api) emblem, or drain with [Kokusho, the Evening Star](https://scryfall.com/card/ima/95/kokusho-the-evening-star?utm_source=api)

Reasons for the list, cuts, and rules checks are in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).

## Category access by turn three

\[
P(X \ge 1)=1-\frac{\binom{99-K}{10}}{\binom{99}{10}}
\]

## How the deck works

[Iname, Death Aspect](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api) costs `{4}{B}{B}`. When it enters, search the library for any number of Spirit cards and put them into the graveyard. The 99 contains **20 Spirits**. Milling all of them puts exactly twenty creature cards in the yard, which is the [Mortal Combat](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/160/mortal-combat?utm_source=api) threshold.

Fast mana (`+1 Mana` / `+2 Mana` / `+3 Mana`) exists to put [Iname](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api) on the table as soon as possible, often through [Dark Ritual](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/793/dark-ritual?utm_source=api), [Cabal Ritual](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/2199/cabal-ritual?utm_source=api), sacrifice dorks, and [Lake of the Dead](https://scryfall.com/card/all/140/lake-of-the-dead?utm_source=api). After the dump, [Songs of the Damned](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/115/songs-of-the-damned?utm_source=api) turns the yard into a huge burst of `{B}`.

The legend package ("Those Who Came Before") is the payload: drain, discard, reanimation, and a colorless secret commander in [The Capitoline Triad](https://scryfall.com/card/acr/1/the-capitoline-triad?utm_source=api). Swarm cards refill the yard if a Spirit was drawn before [Iname](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api) resolved.

## Main lines

### [Mortal Combat](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/160/mortal-combat?utm_source=api)

1. Develop enough mana to cast [Iname](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api). [Cavern of Souls](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/269/cavern-of-souls?utm_source=api) naming Spirit makes it uncounterable.
2. On enter, mill **all** remaining Spirits. Do not leave legends in the library unless you already have twenty creature cards in the yard.
3. Resolve [Mortal Combat](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/160/mortal-combat?utm_source=api) if it is not already in play.
4. Win at the beginning of your next upkeep if twenty or more creature cards are still in your graveyard.

This is delayed, not instant. Graveyard exile or reanimating your own pile can turn the enchantment off.

If you already drew some Spirits, mill the rest and add non-Spirit creatures to the yard: [Blood Pet](https://scryfall.com/card/7ed/121/blood-pet?utm_source=api), [Basal Thrull](https://scryfall.com/card/fem/34d/basal-thrull?utm_source=api), [Nested Shambler](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/95/nested-shambler?utm_source=api), [Sidisi, Undead Vizier](https://scryfall.com/card/dtk/120/sidisi-undead-vizier?utm_source=api), [Doom Weaver](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/34/doom-weaver?utm_source=api), or [The Capitoline Triad](https://scryfall.com/card/acr/1/the-capitoline-triad?utm_source=api). [Final Parting](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/160/final-parting?utm_source=api) can also park a creature in the yard.

### [Living Death](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/185/living-death?utm_source=api) / Bidding

1. Dump Spirits with [Iname](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api).
2. Cast [Living Death](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/185/living-death?utm_source=api) or name Spirit with [Patriarch's Bidding](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/275/patriarchs-bidding?utm_source=api).
3. Opponents sacrifice their creatures ([Living Death](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/185/living-death?utm_source=api)) or also get a type of their choice (Bidding).
4. Win in combat with the legend suite, or loop [Kokusho, the Evening Star](https://scryfall.com/card/ima/95/kokusho-the-evening-star?utm_source=api) death triggers with further recursion.

Do not pair this with an active [Mortal Combat](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/160/mortal-combat?utm_source=api) plan on the same turn: your creatures leave the graveyard, so the upkeep win fails.

### Capitoline Triad

Historic cards here include the many legends, artifacts, and [Urza's Saga](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/259/urzas-saga?utm_source=api). After [Iname](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api), the Triad is steeply discounted. Exiling historic cards from the graveyard with total mana value 30 or greater creates an emblem that sets your creatures to 9/9. That exile **reduces** [Mortal Combat](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/160/mortal-combat?utm_source=api) count, so use it as the alternate close, not as a setup for the enchantment.

### Songs into [Yawgmoth's Will](https://scryfall.com/card/g07/3/yawgmoths-will?utm_source=api)

After a full dump, [Songs of the Damned](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/115/songs-of-the-damned?utm_source=api) adds `{B}` for each creature card in the yard. [Yawgmoth's Will](https://scryfall.com/card/g07/3/yawgmoths-will?utm_source=api) then lets you cast those Spirits (and tutors) from the graveyard; cards that would go to the yard that turn are exiled instead. That line wins through the battlefield, not through [Mortal Combat](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/160/mortal-combat?utm_source=api).

## Win conditions

- [**Mortal Combat**](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/160/mortal-combat?utm_source=api) on a later upkeep with 20+ creature cards in the graveyard.
- Combat after [Living Death](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/185/living-death?utm_source=api), Bidding, or the Triad 9/9 emblem.
- Repeated [Kokusho, the Evening Star](https://scryfall.com/card/ima/95/kokusho-the-evening-star?utm_source=api) death triggers (5 life from each opponent per death).
- [Myojin of Night's Reach](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/610/myojin-of-nights-reach?utm_source=api) hand strip if it was **cast from hand** (divinity counter), not milled.
- [Osseous Sticktwister](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/112/osseous-sticktwister?utm_source=api) drain once delirium is on (see Enable Delirium).

## Early, mid, and late game

**Early:** Keep hands that make 6 mana by turn 3–4. [Gemstone Caverns](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/280/gemstone-caverns?utm_source=api) on the draw, [Lotus Petal](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/7037/lotus-petal?utm_source=api), [Sol Ring](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/211/sol-ring?utm_source=api), [Dark Ritual](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/793/dark-ritual?utm_source=api), and [Cabal Ritual](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/2199/cabal-ritual?utm_source=api) (threshold after a modest yard) are the usual accelerants. [Expedition Map](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/724/expedition-map?utm_source=api) should find [Lake of the Dead](https://scryfall.com/card/all/140/lake-of-the-dead?utm_source=api), [Cavern of Souls](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/269/cavern-of-souls?utm_source=api), or [Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/287/urborg-tomb-of-yawgmoth?utm_source=api). [Urza's Saga](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/259/urzas-saga?utm_source=api) chapter III finds a `{0}` or `{1}` artifact: [Lotus Petal](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/7037/lotus-petal?utm_source=api), [Jeweled Amulet](https://scryfall.com/card/mbc/75/jeweled-amulet?utm_source=api), [Sol Ring](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/211/sol-ring?utm_source=api), or [Expedition Map](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/724/expedition-map?utm_source=api).

**Mid:** Cast [Iname](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api), preferably through Cavern. Tutor [Mortal Combat](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/160/mortal-combat?utm_source=api) if the dump will hit 20. Hold [Toxic Deluge](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/161/toxic-deluge?utm_source=api) for opposing boards; [Iname](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api) itself is expendable after the trigger.

**Late:** If Combat is answered, switch to Songs mana plus [Living Death](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/185/living-death?utm_source=api), [Yawgmoth's Will](https://scryfall.com/card/g07/3/yawgmoths-will?utm_source=api), or the Triad. Recursion ([Chthonian Nightmare](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/83/chthonian-nightmare?utm_source=api), [Junji, the Midnight Sky](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/183/junji-the-midnight-sky?utm_source=api), [Eerie Soultender](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/92/eerie-soultender?utm_source=api)) rebuilds after exile-light interaction.

## Mulligan guide

Keep:

- 6 mana by turn 4 with at least two sources of `{B}`.
- A tutor plus enough mana to use it before the table can stack graveyard hate.
- [Gemstone Caverns](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/280/gemstone-caverns?utm_source=api) when on the draw if the remaining six still function.
- [Scheming Symmetry](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/113/scheming-symmetry?utm_source=api) only with [Cavern of Souls](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/269/cavern-of-souls?utm_source=api) or a plan to win immediately after stacking [Mortal Combat](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/160/mortal-combat?utm_source=api) or [Iname](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api).

Ship:

- Hands of expensive Spirits with no ritual or land ramp.
- All-tutor hands that cannot pay `{4}{B}{B}` or a tutor this game.
- [Lake of the Dead](https://scryfall.com/card/all/140/lake-of-the-dead?utm_source=api) with no [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api) to sacrifice.

## Important sequencing and rules notes

- [Iname](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api)'s search is optional. Always mill every Spirit unless you are protecting a specific cast-from-hand Myojin or you already have Combat locked.
- [Myojin of Grim Betrayal](https://scryfall.com/card/nec/34/myojin-of-grim-betrayal?utm_source=api) and [Myojin of Night's Reach](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/610/myojin-of-nights-reach?utm_source=api) only get their counters if **cast from hand**. Milling them is correct for [Mortal Combat](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/160/mortal-combat?utm_source=api) and [Living Death](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/185/living-death?utm_source=api), and wrong if you wanted the activated ability.
- Grim Betrayal's ability returns creature cards put into graveyards **this turn** from anywhere. [Iname](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api)'s mill counts. That requires the Myojin in play with a counter, which means you cast it rather than milling it.
- [Nameless Inversion](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/113/nameless-inversion?utm_source=api) is a Kindred instant with changeling, not a creature. [Iname](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api) cannot mill it. Together with [Sorcerer's Broom](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/232/sorcerers-broom?utm_source=api) (artifact creature) it helps delirium for [Osseous Sticktwister](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/112/osseous-sticktwister?utm_source=api) and [Demonic Counsel](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/92/demonic-counsel?utm_source=api).
- [Nether Shadow](https://scryfall.com/card/30a/410/nether-shadow?utm_source=api) needs three creature cards **above it** in the graveyard. After a bulk mill, order is whatever you choose while searching; stack Shadow near the bottom of the milled pile if you want it back.
- [Krovikan Horror](https://scryfall.com/card/all/53/krovikan-horror?utm_source=api) needs a creature card directly above it. Same ordering note.
- [Omarthis, Ghostfire Initiate](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/708/omarthis-ghostfire-initiate?utm_source=api) is a colorless Spirit; it mills with [Iname](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api), feeds the Triad, and manifests on death (the [Manifest](https://scryfall.com/card/tsoc/30/manifest?utm_source=api) extras).
- [Pus Kami](https://scryfall.com/card/bok/79/pus-kami?utm_source=api) soulshifts a Spirit with mana value 6 or less; [Iname](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api) itself is 6.
- [Crypt Ghast](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/70/crypt-ghast?utm_source=api) is a Spirit, so [Iname](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api) will mill it unless you already cast it.

## Weaknesses and what to protect

The deck is linear and graveyard-dependent. Rest in Peace effects stop both Combat and the reanimation plan. Counterspells on [Iname](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api) hurt unless [Cavern of Souls](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/269/cavern-of-souls?utm_source=api) is out. There is almost no protection and very little card draw ([Doom Weaver](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/34/doom-weaver?utm_source=api), [Castle Locthwain](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/884/castle-locthwain?utm_source=api)).

Protect the [Iname](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/118/iname-death-aspect?utm_source=api) trigger (Cavern), the Combat enchantment until the upkeep, and the graveyard itself. If hate is inevitable, win the same turn with Songs plus [Living Death](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/185/living-death?utm_source=api) or [Yawgmoth's Will](https://scryfall.com/card/g07/3/yawgmoths-will?utm_source=api) instead of waiting on an upkeep.
