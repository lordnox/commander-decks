# Decisions

## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, the primer, and the assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a cut back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated session notes so an argument does not live only in chat.

Every unique card needs a Cards in line. The other headings appear when there is something to record.

## Goals

- **Politics** — Secret council is a bluff: take the draws, or take the free permanent, and never let the table know which.
- **Theft** — Close by borrowing someone else's best army for one combat, not by keeping their cards.

## Rankings

First pass: jank 8, fun 8, mean 6, Politics 9, Theft 7. The vote and Show and Tell tax are the joke; Reins is a one-turn borrow, not a lock.

## Rules

- Círdan: vote for a player. Draw equal to votes received. Only players with **zero** votes may put a permanent in. Voting for yourself blocks your own dump.
- Illusion of Choice chooses every ballot that turn, including Círdan, Elrond, Selvala's Stampede, Split Decision, Trap, Sail, and Coercive Portal.
- Show and Tell / Círdan / Eureka / Hypergenesis / Braids / Stampede **put** permanents onto the battlefield. Cast triggers do not fire.
- Fight is not combat: first strike and menace on Sire of Seven Deaths do not apply.
- Reins is a swap with **one** opponent until end of turn. Attack the players you did not swap with, or they block with our creatures. Twincast can target a different opponent.
- Pathbreaker Ibex's attack trigger is useless on the Reins turn if we gave the Ibex away. Stampede is the spell that cares about power after the swap.
- End-Raze Forerunners should enter **after** Reins so the borrowed creatures get the ETB pump.
- Braids cheats artifact, creature, or land only.
- Willbreaker was rejected: it makes Triangle of War's opponent-creature target illegal.
