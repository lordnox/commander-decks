It spends its own life to draw cards, refuses to fight over the board, swaps life totals with whoever is winning, and then drains the table with a [Cabal Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/301/cabal-coffers?utm_source=api) X spell. ## How the deck works

Most decks treat life as a buffer. This one treats it as a currency with a refund at the end.

Life goes out through [Wall of Blood](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/82/wall-of-blood?utm_source=api), [Necrologia](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/7ED-149/necrologia?utm_source=api), [Dark Confidant](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/94/dark-confidant?utm_source=api), [Dark Tutelage](https://scryfall.com/card/m11/90/dark-tutelage?utm_source=api), [Hatred](https://scryfall.com/card/exo/64/hatred?utm_source=api), [Selenia, Dark Angel](https://scryfall.com/card/tmp/270/selenia-dark-angel?utm_source=api), the Talismans, and five painful lands. Most of that spending buys cards, which is how the deck finds its finishers without running a single tutor.

Life comes back in one of two ways. Either a drain spell resolves and the deck gains everything it deals, or a life-exchange effect hands your low total to somebody who is doing well and hands you theirs. Both are wins, which is why being at 4 life here is a position rather than an emergency.

Nothing in that plan involves attacking, so the deck buys time with prevention instead of removal. Seven instant-speed fogs, two creature taxes, eight counterspells, and a commander who switches off one attacker every turn keep the board from mattering while the life engine runs.

## Mana engine

The finishers are capped by black mana, not by total mana, which is why the deck is built around [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api) count rather than around rocks.

[Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/287/urborg-tomb-of-yawgmoth?utm_source=api) or [Blanket of Night](https://scryfall.com/card/vis/52/blanket-of-night?utm_source=api) makes every land you control a [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api). [Cabal Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/301/cabal-coffers?utm_source=api) then pays `{2}` and taps for black equal to that count.

[Crypt Ghast](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/70/crypt-ghast?utm_source=api), [Nirkana Revenant](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/134/nirkana-revenant?utm_source=api), and [Bubbling Muck](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/54/bubbling-muck?utm_source=api) all say the same thing: whenever you tap a [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api) for mana, add an additional `{B}`. Read that carefully, because it does **not** double Coffers. Tapping Coffers is a single [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api) tap, so it gets exactly one extra black. The doubling happens on every *other* [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api) you tap, and that is where most of the mana actually comes from. Ten lands under Urborg with [Crypt Ghast](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/70/crypt-ghast?utm_source=api) out is roughly 27 black if you tap everything: nine lands at two each, minus the `{2}` for Coffers, plus eleven off the Coffers activation itself. That is [Exsanguinate](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/173/exsanguinate?utm_source=api) for 24.

[Bubbling Muck](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/54/bubbling-muck?utm_source=api) is a one-mana third copy of that trigger for a single turn. It is symmetrical, but nobody else at the table is about to tap eight Swamps.

The scaling line is **[Deserted Temple](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/301/deserted-temple?utm_source=api) + [Rings of Brighthearth](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/335/rings-of-brighthearth?utm_source=api)**. Rings only copies activated abilities that are *not* mana abilities, so it cannot copy [Cabal Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/301/cabal-coffers?utm_source=api) or [Magus of the Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/c14/148/magus-of-the-coffers?utm_source=api) — but [Deserted Temple](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/301/deserted-temple?utm_source=api)'s `{1}`, tap: untap target land qualifies. Activate Temple targeting Coffers, pay `{2}` to copy it, and point the copy at [Deserted Temple](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/301/deserted-temple?utm_source=api) itself. Both end up untapped, so you can do it again. Each cycle costs `{5}` all told and returns black equal to your [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api) count, which makes it net positive from six Swamps and arbitrarily large after that. It is a mana engine, not a win by itself — you still have to cast the drain.

Every mana ability here resolves without using the stack, so you can build the lethal X spell with counterspell mana still untapped.

## Win conditions

1. **The drain turn.** Untap with Coffers and a [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api)-trigger creature, cast [Exsanguinate](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/173/exsanguinate?utm_source=api) or [Debt to the Deathless](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/176/debt-to-the-deathless?utm_source=api) for a lethal X, and gain it all back. Debt drains 2 per X per opponent, so it needs roughly half the mana [Exsanguinate](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/173/exsanguinate?utm_source=api) does in a four-player pod.
2. **The transfusion.** Dump to 1–4 life with [Wall of Blood](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/82/wall-of-blood?utm_source=api), then hand that total to the player who is actually threatening the table. [Mirror Universe](https://scryfall.com/card/olep/16/mirror-universe?utm_source=api) and [Magus of the Mirror](https://scryfall.com/card/cns/117/magus-of-the-mirror?utm_source=api) do this to one player; [Repay in Kind](https://scryfall.com/card/roe/125/repay-in-kind?utm_source=api) sets everyone to the lowest total at once, which usually puts the whole table into [Exsanguinate](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/173/exsanguinate?utm_source=api) range.
3. **Draw damage.** [Psychosis Crawler](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/234/psychosis-crawler?utm_source=api) drains *each* opponent on every draw, so it is the true clock; [Queza, Augur of Agonies](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/212/queza-augur-of-agonies?utm_source=api) drains one target opponent per draw, which lets you spread the damage or focus a single player. [Necrologia](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/7ED-149/necrologia?utm_source=api) for six with both out is 6 to the table and 6 more to whoever Queza keeps hitting.
4. **Their own attack.** [Inkshield](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/316/inkshield?utm_source=api) and [Comeuppance](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/58/comeuppance?utm_source=api) turn a lethal swing at you into a board or into a dead attacker. This is the aikido win the deck is named for, and it is common enough to play toward.

## Early, mid, and late game

**Turns 1–3.** Land, rock, tax creature. Play [Esper Sentinel](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/12/esper-sentinel?utm_source=api) or [Dark Confidant](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/94/dark-confidant?utm_source=api) on curve and let them run. Do not cast [Lady Evangela](https://scryfall.com/card/leg/240/lady-evangela?utm_source=api) yet unless the board demands it; there is nothing to prevent.

**Turns 4–7.** Build [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api) count and hold up interaction. This is the draw-go window: keep two or three mana open, use it on a fog or a counter, and spend everything you did not need on a life-for-cards spell in the end step. [Necrologia](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/7ED-149/necrologia?utm_source=api) is specifically an end-of-turn card for exactly this.

**Turn 8 onward.** Land [Cabal Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/301/cabal-coffers?utm_source=api) alongside [Crypt Ghast](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/70/crypt-ghast?utm_source=api) or [Nirkana Revenant](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/134/nirkana-revenant?utm_source=api), count the table's life totals, and decide whether the mana is there. If it is short, spend the turn on [Blue Sun's Zenith](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/44/blue-suns-zenith?utm_source=api) instead and try again next turn — the fort is designed to let you take the extra turn.

## Mulligan guide

Keep a hand with two or three lands and at least two of: a rock, a life-for-cards spell, a cheap fog, a counterspell.

- Keep hands with early defense even if the mana engine is missing; the deck draws enough to find Coffers.
- Ship hands with the finishers but no way to survive to cast them. [Debt to the Deathless](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/176/debt-to-the-deathless?utm_source=api) in an opening hand is close to a blank.
- Ship hands with the exchange package and no life payment. [Mirror Universe](https://scryfall.com/card/olep/16/mirror-universe?utm_source=api) at 40 life does nothing.
- One-land hands are unkeepable. There are no rituals here other than [Bubbling Muck](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/54/bubbling-muck?utm_source=api), which needs lands to work.

## Sequencing and rules notes

- **[Repay in Kind](https://scryfall.com/card/roe/125/repay-in-kind?utm_source=api) sets, it does not distribute.** Every player's total becomes the lowest total among all players. Cast it while you are the low player, never before.
- **[Mister Negative](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/135/mister-negative?utm_source=api)'s exchange is an entry trigger**, so it can be countered and it can be repeated. [Ephemerate](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/7/ephemerate?utm_source=api) rebounds, giving a second exchange the following turn.
- **[Wall of Blood](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/82/wall-of-blood?utm_source=api) has no cap and works at instant speed.** Hold the pump until the exchange effect is already on the stack and cannot be answered, then dump.
- **Both Mirrors only work during your upkeep.** [Mirror Universe](https://scryfall.com/card/olep/16/mirror-universe?utm_source=api) and [Magus of the Mirror](https://scryfall.com/card/cns/117/magus-of-the-mirror?utm_source=api) say "activate only during your upkeep," so plan the life dump for that step rather than the turn before. Everything else the deck does can wait until an opponent's end step.
- **[Selenia, Dark Angel](https://scryfall.com/card/tmp/270/selenia-dark-angel?utm_source=api) returns itself for 2 life**, so it is an unbounded life sink limited only by the mana to recast it.
- **[Lady Evangela](https://scryfall.com/card/leg/240/lady-evangela?utm_source=api) shuts off one creature completely.** She prevents all combat damage that would be dealt by target creature this turn, for `{W}{B}` and a tap. That answers the Voltron commander or the one huge trampler every turn, but it does nothing against a wide board — that is what the fogs are for. [Minamo, School at Water's Edge](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/279/minamo-school-at-waters-edge?utm_source=api) can untap her or [Sokrates, Athenian Teacher](https://scryfall.com/card/acr/67/sokrates-athenian-teacher?utm_source=api) for a second activation, and [Adept Watershaper](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/3/adept-watershaper?utm_source=api) makes them indestructible while tapped.
- **Do not tap out on your own turn** unless the drain is lethal. The entire fort assumes two or three open mana.

- **[Energy Arc](https://scryfall.com/card/all/106/energy-arc?utm_source=api) fog-and-untaps.** Target the attacking team for a two-mana fog, or include your tapped clerics so [Lady Evangela](https://scryfall.com/card/leg/240/lady-evangela?utm_source=api) and Sokrates stand back up in the same combat.
- **[Sokrates, Athenian Teacher](https://scryfall.com/card/acr/67/sokrates-athenian-teacher?utm_source=api) only stops combat damage to players**, not to creatures or planeswalkers, and the draw is half that damage rounded down. Hexproof while untapped; Watershaper covers the tapped window.
- **[Scapegoat](https://scryfall.com/card/sth/14/scapegoat?utm_source=api) bounces your creatures**, after you sacrifice one. [Kami of False Hope](https://scryfall.com/card/bok/10/kami-of-false-hope?utm_source=api) is the cheapest fodder. It cannot bounce an opponent's attacker.
- **Cast the fog, not the counterspell**, when both answer the same attack. Counterspells are reserved for whatever tries to stop the exchange or the drain.

## Weaknesses

- **Enchantment and artifact hate.** [Mirror Universe](https://scryfall.com/card/olep/16/mirror-universe?utm_source=api), [Axis of Mortality](https://scryfall.com/card/xln/3/axis-of-mortality?utm_source=api), and [Rings of Brighthearth](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/335/rings-of-brighthearth?utm_source=api) are all answerable permanents. This is why the creature versions ([Magus of the Mirror](https://scryfall.com/card/cns/117/magus-of-the-mirror?utm_source=api), [Magus of the Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/c14/148/magus-of-the-coffers?utm_source=api)) are in the list.
- **Land destruction and [Cabal Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/301/cabal-coffers?utm_source=api) removal.** Losing Coffers costs several turns. [Ghostly Flicker](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/98/ghostly-flicker?utm_source=api) can save it in response.
- **Being at low life with no exchange in hand.** The payment suite is a real risk if the deck spends without a plan. Only dump below 10 with an exchange, a drain, or a fog available.
- **Noncombat damage and mass drain.** Every fog in the deck is a combat-damage effect except [Comeuppance](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/58/comeuppance?utm_source=api). An opposing [Exsanguinate](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/173/exsanguinate?utm_source=api) hits you hardest of anyone at the table.
- **Fast combo.** With zero Game Changers, no tutors, and eight counterspells, the deck interacts well but cannot race a turn-four kill.
