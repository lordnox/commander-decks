A Dimir politics deck that builds creatures too painful to keep, then hands them to other players. [**Jon Irenicus, Shattered One**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/278/jon-irenicus-shattered-one?utm_source=api) goads the gift, pads it with two +1/+1 counters, and draws whenever a creature you own but do not control attacks.

## How the deck works

1. Ramp into [**Jon Irenicus, Shattered One**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/278/jon-irenicus-shattered-one?utm_source=api) on turn three or four, then keep at least one present in play by the end step.
2. Gift a drawback creature. Irenicus taps it, goads it for the rest of the game, and gives it "This creature can't be sacrificed," so the recipient cannot edict it away.
3. Draw from the attacks those creatures are forced to make, plus [**Sygg, River Cutthroat**](https://scryfall.com/card/znc/103/sygg-river-cutthroat?utm_source=api) whenever an opponent loses three life.
4. Use extra Giving spells to hand off noncreature permanents Irenicus cannot move, especially [**Illusions of Grandeur**](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/79/illusions-of-grandeur?utm_source=api).
5. Close by locking one player out of creature spells, making another unable to win, or feeding the table into goaded, life-draining attackers.

The deck wins by making other people's turns worse, not by assembling a two-card infinite.

## Main synergy patterns

### Irenicus gifts, not generic [Donate](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/31/donate?utm_source=api)

Irenicus can move only a creature you control, and only at the beginning of your end step. [**Donate**](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/31/donate?utm_source=api) is the card that actually gives away [**Illusions of Grandeur**](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/79/illusions-of-grandeur?utm_source=api). [**Shifting Grift**](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/66/shifting-grift?utm_source=api) can exchange enchantments or artifacts as well. [**Sleeper Agent**](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/178/sleeper-agent?utm_source=api) donates itself on enter and does not need the commander.

### Cannot-sacrifice lock

The Irenicus clause stops the recipient from sacrificing the gifted creature. [**Greater Harvester**](https://scryfall.com/card/dst/44/greater-harvester?utm_source=api) and [**Demonic Taskmaster**](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/104/demonic-taskmaster?utm_source=api) still demand a sacrifice each upkeep, so they eat the rest of that player's board. [**Phyrexian Negator**](https://scryfall.com/card/dde/1/phyrexian-negator?utm_source=api) does the same whenever it is damaged. Targeted edicts also fail against the gift itself.

### [Illusions of Grandeur](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/79/illusions-of-grandeur?utm_source=api)

1. Cast [**Illusions of Grandeur**](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/79/illusions-of-grandeur?utm_source=api) and gain 20 life.
2. Resolve [**Donate**](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/31/donate?utm_source=api) targeting an opponent and the enchantment.
3. That player now owes cumulative upkeep {2} per age counter. When they let it go, they lose 20 life.

This is a finite life swing, not a loop. Hold the [Donate](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/31/donate?utm_source=api) until the opponent cannot pay or until you need the 20-life cushion.

### Creature-spell lock

[**Steel Golem**](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/344/steel-golem?utm_source=api) and [**Grid Monitor**](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/183/grid-monitor?utm_source=api) say "You can't cast creature spells" for their controller. Gift one to the creature deck at the table. They can still play noncreature spells and attack with the golem, which draws you a card.

### Copy a present

[**Mystic Reflection**](https://scryfall.com/card/khm/69/mystic-reflection?utm_source=api) makes the next creature or planeswalker enter as a copy of a chosen nonlegendary present, so a second [**Steel Golem**](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/344/steel-golem?utm_source=api) or [**Sleeper Agent**](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/178/sleeper-agent?utm_source=api) can go to a different opponent. [**Amphin Mutineer**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/143/amphin-mutineer?utm_source=api) is removal that also leaves a token if you need a cheap body to gift.

## Win conditions

- Attrition: goaded presents plus [**Parasitic Impetus**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/222/parasitic-impetus?utm_source=api) and [**Sower of Discord**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/187/sower-of-discord?utm_source=api) drain the table while you draw.
- Hard locks: [**Abyssal Persecutor**](https://scryfall.com/card/ima/78/abyssal-persecutor?utm_source=api) stops its controller from winning; remove it only when you are ready to take the game. [**Archfiend of the Dross**](https://scryfall.com/card/one/82/archfiend-of-the-dross?utm_source=api) clocks a player out in four of their upkeeps if the oil stays on it.
- [**Taniwha**](https://scryfall.com/card/mir/95/taniwha?utm_source=api) phases out the recipient's lands each of their upkeeps, which often ends that player's ability to play Magic.
- [**Yukora, the Prisoner**](https://scryfall.com/card/bok/90/yukora-the-prisoner?utm_source=api) wipes the recipient's non-Ogre creatures when it leaves; point removal at it only after it has changed control.
- [**Bribery**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/77/bribery?utm_source=api) and [**Reanimate**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/155/reanimate?utm_source=api) steal a finisher, which Irenicus can then gift or keep.

## Game progression

### Early game

Keep two or three lands plus [**Sol Ring**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/211/sol-ring?utm_source=api), [**Arcane Signet**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/192/arcane-signet?utm_source=api), [**Dimir Signet**](https://scryfall.com/card/clu/221/dimir-signet?utm_source=api), or [**Dark Ritual**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/793/dark-ritual?utm_source=api). [**Sleeper Agent**](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/178/sleeper-agent?utm_source=api) on turn one is a real present even before the commander. [**Mystic Remora**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/59/mystic-remora?utm_source=api) pays for itself in this seat.

### Mid game

Cast Irenicus with a present already in play so the first end step does work. Prefer [**Steel Golem**](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/344/steel-golem?utm_source=api) or [**Master of the Feast**](https://scryfall.com/card/jou/75/master-of-the-feast?utm_source=api) against creature decks, [**Rotting Regisaur**](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/111/rotting-regisaur?utm_source=api) against greedy hands, and [**Flesh Reaver**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/86/flesh-reaver?utm_source=api) against the player who will be forced to block. Hold [**Counterspell**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/114/counterspell?utm_source=api) or [**An Offer You Can't Refuse**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/160/an-offer-you-cant-refuse?utm_source=api) for the answer to Irenicus.

### Late game

Stack locks: one player cannot cast creatures, another cannot win, a third is attacking into [**Public Enemy**](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/55/public-enemy?utm_source=api). [**Reins of Power**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/734/reins-of-power?utm_source=api) and [**Domineering Will**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/719/domineering-will?utm_source=api) steal a combat step when someone tries to race. [**Cabal Coffers**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/301/cabal-coffers?utm_source=api) with [**Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/287/urborg-tomb-of-yawgmoth?utm_source=api) pays for [**Blasphemous Edict**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/57/blasphemous-edict?utm_source=api) or [**Killing Wave**](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/121/killing-wave?utm_source=api) after your gifts are already on other boards.

## Mulligan guide

Keep hands with:

- two or three lands that make both blue and black by turn three;
- cheap ramp or [**Dark Ritual**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/793/dark-ritual?utm_source=api);
- at least one present costing four or less, or [**Sleeper Agent**](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/178/sleeper-agent?utm_source=api).

Mulligan hands that are only expensive Demons, only interaction with no gift, or that cannot cast Irenicus on curve. A hand of [**Illusions of Grandeur**](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/79/illusions-of-grandeur?utm_source=api) without [**Donate**](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/31/donate?utm_source=api) or another Giving spell is usually a mulligan.

## Important sequencing and rules notes

- Irenicus targets both an opponent and a creature you control. If you have no creature, the ability does nothing useful; keep a cheap body or wait.
- The gift is goaded for the rest of the game and cannot attack you, so you usually do not need extra goad once Irenicus has moved it.
- "Can't be sacrificed" does not save the creature from destroy, exile, bounce, or -X/-X. [**Feed the Swarm**](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/257/feed-the-swarm?utm_source=api) and [**Ravenform**](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/167/ravenform?utm_source=api) still answer a present that came back or went to the wrong player.
- [**Wrong Turn**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/107/wrong-turn?utm_source=api) removes the creature from combat if you change its controller mid-combat.
- [**Illusions of Grandeur**](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/79/illusions-of-grandeur?utm_source=api) is an enchantment. Irenicus cannot donate it.
- [**Abyssal Persecutor**](https://scryfall.com/card/ima/78/abyssal-persecutor?utm_source=api) uses its controller as "you." After you gift it, that opponent cannot win and you cannot lose until it leaves.
- [**Master of the Feast**](https://scryfall.com/card/jou/75/master-of-the-feast?utm_source=api) draws for each of the controller's opponents. Once an opponent has it, you draw on their upkeep.
- [**Taniwha**](https://scryfall.com/card/mir/95/taniwha?utm_source=api) phases out lands the recipient controls, including lands they just played, so they often skip their main phase mana.
- [**Exchange of Words**](https://scryfall.com/card/unf/45/exchange-of-words?utm_source=api) swaps text boxes, not control. Use it to put a drawback onto a creature that will stay with an opponent, or to blank a present you accidentally still control.
- [**Sudden Substitution**](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/11/sudden-substitution?utm_source=api) has split second: trade a present for a noncreature spell on the stack, then they may retarget it.
- [**Modify Memory**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/83/modify-memory?utm_source=api) draws three only if you control neither exchanged creature, so swapping two opponents' creatures is the card-advantage mode.
- [**Demonic Hordes**](https://scryfall.com/card/30a/397/demonic-hordes?utm_source=api), [**Demonic Tutor**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/150/demonic-tutor?utm_source=api), and [**Vampiric Tutor**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/108/vampiric-tutor?utm_source=api) are maybeboard. The [Bird](https://scryfall.com/card/tbng/4/bird?utm_source=api) and [Salamander Warrior](https://scryfall.com/card/tmkc/8/salamander-warrior?utm_source=api) tokens are extras, not deck cards.

## Weaknesses and priorities

The deck folds if Irenicus never stays in play: most presents are actively bad while you still control them. Protect the commander with [**Illusionist's Gambit**](https://scryfall.com/card/c13/47/illusionists-gambit?utm_source=api) on a lethal combat and with cheap counters on the stack. Graveyard recursion is only [**Reanimate**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/155/reanimate?utm_source=api), so do not gift your last body into a board wipe you cannot recover from. Players will exile the locks rather than try to sacrifice them; [**Otawara, Soaring City**](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/271/otawara-soaring-city?utm_source=api) and [**Feed the Swarm**](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/257/feed-the-swarm?utm_source=api) are your answers when a present lands on the wrong seat.
