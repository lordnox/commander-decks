Four-color partners: [**Vial Smasher the Fierce**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/239/vial-smasher-the-fierce?utm_source=api) burns a random opponent for the mana value of your first spell each turn, and [**Ishai, Ojutai Dragonspeaker**](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/12/ishai-ojutai-dragonspeaker?utm_source=api) grows whenever they cast. The 99 is a [Howling Mine](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/257/howling-mine?utm_source=api) / Nekusar pile that feeds everyone extra cards, then charges for every draw and every discard.

## How the deck works

1. Cast [**Vial Smasher the Fierce**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/239/vial-smasher-the-fierce?utm_source=api) on three or [**Ishai, Ojutai Dragonspeaker**](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/12/ishai-ojutai-dragonspeaker?utm_source=api) on four. Smasher wants you to play a spell every turn; [Ishai](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/12/ishai-ojutai-dragonspeaker?utm_source=api) wants the table to keep casting.
2. Put out extra-draw engines: [**Howling Mine**](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/257/howling-mine?utm_source=api), [**Kami of the Crescent Moon**](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/107/kami-of-the-crescent-moon?utm_source=api), [**Font of Mythos**](https://scryfall.com/card/con/136/font-of-mythos?utm_source=api), [**Dictate of Kruphix**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/587/dictate-of-kruphix?utm_source=api), [**Temple Bell**](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/277/temple-bell?utm_source=api), [**Scrawling Crawler**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/132/scrawling-crawler?utm_source=api), [**Stormfist Crusader**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/234/stormfist-crusader?utm_source=api), [**Fevered Visions**](https://scryfall.com/card/soi/244/fevered-visions?utm_source=api), [**Spiteful Visions**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/233/spiteful-visions?utm_source=api), [**Nekusar, the Mindrazer**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/349/nekusar-the-mindrazer?utm_source=api).
3. Put out draw punishers: [**Underworld Dreams**](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/121/underworld-dreams?utm_source=api), [**Fate Unraveler**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/141/fate-unraveler?utm_source=api), [**Razorkin Needlehead**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/153/razorkin-needlehead?utm_source=api), [**Kederekt Parasite**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/144/kederekt-parasite?utm_source=api), [**Sheoldred, the Apocalypse**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/107/sheoldred-the-apocalypse?utm_source=api), [**Ebony Owl Netsuke**](https://scryfall.com/card/sok/154/ebony-owl-netsuke?utm_source=api). Kederekt needs any red permanent; Smasher, [**Nekusar, the Mindrazer**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/349/nekusar-the-mindrazer?utm_source=api), or a [Mountain](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/323/mountain?utm_source=api)-type land is enough.
4. Wheel when the stack is live: [**Windfall**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/123/windfall?utm_source=api), [**Magus of the Wheel**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/241/magus-of-the-wheel?utm_source=api), [**Jace's Archivist**](https://scryfall.com/card/c15/95/jaces-archivist?utm_source=api), [**Dark Deal**](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/161/dark-deal?utm_source=api), [**Burning Inquiry**](https://scryfall.com/card/m10/128/burning-inquiry?utm_source=api), [**Wheel and Deal**](https://scryfall.com/card/ons/121/wheel-and-deal?utm_source=api), [**Molten Psyche**](https://scryfall.com/card/som/98/molten-psyche?utm_source=api). Discard payoffs ([**Liliana's Caress**](https://scryfall.com/card/m11/103/lilianas-caress?utm_source=api), [**Waste Not**](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/117/waste-not?utm_source=api), [**Bone Miser**](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/15/bone-miser?utm_source=api), [**Sangromancer**](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/152/sangromancer?utm_source=api)) convert the discard step into drain, mana, and bodies.
5. Win by stacking damage on extra draws, by [**Psychosis Crawler**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/234/psychosis-crawler?utm_source=api) hitting as you draw, by [**Molten Psyche**](https://scryfall.com/card/som/98/molten-psyche?utm_source=api) with metalcraft after a wheel, or by [**Laboratory Maniac**](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/71/laboratory-maniac?utm_source=api) if Font and Folio empty your library first. [**Ishai, Ojutai Dragonspeaker**](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/12/ishai-ojutai-dragonspeaker?utm_source=api) is a backup flying clock.

There is no two-card infinite. [**Forced Fruition**](https://scryfall.com/card/lrw/66/forced-fruition?utm_source=api) plus [**Sheoldred, the Apocalypse**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/107/sheoldred-the-apocalypse?utm_source=api) is a from-hand dump: each spell an opponent casts draws them seven and costs them fourteen life.

## Main synergy patterns

### Jekyll and Hyde

[**Vial Smasher the Fierce**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/239/vial-smasher-the-fierce?utm_source=api) wants you to cast something every turn, including hug artifacts and wheels. [**Ishai, Ojutai Dragonspeaker**](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/12/ishai-ojutai-dragonspeaker?utm_source=api) wants opponents to keep spending the extra cards you gave them. Play Smasher first when the table is empty; play [Ishai](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/12/ishai-ojutai-dragonspeaker?utm_source=api) when you already have a Mine out and expect a busy stack.

### Extra cards are a tax

A [**Howling Mine**](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/257/howling-mine?utm_source=api) or [**Font of Mythos**](https://scryfall.com/card/con/136/font-of-mythos?utm_source=api) looks generous until [**Sheoldred, the Apocalypse**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/107/sheoldred-the-apocalypse?utm_source=api) or [**Razorkin Needlehead**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/153/razorkin-needlehead?utm_source=api) is in play. Font plus Sheoldred is six life from the extra draw-step cards alone, before anyone wheels. [**Ebony Owl Netsuke**](https://scryfall.com/card/sok/154/ebony-owl-netsuke?utm_source=api) starts hitting for four once those extra cards push a hand to seven.

### Wheel while the meters are running

[**Windfall**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/123/windfall?utm_source=api) and [**Jace's Archivist**](https://scryfall.com/card/c15/95/jaces-archivist?utm_source=api) make every player draw a new hand. Each card drawn triggers the slug permanents, and each card discarded triggers [**Liliana's Caress**](https://scryfall.com/card/m11/103/lilianas-caress?utm_source=api) and [**Waste Not**](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/117/waste-not?utm_source=api). [**Wheel and Deal**](https://scryfall.com/card/ons/121/wheel-and-deal?utm_source=api) skips your own discard. [**Molten Psyche**](https://scryfall.com/card/som/98/molten-psyche?utm_source=api) with three artifacts (rocks plus a Mine or Crawler) deals damage equal to the cards just drawn.

### [Forced Fruition](https://scryfall.com/card/lrw/66/forced-fruition?utm_source=api) is the dump

[**Forced Fruition**](https://scryfall.com/card/lrw/66/forced-fruition?utm_source=api) is not a hug toy once a punisher is out. An opponent who casts a spell draws seven. With Sheoldred that is fourteen life; with Needlehead or Dreams it is seven; with both it stacks. Hold the enchantment until at least one slug permanent is safe.

## Win conditions

- Stacked draw punishers plus extra draw or a wheel drain the table.
- [**Psychosis Crawler**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/234/psychosis-crawler?utm_source=api) plus a large draw (Folio, Font, Archivist) hits each opponent for the cards you draw.
- [**Molten Psyche**](https://scryfall.com/card/som/98/molten-psyche?utm_source=api) metalcraft after a wheel.
- [**Ishai, Ojutai Dragonspeaker**](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/12/ishai-ojutai-dragonspeaker?utm_source=api) commander damage if the slug is removed and [Ishai](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/12/ishai-ojutai-dragonspeaker?utm_source=api) has been growing.
- [**Laboratory Maniac**](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/71/laboratory-maniac?utm_source=api) if you draw through an empty library first.
- [**Breach the Multiverse**](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/94/breach-the-multiverse?utm_source=api) or [**Afterlife from the Loam**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/25/afterlife-from-the-loam?utm_source=api) after graveyards are full.

## Early, mid, and late game

**Early.** Keep hands with two lands, a two-mana rock, and either a cheap Mine effect or a one-to-three-mana punisher. Cast Smasher on three when you have a follow-up spell. Do not wheel into an empty board.

**Mid.** Land the second engine. A Mine without a punisher is group hug; a punisher without extra draw is slow. Sequence Sheoldred or Nekusar before Font if you can. Use [**Smuggler's Share**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/84/smugglers-share?utm_source=api) and [**Faerie Mastermind**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/114/faerie-mastermind?utm_source=api) to get paid for the cards you gave away.

**Late.** Wheel or Folio for the dump. [**Reliquary Tower**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/398/reliquary-tower?utm_source=api) and Folio keep your stuffed hand. If the table has answers, pivot to [Ishai](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/12/ishai-ojutai-dragonspeaker?utm_source=api) attacks or Lab Man. Wipes are for when someone else assembled a board that outraces the drain.

## Mulligan guide

Keep:

- Two or three lands including at least one source toward [**Vial Smasher the Fierce**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/239/vial-smasher-the-fierce?utm_source=api) or a two-drop engine.
- A two-mana rock plus [Howling Mine](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/257/howling-mine?utm_source=api), Kami, Stormfist, Kederekt, or Caress.
- Sheoldred or Nekusar with enough mana to cast them by turn four or five.

Ship:

- All interaction and no engine.
- A wheel with no punisher and no discard payoff.
- One-landers unless the rest is perfect cheap rocks.

## Tutor priorities

The list has no dedicated tutors. [**See Double**](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/77/see-double?utm_source=api) copies a wheel or a punisher. [**Palantír of Orthanc**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/247/palant%C3%ADr-of-orthanc?utm_source=api) is slow selection, not a search.

## Sequencing and rules notes

- [**Kederekt Parasite**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/144/kederekt-parasite?utm_source=api) only pings if you control a red permanent. Smasher, Nekusar, Stormfist, Needlehead, Magus, Red Death, or a [Mountain](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/323/mountain?utm_source=api)-type land all count. Signets and Talismans do not.
- [**Vial Smasher the Fierce**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/239/vial-smasher-the-fierce?utm_source=api) triggers on the first spell you cast each turn, including opponents' turns, and picks the opponent at random. Instant wheels on their turn still ping.
- [**Spiteful Visions**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/233/spiteful-visions?utm_source=api) and [**Nekusar, the Mindrazer**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/349/nekusar-the-mindrazer?utm_source=api) damage you when you draw. [**Sheoldred, the Apocalypse**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmu/107/sheoldred-the-apocalypse?utm_source=api) and [**Sangromancer**](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/152/sangromancer?utm_source=api) are the life buffer.
- [**Laboratory Maniac**](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/71/laboratory-maniac?utm_source=api) wins instead of drawing from an empty library; extra-draw triggers still happen, but that card never reaches your hand.
- [**Forced Fruition**](https://scryfall.com/card/lrw/66/forced-fruition?utm_source=api) is a cast trigger, so it happens before the spell resolves. Countermagic does not stop the seven cards.
- Brotherhood on [**Struggle for Project Purity**](https://scryfall.com/card/pip/39/struggle-for-project-purity?utm_source=api) is the default: each opponent draws one, you draw that many.

## Weaknesses

- The slug permanents are the plan and have almost no protection; one sweep or targeted kill resets the clock.
- No fast mana and no tutors: if both commanders die twice, the pair is expensive.
- Symmetric draw can hand a combo deck its win if you hug without a punisher.
- Effects that cap draws at one turn the hug off and leave you paying [Spiteful Visions](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/233/spiteful-visions?utm_source=api).
- [Ishai](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/12/ishai-ojutai-dragonspeaker?utm_source=api) dies to bounce or an edict and comes back slow.
