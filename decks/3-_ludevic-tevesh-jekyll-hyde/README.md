## How the deck works

The deck has two halves:

1. **Jekyll offers the cards.** [**Howling Mine**](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/257/howling-mine), [**Kami of the Crescent Moon**](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/107/kami-of-the-crescent-moon), [**Temple Bell**](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/277/temple-bell?utm_source=api), [Ludevic](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/37/ludevic-necro-alchemist?utm_source=api), and the political draw spells let everyone keep playing.
2. **Hyde charges for them.** [**Underworld Dreams**](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/121/underworld-dreams), [**Fate Unraveler**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/141/fate-unraveler?utm_source=api), [**Razorkin Needlehead**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/153/razorkin-needlehead), [**Kederekt Parasite**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/144/kederekt-parasite), and other independent permanents make every offered card hurt.

Do not begin with the gift unless the table genuinely needs the help. Establish a punisher or defensive position first, then expose one draw engine. Additional draw engines are closers, not harmless value.

## Win conditions

### One punisher, then a wheel

With one or more draw or discard punishers already visible, resolve [**Windfall**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/123/windfall?utm_source=api), [**Dark Deal**](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/161/dark-deal?utm_source=api), [**Wheel and Deal**](https://scryfall.com/card/ons/121/wheel-and-deal?utm_source=api), or [**Magus of the Wheel**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/241/magus-of-the-wheel?utm_source=api). Count draw and discard triggers separately for each opponent. This is the normal finish after several turns of incremental damage.

### [Forced Fruition](https://scryfall.com/card/lrw/66/forced-fruition?utm_source=api) reveals Hyde

[**Forced Fruition**](https://scryfall.com/card/lrw/66/forced-fruition) triggers whenever an opponent casts a spell. Behind one one-damage punisher, each spell adds seven damage; behind several, an opponent can no longer cast safely. Fruition does nothing to protect itself, so cast it only after punishment is present and preferably when [**Arcane Denial**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/147/arcane-denial?utm_source=api) or another answer is available.

### Target the player who drew too much

[**Cerebral Vortex**](https://scryfall.com/card/gpt/107/cerebral-vortex) draws its target two cards, then counts every card that player has drawn that turn. After a wheel or Fruition trigger, it can remove the surviving player without needing another permanent.

[**Psychosis Crawler**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/234/psychosis-crawler) and [**Black Waltz No. 3**](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/214/black-waltz-no-3) provide the backup route: the deck's own cards drawn and noncreature spells keep damaging the table even when opposing-draw punishment is absent.

## Game plan

### Early game

Prioritize three lands or two lands plus a two-mana rock. A hand should also contain a cheap punisher, a deterrent, or interaction. [**Baleful Strix**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/215/baleful-strix) is ideal on turn two because it replaces itself and changes combat immediately. [**Razorkin Needlehead**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/153/razorkin-needlehead) or [**Stormfist Crusader**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/234/stormfist-crusader?utm_source=api) starts the clock.

[Ludevic](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/37/ludevic-necro-alchemist?utm_source=api) is a turn-three political play only when an opponent is likely to lose life without encouraging a free attack at this seat. The deck does not need either commander before deploying its first engine.

### Midgame

Tevesh normally enters when the table is beginning to pressure life totals. Use `+2` first: two Thrulls protect him and provide material for [**Victimize**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/198/victimize?utm_source=api). Use `+1` on a spare Thrull or spent utility creature when cards are more important than blockers.

Keep one hidden answer available if possible. Deathtouch discourages one large attacker; [**Fire Covenant**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/54/fire-covenant) and [**Sickening Dreams**](https://scryfall.com/card/tor/83/sickening-dreams) handle width, while [**Soul Shatter**](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/127/soul-shatter) and [**Make an Example**](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/37/make-an-example) bypass hexproof Voltron commanders.

### Late game

Stop giving cards merely for value. Count the damage from each draw and discard, identify the opponent least exposed to it, and choose a wheel that preserves the needed hand. [**Wheel and Deal**](https://scryfall.com/card/ons/121/wheel-and-deal?utm_source=api) leaves your own hand intact; [**Khorvath's Fury**](https://scryfall.com/card/bbd/59/khorvaths-fury) separates friends from foes; [**Urza's Guilt**](https://scryfall.com/card/pls/130/urzas-guilt?utm_source=api) touches draw, discard, and life totals simultaneously.

## Mulligan guide

Keep:

- three lands, or two lands plus a castable two-mana rock;
- access to blue and black by turn two or three;
- one punisher, deterrent, or defensive answer;
- a draw engine only when the hand can avoid helping the table before it is safe.

Mulligan:

- two-land hands whose only support costs three or more;
- hands made entirely of Mine effects and wheels with no punishment;
- hands made entirely of punishers with no cards or mana to bridge into the midgame;
- triple-black cards without a credible third black source.

## Sequencing and politics

- **Teeth before gifts.** A turn-two [Howling Mine](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/257/howling-mine?utm_source=api) is not acceleration for this deck; it is acceleration for three opponents. Delay it unless the table bargain is worth that cost.
- **[Ludevic](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/37/ludevic-necro-alchemist?utm_source=api) rewards damage elsewhere.** Explain that attacking another opponent can unlock the active player's end-step card. Attacking only you does not satisfy [Ludevic](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/37/ludevic-necro-alchemist?utm_source=api).
- **Hold [Temple Bell](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/277/temple-bell?utm_source=api).** Its tap ability is optional timing. Use it after a punisher resolves, to help an opponent find an answer to a shared threat, or immediately before your untap.
- **Kederekt needs a red permanent.** Lands that produce red are normally colourless permanents and do not enable it. [Ludevic](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/37/ludevic-necro-alchemist?utm_source=api) and the red engine permanents do.
- **Do not sacrifice [Ludevic](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/37/ludevic-necro-alchemist?utm_source=api) casually.** Tevesh draws three for sacrificing another commander, but losing the political engine and paying commander tax is rarely worth one additional card.
- **[Arcane Denial](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/147/arcane-denial?utm_source=api) pays later.** The countered spell's controller may draw two during their next upkeep; those draws become damage when a punisher survives.

## Weaknesses

- The deck can still accelerate opponents if it draws gifts without punishment. Mulligan and sequence against that failure.
- Enchantment removal can erase several engines over a game. [**Crystal Chimes**](https://scryfall.com/card/c15/250/crystal-chimes?utm_source=api) is slow, so do not deploy every enchantment into one wipe.
- Draw limits and replacement effects can blank wheels. [**Chaos Warp**](https://scryfall.com/card/mbc/72/chaos-warp?utm_source=api), [**Otawara, Soaring City**](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/271/otawara-soaring-city), and [Arcane Denial](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/147/arcane-denial?utm_source=api) are reserved for these plan-breaking permanents.
- Deathtouch changes attack selection but does not prevent trample damage or several attackers. Keep the ambush cards hidden.
- [**Phyrexian Tyranny**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/262/phyrexian-tyranny?utm_source=api) and [**Spiteful Visions**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/233/spiteful-visions?utm_source=api) also tax their controller. [**Sangromancer**](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/152/sangromancer) is the principal way to recover that life.
