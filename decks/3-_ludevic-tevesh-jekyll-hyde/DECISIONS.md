## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, primer, and assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a cut back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.

Every unique card needs a Cards in line. The other headings appear when there is something to record.

## Primer

- Normal play should establish an independent punisher before exposing large symmetric draw. Forced Fruition is the Hyde reveal, not an early value play.
- The deck is expected to threaten a decisive wheel around turns seven or eight. A rare earlier kill requires several visible punishers and is not the advertised pace.

## Rules

- **Kederekt Parasite** needs a red permanent, not merely a land that produces red. Ludevic, Stormfist Crusader, Fevered Visions, Spiteful Visions, Red Death, Nekusar, Glint-Horn Buccaneer, and Black Waltz can enable it.
- **Ludevic** checks whether a player other than Ludevic's controller lost life during the turn. A draw-punisher damaging an opponent satisfies that condition; only the active player's end step normally occurs each turn.
- **Forced Fruition** triggers when an opponent casts a spell. Countering that spell does not remove the seven-card trigger already on the stack.
- **Cerebral Vortex** counts every card the target has drawn during the turn, including cards drawn before Vortex resolves.
- **Sickening Dreams** discards cards as an additional cost. Bone Miser and Glint-Horn Buccaneer trigger during casting and their triggers resolve above Sickening Dreams.
- **Illusionist's Gambit** removes attackers from combat and protects both the player and Tevesh; the additional combat must attack elsewhere if able.
- **Make an Example** works against a lone creature because its controller must make two piles and the populated pile can be chosen.

## Talks

### 2026-09-15 — Autonomous rebuild lock

Plan: establish commander-independent draw punishers, use political and symmetric draw to create cards and pressure, then convert a wheel into the final table-wide damage. Backup route: Tevesh and deterrent creatures stabilize while Psychosis Crawler, Black Waltz, or discard punishment converts the pilot's own draw and wheel costs.

Target: Bracket 3−, normal decisive turn seven or eight, earliest credible high roll around turn six, no tutors, compact combos, Game Changers, permanent fast mana, or free protection. Turn-five milestone: four mana sources plus either a punisher and draw engine, or a defensive permanent with interaction available. Commander removal must not switch off the 99.

Commander comparison:

| Pair | What it supplies | Why it lost or won |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ludevic + Tevesh Szat | Grixis, political draw, blockers, removal-resistant cards | **Chosen:** both commanders support survival and cards without exposing the damage engine |
| Vial Smasher + Kydele | Four colours, large mana after drawing | Green adds redundant group draw, the pair advertises cEDH power, and Vial's random damage is incidental |
| Ludevic + Silas Renn | Grixis and artifact recursion | Strong recovery, but it distorts the 99 toward combat and artifacts |
| Vial Smasher + Ishai | Four colours and two unrelated damage clocks | White contributed little and Ishai required an unsupported Voltron plan |

Packages:

| Package | Job | Failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draw bargain | Offer cards through Mine effects, Ludevic, Temple Bell, Vision Skeins, and wheels | Casting gifts before punishment accelerates opponents |
| Punishment | Underworld Dreams family, discard payoffs, and self-draw conversion | Multiple payoffs without an enabler do little |
| Ambush defence | Strix and Waltz deter attacks; Darkness, Gambit, edicts, and selective wipes punish commitment | Deathtouch alone does not stop wide boards or trample damage |
| Recovery | Victimize, Phyrexian Reclamation, Crystal Chimes, Takenuma | Artifact engines remain the hardest permanent type to recover |

Mana starts at 37 true lands, no MDFC spell backs, and nine two-mana ramp pieces. Blue is needed on turn two for Strix and the draw engine; black is needed early for Darkness and reaches triple black for Underworld Dreams; red becomes important from turn three for wheels and Ludevic. Xander's Lounge is the only always-tapped land; it brings all three basic land types and cycling.
