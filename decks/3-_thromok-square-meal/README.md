## How the deck works

1. Develop Forests and dorks. One-drops are mana and later meals.
2. Print tokens: goblins, saprolings, Spawn, Mitotic splits, or [Kher Keep](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/270/kher-keep?utm_source=api) kobolds.
3. Cast a devourer with the buffet on the table. Thromok wants four or more bodies (16/16). [Mycoloth](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/276/mycoloth?utm_source=api) wants two or three so the upkeep saprolings refill the plate.
4. Give the leftover giant haste and trample, or convert it with [Fling](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/320/fling?utm_source=api), [Pandemonium](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/TPR-149/pandemonium?utm_source=api), or [Greater Good](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/174/greater-good?utm_source=api).
5. If Thromok is taxed, bounce it with [Temur Sabertooth](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/315/temur-sabertooth?utm_source=api) or [Erratic Portal](https://scryfall.com/card/mb2/220/erratic-portal?utm_source=api) once more meals exist, or win with the token pile and [Second Harvest](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/213/second-harvest?utm_source=api).

## Win conditions

- Combat: Thromok or a Devour 2–3 dragon with haste/trample. Five meals on Thromok is 25 commander damage if it connects.
- Direct damage: [Pandemonium](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/TPR-149/pandemonium?utm_source=api) on a large enter, [Fling](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/320/fling?utm_source=api) / [Kazuul's Fury](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/146/kazuuls-fury-kazuuls-cliffs?utm_source=api) / [Grab the Reins](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/126/grab-the-reins?utm_source=api).
- Backup: token army after [Second Harvest](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/213/second-harvest?utm_source=api) or [Mycoloth](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/276/mycoloth?utm_source=api) upkeeps, with [Squirrel Nest](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/177/squirrel-nest?utm_source=api) adding a body every turn.

None of these lines are infinite. [Pattern of Rebirth](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/176/pattern-of-rebirth?utm_source=api) finding [Mycoloth](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/276/mycoloth?utm_source=api) is a finite setup, and [Goblin Warrens](https://scryfall.com/card/6ed/187/goblin-warrens?utm_source=api) nets one body per activation for three mana.

## Early, mid, and late

**Early:** Keep two lands of both colors or a dork plus a [Forest](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/325/forest?utm_source=api). A turn-two [Nest Invader](https://scryfall.com/card/pca/69/nest-invader?utm_source=api), [Mogg War Marshal](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/DMR-131/mogg-war-marshal?utm_source=api), or [Thallid Devourer](https://scryfall.com/card/fem/75/thallid-devourer?utm_source=api) is a real start. Mulligan a hand with only six-drops.

**Mid:** Hold Thromok until you have meals. Four tokens is the first serious swallow. [Mycoloth](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/276/mycoloth?utm_source=api) with two meals is often the better turn-five play if Thromok would be a 4/4.

**Late:** Bounce-and-eat, [Sneak Attack](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/139/sneak-attack?utm_source=api) a second devourer, or [Greater Good](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/174/greater-good?utm_source=api) the giant and recast. [Caldera Hellion](https://scryfall.com/card/ala/95/caldera-hellion?utm_source=api) can reset small boards if you fed it enough to live through 3 damage.

## Mulligan

Keep:

- Two lands (ideally [Forest](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/325/forest?utm_source=api) plus red) and a one- or two-mana play.
- Token maker plus a devourer in the four-to-five range.
- [Sneak Attack](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/139/sneak-attack?utm_source=api) plus any devourer and a red source.

Put back:

- All finishers, no meals.
- Thromok plus lands and no way to have creatures by turn five.
- Heavy red with no green for the auras and Claims.
- The trap keep: four lands and two ramp spells with no token maker. It reads like a fine hand and produces a 4/4 Thromok on turn six.

## Tutor priorities

[Pattern of Rebirth](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/176/pattern-of-rebirth?utm_source=api) on a token you intend to eat:

1. [Mycoloth](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/276/mycoloth?utm_source=api) if you need a refill engine.
2. Thromok only if he is stuck in the command zone under tax and you have a large board.
3. [Eternal Witness](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/286/eternal-witness?utm_source=api) to recast Pattern or a Burst.
4. [Caldera Hellion](https://scryfall.com/card/ala/95/caldera-hellion?utm_source=api) or [Ravenous Tyrannosaurus](https://scryfall.com/card/mar/93/ravenous-tyrannosaurus?utm_source=api) if the table is clogged.

## Sequencing and rules

- Devour happens as the creature enters. Power is already set for [Pandemonium](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/TPR-149/pandemonium?utm_source=api) and [Skullmulcher](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/180/skullmulcher?utm_source=api).
- Do not eat every creature if you need blockers or convoke for [Sprout Swarm](https://scryfall.com/card/fut/138/sprout-swarm?utm_source=api).
- [Caldera Hellion](https://scryfall.com/card/ala/95/caldera-hellion?utm_source=api) needs toughness 4+ after devour or it dies to its own 3 damage.
- [Saproling Burst](https://scryfall.com/card/wc01/jt113/saproling-burst?utm_source=api) tokens vanish if Burst leaves; swallow them first if that is the plan.
- [Voracious Dragon](https://scryfall.com/card/ddg/56/voracious-dragon?utm_source=api) only counts Goblins among the creatures it ate.
- [Mycoloth](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/276/mycoloth?utm_source=api) makes a saproling per +1/+1 counter, not per power. Cast with nothing to eat, it is a 4/4 that makes nothing; feed it at least one meal or it is not an engine.
- [Goblin Warrens](https://scryfall.com/card/6ed/187/goblin-warrens?utm_source=api) sacrifices as a cost, so those goblins die for [Fecundity](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/165/fecundity?utm_source=api) and [Mogg War Marshal](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/DMR-131/mogg-war-marshal?utm_source=api). That is not devour; only a creature entering can devour.
- [Fists of Ironwood](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/139/fists-of-ironwood?utm_source=api) needs a creature already in play, so it is not a turn-two play on an empty board. It is best on a token you are about to eat anyway.
- [Deranged Hermit](https://scryfall.com/card/g04/5/deranged-hermit?utm_source=api) echo is often worth skipping: let the Hermit die, keep the three squirrels, and spend the mana on the eater instead.

## Weaknesses

- Graveyard hate barely matters; exile wipes and Rest in Peace-style effects are fine, but board wipes before you swallow are painful.
- A wide board of blockers stops the single-giant clock; keep a [Fling](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/320/fling?utm_source=api) effect or trample rather than attacking into it.
- Thromok removed twice is the hard case: at seven mana he needs a fresh buffet in the same turn, which realistically means [Temur Sabertooth](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/315/temur-sabertooth?utm_source=api) or a standing [Squirrel Nest](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/177/squirrel-nest?utm_source=api).
- Commander tax without bounce makes Thromok a 7+ mana 0/0 that still needs meals.
- No blue stack interaction. [Rhythm of the Wild](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/217/rhythm-of-the-wild?utm_source=api) and [Vines of Vastwood](https://scryfall.com/card/mm2/168/vines-of-vastwood?utm_source=api) are the counter-spell answers.
