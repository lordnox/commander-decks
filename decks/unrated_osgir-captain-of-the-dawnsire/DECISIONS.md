# Decisions

## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, the primer, and the assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a cut back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated session notes so an argument does not live only in chat.

Every unique card needs a Cards in line. The other headings appear when there is something to record.

## Primer

- The namesake is Dawnsire as a stationed closer, not as a Voltron commander. Osgir copies of Dawnsire are legendary, so the legend rule keeps one token.
- Arcbond plus a wipe is finite table damage, not a loop. Phyrexian Vindicator prevents damage, so Arcbond does not see those events.

## Rules

- **Osgir copies.** The ability is a sorcery-speed tap that exiles an artifact card from your graveyard and makes two tokens that are copies of that card. Nonlegendary artifacts yield two permanents. Legendary artifacts, including Dawnsire, yield two legendary tokens and the legend rule leaves one.
- **Arcbond vs Vindicator.** Arcbond triggers when the chosen creature is *dealt* damage. Phyrexian Vindicator prevents the damage and then deals that much to another target, so Arcbond does not trigger from that prevented event.
- **Arcbond vs Gideon's Sacrifice.** Replacement that changes the recipient still deals the damage to the chosen creature, so Arcbond and reflectors see it.
- **Boros Reckoner.** `{R/W}: this creature deals 1 damage to target creature` can target itself and start its own reflection trigger.
- **Tune Up.** The Vehicle clause does not animate Spacecraft. Dawnsire and Warmaker stay noncreatures until stationed.
- **Wrathful Red Dragon.** The delayed damage is dealt by the Dragon that was damaged, and the target cannot be a Dragon.
