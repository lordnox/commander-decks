Boros reconstruction: dump cheap artifacts, exile them with [**Osgir, the Reconstructor**](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/8/osgir-the-reconstructor?utm_source=api), and keep two copies. The copies that matter are damage reflectors and the named ship, [**Dawnsire, Sunstar Dreadnought**](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/238/dawnsire-sunstar-dreadnought?utm_source=api). Feed those bodies with red sweepers and [**Arcbond**](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/91/arcbond?utm_source=api), then station Dawnsire so an attack step can delete a creature or planeswalker.

**Commander:** [**Osgir, the Reconstructor**](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/8/osgir-the-reconstructor?utm_source=api)<br>
**Deck size:** 100 cards<br>
**Primary plan:** reconstruct reflectors and wellsprings, then convert a wipe or a fight into player damage<br>
**Backup plan:** station [**Dawnsire, Sunstar Dreadnought**](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/238/dawnsire-sunstar-dreadnought?utm_source=api) and win through combat and 100-damage station triggers

## How the deck works

[Osgir](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/8/osgir-the-reconstructor?utm_source=api) wants artifacts in the graveyard, not a pretty battlefield. Sacrifice a wellspring, statue, or [Nutrient Block](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/243/nutrient-block?utm_source=api), then pay its mana value to exile that card and make two tokens. Cheap rocks and spellbombs are fuel. [Goblin Welder](https://scryfall.com/card/cm2/101/goblin-welder?utm_source=api) and [Goblin Engineer](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/128/goblin-engineer?utm_source=api) move the same pieces without waiting for [Osgir](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/8/osgir-the-reconstructor?utm_source=api). [Oswald Fiddlebender](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/28/oswald-fiddlebender?utm_source=api) climbs mana value one step at a time.

The copies you actually want are [Brash Taunter](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/161/brash-taunter?utm_source=api), [Boros Reckoner](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/201/boros-reckoner?utm_source=api), [Spitemare](https://scryfall.com/card/ddh/16/spitemare?utm_source=api), [Mogg Maniac](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/STH-94/mogg-maniac?utm_source=api), and [Truefire Captain](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/235/truefire-captain?utm_source=api). Those creatures turn damage dealt to them into damage to players or any target. [Phyrexian Vindicator](https://scryfall.com/card/one/27/phyrexian-vindicator?utm_source=api) is the exception: it *prevents* the damage and throws the prevented amount elsewhere, so it does not feed [Arcbond](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/91/arcbond?utm_source=api).

Once a reflector is in play, manufacture the hit. Taunter fights. Reckoner pings itself. [Gideon's Sacrifice](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/68/gideons-sacrifice?utm_source=api) dumps a whole turn of damage onto one body. [Blasphemous Act](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/163/blasphemous-act?utm_source=api), [Chain Reaction](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/121/chain-reaction?utm_source=api), and [Spiteful Banditry](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/149/spiteful-banditry?utm_source=api) hit every creature, including yours. [Arcbond](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/91/arcbond?utm_source=api) on a reflector then makes that one damage event also hit every other creature and each player. Equip [Basilisk Collar](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/669/basilisk-collar?utm_source=api) or [Shadowspear](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/236/shadowspear?utm_source=api) if you need the life.

[Dawnsire, Sunstar Dreadnought](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/238/dawnsire-sunstar-dreadnought?utm_source=api) is the other closer. Station it with tapped creatures (Losheel's artifact attackers are ideal). At 10+ charge counters, each attack step can deal 100 to a creature or planeswalker. At 20+ it flies. [Osgir](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/8/osgir-the-reconstructor?utm_source=api) can copy it, but both tokens are legendary: the legend rule leaves one. [Warmaker Gunship](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/167/warmaker-gunship?utm_source=api) is the smaller station ship; [RMS Titanic](https://scryfall.com/card/who/93/rms-titanic?utm_source=api) is the Vehicle that dies into Treasures.

## Main lines

**Wellspring loop (value, not infinite).** Sacrifice [Ichor Wellspring](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/245/ichor-wellspring?utm_source=api) or [Prized Statue](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/334/prized-statue?utm_source=api) to [Osgir](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/8/osgir-the-reconstructor?utm_source=api)'s pump or to Oswald. Draw or [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/f17/11/dinosaur-treasure?utm_source=api). [Osgir](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/8/osgir-the-reconstructor?utm_source=api) exiles the card and makes two copies. Each copy enters and dies later for more cards. Welder can swap a copy for a reflector sitting in the yard.

**Fight and ping.** Put [Brash Taunter](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/161/brash-taunter?utm_source=api) or a copy into play. Fight a large creature, or have [Boros Reckoner](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/201/boros-reckoner?utm_source=api) deal 1 to itself. The reflection trigger aims at a player. [Donna Noble](https://scryfall.com/card/who/82/donna-noble?utm_source=api) soulbonded to that body doubles the ping.

**[Arcbond](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/91/arcbond?utm_source=api) wipe.** Cast [Arcbond](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/91/arcbond?utm_source=api) targeting an indestructible or large reflector, then [Blasphemous Act](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/163/blasphemous-act?utm_source=api) or [Chain Reaction](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/121/chain-reaction?utm_source=api). The wipe deals N to the reflector. [Arcbond](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/91/arcbond?utm_source=api) makes that creature deal N to each other creature and each player. The reflector's own trigger still fires. This is one lethal burst, not a loop. Do not [Arcbond](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/91/arcbond?utm_source=api) [Phyrexian Vindicator](https://scryfall.com/card/one/27/phyrexian-vindicator?utm_source=api): prevented damage never triggers [Arcbond](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/91/arcbond?utm_source=api).

**Station Dawnsire.** Tap creatures to put charge counters on [Dawnsire](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/238/dawnsire-sunstar-dreadnought?utm_source=api) as a sorcery. At 10+, attack with anything; the 100-damage trigger can clear a blocker or a walker. [Losheel, Clockwork Scholar](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/38/losheel-clockwork-scholar?utm_source=api) lets artifact-creature attackers ignore combat damage while they station.

## Win conditions

- Reflector plus manufactured damage (fight, self-ping, routed wipe) kills players.
- [Arcbond](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/91/arcbond?utm_source=api) plus a board-wide damage spell hits the table at once.
- Stationed Dawnsire deletes blockers, then combat and later station triggers finish.
- [Cavern-Hoard Dragon](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/31/cavern-hoard-dragon?utm_source=api) and [Atsushi, the Blazing Sky](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/236/atsushi-the-blazing-sky?utm_source=api) with [Wrathful Red Dragon](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/207/wrathful-red-dragon?utm_source=api) turn Dragon damage into extra pings.

## Early, mid, and late game

**Early.** Keep artifact lands, a two-mana rock, and a cheap wellspring or Engineer. Play [Osgir](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/8/osgir-the-reconstructor?utm_source=api) when you can activate the same turn or the next. Do not empty the hand of interactable artifacts before he arrives.

**Mid.** Reconstruct the first reflector or a pair of wellsprings. [Patriar's Seal](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/332/patriars-seal?utm_source=api) untaps [Osgir](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/8/osgir-the-reconstructor?utm_source=api) for a second activation. Hold a wipe or [Arcbond](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/91/arcbond?utm_source=api) until a reflector can survive it. Start stationing Dawnsire if the table is clogged.

**Late.** [Copy](https://scryfall.com/card/tmsc/1/copy?utm_source=api) the best reflector you have already used. Overload [Vandalblast](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/168/vandalblast?utm_source=api) only when you will not need your own rocks. Station Dawnsire to 20 if you need a flyer. [Seize the Spotlight](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/179/seize-the-spotlight?utm_source=api) is a political steal or a [Treasure](https://scryfall.com/card/f17/11/dinosaur-treasure?utm_source=api) refill.

## Mulligan

Keep: two lands of both colors or a dual plus a rock, one cheap artifact to sacrifice, and either a reflector, Engineer/Welder, or [Osgir](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/8/osgir-the-reconstructor?utm_source=api)-relevant draw. Six-land hands with no artifacts bounce. A Dawnsire without station fodder or [Osgir](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/8/osgir-the-reconstructor?utm_source=api) is a slow keep only if the rest of the hand rebuilds.

## Sequencing and rules

- [Osgir](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/8/osgir-the-reconstructor?utm_source=api)'s copy ability is a sorcery and taps him. Untap with [Patriar's Seal](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/332/patriars-seal?utm_source=api) before trying twice.
- Two copies of a legendary artifact (Dawnsire) leave one token.
- [Tune Up](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/33/tune-up?utm_source=api) animates Vehicles, not Spacecraft.
- [Gideon's Sacrifice](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/68/gideons-sacrifice?utm_source=api) replacement still *deals* the damage to the chosen creature, so reflectors and [Arcbond](https://scryfall.com/card/frf/91/arcbond?utm_source=api) see it.
- [Wrathful Red Dragon](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/207/wrathful-red-dragon?utm_source=api)'s damage cannot target a Dragon.

## Weaknesses

Graveyard hate stops [Osgir](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/8/osgir-the-reconstructor?utm_source=api) cold; Welder and Engineer are the backup. The reflector plan needs a damage event, so a fog or a board that never fights stalls you. Opponent hexproof and indestructible blank Dawnsire's 100-damage trigger unless [Shadowspear](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/236/shadowspear?utm_source=api) has already stripped them. The mana base is heavy on basics and tapped artifact lands, so keep mixed sources.
