A Naya ETB party: [**Rocco, Cabaretti Caterer**](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/218/rocco-cabaretti-caterer?utm_source=api) fetches the guest of honor, [**Norin the Wary**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsp/171/norin-the-wary?utm_source=api), who spends the rest of the game walking in and out of exile. Every return is another creature entering, which the ping suite turns into damage, the draw suite turns into cards, and [**Aura Shards**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmd/182/aura-shards?utm_source=api) turns into artifact and enchantment removal. Combat is the backup when [**Champion of Lambholt**](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/293/champion-of-lambholt?utm_source=api) outgrows the table.

## How the deck works

1. Ramp into [**Rocco, Cabaretti Caterer**](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/218/rocco-cabaretti-caterer?utm_source=api) and pay X for the creature the board actually needs: [**Norin the Wary**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsp/171/norin-the-wary?utm_source=api) at X=1, a ping body at two or three, or [**Champion of Lambholt**](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/293/champion-of-lambholt?utm_source=api) when you already have blinkers.
2. Keep [Norin](https://scryfall.com/card/tsp/171/norin-the-wary?utm_source=api) (or [**Saltskitter**](https://scryfall.com/card/fut/14/saltskitter?utm_source=api)) blinking. A spell or an attack exiles him; he returns at the next end step. That is typically one enter per turn that saw a spell or an attack, so a four-player table can give you several enters per round.
3. Convert those enters: pings ([**Impact Tremors**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/717/impact-tremors?utm_source=api), [**Witty Roastmaster**](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/131/witty-roastmaster?utm_source=api), [**Molten Gatekeeper**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/128/molten-gatekeeper?utm_source=api), [**Agate Instigator**](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/BLC-21/agate-instigator?utm_source=api)) for the clock, once-per-turn draws ([**Tocasia's Welcome**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/181/tocasias-welcome?utm_source=api), [**Welcoming Vampire**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/140/welcoming-vampire?utm_source=api), [**Enduring Innocence**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/6/enduring-innocence?utm_source=api)) for fuel, and [**Aura Shards**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmd/182/aura-shards?utm_source=api) to clear opposing engines.
4. Double the relevant triggers with [**Panharmonicon**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/310/panharmonicon?utm_source=api) and [**Gandalf the White**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/19/gandalf-the-white?utm_source=api), then close with stacked pings or an unblockable [**Champion of Lambholt**](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/293/champion-of-lambholt?utm_source=api) swing, with [**End-Raze Forerunners**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/822/end-raze-forerunners?utm_source=api) as the late combat overlay.

There is no infinite loop. The deck wins because creatures keep entering.

## Main synergy patterns

### Norin is one enter per involved turn

[**Norin the Wary**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsp/171/norin-the-wary?utm_source=api) triggers while he is on the battlefield. A spell or an attacking creature exiles him; he returns at the beginning of the **next** end step. Spells cast while he is already in exile do not retrigger. Count on one enter at the end of each turn that had a spell or an attack, not a storm of enters on a single stack.

[**Saltskitter**](https://scryfall.com/card/fut/14/saltskitter?utm_source=api) instead leaves whenever **another** creature enters, so it blinks off Norin, tokens, and opposing creatures. It is the backup blinker when Norin is in the yard or [Rocco](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/218/rocco-cabaretti-caterer?utm_source=api) is taxed.

### Pings stack; Torbran multiplies red ones

Each ping piece is a separate trigger. Two Tremors-style effects deal 2 to each opponent per enter; [**Panharmonicon**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/310/panharmonicon?utm_source=api) copies those triggers when a creature (or artifact) entering caused them. [**Torbran, Thane of Red Fell**](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/147/torbran-thane-of-red-fell?utm_source=api) adds 2 to each red hit against opponents, so a single [**Impact Tremors**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/717/impact-tremors?utm_source=api) becomes 3 per opponent per enter.

[**Outpost Siege**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/804/outpost-siege?utm_source=api) on Dragons pings when a creature you control **leaves**, which is Norin's exile trigger, not his return. [**Arbaaz Mir**](https://scryfall.com/card/acr/46/arbaaz-mir?utm_source=api) needs a nontoken **historic** enter: Norin, other legends, and artifacts count; generic tokens do not.

### [Aura Shards](https://scryfall.com/card/cmd/182/aura-shards?utm_source=api) is the Game Changer

Every creature you control that enters may destroy an artifact or enchantment. With Norin looping, that is optional, repeatable, and table-wide. It is the reason this list is not a Core deck. Use it on the engine that would stop the pings, not on every mana rock on sight if politics still matter.

### Champion is the combat plan

[**Champion of Lambholt**](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/293/champion-of-lambholt?utm_source=api) grows on every other creature enter. Once its power exceeds the blockers, the whole team is unblockable. Tokens from [**Genesis Chamber**](https://scryfall.com/card/bbd/235/genesis-chamber?utm_source=api), [**Blessed Sanctuary**](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/1/blessed-sanctuary?utm_source=api), and [**Saradoc, Master of Buckland**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/282/saradoc-master-of-buckland?utm_source=api) feed it. [**End-Raze Forerunners**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/822/end-raze-forerunners?utm_source=api) then gives the team +2/+2, vigilance, and trample for the actual lethal swing.

### Kodama is not a loop

[**Kodama of the East Tree**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/239/kodama-of-the-east-tree?utm_source=api) puts a lesser-or-equal permanent from hand onto the battlefield when another permanent enters **without** using this ability. Bounce lands ([**Boros Garrison**](https://scryfall.com/card/clu/231/boros-garrison?utm_source=api) and friends) let you replay a land and drop another cheap permanent. That is burst value, not an infinite: each extra permanent still has to come from hand.

## Win conditions

- **Stacked ETB pings**: two or three ping pieces plus a blinker drain 40 over a few turn cycles; [**Torbran, Thane of Red Fell**](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/147/torbran-thane-of-red-fell?utm_source=api) or [**Panharmonicon**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/310/panharmonicon?utm_source=api) shortens that.
- **Unblockable combat**: [**Champion of Lambholt**](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/293/champion-of-lambholt?utm_source=api) plus tokens, finished by [**End-Raze Forerunners**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/822/end-raze-forerunners?utm_source=api) or [**Legolas Greenleaf**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/40/legolas-greenleaf?utm_source=api) / [**Gimli of the Glittering Caves**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/32/gimli-of-the-glittering-caves?utm_source=api) as extra combat damage.
- [**Rose Room Treasurer**](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/51/rose-room-treasurer?utm_source=api): after the first two Alliance Treasures, later enters let you pay X to deal X; a late mana dump finishes a wounded player.

## Game progression

### Early game

Ramp: [**Rampant Growth**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/265/rampant-growth?utm_source=api), [**Farseek**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/173/farseek?utm_source=api), [**Sakura-Tribe Elder**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/285/sakura-tribe-elder?utm_source=api), Talismans, [**Arcane Signet**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/192/arcane-signet?utm_source=api). A turn-three or turn-four [Rocco](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/218/rocco-cabaretti-caterer?utm_source=api) for [Norin](https://scryfall.com/card/tsp/171/norin-the-wary?utm_source=api) is the default. Keep a one-mana removal or [**Fog**](https://scryfall.com/card/ema/167/fog?utm_source=api) if a seat is clearly aggro.

### Mid game

Land the first ping or [**Aura Shards**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmd/182/aura-shards?utm_source=api), then a draw enchantment that triggers once per turn so Norin still pays you. [**Gala Greeters**](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/148/gala-greeters?utm_source=api) and [**Rose Room Treasurer**](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/51/rose-room-treasurer?utm_source=api) convert extra enters into Treasure. Recast [Rocco](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/218/rocco-cabaretti-caterer?utm_source=api) for Champion or a second ping if the first guest died.

### Late game

Copy triggers, grow Champion, and pick a lane: ping the table out or swing through. [**Austere Command**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/121/austere-command?utm_source=api) and [**Dusk // Dawn**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/61/dusk-dawn?utm_source=api) reset wide boards while leaving small Norin-sized creatures; [**Blasphemous Act**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/163/blasphemous-act?utm_source=api) is the panic button when you are behind.

## Mulligan guide

Keep hands with:

- two or three lands that can make [Rocco](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/218/rocco-cabaretti-caterer?utm_source=api)'s three colors by turn four;
- a two-mana ramp spell or rock;
- at least one ping, draw-on-ETB, or cheap blinker if [Rocco](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/218/rocco-cabaretti-caterer?utm_source=api) is going to be late.

Mulligan one-land hands, all five-plus drops, and hands with only sweepers.

## Important sequencing and rules notes

- [**Rocco, Cabaretti Caterer**](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/218/rocco-cabaretti-caterer?utm_source=api) searches only if you **cast** him. Blink, reanimation, and putting him onto the battlefield some other way skip the tutor.
- [**Panharmonicon**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/310/panharmonicon?utm_source=api) cares that an **artifact or creature** entering caused the trigger. Creature-ETB pings on enchantments still double. Enchantments entering do not.
- [**Gandalf the White**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/19/gandalf-the-white?utm_source=api) doubles triggers caused by a **legendary or artifact** entering or leaving. Norin is both a leave and an enter; token Myr from [**Genesis Chamber**](https://scryfall.com/card/bbd/235/genesis-chamber?utm_source=api) are artifacts and also double.
- [**Genesis Chamber**](https://scryfall.com/card/bbd/235/genesis-chamber?utm_source=api) is symmetric while **untapped** and has no ability to tap itself off; bounce or destroy it if opponents are getting too many Myr.
- [**Blessed Sanctuary**](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/1/blessed-sanctuary?utm_source=api) prevents **noncombat** damage to you and your creatures. It does not stop your pings from hitting opponents. It does stop opposing burn aimed at you.
- [**Confusion in the Ranks**](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/87/confusion-in-the-ranks?utm_source=api) exchanges permanents on **every** artifact, creature, or enchantment enter, including Norin's. You will steal and donate constantly; play it only when you want chaos, not when you need a stable ping board.
- [**Freestrider Lookout**](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/163/freestrider-lookout?utm_source=api) ramps once per turn when you commit a crime (targeting an opponent). Removal, [**Aura Shards**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmd/182/aura-shards?utm_source=api), and [**Skullwinder**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/207/skullwinder?utm_source=api) all count.
- [**Dusk // Dawn**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/61/dusk-dawn?utm_source=api): [Dusk](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/61/dusk-dawn?utm_source=api) destroys power 3 or greater; Norin, wardens, and most draw creatures survive. [Dawn](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/61/dusk-dawn?utm_source=api) later returns small creatures from your graveyard.

## Weaknesses and what to protect

Graveyard hate does little; exile-based commander tax and [Norin](https://scryfall.com/card/tsp/171/norin-the-wary?utm_source=api) sitting in the command zone after a kill are the real tempo hits. Enchantment sweepers remove [**Impact Tremors**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/717/impact-tremors?utm_source=api), [**Aura Shards**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmd/182/aura-shards?utm_source=api), and the draw auras at once — recast [Rocco](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/218/rocco-cabaretti-caterer?utm_source=api) for a creature ping if the enchantments die. Rest in Peace-style effects do not stop Norin's exile-return. Lifegain from [**Soul Warden**](https://scryfall.com/card/mm3/24/soul-warden?utm_source=api) and [**Essence Warden**](https://scryfall.com/card/cma/106/essence-warden?utm_source=api) is a cushion, not a lock. Fast combo decks that ignore the board will outpace a ping clock that needs several turn cycles.
