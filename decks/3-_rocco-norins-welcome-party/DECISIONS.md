# Decisions

## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, the primer, and the assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a cut back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated session notes so an argument does not live only in chat.

Every unique card needs a Cards in line. The other headings appear when there is something to record.

## Goals

- **Blink** — Norin and Saltskitter exile-return every cycle; the 99 fires on those enters.
- **Burn** — Those enters are table-wide damage, with combat as backup.

## Rankings

See `rankings.json`. First pass: jank 7, fun 7, mean 5, Blink 8, Burn 8. Same 99 as Endless Wariness; identity is blink-then-burn.

## Primer

- Win-turn claims assume Norin returns once per involved turn, not once per spell on the stack, matching his exile-until-next-end-step wording.
- Aura Shards is named as the Game Changer in the assessment sentence's supporting notes rather than as a combo.

## Rules

- Rocco's search requires that he was cast.
- Panharmonicon doubles triggers caused by artifact or creature enters, including creature-caused ping triggers on enchantments.
- Gandalf doubles triggers caused by legendary or artifact permanents entering or leaving; Norin does both.
- Genesis Chamber makes a Myr only while it is untapped; there is no activated ability to turn it off.
- Blessed Sanctuary prevents noncombat damage to you and your creatures, not the damage your pings deal to opponents.
- Kodama of the East Tree cannot put the card that triggered it, and cannot loop on its own put-into-play events.

## Talks

### 2026-08-27 — First assessment

Bracket 3−. Live Commander page (https://magic.wizards.com/en/formats/commander) still caps Game Changers at 0 in Brackets 1–2 and up to 3 in Bracket 3. Scryfall `is:gamechanger` matches **Aura Shards** as the only Game Changer in the 99. No two-card infinite, extra turns, or mass land denial. Pace is a stacked-ping or Champion combat win around turn ten to twelve; a Norin plus two red pings and Torbran can reasonably close around turn eight. That is Bracket 3 because of Aura Shards used as a repeatable value engine, and 3− because the list has no compact combo, little fast mana, and a clock that still needs several turn cycles. Not Bracket 2: Aura Shards is a hard-limit miss. Not Bracket 3 middle or 4: the goldfish is closer to eight-plus turns, interaction is paid spot removal, and Rocco is the only tutor.
