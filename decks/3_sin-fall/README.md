## How the deck works

[Sin](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/242/sin-spiras-punishment?utm_source=api) costs `{4}{B}{G}{U}`. Whenever it enters or attacks, exile a permanent card from your graveyard at random, create a tapped token copy, and **if that card was a land, repeat**. The loop stops on the first nonland permanent (or when no permanents remain).

That is why the list is full of lands, land-sac, and yard tutors, and why several strong landfall cards are tagged Avoid with Sin: they are better as unique engines than as the random copy that ends the chain.

Fill the graveyard with **lands plus one jackpot**. [Scapeshift](https://scryfall.com/card/m19/201/scapeshift?utm_source=api), [Rain of Filth](https://scryfall.com/card/usg/151/rain-of-filth?utm_source=api), [Lotus Field](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/385/lotus-field?utm_source=api), [Reprocess](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/2107/reprocess?utm_source=api), and [Pitiless Carnage](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/98/pitiless-carnage?utm_source=api) put lands there on purpose. [Buried Alive](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/273/buried-alive?utm_source=api), [Entomb](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/82/entomb?utm_source=api), [Unmarked Grave](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/106/unmarked-grave?utm_source=api), and [Final Parting](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/160/final-parting?utm_source=api) park [Portal to Phyrexia](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/240/portal-to-phyrexia?utm_source=api), [Famished Worldsire](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/182/famished-worldsire?utm_source=api), [Valgavoth, Terror Eater](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/120/valgavoth-terror-eater?utm_source=api), [Ugin, the Spirit Dragon](https://scryfall.com/card/m21/1/ugin-the-spirit-dragon?utm_source=api), or a Summon. [Cling to Dust](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/87/cling-to-dust?utm_source=api), [Pit of Offerings](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/278/pit-of-offerings?utm_source=api), and evidence on [Analyze the Pollen](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/150/analyze-the-pollen?utm_source=api) exile the wrong permanents first.

Ramp ([Burgeoning](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/143/burgeoning?utm_source=api), [Summer Bloom](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/160/summer-bloom?utm_source=api), [Explore](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/259/explore?utm_source=api), bounce lands) exists to hit seven mana and to trigger [Field of the Dead](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/247/field-of-the-dead?utm_source=api). Token copies of lands keep those names, so a Sin land-loop still grows zombies if you already have seven different names.

## Main lines

### Sin land chain into a jackpot

1. Get lands into the graveyard. Prefer sac outlets and fetches over milling [Hedron Crab](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/155/hedron-crab?utm_source=api) / [Hermit Druid](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/202/hermit-druid?utm_source=api) if those creatures would sit in the yard.
2. Put **one** jackpot permanent in the yard ([Portal to Phyrexia](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/240/portal-to-phyrexia?utm_source=api) via [Entomb](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/82/entomb?utm_source=api) or [Unmarked Grave](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/106/unmarked-grave?utm_source=api); creatures via [Buried Alive](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/273/buried-alive?utm_source=api)).
3. Exile leftover nonland permanents you do not want copied.
4. Cast [Sin](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/242/sin-spiras-punishment?utm_source=api) ([Cavern of Souls](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/269/cavern-of-souls?utm_source=api) naming Avatar or Leviathan). Each land exile makes another tapped land token; the first nonland becomes the jackpot copy.
5. Copies enter tapped. Wait for the untap step, or win off an enter trigger ([Portal to Phyrexia](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/240/portal-to-phyrexia?utm_source=api)). A copied [Summon: Leviathan](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/77/summon:-leviathan?utm_source=api) is a new saga: it gets a lore counter and chapter I still bounces non-sea creatures. [Finale of Devastation](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/289/finale-of-devastation?utm_source=api) at X ≥ 10 gives haste to creatures you control, but that does not untap the Sin tokens, so only untapped creatures can attack.

This is not infinite. It is one trigger (enter or attack) that repeats only while it keeps hitting lands.

### [Scapeshift](https://scryfall.com/card/m19/201/scapeshift?utm_source=api) into Field

1. Control several lands, including enough unique names after the search.
2. Sacrifice them to [Scapeshift](https://scryfall.com/card/m19/201/scapeshift?utm_source=api) and find [Field of the Dead](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/247/field-of-the-dead?utm_source=api) plus bounce lands and duals.
3. Each new land can make a Zombie if you have seven different names. Sacrificed lands remain in the yard for Sin.

### [Will of the Sultai](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/49/will-of-the-sultai?utm_source=api) / [Summon: Titan](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/204/summon:-titan?utm_source=api)

With the commander in play, [Will of the Sultai](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/49/will-of-the-sultai?utm_source=api) can mill three **and** return all lands from your graveyard. That is the opposite of a Sin setup: it **empties** land cards from the yard. Use it to explode onto the battlefield after a mill, or wait until after Sin has already copied a jackpot. [Summon: Titan](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/204/summon:-titan?utm_source=api) chapter II does the same land return.

- [Field of the Dead](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/247/field-of-the-dead?utm_source=api) and [Scute Swarm](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/197/scute-swarm?utm_source=api) go wide.

- [Finale of Devastation](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/289/finale-of-devastation?utm_source=api) to put an untapped creature in and pump; if X ≥ 10 that creature has haste. [Colossification](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/392/colossification?utm_source=api) on Sin taps it when the Aura enters.
- [Retreat to Hagra](https://scryfall.com/card/c18/116/retreat-to-hagra?utm_source=api) drain if the landfall engine is online and you are not about to Sin-copy it.

## Early, mid, and late game

**Early:** Hit land drops. [Burgeoning](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/143/burgeoning?utm_source=api), fetchlands, and two-mana ramp matter more than milling. Keep [Toxic Deluge](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/161/toxic-deluge?utm_source=api) or a modal land if the table is fast.

**Mid:** Decide whether you are building a **clean** yard (lands + one jackpot) or a **board** (Field, Swarm, Crab). Do not do both blindly. Cast Sin when the lottery is rigged, not when the yard is a jumble of Avoid cards.

**Late:** Attack with Sin for a second trigger, X-spell the table, or replay Sin if it died ([Malakir Rebirth](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/111/malakir-rebirth-malakir-mire?utm_source=api) on the stack).

## Mulligan guide

Keep hands that do **two** of: colored mana, a ramp spell or extra land drop, and a way to affect the yard (fetch, Loam, a tutor, or sac).

Ship hands that are all seven-drops, or that are mill-only with no mana. [Hermit Druid](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/202/hermit-druid?utm_source=api) without a filter plan is a trap keep.

## Important sequencing and rules notes

- Sin's repeat checks **the card you just exiled**. A nonland copy ends the process even if many lands are still in the graveyard.
- Copied lands are tokens. They still have the copied name for [Field of the Dead](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/247/field-of-the-dead?utm_source=api).
- Copies are **tapped** and are not the legendary Sin, so you can keep extra Portals or Worldsire tokens. Haste does not untap them.
- [Colossification](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/392/colossification?utm_source=api) taps the enchanted creature when the Aura enters.
- [Will of the Sultai](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/49/will-of-the-sultai?utm_source=api) land return fights Sin's fuel. Sequence it as a board explosion, not as setup.
- [Hermit Druid](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/202/hermit-druid?utm_source=api) with eight basics still mills a huge nonland pile. Filter before Sin, or do not activate.

## Weaknesses and what to protect

Graveyard hate blanks Sin. Hold [Boseiju, Who Endures](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/266/boseiju-who-endures?utm_source=api) / [Otawara, Soaring City](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/271/otawara-soaring-city?utm_source=api) for Rest in Peace effects when you can. Counterspells eat the seven-mana commander; [Cavern of Souls](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/269/cavern-of-souls?utm_source=api) and [Malakir Rebirth](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/111/malakir-rebirth-malakir-mire?utm_source=api) are the main answers. A random Avoid-with-Sin hit is a wasted enter trigger, so protect the **yard quality**, not only Sin on the battlefield.
