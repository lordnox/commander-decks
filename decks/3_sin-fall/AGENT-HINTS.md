# Sin-fall — agent hints

Primer owns the plan. This file is the seat checklist.

## Always ask

1. How much mana does Sin cost with tax, and what land or ramp line reaches
   that number next turn without relying on a fragile token?
2. Which permanent should stop Sin's random land chain? Keep one jackpot in
   the graveyard when possible; extra nonlands lower the lottery quality.
3. Does a tutor in hand have a useful setup target now, even if Finale cannot
   be cast for ten?
4. Can graveyard filtering remove an excess jackpot while turning into land
   or ramp?

## Malevolent Rumble

- Choose the permanent for the immediate line. If Sin needs the next land
  drop, take a revealed land and mill Ugin, Portal, or another jackpot for
  Sin to copy.
- Rescue Hedron Crab, Hermit Druid, Scute Swarm, Mossborn Hydra, or another
  `Avoid with Sin` permanent rather than polluting the graveyard with it.
- The Eldrazi Spawn pays one generic toward Sin, but do not make the entire
  next turn depend on a 0/1 surviving when Rumble can secure a land.

## Tutors and filtering

- Finale of Devastation is useful below X=10. At X=3 compare Mossborn Hydra
  and Scute Swarm as landfall engines; at X=2 compare Hermit Druid; at X=1
  compare Hedron Crab. Include the current and next land drops in that choice.
- Analyze the Pollen can collect evidence 8 with one eight-mana jackpot.
  Exiling an excess Famished Worldsire can improve Sin's lottery while
  finding Icetill Explorer.
- After Pollen finds Icetill Explorer, include the `{2}{G}{G}` cast and both
  legal land plays in the same turn's mana worksheet.
- Nature's Lore, Burgeoning, and Icetill Explorer are the rebuild line.
  "Rebuild mana first" is not a reason to pass while those spells are
  castable.
- Buried Alive is correct only when the graveyard has enough lands and needs
  a jackpot. Do not put three competing stop cards in merely because the
  spell can find three creatures.

## Politics

If another seat shows graveyard exile, state which public jackpot Sin needs
and negotiate before committing more cards. A short nonaggression offer can
be worth more than losing the whole prepared graveyard.
