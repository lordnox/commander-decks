# Misty Critters — agent hints

Primer owns the plan. This file is the seat checklist.

## Always ask

1. How many Squirrels (and other tokens) can Hazel tap, and is the copy
   trigger still ahead this end step?
2. Is there a one-drop token seed that this mana can actually cast **now**?
3. Does combat this turn beat holding the board for Hazel mana, fogs, or
   the Odd Acorn Gang?
4. Is an anthem or lord in hand that must hit **before** attackers?

## Sequencing

- Turn one with Forest and Squirrel Sanctuary: cast it. A 1/1 and an
  enchantment on an empty table is free development.
- Prefer Hazel or Chittering Witch over a three-mana body that leaves one
  unused mana and no interaction to hold.
- Play Squirrel Sovereign and other anthems precombat.
- Copy a Squirrel at end step unless a better token is in play.
- Do not cite an opponent's unrevealed tutor or hand card as the reason
  to attack.

## Combat

Do not send a 1/1 Squirrel at a 3/5 with no trick, pump, or sacrifice
outlet. That risks the token Hazel wants to copy. Wide attacks need a
reason: lethal, trample through, Rotcaller drain already on the stack, or
a deal.

Pay Baird and similar taxes or do not attack that seat.

## Closing

Triumph of the Hordes, Rotcaller, and a Gang-pumped trampler are the
finishes. Count infect and commander damage before a "just one more token"
turn.
