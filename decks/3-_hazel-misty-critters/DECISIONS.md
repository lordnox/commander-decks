# Decisions

## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, the primer, and the assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a cut back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated session notes so an argument does not live only in chat.

Every unique card needs a Cards in line. The other headings appear when there is something to record.

## Goals

- **Squirrels** — Commanders, lords, and makers are squirrel-native; other tribes feed the same swarm.
- **Tokens** — The kill is a copied critical mass, then overrun or Triumph.

## Rankings

See `rankings.json`. First pass: jank 6, fun 8, mean 5, Squirrels 9, Tokens 9. Linear go-wide, not a lock.

## Cards in

- **Hazel of the Rootbloom** — Commands the deck by turning expendable tokens into mana and copying the best Squirrel token twice each end step.
- **Valley Rotcaller** — Converts a wide Squirrel, Bat, Lizard, and Rat board into attack-trigger drain before combat damage matters.
- **The Odd Acorn Gang** — Creates the central tap-versus-attack decision, grants trample, and rewards spreading combat damage across opponents.
- **The Unbeatable Squirrel Girl** — Provides a Squirrel on entry or attack and converts Hazel's mana burst into a multiplicative token payoff.
- **Chatterfang, Squirrel General** — Adds Squirrels to every token event and turns excess Squirrels into creature removal.
- **Camellia, the Seedmiser** — Gives Squirrels menace, turns sacrificed Food into more Squirrels, and offers a late-game team pump.
- **Squirrel Sovereign** — Supplies a cheap permanent anthem that moves 1/1 Squirrels out of common damage-sweeper range.
- **Toski, Bearer of Secrets** — Rewards the line where the swarm is left untapped and allowed to connect rather than being spent through Hazel.
- **Champion of Lambholt** — Grows from every creature token and eventually makes the wide board difficult or impossible to block.
- **Deep Forest Hermit** — Produces four Squirrels at once and temporarily turns them into meaningful attackers.
- **Drey Keeper** — Leaves two Squirrels behind and provides an activated menace pump for a committed combat turn.
- **Scurry Oak** — Repeatedly creates Squirrels as counters accumulate and gives Champion and other counter effects another payoff.
- **Nested Shambler** — Is an early creature whose death replaces it with Squirrels and scales with the Gang's sorcery-speed pump.
- **Honored Dreyleader** — Becomes a large trampler from the Food-and-Squirrel board without consuming the rest of the swarm.
- **Tireless Provisioner** — Converts land drops into tokens that Hazel can tap, Academy Manufactor can multiply, and Chatterfang can supplement.
- **Academy Manufactor** — Turns each Clue, Food, or Treasure event into three useful tokens and greatly increases Hazel's available mana.
- **Mirkwood Bats** — Supplies Bat representation while draining every opponent from the deck's frequent token creation and sacrifice.
- **Iridescent Vinelasher** — Supplies a Lizard body, an offspring token, and repeatable landfall drain that bypasses combat.
- **Agate-Blade Assassin** — Adds another Lizard and drains on attack without needing to survive combat or connect.
- **Lord Skitter, Sewer King** — Produces a Rat every combat and incidentally keeps opposing graveyards under control.
- **Piper of the Swarm** — Repeatedly creates Rats, gives them menace, and threatens to steal a creature when the Rat count is high.
- **Chittering Witch** — Creates one Rat per opponent and converts expendable bodies into scalable creature shrinkage.
- **Experimental Confectioner** — Links the Food package to Rat production, especially with Tireless Provisioner and Tippy-Toe.
- **Nadier's Nightblade** — Drains whenever tokens leave, rewarding sacrifice, chump blocking, Treasures, Food, and temporary token copies.
- **Species Specialist** — Names Squirrel in most games and turns token deaths into cards without requiring combat damage.
- **Eternal Witness** — Recovers a destroyed engine, spent interaction spell, fog, or finisher.
- **Quest for Renewal** — Keeps the extra-untap role after Seedborn Muse was cut as a Game Changer. After a few token taps, it untaps creatures on opponents' turns so Hazel mana and defensive interaction can coexist without erasing the own-turn combat decision. Unlike Muse, it does not untap lands or artifact tokens.
- **Tippy-Toe, Terrific Partner** — Adds Food to each token event and draws at end step after Rotcaller, Food, or another life-gain effect resolves.
- **Chatter of the Squirrel** — Provides an inexpensive Squirrel seed twice through flashback.
- **Squirrel Sanctuary** — Seeds Hazel early and can return to hand after a nontoken creature dies for repeatable token production.
- **Squirrel Nest** — Creates one Squirrel each turn without spending another card and ensures Hazel always has a copy target.
- **Chitterspitter** — Turns spare tokens into a growing Squirrel anthem while producing its own Squirrels.
- **Verdant Command** — Creates two Squirrels at instant speed while also answering a graveyard card or planeswalker activation.
- **Acorn Harvest** — Produces two Squirrels twice, using life rather than additional mana for its flashback cost.
- **Revenge of the Rats** — Converts a stocked graveyard into a wide Rat board and can be cast again through flashback.
- **Second Harvest** — Copies the entire token board at instant speed for the deck's largest general-purpose expansion turn.
- **For the Common Good** — Copies the best token X times and makes the token board indestructible through the following turn.
- **Rootcast Apprenticeship** — Flexibly copies a token up to three times or mixes copying with counters and targeted protection.
- **Esika's Chariot** — Supplies two bodies and copies the best token whenever the Vehicle attacks.
- **Parallel Lives** — Doubles every creature and utility token without helping opponents.
- **Springleaf Parade** — Creates changeling tokens that satisfy every Rotcaller creature type and scale from Hazel's large mana turns.
- **Maskwood Nexus** — Makes the whole creature base count for Rotcaller and provides a repeatable changeling-token outlet.
- **Plague of Vermin** — Offers an unusual political life auction that can create a lethal number of Rats when our life total permits.
- **Audience with Trostani** — Rewards the deck's many differently named creature and utility tokens with a substantial card refill.
- **Shamanic Revelation** — Draws for the whole creature board and can recover life once pumped creatures reach power four.
- **Skullclamp** — Converts disposable 1/1 tokens into efficient cards and discourages opponents from sweeping without consequence.
- **Nature's Lore** — Finds an untapped Forest dual when available and advances Hazel without consuming a later token.
- **Three Visits** — Provides a second two-mana Forest search to improve early color consistency.
- **Arcane Signet** — Fixes both commander colors and helps cast Hazel on schedule.
- **Triumph of the Hordes** — Gives the version-one list a decisive go-wide finisher when ordinary damage cannot close the table.
- **Assassin's Trophy** — Answers any opposing permanent at instant speed for two mana.
- **Beast Within** — Removes any permanent at instant speed, with a token drawback that is minor beside the swarm.
- **Tear Asunder** — Exiles an artifact or enchantment cheaply and becomes broad nonland removal when kicked.
- **Feed the Swarm** — Covers both creatures and enchantments from black when green removal is unavailable.
- **Go for the Throat** — Provides efficient instant-speed creature removal without any zero-mana clause.
- **Pest Infestation** — Scales into multiple artifact or enchantment answers while leaving tokens for Hazel and Chatterfang.
- **Swarmyard Massacre** — Creates Squirrels and can operate as a one-sided wipe when the board is predominantly Rats and Squirrels.
- **Binding the Old Gods** — Removes a nonland permanent, finds a Forest, and later grants deathtouch for an attack.
- **Pick Your Poison** — Efficiently covers flying creatures, artifacts, and enchantments through opponent sacrifice.
- **Culling Ritual** — Resets opposing low-cost engines and token boards while refunding mana for rebuilding first.
- **Arachnogenesis** — Prevents combat damage and creates a defensive token wall proportional to the attack.
- **Tangle** — Fogs a bad combat and keeps the attacking creatures tapped for an additional turn.
- **Constant Mists** — Converts an excess land into repeatable combat insurance through buyback.
- **Heroic Intervention** — Protects the developed board from destruction and targeted removal for a paid two-mana cost.
- **Command Tower** — Provides either commander color without entering tapped.
- **Exotic Orchard** — Usually provides both colors in multiplayer without a life or tempo cost.
- **Overgrown Tomb** — Is a fetchable Forest and Swamp that can enter untapped when tempo matters.
- **Woodland Cemetery** — Usually enters untapped because the mana base contains many Forests and Swamps.
- **Llanowar Wastes** — Supplies either color immediately with optional life payment.
- **Blooming Marsh** — Provides painless early fixing when casting the deck's cheap setup cards matters most.
- **Deathcap Glade** — Enters untapped in developed turns and fixes both colors.
- **Undergrowth Stadium** — Is reliably untapped in a normal multiplayer Commander pod.
- **Twilight Mire** — Converts spare green or black mana into the exact Golgari combination needed.
- **Necroblossom Snarl** — Uses the basic-heavy mana base to enter untapped while fixing both colors.
- **Golgari Rot Farm** — Provides two colors and an extra virtual land drop at the cost of entering tapped.
- **Path of Ancestry** — Fixes both colors and scries when casting the many Squirrel creatures.
- **Secluded Courtyard** — Names Squirrel and supports both creature spells and activated abilities.
- **Unclaimed Territory** — Names Squirrel to cast the deck's primary creature type with either color.
- **Three Tree City** — Converts a developed Squirrel board into a large burst of green mana.
- **Swarmyard** — Regenerates the deck's Squirrels and Rats while still functioning as a mana source.
- **Bojuka Bog** — Adds graveyard interaction to a land slot.
- **Takenuma, Abandoned Mire** — Is an untapped black source or a difficult-to-counter recursion spell when channeled.
- **Boseiju, Who Endures** — Is an untapped green source or paid interaction against an artifact, enchantment, or nonbasic land.
- **Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth** — Turns utility lands into green sources and enables Chatterfang's forestwalk against opponents.
- **Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth** — Turns utility lands into black sources and improves the colorless-heavy land draws.
- **Nurturing Peatland** — Fixes both colors early and cashes itself in for a card when lands are plentiful.
- **Fabled Passage** — Finds either basic color and increases landfall output for Tireless Provisioner and Iridescent Vinelasher.
- **Myriad Landscape** — Supplies a land-slot ramp effect that finds two basics in slower games.
- **Forest** — Eight copies provide reliable green mana and enough basic targets for land-search effects.
- **Swamp** — Four copies provide resilient black mana and basic targets without overloading the green-heavy curve.

## Cards out

- **Seedborn Muse** — Previously filled the extra-untap slot: it untapped the token board on opponents' turns so Hazel mana and defensive interaction could coexist without erasing the own-turn combat decision. It left the 99 because it is a Game Changer; Quest for Renewal is the replacement.
