# Decisions

## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, the primer, and the assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a card back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated session notes so an argument does not live only in chat.

Every unique card needs a Cards in line. The other headings appear when there is something to record.

## Goals

- **Polymorph** — Spare bodies become random nonlegendary monsters; the 99 is a hit list plus fodder.
- **Tokens** — Interaction leaves Zombies, Armies, Crabs, and Scions behind so Jalira always has bait.

## Rankings

See `rankings.json`. First pass: jank 7, fun 8, mean 4, Polymorph 9, Tokens 8. Theft exists to feed Jalira, not as a repeating steal plan.

## Cards in

- **Ancient Silver Dragon** — Eight-power flyer that can refill the hand on a hit; a draw payoff among the hits rather than a hard-cast plan.
- **Ancient Stone Idol** — Twelve-power flash trampler that can be cheaper in a pile-up and leaves a 6/12 when it dies; a hit that still pays fodder.
- **Arcane Signet** — Untapped two-mana rock; the cleanest way to cast Jalira on three.

## Talks

### 2026-08-27 — Assessment: Bracket 2+

Checked against the live Wizards Commander page (Brackets beta: 0 Game Changers in Brackets 1–2, up to 3 in Bracket 3) and the February 9, 2026 brackets update, plus a fresh Scryfall `is:gamechanger` query (53 cards, including Farewell and Biorhythm). The 100 contains zero Game Changers, no two-card infinite combo, no extra turns, no mass land denial, and no true tutors — Brainstorm, Brainsurge, Dream Cache, and See Beyond only restack or shuffle. Fast mana is ordinary rocks (Signet, Diamonds, Bauble, Medallion); no Sol Ring or Vault. The plan is commander-dependent battlecruiser: Jalira on turn three off a rock or turn four naturally, first flip on turn five (or turn four with Thousand-Year Elixir and a token already in play). Training Grounds makes activations {U}, but a revealed Void Winnower or It That Betrays on turn four is a threat, not a table kill. Summon: Bahamut flipped on turn five still needs about three of your turns before chapter IV. Repeatable lethal is two or three attacks with 8–12 power plus Rogue's Passage, which lands around turn nine to eleven in a goldfish and later if Jalira is taxed. Card quality and the interaction-as-fodder package sit at the top of Core, not Upgraded: the deck cannot reliably present a win by turn six, and the win is telegraphed and board-based. Oracle checks: Jalira's sacrifice is an activation cost; Illusionist's Bracers copies the ability without a second sacrifice; Polymorph/Proteus Staff find any creature (including legendary), unlike Jalira.
