# [Jalira, Master Polymorphist](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api) — The Polyfisher

> Bracket 2+ mono-blue [Jalira](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api) polymorph deck with no Game Changers. Usually threatens combat kills around turn nine to eleven; a high-roll [Training Grounds](https://scryfall.com/card/mat/9/training-grounds?utm_source=api) plus [Thousand-Year Elixir](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/271/thousand-year-elixir?utm_source=api) line can reasonably put a monster on the battlefield on turn four, which is not yet a win.

A mono-blue polymorph deck where the interaction suite doubles as bait. Nearly every cheap spell leaves a token behind — an [Orc Army](https://scryfall.com/card/tltr/5/orc-army?utm_source=api), a decayed [Zombie](https://scryfall.com/card/cc2/9/snake-zombie?utm_source=api), a [Crab](https://scryfall.com/card/tmh2/2/crab?utm_source=api), a [Fish](https://scryfall.com/card/tfdn/10/fish?utm_source=api) — and [**Jalira, Master Polymorphist**](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api) trades any of them for the first nonlegendary creature off the top of the library. Since the twenty creatures in the 99 average around eight power, every cast-off token is a lottery ticket that only has winning numbers.

## How the deck works

1. Spend the early turns on rocks and cheap interaction. The interaction is the token engine: [**Saruman's Trickery**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/68/sarumans-trickery?utm_source=api), [**Abstruse Interference**](https://scryfall.com/card/ogw/40/abstruse-interference?utm_source=api), and [**Flip the Switch**](https://scryfall.com/card/mid/54/flip-the-switch?utm_source=api) counter something and leave a body, [**Callous Dismissal**](https://scryfall.com/card/war/44/callous-dismissal?utm_source=api) and [**Deceive the Messenger**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/47/deceive-the-messenger?utm_source=api) amass while they tempo, and [**Hard Evidence**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/46/hard-evidence?utm_source=api) builds a wall plus a [Clue](https://scryfall.com/card/ttmc/2/clue?utm_source=api) for one mana.
2. Land [**Jalira, Master Polymorphist**](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api) and keep her safe. [**Training Grounds**](https://scryfall.com/card/mat/9/training-grounds?utm_source=api) drops her activation to {U}, and [**Thousand-Year Elixir**](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/271/thousand-year-elixir?utm_source=api) lets her fish the turn she arrives.
3. Activate: {2}{U}, tap, sacrifice a token — reveal until the first nonlegendary creature and put it straight onto the battlefield. The hit list runs from [**Star Whale**](https://scryfall.com/card/who/55/star-whale?utm_source=api) to [**It That Betrays**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/805/it-that-betrays?utm_source=api); there is no bad pull, only smaller ones.
4. Rig the lottery. [**Brainstorm**](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/155/brainstorm?utm_source=api), [**Brainsurge**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/53/brainsurge?utm_source=api), [**Dream Cache**](https://scryfall.com/card/c18/88/dream-cache?utm_source=api), and [**See Beyond**](https://scryfall.com/card/pca/25/see-beyond?utm_source=api) put a stranded monster from hand on top of the library, so the next activation is a named shot instead of a random one.
5. Go again. [**Patriar's Seal**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/332/patriars-seal?utm_source=api) untaps [Jalira](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api) every turn, [**Tidal Force**](https://scryfall.com/card/c13/62/tidal-force?utm_source=api) untaps her on every upkeep, and [**Illusionist's Bracers**](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/260/illusionists-bracers?utm_source=api) turns one sacrifice into two monsters.
6. Close with battlecruiser math: [**Rogue's Passage**](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/212/rogues-passage?utm_source=api) pushes a 10/10 through, [**Summon: Bahamut**](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/1/summon:-bahamut?utm_source=api) burns the table on its final chapter, and [**Ulamog's Dreadsire**](https://scryfall.com/card/m3c/38/ulamogs-dreadsire?utm_source=api) manufactures a 10/10 every turn.

## Main synergy patterns

### Rigged flips

[Jalira](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api) reveals until a nonlegendary creature, so a monster placed on top with [**Brainstorm**](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/155/brainstorm?utm_source=api) or [**Brainsurge**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/53/brainsurge?utm_source=api) is a guaranteed hit. A twelve-mana [**It That Betrays**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/805/it-that-betrays?utm_source=api) stuck in hand becomes a four-mana instant-speed threat: put it back with [**Brainstorm**](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/155/brainstorm?utm_source=api), then activate. [**Dream Cache**](https://scryfall.com/card/c18/88/dream-cache?utm_source=api) and [**See Beyond**](https://scryfall.com/card/pca/25/see-beyond?utm_source=api) do the same from a worse position, and [**Midnight Clock**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/100/midnight-clock?utm_source=api) resets a hand full of uncastable giants into a fresh seven.

### Theft into the blender

[**Ray of Command**](https://scryfall.com/card/ddm/29/ray-of-command?utm_source=api), [**Domineering Will**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/719/domineering-will?utm_source=api), and [**Tempted by the Oriq**](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/58/tempted-by-the-oriq?utm_source=api) only borrow creatures — unless [Jalira](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api) eats them. Sacrificing a stolen creature is permanent removal, denies the return, and pays out a monster. [**Mind Flayer**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/728/mind-flayer?utm_source=api) keeps its dominated target as long as it stays on the battlefield, and since you control the target, [Jalira](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api) can sacrifice it directly. [**Blue Sun's Twilight**](https://scryfall.com/card/one/43/blue-suns-twilight?utm_source=api) at X≥5 keeps the token copy even after the original goes home.

### Interaction that reloads the engine

[**Pongify**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/160/pongify?utm_source=api), [**Rapid Hybridization**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/198/rapid-hybridization?utm_source=api), and [**Resculpt**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/201/resculpt?utm_source=api) are premium removal here because the token they leave is either an opposing 3/3 (fine) or, aimed at your own spent artifact, fresh fodder: [**Resculpt**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/201/resculpt?utm_source=api) turns a cracked [Clue](https://scryfall.com/card/ttmc/2/clue?utm_source=api) or an obsolete [**Guardian Idol**](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/467/guardian-idol?utm_source=api) into a 4/4 [Elemental](https://scryfall.com/card/f18/2/citys-blessing-elemental?utm_source=api). [**Guardian Idol**](https://scryfall.com/card/jmp/467/guardian-idol?utm_source=api) itself animates for {2} and hops into the blender once its mana is no longer needed.

### The Faerie conveyor belt

[**Faerie Artisans**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/92/faerie-artisans?utm_source=api) copies every nontoken creature an opponent plays, but exiles the previous copy when a new one arrives. Sacrifice the current copy to [Jalira](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api) before it gets replaced — the Artisans convert opponents' development into a steady drip of free activations. The Artisans are also the smallest creature [Jalira](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api) can reveal; a flip into them still yields a token engine.

### Monsters that keep giving

[**Astral Dragon**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/664/astral-dragon?utm_source=api) clones the best noncreature permanent twice. [**Hulking Metamorph**](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/79/hulking-metamorph?utm_source=api) enters as a 7/7 copy of your best artifact or creature — or is cast early for {2}{U}{U} as a 3/3 via prototype and fed to [Jalira](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api) later. [**Diluvian Primordial**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/96/diluvian-primordial?utm_source=api) casts an instant or sorcery out of every opponent's graveyard, and [**Triplicate Titan**](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/79/triplicate-titan?utm_source=api) and [**Ancient Stone Idol**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/222/ancient-stone-idol?utm_source=api) leave more bodies when they die.

## Win conditions

- **Battlecruiser damage**: two or three flips produce twenty-plus power; [**Rogue's Passage**](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/212/rogues-passage?utm_source=api) makes any one of them lethal-by-installments unblockable.
- **[Summon: Bahamut](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/1/summon:-bahamut?utm_source=api)**: chapter IV hits each opponent for the total mana value of your other permanents — with one ten-drop and a few rocks out, that is usually over twenty to every head.
- **[Ulamog's Dreadsire](https://scryfall.com/card/m3c/38/ulamogs-dreadsire?utm_source=api)**: a 10/10 every turn wins any stalled board, and each token later feeds [Jalira](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api) or [**Idol of False Gods**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/210/idol-of-false-gods?utm_source=api) counters.
- **[It That Betrays](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/805/it-that-betrays?utm_source=api)**: annihilator 2 plus stealing everything opponents sacrifice ends games in two attacks.

## Game progression

### Early game

Islands, a rock on two, interaction held up. Cast [**Hard Evidence**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/46/hard-evidence?utm_source=api) or an amass spell only when it does something; the tokens do not rot. [**War Room**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/422/war-room?utm_source=api) and [Clue](https://scryfall.com/card/ttmc/2/clue?utm_source=api) tokens keep cards flowing. Monsters in the opening hand are not dead weight forever — but see the mulligan guide.

### Mid game

[Jalira](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api) on four (or three off a rock), first activation on five with protection up, or on the same turn with [**Thousand-Year Elixir**](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/271/thousand-year-elixir?utm_source=api). Prefer flipping at instant speed on the end step before your turn: the monster unmorphs the table's plans and attacks after untapping. Start rigging with [**Brainstorm**](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/155/brainstorm?utm_source=api) once a giant is stuck in hand.

### Late game

Chain activations with [**Patriar's Seal**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/332/patriars-seal?utm_source=api) and [**Tidal Force**](https://scryfall.com/card/c13/62/tidal-force?utm_source=api). [**Void Winnower**](https://scryfall.com/card/bfz/17/void-winnower?utm_source=api) shuts most decks half off; [**Platinum Emperion**](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/233/platinum-emperion?utm_source=api) blanks burn and drain outs; [**Hullbreaker Horror**](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/68/hullbreaker-horror?utm_source=api) turns every fodder spell into a bounce. Finish through [**Rogue's Passage**](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/212/rogues-passage?utm_source=api) or Bahamut's Mega Flare.

## Mulligan guide

Keep hands with:

- three lands or two lands plus a rock;
- at least one cheap token producer or counterspell;
- at most one monster — the library wants them more than your hand does.

Ship hands with two or more raw giants: hard-casting is the backup plan, not the plan. A hand with [Jalira](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api), a rock, and [**Hard Evidence**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/46/hard-evidence?utm_source=api) is close to ideal.

## Important sequencing and rules notes

- [Jalira](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api)'s sacrifice is part of the activation cost — opponents cannot kill the token in response to deny the flip. Killing [Jalira](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api) in response does not stop the reveal either; the ability already exists on the stack.
- [Jalira](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api) finds only **nonlegendary** creatures, and she is the deck's only legendary creature, so every reveal stops on a real hit. [**Polymorph**](https://scryfall.com/card/m10/67/polymorph?utm_source=api) and [**Proteus Staff**](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/230/proteus-staff?utm_source=api) find *any* creature card, and both can target opponents' permanents; pointing [**Proteus Staff**](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/230/proteus-staff?utm_source=api) at an opposing token can dig them into a threat, so save it for your own tokens or for creature-light decks. An opposing commander bottomed by the Staff simply returns to the command zone, so it is not commander removal.
- [**Illusionist's Bracers**](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/260/illusionists-bracers?utm_source=api) copies [Jalira](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api)'s ability without paying costs: one sacrifice, two separate reveals. The copy resolves first; you may [**Brainstorm**](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/155/brainstorm?utm_source=api) in between with the original still on the stack.
- [**Summon: Bahamut**](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/1/summon:-bahamut?utm_source=api) put onto the battlefield by a flip still gets its lore counters — one as it enters and one after each of your draw steps — and is sacrificed after chapter IV. Roughly three turns of removal and draw, then Mega Flare.
- Decayed Zombies from [**Flip the Switch**](https://scryfall.com/card/mid/54/flip-the-switch?utm_source=api) and [**Revenge of the Drowned**](https://scryfall.com/card/mid/72/revenge-of-the-drowned?utm_source=api) cannot block and die after attacking — sacrifice them to [Jalira](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api) instead; they were always going to die.
- Amass grows a single Army token; a fresh amass after the Army was sacrificed simply builds a new one from zero.
- [**Faerie Artisans**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/92/faerie-artisans?utm_source=api) exiles its previous token when a new one is created — sacrifice or respond before the replacement trigger resolves.
- [**Platinum Emperion**](https://scryfall.com/card/uma/233/platinum-emperion?utm_source=api) stops *your* life total from changing too: no paying life for [**Fountainport**](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/253/fountainport?utm_source=api)'s [Fish](https://scryfall.com/card/tfdn/10/fish?utm_source=api), no lifegain from [**Springjack Pasture**](https://scryfall.com/card/c13/326/springjack-pasture?utm_source=api), and no losing to anything but combat math you can see coming.
- [**Void Winnower**](https://scryfall.com/card/bfz/17/void-winnower?utm_source=api) stops opponents casting even-mana-value spells — and zero is even, so tokens' friends like Clues still work for you while most rocks, sweepers, and counterspells across the table go dark.
- [**Star Whale**](https://scryfall.com/card/who/55/star-whale?utm_source=api) grants other creatures you control ward {2}, which covers [Jalira](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api); it can also be suspended for two mana on turn two as a discount.
- [**Hulking Metamorph**](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/79/hulking-metamorph?utm_source=api) in hand is castable for {2}{U}{U} via prototype as a 3/3 copy — an aggressively costed fodder body that [Jalira](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/63/jalira-master-polymorphist?utm_source=api) later trades in.
