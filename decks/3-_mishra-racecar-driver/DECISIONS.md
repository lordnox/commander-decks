# Mishra — Racecar Driver: Decision Log

## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, the primer, and the assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a cut back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated session notes so an argument does not live only in chat.

Every unique card needs a Cards in line. The other headings appear when there is something to record.

## Goals

- **Vehicles** — Warform skips crew on the racers and reuses their attack triggers.
- **Artifacts** — Almost every payoff is something Mishra can copy in combat.

## Rankings

See `rankings.json`. First pass: jank 7, fun 7, mean 5, Vehicles 8, Artifacts 9. Known commander, weird crew-skip finish.

## Cards in

This log records why every card entered the list. Future changes should append a dated entry identifying the card added, the card removed, and the reason.

### 2026-08-23 — Backfill

### Commander and copy engines

- **Mishra, Eminent One** — Commander: turns a noncreature artifact into a hasty 4/4 Warform each combat so Vehicles attack without crewing and ETB artifacts pay off twice.
- **Brudiclad, Telchor Engineer** — Turns leftover artifact tokens into copies of the best token after Mishra has already made a Warform.
- **Cybermen Squadron** — Gives the artifact army myriad so Warforms and Vehicles multiply in combat.
- **Esoteric Duplicator** — Replaces sacrificed artifacts, including spent Warform fodder, with permanent copies at the next end step.
- **Mirrorworks** — Pays for a nontoken artifact copy as it enters, keeping value after Mishra leaves.
- **Mechanized Production** — Alternate win on eight same-name artifacts and a repeatable copy engine for Vibranium or cheap rocks.
- **Simulacrum Synthesizer** — Makes Construct tokens whenever a sizable artifact enters, converting the rock suite into a board.
- **Machine God's Effigy** — Copies a useful creature as a mana rock that Mishra can later Warform.

### Mishra targets

- **Ichor Wellspring** — Draws on entry and death, so a Warform copy is two cards for one combat.
- **Hulldrifter** — Vehicle that draws two when it enters; Warform version needs no crew.
- **Cryogen Relic** — Cheap draw rock that still functions when copied in combat.
- **Coveted Jewel** — Huge mana and cards when copied; the original stays as a ramp engine.
- **The Mightstone and Weakstone** — Warform can draw two or snipe a creature while the original remains a Meld piece and mana rock.
- **Cursed Mirror** — Early mana that can clone a creature, then becomes a repeatable clone when Warformed.
- **Imposter Mech** — Vehicle clone that Mishra can attack with immediately.
- **Damocles Base, Sword of Kang** — Draw/impulse Vehicle worth copying each combat.
- **Getaway Barrel** — Sac outlet and card filter; limited creature-card hits are accepted for the artifact density.
- **Imskir Iron-Eater** — Artifact payoff that turns rocks into cards and damage.
- **Mole Module** — Makes moles and draws as a copyable artifact.
- **N'Yami-Class Mother Ship** — Station Vehicle with card flow that Warform can crew-skip.
- **The Indomitable** — Pirate Vehicle that draws on combat damage.
- **The Thanos-Copter** — Animates the rest of the Vehicles for a fleet attack when copied at beginning of combat.
- **Shuri's Fabricator** — Starts the Vibranium token supply Mishra and Mechanized Production want.
- **Vibranium Mining Mech** — Extra Vibranium on ETB and attack, so a Warform can make two in one combat.
- **RMS Titanic** — Treasure burst after combat damage; a Warform can explode before the delayed sacrifice.
- **Loki's Scepter** — Theft rock that becomes a stolen permanent when Warformed.
- **Hoard Hauler** — Treasure Vehicle that converts combat into mana.
- **Artificer Class** — Levels into extra artifacts and card advantage while counting as a copyable enchantment-artifact.
- **Mouser Foundry** — Makes Cats from cheap artifacts so Mishra always has a board even without a Vehicle.
- **Thopter Spy Network** — Token factory and repeatable draw that also supplies flying attackers.
