## Deck summary

[**Mishra, Eminent One**](https://scryfall.com/card/brc/1/mishra-eminent-one?utm_source=api) creates a hasty **[Mishra](https://scryfall.com/card/brc/1/mishra-eminent-one?utm_source=api)'s Warform** copy of one noncreature artifact at the beginning of combat. The copy keeps the artifact's abilities, becomes a 4/4 artifact creature, attacks immediately, and is normally sacrificed at the next end step.

The deck uses that trigger in three ways:

- copy artifacts with strong enters-the-battlefield abilities for immediate value;
- copy Vehicles so they can attack without being crewed;
- build artifact and token density until a repeated damage engine or [**Mechanized Production**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/109/mechanized-production?utm_source=api) ends the game.



## How the deck works

1. Develop mana with Signets, Talismans, artifact lands, and value artifacts.
2. Cast [Mishra](https://scryfall.com/card/brc/1/mishra-eminent-one?utm_source=api) with at least one useful noncreature artifact already available.
3. Choose the best Warform target each combat: cards, removal, mana, or damage.
4. Use the hasty 4/4 body to attack, crew another Vehicle, or gain a useful death trigger.
5. Protect [Mishra](https://scryfall.com/card/brc/1/mishra-eminent-one?utm_source=api) and the key artifacts while the repeated copies generate an advantage.

A copied Vehicle is already a creature because [Mishra](https://scryfall.com/card/brc/1/mishra-eminent-one?utm_source=api) makes the Warform a 4/4 artifact creature. It does not need to be crewed.

## Main synergy patterns

### ETB racing

Copy [**Hulldrifter**](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/47/hulldrifter?utm_source=api) to draw two cards, [**Demonic Junker**](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/83/demonic-junker?utm_source=api) to remove creatures, or [**The Mightstone and Weakstone**](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/238/the-mightstone-and-weakstone?utm_source=api) to draw two or give a creature -5/-5. The Warform can then attack or crew another Vehicle before being sacrificed.

[**Ichor Wellspring**](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/245/ichor-wellspring?utm_source=api) is especially efficient: the copy draws when it enters and again when [Mishra](https://scryfall.com/card/brc/1/mishra-eminent-one?utm_source=api)'s delayed trigger sacrifices it.

### Vehicle assault

Copying [**Reaver Titan**](https://scryfall.com/card/40k/163/reaver-titan?utm_source=api) creates a hasty 4/4 with its attack trigger, dealing 5 damage to each opponent when it attacks. [**Rolling Hamsphere**](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/39/rolling-hamsphere?utm_source=api) creates three Hamsters and deals damage on every copied attack.

A Warform copy of [**The Thanos-Copter**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/753/the-thanos-copter?utm_source=api) enters during the beginning-of-combat step and turns the rest of the Vehicles into creatures until end of turn, enabling a full-fleet attack without individual crew payments.

### Vibranium production

[**Shuri's Fabricator**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/114/shuris-fabricator?utm_source=api) begins the supply of indestructible Vibranium tokens. [**Vibranium Mining Mech**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/117/vibranium-mining-mech?utm_source=api) creates another whenever it enters or attacks, so a Warform copy can create one immediately and another when it attacks.

Enchant a Vibranium with [**Mechanized Production**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/109/mechanized-production?utm_source=api) to make another every upkeep. At eight artifacts named Vibranium, its alternate win condition triggers.

### Permanent copies

[**Mirrorworks**](https://scryfall.com/card/c18/211/mirrorworks?utm_source=api) can copy nontoken artifacts as they enter. [**Esoteric Duplicator**](https://scryfall.com/card/big/5/esoteric-duplicator?utm_source=api) can replace sacrificed artifacts with permanent copies at the next end step, including artifacts sacrificed through their own abilities. These engines help the deck keep value after [Mishra](https://scryfall.com/card/brc/1/mishra-eminent-one?utm_source=api) is removed.

## Win conditions

- Repeated [**Reaver Titan**](https://scryfall.com/card/40k/163/reaver-titan?utm_source=api) Warforms deal 5 to every opponent per attack.
- [**Rolling Hamsphere**](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/39/rolling-hamsphere?utm_source=api) builds Hamsters and converts their count into direct damage.
- [**Knight Paladin**](https://scryfall.com/card/40k/160/knight-paladin?utm_source=api) Warforms deal 4 damage to every opponent on entry.
- [**The Thanos-Copter**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/753/the-thanos-copter?utm_source=api) enables large attacks from the entire Vehicle fleet.
- [**Mechanized Production**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/109/mechanized-production?utm_source=api) wins with eight matching Vibranium or other same-name artifact tokens.
- [**Simulacrum Synthesizer**](https://scryfall.com/card/big/6/simulacrum-synthesizer?utm_source=api), [**Brudiclad, Telchor Engineer**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/299/brudiclad-telchor-engineer?utm_source=api), and the artifact-token engines build a conventional combat army.

## Game progression

### Early game

Prioritize two- and three-mana acceleration, artifact lands, and a useful [Mishra](https://scryfall.com/card/brc/1/mishra-eminent-one?utm_source=api) target. [**Ichor Wellspring**](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/245/ichor-wellspring?utm_source=api), [**Cursed Mirror**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/242/cursed-mirror?utm_source=api), and [**Hulldrifter**](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/47/hulldrifter?utm_source=api) are ideal setup cards.

### Mid game

Cast [Mishra](https://scryfall.com/card/brc/1/mishra-eminent-one?utm_source=api) when a useful copy target is ready. Select value or removal first rather than exposing the commander for only a 4/4 attack. Use the Warform to crew a second Vehicle when that produces more value than attacking with the token itself.

### Late game

Shift from card advantage to damage. Copy [**Reaver Titan**](https://scryfall.com/card/40k/163/reaver-titan?utm_source=api), [**Knight Paladin**](https://scryfall.com/card/40k/160/knight-paladin?utm_source=api), or [**Rolling Hamsphere**](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/39/rolling-hamsphere?utm_source=api), or use [**The Thanos-Copter**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/753/the-thanos-copter?utm_source=api) to animate the fleet. Protect [**Mechanized Production**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/109/mechanized-production?utm_source=api) when the Vibranium count approaches eight.

## Mulligan guide

Keep hands with:

- three lands, or two lands plus reliable two-mana acceleration;
- access to blue, black, and red by the time [Mishra](https://scryfall.com/card/brc/1/mishra-eminent-one?utm_source=api) is cast;
- at least one noncreature artifact worth copying;
- preferably a draw engine or early interaction.

Mulligan hands that contain only expensive Vehicles, lack early artifacts, or cannot produce all three colors.

## Important sequencing and rules notes

- [Mishra](https://scryfall.com/card/brc/1/mishra-eminent-one?utm_source=api) targets a noncreature artifact when the beginning-of-combat trigger goes on the stack. Do not crew the intended target before that trigger resolves.
- The Warform has haste only until end of turn and is sacrificed at the beginning of the next end step.
- A Warform keeps copied enters, leaves, death, combat-damage, and attack abilities.
- Copying a legendary artifact is normally safe because the token is named **[Mishra](https://scryfall.com/card/brc/1/mishra-eminent-one?utm_source=api)'s Warform**, not the copied card's name.
- The Warform is a creature with mana value equal to the copied artifact's mana value; only its base power and toughness become 4/4.
- [**Esoteric Duplicator**](https://scryfall.com/card/big/5/esoteric-duplicator?utm_source=api) creates its replacement copy at the beginning of the next end step; it does not save the artifact immediately.
- [**Getaway Barrel**](https://scryfall.com/card/hob/98/getaway-barrel?utm_source=api) looks for a creature card when it dies. Most Vehicles are noncreature cards in the library, so its hit count is deliberately limited.
- [**RMS Titanic**](https://scryfall.com/card/who/93/rms-titanic?utm_source=api) sacrifices itself after combat damage to create Treasures. A Warform copy can do this before [Mishra](https://scryfall.com/card/brc/1/mishra-eminent-one?utm_source=api)'s normal end-step sacrifice.

## Weaknesses and priorities

The deck depends heavily on [Mishra](https://scryfall.com/card/brc/1/mishra-eminent-one?utm_source=api) surviving into combat and on maintaining noncreature artifacts. Protect [**Mishra**](https://scryfall.com/card/brc/1/mishra-eminent-one?utm_source=api), [**Padeem**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/109/padeem-consul-of-innovation?utm_source=api), and the permanent copy engines from targeted removal. Artifact sweepers and graveyard exile can cut off both the board and the recursion plan, so avoid committing every expensive Vehicle at once.
