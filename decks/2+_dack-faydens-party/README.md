## How the deck works

[**Dack Fayden, Helping Hand**](https://scryfall.com/card/frc/4/dack-fayden-helping-hand?utm_source=api) is a 4/6 for `{4}{W}{W}`. When he enters, reveal from the top until you hit X creature cards, where X is the number of opponents. Those creatures enter under **you**, then they are goaded for the rest of the game, then each opponent is given a different one.

The pile is built so those enters matter. [**Fiend Hunter**](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/167/fiend-hunter?utm_source=api), [**Banisher Priest**](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/173/banisher-priest?utm_source=api), [**Palace Jailer**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/140/palace-jailer?utm_source=api), [**Angel of Sanctions**](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/61/angel-of-sanctions?utm_source=api), and [**Bronzebeak Foragers**](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/69/bronzebeak-foragers?utm_source=api) exile opposing permanents before they change teams. [**Meteor Golem**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/205/meteor-golem?utm_source=api), [**Curious Colossus**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/12/curious-colossus?utm_source=api), [**Githzerai Monk**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/20/githzerai-monk?utm_source=api), and [**Subjugator Angel**](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/43/subjugator-angel?utm_source=api) wipe, shrink, or tap as they arrive. [**Rumor Gatherer**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/705/rumor-gatherer?utm_source=api) and [**Enduring Innocence**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/6/enduring-innocence?utm_source=api) turn the dump into cards if they are already in play. [**Zetalpa, Primal Dawn**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/142/zetalpa-primal-dawn?utm_source=api) and [**Soul of Eternity**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/50/soul-of-eternity?utm_source=api) are the guests you would rather keep; give them only when the goad clock is the point.

After the gifts leave, blink Dack. [**Restoration Angel**](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/38/restoration-angel?utm_source=api), [**Salvation Swan**](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/28/salvation-swan?utm_source=api), [**Flickerwisp**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/11/flickerwisp?utm_source=api), [**Flicker of Fate**](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/16/flicker-of-fate?utm_source=api), and [**Voyager Staff**](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/271/voyager-staff?utm_source=api) restamp him. [**Abdel Adrian, Gorion's Ward**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/2/abdel-adrian-gorions-ward?utm_source=api), [**Glorious Protector**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/695/glorious-protector?utm_source=api), and [**Lumbering Battlement**](https://scryfall.com/card/rna/15/lumbering-battlement?utm_source=api) exile a pile of your own permanents and return them together.

Blink spells that return a creature under its **owner's** control reclaim a donated card you still own. That is how a gifted [Fiend Hunter](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/167/fiend-hunter?utm_source=api) comes home without giving the exiled card back first: blink the hunter, it leaves (cage opens), then it re-enters under you and can cage something else.

## Win conditions

Combat. Donated creatures are goaded, so they attack someone else each turn and chip the table while your cages stay closed. Keep [**Zetalpa, Primal Dawn**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/142/zetalpa-primal-dawn?utm_source=api), [**Salvation Colossus**](https://scryfall.com/card/m3c/43/salvation-colossus?utm_source=api), or [**Soul of Eternity**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/50/soul-of-eternity?utm_source=api) when you can; encore on Indemnity or Soul splits extra attackers. [**Realm-Cloaked Giant**](https://scryfall.com/card/afc/70/realm-cloaked-giant-cast-off?utm_source=api)'s adventure and [**Ondu Inversion**](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/30/ondu-inversion-ondu-skyruins?utm_source=api) reset a board that got too even. [**Parting Gust**](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/24/parting-gust?utm_source=api) with the Fish gift is hard exile when a guest became a problem. [**Perch Protection**](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/BLC-11/perch-protection?utm_source=api) is the fog: four Birds, and a Gifted extra turn phases you out until your next turn.

## Early, mid, and late

**Early.** Land, a two-mana rock, then a three- or four-mana rock. [**Moonsilver Key**](https://scryfall.com/card/mid/255/moonsilver-key?utm_source=api), [**World Map**](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/270/world-map?utm_source=api), and [**Expedition Map**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/724/expedition-map?utm_source=api) find [Plains](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/317/plains?utm_source=api) or the blink deserts. [**Rumor Gatherer**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/705/rumor-gatherer?utm_source=api) or [**Enduring Innocence**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/6/enduring-innocence?utm_source=api) before Dack turns the dump into cards. Loot with [**Collector's Vault**](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/244/collectors-vault?utm_source=api), [**Daily Bugle Newspaper**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/749/daily-bugle-newspaper?utm_source=api), or [**Thrór's Map**](https://scryfall.com/card/hob/179/thr%C3%B3rs-map?utm_source=api) so Dack is staring at creatures instead of rocks.

**Mid.** Cast Dack with at least one blink in hand or a [**Fiend Hunter**](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/167/fiend-hunter?utm_source=api) on board. Take the ETBs, hand out the goaded bodies, then restamp Dack the same turn if the mana is there. Hold [**Restoration Angel**](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/38/restoration-angel?utm_source=api) or [**Getaway Glamer**](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/14/getaway-glamer?utm_source=api) for the response.

**Late.** Reclaim the best gifts, or dump [**Zetalpa**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/142/zetalpa-primal-dawn?utm_source=api) / [**Salvation Colossus**](https://scryfall.com/card/m3c/43/salvation-colossus?utm_source=api) on your side and attack through the goaded mess. [**Endless Sands**](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/272/endless-sands?utm_source=api) can store Dack through a wipe and return him later. [**Perch Protection**](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/BLC-11/perch-protection?utm_source=api) buys a rotation if someone is swinging for the table.

## Mulligan

Keep two or three [Plains](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/317/plains?utm_source=api), a two-mana rock, and a path to six by turn four or five. A cage in hand is a keep; an all-seven-drop hand with two lands is not. [**Sol Ring**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/211/sol-ring?utm_source=api) plus a land is a snap keep. A looter without ramp is only a keep if the other five cards still hit six on time.

## Sequencing

1. Dack's creatures enter under you. Let every enter trigger resolve **before** the control change; pick cages first if several are on the stack.
2. Give the worst guest to the player who most needs to be attacking someone else. Keep the indestructible closer if the dump found one and you have a blink to reclaim it.
3. [**Fiend Hunter**](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/167/fiend-hunter?utm_source=api) can exile Dack himself if you need a cheap restamp and [**High Market**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/380/high-market?utm_source=api) is open.
4. [**Restoration Angel**](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/38/restoration-angel?utm_source=api) cannot blink another Angel. Use [**Flicker of Fate**](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/16/flicker-of-fate?utm_source=api), [**Flickerwisp**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/11/flickerwisp?utm_source=api), or Abdel for Angel-heavy dumps.
5. [**Parting Gust**](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/24/parting-gust?utm_source=api) without the gift is a blink; with the gift it is removal. Do not gift when you meant to restamp Dack.
6. [**Palace Jailer**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/140/palace-jailer?utm_source=api)'s cage lasts until an opponent becomes the monarch, not until Jailer leaves. Stay the monarch; the goaded Jailer cannot legally attack you.
7. Rest-of-game goad is on those permanents. A blink makes a new object, so goad does not follow a body you steal back.

## Weaknesses

Six mana before the deck does the thing. Graveyard exile and Rest in Peace turn [Angel of Indemnity](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/133/angel-of-indemnity?utm_source=api) and the End Game extras off. Homeward Path is irrelevant (you already own the gifts), but a steal effect on Dack is ugly. Board wipes that hit Dack before a restamp end the party until you recast him. Creature-light topdecks make Dack reveal half the library for three bodies.
