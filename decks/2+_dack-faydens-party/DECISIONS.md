## How to use

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a cut back.

Every unique card needs a Cards in line. The other headings appear when there is something to record.

## Goals

- **Donate** — Dack gives each opponent a creature after you take its enter trigger; owner-return blinks reclaim the gift, they do not become the opponent's card.
- **Blink** — Restamp Dack and the cages. Self-blink and owner-return blinks are the engine, not a control shell.
- **Goad** — Donated creatures are goaded for the rest of the game so they keep attacking someone else.
- **Cages** — Fiend Hunter-style ETBs exile opposing permanents before the creature changes teams.

## Rankings

First pass: jank 8, fun 8, mean 5, Donate 9, Blink 8, Goad 7, Cages 8. Mean stays moderate because cages are telegraphed and the table still plays; goad is only on Dack's dump.

- **Culling Dais** — Donate leaves no fodder; sacrificing a cage opens it. Replaced by Rumor Gatherer.
- **Helvault** — Returns only when it dies, and the 99 has no artifact sacrifice. Endless Sands already delayed-blinks. Replaced by Palace Jailer.
- **Matzalantli, the Great Door** — Loot with no Treasure and a late transform. Replaced by Beza, the Bounding Spring.
- **Phyrexian Vault** — Pay-mana sac-draw with nothing to feed it. Replaced by Perch Protection.

## Rules

Dack puts the revealed creatures onto the battlefield under your control, their enter triggers go on the stack, and only after that does each opponent gain control of one. Blink spells that return a creature under its owner's control reclaim a donated card you own. Rest-of-game goad is attached to those permanents; a zone change makes a new object, so the goad does not follow a blinked body.
