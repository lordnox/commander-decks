Simic secret-council cheat: [**Círdan the Shipwright**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/50/c%C3%ADrdan-the-shipwright?utm_source=api) attacks, the table votes, and you either draw or put a monster into play. When the vote will not give you zero, [**Show and Tell**](https://scryfall.com/card/cn2/121/show-and-tell?utm_source=api) and its cousins still dump. The game ends in one combat after [**Reins of Power**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/734/reins-of-power?utm_source=api) borrows the best army.

## How the deck works

[**Círdan the Shipwright**](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/50/c%C3%ADrdan-the-shipwright?utm_source=api) is a 3/4 with vigilance. Whenever he enters or attacks, each player secretly votes for a player. Everyone draws equal to the votes they received. Anyone who received **no** votes may put a permanent from hand onto the battlefield.

That is two modes on one trigger. Vote-for-Círdan is "I needed cards." Zero votes is [Show and Tell](https://scryfall.com/card/cn2/121/show-and-tell?utm_source=api). Voting for yourself always blocks your own dump. [**Illusion of Choice**](https://scryfall.com/card/cn2/31/illusion-of-choice?utm_source=api) lets you fill in every ballot for a turn: all votes on you to draw four (and let the table dump), or spread across opponents so only you cheat.

When the council will not cooperate, the 99 still cheats: [**Show and Tell**](https://scryfall.com/card/cn2/121/show-and-tell?utm_source=api), [**Eureka**](https://scryfall.com/card/leg/185/eureka?utm_source=api), [**Hypergenesis**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/210/hypergenesis?utm_source=api), [**Braids, Conjurer Adept**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/76/braids-conjurer-adept?utm_source=api), and [**Selvala's Stampede**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/269/selvalas-stampede?utm_source=api). Those cards put permanents onto the battlefield, so cast triggers do not fire.

## Win conditions

The named kill is combat. [**Reins of Power**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/734/reins-of-power?utm_source=api) untaps both creature teams, swaps them with one opponent until end of turn, and gives haste. Attack the players you did **not** swap with. [**Twincast**](https://scryfall.com/card/m10/78/twincast?utm_source=api) or [**Split Decision**](https://scryfall.com/card/cns/25/split-decision?utm_source=api) can copy Reins onto a second opponent. Then [**Overwhelming Stampede**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/178/overwhelming-stampede?utm_source=api) sets trample and +X from the greatest power you control.

[**End-Raze Forerunners**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/822/end-raze-forerunners?utm_source=api) should enter after the swap so the borrowed creatures get the pump. [**Concordant Crossroads**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/141/concordant-crossroads?utm_source=api) lets your own cheated creatures attack the turn they enter. [**Illusionist's Gambit**](https://scryfall.com/card/c13/47/illusionists-gambit?utm_source=api) is the redirect: their attack takes a second combat that cannot hit you.

Backup is just attacking with dumped monsters after fights have cleared the blockers: [**Sire of Seven Deaths**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/1/sire-of-seven-deaths?utm_source=api), [**Pathbreaker Ibex**](https://scryfall.com/card/cma/132/pathbreaker-ibex?utm_source=api), [**Thunderfoot Baloth**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/201/thunderfoot-baloth?utm_source=api).

## Early, mid, and late

**Early.** One-mana dork or aura, then [Nature's Lore](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/177/natures-lore?utm_source=api) / [Three Visits](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/181/three-visits?utm_source=api) / a signet. Keep a land drop. Círdan on turn three is the high roll; turn four or five is fine.

**Mid.** Attack with Círdan every turn. Take draws until you have a monster and an answer. If the table starts denying you zero, cast [Show and Tell](https://scryfall.com/card/cn2/121/show-and-tell?utm_source=api) or Braids instead of begging. Fight their titan with Kogla, [Thorn Mammoth](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/323/thorn-mammoth?utm_source=api), or [Triangle of War](https://scryfall.com/card/vis/158/triangle-of-war?utm_source=api).

**Late.** Hold Reins plus a pump spell. Minamo can untap Círdan for a second council in a turn. [Reliquary Tower](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/398/reliquary-tower?utm_source=api) keeps the huge hands.

## Mulligan

Keep: two lands (one green), a one-mana ramp spell, and a way to hit five by turn three or four. A [Show and Tell](https://scryfall.com/card/cn2/121/show-and-tell?utm_source=api) in the opener is a keep only if you also have a monster worth sharing. All-monster hands with two lands go back. [Illusion of Choice](https://scryfall.com/card/cn2/31/illusion-of-choice?utm_source=api) is a luxury, not a must-keep.

## Sequencing

1. Do not vote for yourself if you intend to dump.
2. [Illusion of Choice](https://scryfall.com/card/cn2/31/illusion-of-choice?utm_source=api) on Círdan's trigger, not randomly in the dark.
3. Reins, then End-Raze or Stampede — never the reverse if the pump is an ETB.
4. [Twincast](https://scryfall.com/card/m10/78/twincast?utm_source=api) Reins targeting a different player than the original.
5. Cheated creatures have summoning sickness unless Crossroads, Reins, or native haste is around.

## Weaknesses

The table can put a single vote on you and turn Círdan into a cantrip. Creature wipes empty both the dump and the Reins setup. Homeward Path undoes Elrond. Graveyard-shuffle Colossi and extra-turn Eldrazi are not in the 99; the maybeboard holds [Darksteel Colossus](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/671/darksteel-colossus?utm_source=api), [Blightsteel Colossus](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/235/blightsteel-colossus?utm_source=api), [Void Winnower](https://scryfall.com/card/bfz/17/void-winnower?utm_source=api), and [It That Betrays](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/805/it-that-betrays?utm_source=api) when the table needs a meaner statue.
