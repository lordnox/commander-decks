Esper aikido control that looks like a harmless old cleric. It spends its own life to draw cards, refuses to fight over the board, swaps life totals with whoever is winning, and then drains the table with a [Cabal Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/301/cabal-coffers?utm_source=api) X spell. Zero Game Changers and zero card tutors by design.

**Commander:** [Lady Evangela](https://scryfall.com/card/leg/240/lady-evangela?utm_source=api)  
**Deck size:** 100 cards  
**Primary plan:** pay life for cards, hold up fogs and counters, then win with [Exsanguinate](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/173/exsanguinate?utm_source=api), [Debt to the Deathless](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/176/debt-to-the-deathless?utm_source=api), then finish the survivor with [Drain Life](https://scryfall.com/card/5ed/156/drain-life?utm_source=api)  
**Backup plan:** dump to single-digit life, swap totals with the archenemy, and let [Queza, Augur of Agonies](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/212/queza-augur-of-agonies?utm_source=api) finish while the fogs hold

## How the deck works

Most decks treat life as a buffer. This one treats it as a currency with a refund at the end.

Life goes out through [Wall of Blood](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/82/wall-of-blood?utm_source=api), [Necrologia](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/7ED-149/necrologia?utm_source=api), [Dark Confidant](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/94/dark-confidant?utm_source=api), [Dark Tutelage](https://scryfall.com/card/m11/90/dark-tutelage?utm_source=api), [Selenia, Dark Angel](https://scryfall.com/card/tmp/270/selenia-dark-angel?utm_source=api), [Blood Celebrant](https://scryfall.com/card/lgn/61/blood-celebrant?utm_source=api), [Pain's Reward](https://scryfall.com/card/sok/85/pains-reward?utm_source=api), [Snuff Out](https://scryfall.com/card/gvl/53/snuff-out?utm_source=api), and three painful lands. Most of that spending buys cards, which is how the deck finds its finishers without running a single tutor. [Children of Korlis](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/14/children-of-korlis?utm_source=api) and [Tainted Sigil](https://scryfall.com/card/arb/83/tainted-sigil?utm_source=api) refund life lost this turn when the dump did not get an exchange. The Sigil also counts life everyone else lost, so it is the bigger valve after combat or a drain.

Life comes back in one of two ways. Either a drain spell resolves and the deck gains everything it deals, or a life-exchange effect hands your low total to somebody who is doing well and hands you theirs. Both are wins, which is why being at 4 life here is a position rather than an emergency. [Phial of Galadriel](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/248/phial-of-galadriel?utm_source=api) doubles that drain refund while you are at 5 or less.

Nothing in that plan involves attacking, so the deck buys time with prevention instead of removal. Seven combat fogs, [Everybody Lives!](https://scryfall.com/card/who/18/everybody-lives!?utm_source=api) against mass drain, two creature taxes plus [Wall of Shards](https://scryfall.com/card/csp/23/wall-of-shards?utm_source=api), six counterspells, and a commander who switches off one attacker every turn keep the board from mattering while the life engine runs. [Bender's Waterskin](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/255/benders-waterskin?utm_source=api) is the draw-go rock: it untaps on each opponent's turn, so the mana you hold for fogs keeps coming back. [Phial of Galadriel](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/248/phial-of-galadriel?utm_source=api) is the other `{3}` rock: any color, plus the empty-hand draw and the low-life refund.

[Blue Sun's Zenith](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/44/blue-suns-zenith?utm_source=api), [Arcane Denial](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/147/arcane-denial?utm_source=api), and [Lotho, Corrupt Shirriff](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/213/lotho-corrupt-shirriff?utm_source=api) all reward keeping mana open. Zenith converts unused Coffers mana into hidden cards at the last opponent's end step, [Arcane Denial](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/147/arcane-denial?utm_source=api) protects the decisive spell while compensating its target, and [Lotho, Corrupt Shirriff](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/213/lotho-corrupt-shirriff?utm_source=api) turns players' second spells into Treasures and political bargaining chips. All three conceal the eventual kill or invite a deal rather than advertising it on the battlefield.

[Orzhov Signet](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/323/orzhov-signet?utm_source=api) and [Dimir Signet](https://scryfall.com/card/clu/221/dimir-signet?utm_source=api) replace the Talismans and Fellwar Stone package. Both preserve black production while covering one secondary color, and neither requires life.

The deck also pretends to be group hug. [Secret Rendezvous](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/166/secret-rendezvous?utm_source=api) is a one-shot bribe: you and a chosen opponent each draw three. [Tenuous Truce](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/87/tenuous-truce?utm_source=api) is the repeatable version: enchant an opponent, both draw on their end step, and the Aura dies if either of you attacks the other. That is the "do not attack, vampires" disguise. [Loran of the Third Path](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/71/loran-of-the-third-path?utm_source=api) taps for the same shared draw and incidentally answers an artifact or enchantment.

## Mana engine

The finishers are capped by black mana, not by total mana, which is why the deck is built around [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api) count rather than around rocks.

The land base is built to make that number large on its own. Of 38 lands, 16 are Swamps before any effect: nine basics plus [Godless Shrine](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/285/godless-shrine?utm_source=api), [Watery Grave](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/306/watery-grave?utm_source=api), [Raffine's Tower](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/254/raffines-tower?utm_source=api), [Shadowy Backstreet](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/268/shadowy-backstreet?utm_source=api), [Undercity Sewers](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/270/undercity-sewers?utm_source=api), [Eclipsed Steppe](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/53/eclipsed-steppe?utm_source=api), and [Sunken Hollow](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/271/sunken-hollow?utm_source=api). A dual with the [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api) subtype counts for [Cabal Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/301/cabal-coffers?utm_source=api), triggers [Crypt Ghast](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/70/crypt-ghast?utm_source=api) and [Nirkana Revenant](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/134/nirkana-revenant?utm_source=api), untaps to [Bubbling Muck](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/54/bubbling-muck?utm_source=api), and can be fetched — so the engine runs without Urborg rather than waiting for it.

[Cabal Stronghold](https://scryfall.com/card/dom/238/cabal-stronghold?utm_source=api) is the exception: it counts *basic* Swamps only, which no [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api)-granting effect ever helps. That is why nine of the sixteen are basics, and why fetch lands should grab a basic when Stronghold is the engine on the table.

[Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/287/urborg-tomb-of-yawgmoth?utm_source=api) and [Blanket of Night](https://scryfall.com/card/vis/52/blanket-of-night?utm_source=api) then make *every* land you control a [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api), roughly doubling the count. [Cabal Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/301/cabal-coffers?utm_source=api) pays `{2}` and taps for black equal to whatever that number is.

[Crypt Ghast](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/70/crypt-ghast?utm_source=api), [Nirkana Revenant](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/134/nirkana-revenant?utm_source=api), and [Bubbling Muck](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/54/bubbling-muck?utm_source=api) all say the same thing: whenever you tap a [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api) for mana, add an additional `{B}`. Read that carefully, because it does **not** double Coffers. Tapping Coffers is a single [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api) tap, so it gets exactly one extra black. The doubling happens on every *other* [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api) you tap, and that is where most of the mana actually comes from. Ten lands under Urborg with [Crypt Ghast](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/70/crypt-ghast?utm_source=api) out is roughly 27 black if you tap everything: nine lands at two each, minus the `{2}` for Coffers, plus eleven off the Coffers activation itself. That is [Exsanguinate](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/173/exsanguinate?utm_source=api) for 25.

[Bubbling Muck](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/54/bubbling-muck?utm_source=api) is a one-mana third copy of that trigger for a single turn. It is symmetrical, but nobody else at the table is about to tap eight Swamps.

[Minamo, School at Water's Edge](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/279/minamo-school-at-waters-edge?utm_source=api) is not part of this engine. It untaps only *legendary* permanents, and neither [Deserted Temple](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/301/deserted-temple?utm_source=api) nor [Cabal Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/301/cabal-coffers?utm_source=api) is legendary.

The scaling line is **[Deserted Temple](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/301/deserted-temple?utm_source=api) + [Rings of Brighthearth](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/335/rings-of-brighthearth?utm_source=api)**. Rings only copies activated abilities that are *not* mana abilities, so it cannot copy [Cabal Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/301/cabal-coffers?utm_source=api) or [Magus of the Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/c14/148/magus-of-the-coffers?utm_source=api) — but [Deserted Temple](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/301/deserted-temple?utm_source=api)'s `{1}`, tap: untap target land qualifies. Activate Temple targeting Coffers, pay `{2}` to copy it, and point the copy at [Deserted Temple](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/301/deserted-temple?utm_source=api) itself. Both end up untapped, so you can do it again. Each cycle costs `{5}` all told and returns black equal to your [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api) count, which makes it net positive from six Swamps and arbitrarily large after that. It is a mana engine, not a win by itself — you still have to cast the drain.

Every mana ability here resolves without using the stack, so you can build the lethal X spell with counterspell mana still untapped.

## Win conditions

1. **The drain turn.** Untap with Coffers and a [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api)-trigger creature, cast [Exsanguinate](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/173/exsanguinate?utm_source=api) or [Debt to the Deathless](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/176/debt-to-the-deathless?utm_source=api) for a lethal X, and gain it all back. Debt drains 2 per X per opponent, so it needs roughly half the mana [Exsanguinate](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/173/exsanguinate?utm_source=api) does in a four-player pod.
2. **The transfusion.** Dump to 1–4 life with [Wall of Blood](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/82/wall-of-blood?utm_source=api), then hand that total to the player who is actually threatening the table. [Mirror Universe](https://scryfall.com/card/leg/287/mirror-universe?utm_source=api) does this to one player; [Repay in Kind](https://scryfall.com/card/roe/125/repay-in-kind?utm_source=api) sets everyone to the lowest total at once, which usually puts the whole table into [Exsanguinate](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/173/exsanguinate?utm_source=api) range. [Mister Negative](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/135/mister-negative?utm_source=api) is the blinkable copy.
3. **Draw damage.** [Queza, Augur of Agonies](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/212/queza-augur-of-agonies?utm_source=api) drains one target opponent per draw. [Necrologia](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/7ED-149/necrologia?utm_source=api) or [Blue Sun's Zenith](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/44/blue-suns-zenith?utm_source=api) can turn that into a focused kill while refilling the hand.
4. **Their own attack.** [Inkshield](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/316/inkshield?utm_source=api) and [Comeuppance](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/58/comeuppance?utm_source=api) turn a lethal swing at you into a board or into a dead attacker. This is the aikido win the deck is named for, and it is common enough to play toward.

## Early, mid, and late game

**Turns 1–3.** Land, rock, tax creature. Play [Dark Confidant](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/94/dark-confidant?utm_source=api) or [Tenuous Truce](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/87/tenuous-truce?utm_source=api) on curve and let them run. Point the Truce at the player least likely to attack you. Do not cast [Lady Evangela](https://scryfall.com/card/leg/240/lady-evangela?utm_source=api) yet unless the board demands it; there is nothing to prevent.

**Turns 4–7.** Build [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api) count and hold up interaction. This is the draw-go window: keep two or three mana open, use it on a fog or a counter, and spend everything you did not need on a life-for-cards spell in the end step. [Necrologia](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/7ED-149/necrologia?utm_source=api) is specifically an end-of-turn card for exactly this.

[Fact or Fiction](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/91/fact-or-fiction?utm_source=api) is the default four-mana refill. [Secret Rendezvous](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/166/secret-rendezvous?utm_source=api) is the three-mana version that also pays a political bribe.

**Turn 8 onward.** Land [Cabal Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/301/cabal-coffers?utm_source=api) alongside [Crypt Ghast](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/70/crypt-ghast?utm_source=api) or [Nirkana Revenant](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/134/nirkana-revenant?utm_source=api), count the table's life totals, and decide whether the mana is there. If it is short, cast [Blue Sun's Zenith](https://scryfall.com/card/a25/44/blue-suns-zenith?utm_source=api) at the last opponent's end step and try again next turn — the fort is designed to let you take the extra turn.

## Mulligan guide

Keep a hand with two or three lands and at least two of: a rock, a life-for-cards spell, a cheap fog, a counterspell.

- Keep hands with early defense even if the mana engine is missing; the deck draws enough to find Coffers.
- Ship hands with the finishers but no way to survive to cast them. [Debt to the Deathless](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/176/debt-to-the-deathless?utm_source=api) in an opening hand is close to a blank.
- Ship hands with the exchange package and no life payment. [Mirror Universe](https://scryfall.com/card/leg/287/mirror-universe?utm_source=api) at 40 life does nothing.
- One-land hands are unkeepable. There are no rituals here other than [Bubbling Muck](https://scryfall.com/card/uds/54/bubbling-muck?utm_source=api), which needs lands to work.

## Sequencing and rules notes

- **[Repay in Kind](https://scryfall.com/card/roe/125/repay-in-kind?utm_source=api) sets, it does not distribute.** Every player's total becomes the lowest total among all players. Cast it while you are the low player, never before.
- **[Mister Negative](https://scryfall.com/card/spm/135/mister-negative?utm_source=api)'s exchange is an entry trigger**, so it can be countered and it can be repeated. [Ephemerate](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/7/ephemerate?utm_source=api) rebounds, giving a second exchange the following turn.
- **[Wall of Blood](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/82/wall-of-blood?utm_source=api) has no cap and works at instant speed.** Hold the pump until the exchange effect is already on the stack and cannot be answered, then dump.
- **[Mirror Universe](https://scryfall.com/card/leg/287/mirror-universe?utm_source=api) only works during your upkeep.** Plan the life dump for that step rather than the turn before. Everything else the deck does can wait until an opponent's end step.
- **[Selenia, Dark Angel](https://scryfall.com/card/tmp/270/selenia-dark-angel?utm_source=api) returns itself for 2 life**, so it is an unbounded life sink limited only by the mana to recast it.
- **[Lady Evangela](https://scryfall.com/card/leg/240/lady-evangela?utm_source=api) shuts off one creature completely.** She prevents all combat damage that would be dealt by target creature this turn, for `{W}{B}` and a tap. That answers the Voltron commander or the one huge trampler every turn, but it does nothing against a wide board — that is what the fogs are for. [Minamo, School at Water's Edge](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/279/minamo-school-at-waters-edge?utm_source=api) can untap her or [Sokrates, Athenian Teacher](https://scryfall.com/card/acr/67/sokrates-athenian-teacher?utm_source=api) for a second activation, since both are legendary.
- **Do not tap out on your own turn** unless the drain is lethal. The entire fort assumes two or three open mana.

- **[Energy Arc](https://scryfall.com/card/all/106/energy-arc?utm_source=api) fog-and-untaps.** Target the attacking team for a two-mana fog, or include your tapped clerics so [Lady Evangela](https://scryfall.com/card/leg/240/lady-evangela?utm_source=api) and Sokrates stand back up in the same combat.
- **[Sokrates, Athenian Teacher](https://scryfall.com/card/acr/67/sokrates-athenian-teacher?utm_source=api) only stops combat damage to players**, not to creatures or planeswalkers, and the draw is half that damage rounded down. Hexproof while untapped; [Minamo, School at Water's Edge](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/279/minamo-school-at-waters-edge?utm_source=api) or [Energy Arc](https://scryfall.com/card/all/106/energy-arc?utm_source=api) stands him back up.
- **[Children of Korlis](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/14/children-of-korlis?utm_source=api) and [Tainted Sigil](https://scryfall.com/card/arb/83/tainted-sigil?utm_source=api) refund life lost this turn.** Do not sacrifice them when you still need the low total for [Mirror Universe](https://scryfall.com/card/leg/287/mirror-universe?utm_source=api) or [Repay in Kind](https://scryfall.com/card/roe/125/repay-in-kind?utm_source=api). The Sigil counts every player's loss, so it is larger after combat or a drain; it has to be in play and tap.
- **[Pain's Reward](https://scryfall.com/card/sok/85/pains-reward?utm_source=api) is an auction.** Bid when you want four cards and a dump; let someone else win when they overpay.
- **[Scapegoat](https://scryfall.com/card/sth/14/scapegoat?utm_source=api) bounces your creatures**, after you sacrifice one. [Kami of False Hope](https://scryfall.com/card/bok/10/kami-of-false-hope?utm_source=api) is the cheapest fodder. It cannot bounce an opponent's attacker.
- **[Minamo, School at Water's Edge](https://scryfall.com/card/chk/279/minamo-school-at-waters-edge?utm_source=api) only untaps *legendary* permanents.** That is [Lady Evangela](https://scryfall.com/card/leg/240/lady-evangela?utm_source=api), [Sokrates, Athenian Teacher](https://scryfall.com/card/acr/67/sokrates-athenian-teacher?utm_source=api), and [Loran of the Third Path](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/71/loran-of-the-third-path?utm_source=api). [Deserted Temple](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/301/deserted-temple?utm_source=api), [Cabal Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/301/cabal-coffers?utm_source=api), and [Magus of the Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/c14/148/magus-of-the-coffers?utm_source=api) are not legendary, so it is never a second Coffers untap.
- **Cast the fog, not the counterspell**, when both answer the same attack. Counterspells are reserved for whatever tries to stop the exchange or the drain.
- **[Tenuous Truce](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/87/tenuous-truce?utm_source=api) dies if either of you attacks the other.** Enchant the player most likely to sit still, never the archenemy. Third parties can still attack either of you.
- **[Everybody Lives!](https://scryfall.com/card/who/18/everybody-lives!?utm_source=api) blanks life loss and winning for the turn.** Use it against an opposing drain or a lethal noncombat hit, never with [Wall of Blood](https://scryfall.com/card/mrd/82/wall-of-blood?utm_source=api) or [Exsanguinate](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/173/exsanguinate?utm_source=api) already in the plan.
- **[Snuff Out](https://scryfall.com/card/gvl/53/snuff-out?utm_source=api) misses black creatures.** Pay 4 life whenever a [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api) is in play; otherwise it is `{3}{B}`. [Swords to Plowshares](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/143/swords-to-plowshares?utm_source=api) and [Anguished Unmaking](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/293/anguished-unmaking?utm_source=api) cover the black ones.
- **[Phial of Galadriel](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/248/phial-of-galadriel?utm_source=api) doubles a drain only if you are still at 5 or less when the life is gained.** Stay in that band for the kill; it turns off after the refill puts you above 5.
- **[Loran of the Third Path](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/71/loran-of-the-third-path?utm_source=api)'s tap can be copied by Rings**, so you and a chosen opponent each draw two. That is still a deal.

## Weaknesses

- **Enchantment and artifact hate.** [Mirror Universe](https://scryfall.com/card/leg/287/mirror-universe?utm_source=api), [Rings of Brighthearth](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/335/rings-of-brighthearth?utm_source=api), [Phial of Galadriel](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/248/phial-of-galadriel?utm_source=api), and [Tainted Sigil](https://scryfall.com/card/arb/83/tainted-sigil?utm_source=api) are answerable permanents. [Magus of the Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/c14/148/magus-of-the-coffers?utm_source=api) is the creature backup for the mana engine; the extra Mirror copies left the list.
- **Land destruction and [Cabal Coffers](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/301/cabal-coffers?utm_source=api) removal.** Losing Coffers costs several turns. [Ghostly Flicker](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/98/ghostly-flicker?utm_source=api) can save it in response.
- **Being at low life with no exchange in hand.** The payment suite is a real risk if the deck spends without a plan. Only dump below 10 with an exchange, a drain, or a fog available.
- **Noncombat damage and mass drain.** Combat fogs miss this. [Comeuppance](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/58/comeuppance?utm_source=api) only reflects *damage*, not life loss. [Everybody Lives!](https://scryfall.com/card/who/18/everybody-lives!?utm_source=api) is the panic button: do not cast it on your own dump or drain turn.
- **Fast combo.** With zero Game Changers, no tutors, and six counterspells, the deck interacts well but cannot race a turn-four kill.
- **Shared draw feeds the table.** [Tenuous Truce](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/87/tenuous-truce?utm_source=api) and [Secret Rendezvous](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/166/secret-rendezvous?utm_source=api) give an opponent cards. Point them at the player who is not threatening the table, never at the archenemy.

It left so the 99 could run [Snuff Out](https://scryfall.com/card/gvl/53/snuff-out?utm_source=api): a [Swamp](https://scryfall.com/card/trk/321/swamp?utm_source=api)-fueled 4-life kill that advances the dump instead of storing colorless for next turn.

[Hatred](https://scryfall.com/card/exo/64/hatred?utm_source=api) is the political ambush: when two opponents arrange a harmless attack to exchange the monarch or another combat reward, it can turn the nominal 1/1 into lethal damage without this deck attacking anyone. It can also enlarge an opponent's attacker aimed at the archenemy. The risk is paying most of your life and five mana into instant-speed removal, so it stays here until the deck proves it can protect that line.

[Blood Celebrant](https://scryfall.com/card/lgn/61/blood-celebrant?utm_source=api) remains in the 99 because it does a different job. It converts surplus black into the exact white or blue mana needed for the deck's difficult costs, pays life in controlled one-point increments, and its mana ability cannot be responded to. If fixing proves unnecessary, Celebrant is the cleanest slot for testing [Hatred](https://scryfall.com/card/exo/64/hatred?utm_source=api).

[Desertion](https://scryfall.com/card/cn2/107/desertion?utm_source=api) is the steal-counter if the table is creature-heavy and five open mana is cheap; Arcanis is the draw engine if the finishers keep missing.

[Wedding Ring](https://scryfall.com/card/voc/32/wedding-ring?utm_source=api) is the louder group-hug draw engine.
