## How the deck works

1. Ramp and cantrip into [**Jhoira of the Ghitu**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/256/jhoira-of-the-ghitu?utm_source=api) on turn three or four.
2. Exile bombs and value spells with her ability, or suspend native cards like [**Ancestral Vision**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/144/ancestral-vision?utm_source=api) and [**Errant Ephemeron**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/66/errant-ephemeron?utm_source=api) from hand.
3. Speed the tub with timer hacks and extra upkeeps.
4. When counters empty, cast for free. Attack with haste creatures, refill with rebound draw, steal with [**Inevitable Betrayal**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/47/inevitable-betrayal?utm_source=api).

This is not a loop. Each suspended card is one free cast. Timer hacks remove a finite number of counters.

## Win conditions

- Combat with free haste creatures from suspend (Kraken, Gargadons, dragons, Chronicler).
- [**Inevitable Betrayal**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/47/inevitable-betrayal?utm_source=api) taking the largest creature in a graveyard after three upkeeps.
- [**Niv-Mizzet, the Firemind**](https://scryfall.com/card/c20/225/niv-mizzet-the-firemind?utm_source=api) pinging off draws once the tub is feeding the hand.
- Attrition via repeating [**Reality Strobe**](https://scryfall.com/card/fut/43/reality-strobe?utm_source=api), [**Arc Blade**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/152/arc-blade?utm_source=api), and [**Capsize**](https://scryfall.com/card/mp2/32/capsize?utm_source=api).

## Early, mid, and late

**Early.** Land, rock, cantrips ([**Portent**](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/74/portent?utm_source=api), [**Serum Visions**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/112/serum-visions?utm_source=api), [**Careful Study**](https://scryfall.com/card/ody/70/careful-study?utm_source=api)). [Suspend](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/68/suspend?utm_source=api) cheap spells if Jhoira is slow. Keep interaction that also speaks the theme ([**Delay**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/61/delay?utm_source=api), [**Rift Bolt**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/122/rift-bolt?utm_source=api)).

**Mid.** Jhoira loads the tub. Play [**Paradox Haze**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsp/71/paradox-haze?utm_source=api) on yourself. Hold [**Fury Charm**](https://scryfall.com/card/ima/130/fury-charm?utm_source=api) or [**Clockspinning**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsp/53/clockspinning?utm_source=api) for the turn a bomb should land. Bounce or wipe with [**Evacuation**](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/156/evacuation?utm_source=api) / [**Starstorm**](https://scryfall.com/card/nec/110/starstorm?utm_source=api) if the table races you.

**Late.** Resolve a pile of free creatures in one or two upkeeps, attack, and use rebound draw to restock. [**Greater Gargadon**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/111/greater-gargadon?utm_source=api) that has been fed sacrifices arrives as a finisher. [**Riptide Laboratory**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/303/riptide-laboratory?utm_source=api) bounces Jhoira if she is under fire.

## Mulligan guide

**Keep.** Two lands with blue, a path to Jhoira by turn four, and either a cantrip, a timer hack, or a cheap suspend spell.

**Mulligan.** One-land hands without [**Wayfarer's Bauble**](https://scryfall.com/card/hoc/211/wayfarers-bauble?utm_source=api) or a cycle land; hands of only expensive hardcasts with no suspend outlet; all interaction and no engine.

**Borderline.** One bomb plus two lands is fine if you can suspend it natively or cast Jhoira soon. A hand of only timer hacks needs a payload in the top few cards.

## Sequencing notes

- Pay `{2}` to load Jhoira; the card always gets four counters, even if its mana value is one.
- Respond to the last-counter trigger only if you need to, and cast optional suspended spells when the board is clear enough to attack.
- Put [**Paradox Haze**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsp/71/paradox-haze?utm_source=api) on yourself, not an opponent, unless you are giving them a bad upkeep joke.
- [**Delay**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/61/delay?utm_source=api) parks their spell for three of *their* upkeeps; it will come back.
- Feed [**Greater Gargadon**](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/111/greater-gargadon?utm_source=api) only while it is suspended.

## Weaknesses

- Exile hate and Stifle effects stop the tub mid-cook.
- Jhoira is a magnet once two or three cards are suspended; [**Riptide Laboratory**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/303/riptide-laboratory?utm_source=api) and counters are the answers.
- The board is thin before free creatures resolve; token and go-wide decks can race.
- No Game Changers and no permanent fast mana: if the table is Upgraded-plus, expect to be behind on raw rate.
