# Decisions

## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, the primer, and the assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a cut back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated session notes so an argument does not live only in chat.

## Primer

- Brew title **Tea Party** is locked from the submitted list: Bumbleflower shares extra cards while the 99 grows +1/+1 counters. Rename if a different title is preferred.
- The second Bumbleflower trigger each turn draws two cards; the primer treats "cast two spells" as the main engine, not "draw extra" in the commander text's first clause.
- Targeting an opponent with Bumbleflower is a crime, which is why Freestrider Lookout is described as on-plan land.
- Loki draws from abilities this deck controls targeting; the primer names Bumbleflower as the usual trigger, not spell targeting.
- Triumph of the Hordes is the listed closer, not an infect tribal plan.
- Force of Will is a cached Game Changer and Sol Ring is kitchen-table permanent fast mana; both stay because the list was imported unchanged.

## Rules

- Bumbleflower's trigger targets an opponent and a creature as part of one ability. That is one crime and one Loki trigger (Loki is once per turn).
- The second resolution of Bumbleflower in a turn draws two; it does not require that the two spells are instants, and Seedborn Muse plus flash lets those two spells happen on an opponent's turn.
- Gift spells draw the gifted opponent a card before other effects, which can be their second draw and trigger Faerie Mastermind, King T'Challa, Gleaming Splendor, and Trouble in Pairs.
- Dreamtide Whale proliferates on *a player*'s second spell, so opponents' double-spells also tick vanishing counters and +1/+1 counters.
