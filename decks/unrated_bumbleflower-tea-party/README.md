Bant extra-spell counters: each spell feeds [**Ms. Bumbleflower**](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/3/ms-bumbleflower?utm_source=api), the second spell of the turn draws two, and those cards plus +1/+1 counters pay the rest of the 99. Opponents get the extra cards; this deck gets the bigger creatures and the second-draw payoffs.

## How the deck works

[**Ms. Bumbleflower**](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/3/ms-bumbleflower?utm_source=api) triggers on every spell: an opponent draws, a creature gets a +1/+1 counter and flying until end of turn, and the second resolution that turn draws two. The first spell is setup; the second spell is the engine.

Put the counter on whatever needs it that turn: [**Incubation Druid**](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/302/incubation-druid?utm_source=api) or [**Kami of Whispered Hopes**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/273/kami-of-whispered-hopes?utm_source=api) for mana, [**Dusk Legion Duelist**](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/11/dusk-legion-duelist?utm_source=api) or [**Pensive Professor**](https://scryfall.com/card/sos/63/pensive-professor?utm_source=api) for a card, [**Basking Broodscale**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/145/basking-broodscale?utm_source=api) for a Spawn, or [**Ouroboroid**](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/201/ouroboroid?utm_source=api) so combat multiplies the whole team. [**Kid Loki**](https://scryfall.com/card/msh/63/kid-loki?utm_source=api) then hexproofs anything that received a counter this turn.

Targeting an opponent with Bumbleflower is a crime, so [**Freestrider Lookout**](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/163/freestrider-lookout?utm_source=api) can drop a land. The same trigger is an ability that targets, so [**Loki, God of Mischief**](https://scryfall.com/card/msh/65/loki-god-of-mischief?utm_source=api) draws once per turn.

[**Seedborn Muse**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/268/seedborn-muse?utm_source=api) and [**Bender's Waterskin**](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/255/benders-waterskin?utm_source=api) untap on other players' turns. [**High Fae Trickster**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/40/high-fae-trickster?utm_source=api), [**Final-Word Phantom**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/22/final-word-phantom?utm_source=api), [**Aang, Swift Savior**](https://scryfall.com/card/tla/204/aang-swift-savior-aang-and-la-oceans-fury?utm_source=api), and [**Whitemane Lion**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/35/whitemane-lion?utm_source=api) spend that mana at instant speed so Bumbleflower can fire twice on someone else's turn.

## Win conditions

Combat is the default. Counters plus flying from Bumbleflower, [**Ouroboroid**](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/201/ouroboroid?utm_source=api) at combat, [**Chasm Skulker**](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/218/chasm-skulker?utm_source=api), and token makers present lethal without a named combo.

[**Triumph of the Hordes**](https://scryfall.com/card/nph/123/triumph-of-the-hordes?utm_source=api) turns that board into a ten-poison clock with trample. [**Jolrael, Mwonvuli Recluse**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/173/jolrael-mwonvuli-recluse?utm_source=api)'s overrun is the backup when tokens outnumber the counters.

There is no two-card infinite in the 99. [**Basking Broodscale**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/145/basking-broodscale?utm_source=api) makes Spawn when it gets counters; that is mana and bodies, not a loop by itself.

## Game plan

### Early game

Keep three lands or two plus [**Burgeoning**](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/143/burgeoning?utm_source=api) / [**Farseek**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/173/farseek?utm_source=api) / a two-mana rock. [**Sol Ring**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/211/sol-ring?utm_source=api) into a signet can put Bumbleflower down on turn three. Hold a counter or [**Swords to Plowshares**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/143/swords-to-plowshares?utm_source=api) if the table is faster than a 1/5 rabbit.

### Midgame

Cast two spells most turns. Use [**Whitemane Lion**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/35/whitemane-lion?utm_source=api) or [**Swiftfoot Boots**](https://scryfall.com/card/mbc/77/swiftfoot-boots?utm_source=api) when Bumbleflower is the engine worth protecting. Spread counters; [**The Ozolith**](https://scryfall.com/card/iko/237/the-ozolith?utm_source=api) banks them through a [**Doomskar**](https://scryfall.com/card/khm/9/doomskar?utm_source=api) or [**Promise of Loyalty**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/142/promise-of-loyalty?utm_source=api).

### Late game

[**Seedborn Muse**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/268/seedborn-muse?utm_source=api) plus flash means three extra Bumbleflower double-spell turns. Close with combat or [**Triumph of the Hordes**](https://scryfall.com/card/nph/123/triumph-of-the-hordes?utm_source=api). [**Force of Will**](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/50/force-of-will?utm_source=api) is the free stop when the extra-draw engine has already filled the hand with blue cards.

## Mulligan guide

Keep:

- Three lands, or two with [**Burgeoning**](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/143/burgeoning?utm_source=api) or a two-mana rock
- A cheap spell so Bumbleflower has a second trigger the turn she enters
- One piece of interaction if the rest of the hand is only ramp

Ship:

- All six-drops with no ramp
- Hands that cannot cast Bumbleflower by turn four
- [**Triumph of the Hordes**](https://scryfall.com/card/nph/123/triumph-of-the-hordes?utm_source=api) without a board plan

## Sequencing and rules

1. Cast the cheaper spell first so the second, more expensive spell still happens.
2. Choose the Bumbleflower opponent who least wants the extra card, then put the counter where this turn's payoff lives.
3. Gift a card only when that draw is their second, or when the gift buys a counter this deck needed.
4. [**Dreamtide Whale**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/59/dreamtide-whale?utm_source=api) proliferates on any player's second spell; use it to add time counters as well as +1/+1 counters.
5. [**Ripples of Potential**](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/77/ripples-of-potential?utm_source=api) proliferates, then phases those permanents out until the next turn — a save, not a same-turn attack pump.

## Weaknesses

The commander paints a target and feeds the table. Without a second spell, she is a 1/5 that donates cards. Sweepers before [**The Ozolith**](https://scryfall.com/card/iko/237/the-ozolith?utm_source=api) reset the counter plan. Graveyard decks eat the extra cards unless [**Endurance**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/51/endurance?utm_source=api) lands. Protect Bumbleflower, Seedborn, and [Ouroboroid](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/201/ouroboroid?utm_source=api); everything else is interchangeable value.
