## How the deck works

1. Ramp with dorks, Signets, and land auras so the commander arrives with mana left over for protection.
2. Burn opponents before casting [**Indoraptor**](https://scryfall.com/card/rex/15/indoraptor-the-perfect-hybrid?utm_source=api) so bloodthirst counters give it a real body.
3. Resolve a damage engine: [**Rite of Passage**](https://scryfall.com/card/5dn/91/rite-of-passage?utm_source=api) to convert pings into permanent size, or a draw payoff such as [**Ripjaw Raptor**](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/253/ripjaw-raptor?utm_source=api).
4. Ping your own creatures repeatedly with [**Pestilence**](https://scryfall.com/card/6ed/149/pestilence?utm_source=api), [**Pyrohemia**](https://scryfall.com/card/cma/86/pyrohemia?utm_source=api), [**Withering Wisps**](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/168/withering-wisps?utm_source=api), [**Noxious Field**](https://scryfall.com/card/pcy/70/noxious-field?utm_source=api), or [**Plague Spitter**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/767/plague-spitter?utm_source=api).
5. Turn the enrage triggers into forced sacrifices, cards, ramp, or direct damage, then finish with a large menace attacker.

The deck is built around a deliberate contradiction: almost every "board wipe" in it is a pinger you point at yourself, and the payoff cards make that damage profitable.

## Main synergy patterns

### Grow instead of dying

[**Rite of Passage**](https://scryfall.com/card/5dn/91/rite-of-passage?utm_source=api) gives a +1/+1 counter to each of your creatures that survives damage, so a repeatable pinger grows your whole board while dealing with opposing x/1s and x/2s. This is the enabler that makes the commander's own enrage safe: once [**Indoraptor**](https://scryfall.com/card/rex/15/indoraptor-the-perfect-hybrid?utm_source=api) has counters, every subsequent ping makes it larger rather than killing it.

### Forced sacrifice attrition

Every point of damage to [**Indoraptor**](https://scryfall.com/card/rex/15/indoraptor-the-perfect-hybrid?utm_source=api) makes a random opponent choose between sacrificing a nontoken creature and taking damage equal to its power. With a mana-driven pinger such as [**Pestilence**](https://scryfall.com/card/6ed/149/pestilence?utm_source=api) and [**Rite of Passage**](https://scryfall.com/card/5dn/91/rite-of-passage?utm_source=api), each activation is a scaling edict plus a growing threat. The opponent is chosen at random, so this is board attrition rather than a targeted kill.

### Damage as card draw

[**Ripjaw Raptor**](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/253/ripjaw-raptor?utm_source=api) draws a card for each instance of damage it takes, and [**Requiem Monolith**](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/113/requiem-monolith?utm_source=api) grants that same ability to another creature for a turn. [**Keen Sense**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/213/keen-sense?utm_source=api) and [**Snake Umbra**](https://scryfall.com/card/nec/130/snake-umbra?utm_source=api) draw whenever the enchanted creature damages an opponent, which includes the commander's enrage damage rather than only combat damage. [**Hunter's Insight**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/296/hunters-insight?utm_source=api) counts combat damage only.

### Untap loop with [Electric Seaweed](https://scryfall.com/card/tmc/23/electric-seaweed?utm_source=api)

Pieces: [**Electric Seaweed**](https://scryfall.com/card/tmc/23/electric-seaweed?utm_source=api), [**Cacophodon**](https://scryfall.com/card/rix/123/cacophodon?utm_source=api), [**Rite of Passage**](https://scryfall.com/card/5dn/91/rite-of-passage?utm_source=api).

1. Tap [**Electric Seaweed**](https://scryfall.com/card/tmc/23/electric-seaweed?utm_source=api) to deal 1 damage to [**Cacophodon**](https://scryfall.com/card/rix/123/cacophodon?utm_source=api).
2. [**Rite of Passage**](https://scryfall.com/card/5dn/91/rite-of-passage?utm_source=api) puts a +1/+1 counter on [**Cacophodon**](https://scryfall.com/card/rix/123/cacophodon?utm_source=api), which survives because its toughness grows at least as fast as the marked damage.
3. Enrage untaps [**Electric Seaweed**](https://scryfall.com/card/tmc/23/electric-seaweed?utm_source=api).
4. Repeat, then send the last activations at opponents' faces.

This restores its starting state and needs no mana, so it is an arbitrarily large amount of damage to any targets. [**Basilisk Collar**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/669/basilisk-collar?utm_source=api) on [**Electric Seaweed**](https://scryfall.com/card/tmc/23/electric-seaweed?utm_source=api) turns the same loop into a one-sided board wipe with lifelink. Note that [**Electric Seaweed**](https://scryfall.com/card/tmc/23/electric-seaweed?utm_source=api) is currently filed under `Cuts`.

### Redirected damage

[**Fiendlash**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/221/fiendlash?utm_source=api) makes the equipped creature deal damage equal to its power to a target player each time it is dealt damage. Unlike the commander's enrage, you choose the player, so equipping a grown [**Indoraptor**](https://scryfall.com/card/rex/15/indoraptor-the-perfect-hybrid?utm_source=api) or [**Cacophodon**](https://scryfall.com/card/rix/123/cacophodon?utm_source=api) converts a ping engine into aimed damage.

### Symmetrical land engine

[**Noxious Field**](https://scryfall.com/card/pcy/70/noxious-field?utm_source=api) plus [**Cacophodon**](https://scryfall.com/card/rix/123/cacophodon?utm_source=api) and [**Rite of Passage**](https://scryfall.com/card/5dn/91/rite-of-passage?utm_source=api) repeats "1 damage to each creature and each player" as often as you like, because the enrage untaps the enchanted land. It hits you too, so only run it out while your life total is comfortably ahead, and stop the loop yourself.

## Win conditions

- A grown [**Indoraptor**](https://scryfall.com/card/rex/15/indoraptor-the-perfect-hybrid?utm_source=api) with menace, plus trample from [**Power Fist**](https://scryfall.com/card/pip/81/power-fist?utm_source=api) or [**Shadowspear**](https://scryfall.com/card/thb/236/shadowspear?utm_source=api), kills through commander damage.
- [**Tainted Strike**](https://scryfall.com/card/som/80/tainted-strike?utm_source=api) on a large [**Indoraptor**](https://scryfall.com/card/rex/15/indoraptor-the-perfect-hybrid?utm_source=api) turns one connection into a ten-poison kill.
- [**Fiendlash**](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/221/fiendlash?utm_source=api) plus any repeatable pinger burns a chosen player out without attacking.
- The [**Electric Seaweed**](https://scryfall.com/card/tmc/23/electric-seaweed?utm_source=api) loop deals unbounded damage once [**Cacophodon**](https://scryfall.com/card/rix/123/cacophodon?utm_source=api) and [**Rite of Passage**](https://scryfall.com/card/5dn/91/rite-of-passage?utm_source=api) are online.
- Grinding enrage edicts strip opposing boards until the raptor attacks unopposed.

## Game progression

### Early game

Play accelerants first: [**Birds of Paradise**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/170/birds-of-paradise?utm_source=api), [**Utopia Sprawl**](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/159/utopia-sprawl?utm_source=api), [**Wild Growth**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/195/wild-growth?utm_source=api), [**Drover of the Mighty**](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/239/drover-of-the-mighty?utm_source=api), and the Signets. [**Biophagus**](https://scryfall.com/card/40k/87/biophagus?utm_source=api) is worth holding for the turn you cast a creature, since its mana adds an extra counter. Cheap pings are usually better spent removing a mana dork than triggering anything.

### Mid game

Cast [**Indoraptor**](https://scryfall.com/card/rex/15/indoraptor-the-perfect-hybrid?utm_source=api) in a turn where you already dealt damage to opponents — [**Boltwave**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/79/boltwave?utm_source=api), [**Flame Rift**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/278/flame-rift?utm_source=api), or [**Fiery Confluence**](https://scryfall.com/card/tle/165/fiery-confluence?utm_source=api) first means it enters with counters and immediately survives your own engine. Hold up [**Without Weakness**](https://scryfall.com/card/hou/81/without-weakness?utm_source=api) or [**Bladed Battle-Fan**](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/91/bladed-battle-fan?utm_source=api) when the commander would otherwise die to a single ping.

### Late game

Establish [**Rite of Passage**](https://scryfall.com/card/5dn/91/rite-of-passage?utm_source=api) with a mana pinger and convert each activation into size and edicts. [**Level Up**](https://scryfall.com/card/tmc/31/level-up?utm_source=api) and [**Thickest in the Thicket**](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/34/thickest-in-the-thicket?utm_source=api) push the commander past blockers, and [**Vraska Joins Up**](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/236/vraska-joins-up?utm_source=api) adds deathtouch counters so the same pings kill anything.

## Mulligan guide

Keep hands with:

- three lands, or two lands plus a one- or two-mana accelerant;
- a way to make black, red, and green by turn three;
- at least one payoff ([**Rite of Passage**](https://scryfall.com/card/5dn/91/rite-of-passage?utm_source=api), [**Ripjaw Raptor**](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/253/ripjaw-raptor?utm_source=api), [**Barbed Servitor**](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/77/barbed-servitor?utm_source=api)) or one cheap engine.

Mulligan hands that are only pingers with no payoff, only expensive enchantments, or that cannot cast the commander by turn four.

## Important sequencing and rules notes

- [**Indoraptor**](https://scryfall.com/card/rex/15/indoraptor-the-perfect-hybrid?utm_source=api) is a 3/1. Without counters or indestructibility, your own 1-damage ping kills it before [**Rite of Passage**](https://scryfall.com/card/5dn/91/rite-of-passage?utm_source=api) resolves, since state-based actions are checked first.
- Bloodthirst counts all damage dealt to your opponents this turn, so [**Boltwave**](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/79/boltwave?utm_source=api) in a four-player game is nine counters before the commander even resolves.
- [**Rite of Passage**](https://scryfall.com/card/5dn/91/rite-of-passage?utm_source=api) requires the creature to survive the damage; indestructibility from [**Without Weakness**](https://scryfall.com/card/hou/81/without-weakness?utm_source=api) or [**Armor of Shadows**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/112/armor-of-shadows?utm_source=api) satisfies that.
- Enrage triggers once per damage event, not per point of damage.
- The enrage opponent is chosen at random, and that opponent chooses which nontoken creature to sacrifice.
- [**Pestilence**](https://scryfall.com/card/6ed/149/pestilence?utm_source=api), [**Pyrohemia**](https://scryfall.com/card/cma/86/pyrohemia?utm_source=api), and [**Withering Wisps**](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/168/withering-wisps?utm_source=api) sacrifice themselves at end step when no creatures remain, so leave a body on the board.
- [**Withering Wisps**](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/168/withering-wisps?utm_source=api) is limited to the number of snow Swamps you control. With [**Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth**](https://scryfall.com/card/tsr/287/urborg-tomb-of-yawgmoth?utm_source=api) in play, every snow-covered basic becomes a snow Swamp, which raises the limit to all fifteen.
- [**Requiem Monolith**](https://scryfall.com/card/eoe/113/requiem-monolith?utm_source=api) makes you lose life equal to the damage as well; do not chain it into a long ping sequence.
- [**Farseek**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/173/farseek?utm_source=api) cannot find a Forest; it fetches [**Smoldering Marsh**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/266/smoldering-marsh?utm_source=api), [**Vernal Fen**](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/419/vernal-fen?utm_source=api), or [**Ziatora's Proving Ground**](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/261/ziatoras-proving-ground?utm_source=api) here.
- [**Last Laugh**](https://scryfall.com/card/tor/68/last-laugh?utm_source=api) damages your own board on every permanent that dies and sacrifices itself once creatures are gone.

## Weaknesses and priorities

The deck asks a fragile 3/1 commander to sit in the middle of its own damage, so instant-speed removal in response to an enrage trigger is the cleanest answer against it. [**Rhythm of the Wild**](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/217/rhythm-of-the-wild?utm_source=api) and [**Hexing Squelcher**](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/145/hexing-squelcher?utm_source=api) cover counterspells, but there is little protection against exile or edicts once the raptor is large. Graveyard-based recursion is limited to [**Bala Ged Recovery**](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/180/bala-ged-recovery-bala-ged-sanctuary?utm_source=api), so avoid over-committing auras and equipment to a single body before you can protect it.
