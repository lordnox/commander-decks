## How it works

[**Baba Lysaga, Night Witch**](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/266/baba-lysaga-night-witch?utm_source=api) taps and sacrifices up to three permanents. If three or more **card types** were among them, each opponent loses 3 life, you gain 3, and you draw 3. Count types, not permanents: an artifact land plus a creature is already three types.

Play a land every turn, drop a cheap artifact, and keep a creature or enchantment. Fetch-sac lands ([**Evolving Wilds**](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/240/evolving-wilds?utm_source=api), [**Harrow**](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/98/harrow?utm_source=api)) and [**Zuran Orb**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/300/zuran-orb?utm_source=api) turn earth into the third type on demand. [**Nature's Chosen**](https://scryfall.com/card/all/97/natures-chosen?utm_source=api) untaps Baba once on your turn for a second rite.

When Baba is gone, [**Life from the Loam**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/96/life-from-the-loam?utm_source=api), [**Ramunap Excavator**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/202/ramunap-excavator?utm_source=api), [**Six**](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/169/six?utm_source=api), and [**Titania, Protector of Argoth**](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/111/titania-protector-of-argoth?utm_source=api) keep the pit paying: recast lands, retrace permanents by discarding a land, or attack with elementals.

## Win conditions

1. Repeat Baba's drain while attacking with whatever did not go in the pit. Three life each opponent per successful rite is incremental; combat closes.
2. [**Titania, Protector of Argoth**](https://scryfall.com/card/eoc/111/titania-protector-of-argoth?utm_source=api) and [**World Shaper**](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/214/world-shaper?utm_source=api) after several land offerings.
3. [**Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/293/jarad-golgari-lich-lord?utm_source=api) dumping a large elemental or another fat creature.
4. [**Living Death**](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/185/living-death?utm_source=api) as a one-shot grave flip when the pit is full. Not a loop.

## Early, mid, and late

**Early.** Hit land drops. Prefer a 0–1 mana artifact and a dork or aura. Cast Baba on three if you already see two types; wait a turn if the only permanents are lands.

**Mid.** Fire the rite when it will actually draw three. Hold [**Vines of Vastwood**](https://scryfall.com/card/mm2/168/vines-of-vastwood?utm_source=api) or [**Malakir Rebirth**](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/111/malakir-rebirth-malakir-mire?utm_source=api) for the turn you tap her. Use edicts on the table's engine, not on random tokens.

**Late.** If Baba is taxed, play lands from the grave and win with Titania or Jarad. [**Constant Mists**](https://scryfall.com/card/sth/104/constant-mists?utm_source=api) buys a race you are winning.

## Mulligan

Keep hands with two lands and a dork or three lands, plus at least one extra type (artifact, aura, or cheap creature). Both colors by turn three. Ship seven-card hands with no artifact, no aura, and no way to fetch a land to sacrifice. One-land hands only with two dorks and a fetch already in hand.

## Sequencing and rules

Sacrifice is a cost. Removal in response to Baba still takes the offerings. Artifact lands count as two types. Food from [Old Rutstein](https://scryfall.com/card/vow/244/old-rutstein?utm_source=api) is an artifact. [Night Soil](https://scryfall.com/card/c13/158/night-soil?utm_source=api) Saprolings are creatures you can offer next turn. [Six](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/169/six?utm_source=api) retrace is a cast from the grave, not a Baba activation.

## Weaknesses

Commander-dependent draw. Grave hate blanks Loam, [Six](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/169/six?utm_source=api), and reanimation. Artifact hate (especially mass) removes the flint half of the type spread. Baba dies to any cheap kill if protection is in hand instead of up.
