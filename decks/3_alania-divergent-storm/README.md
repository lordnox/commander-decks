An Izzet spell-copy deck that spends the early game reducing costs and controlling the table, then resolves one sorcery six times. The primary win is a stack of [Tasha's Hideous Laughter](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/78/tashas-hideous-laughter?utm_source=api) copies; [Crackle with Power](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/95/crackle-with-power?utm_source=api) and [Jaya's Immolating Inferno](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/415/jayas-immolating-inferno?utm_source=api) provide deterministic damage alternatives. The list contains four tutors and no current Game Changers.

## How the deck works

The deck does not mill incrementally. It develops mana, lands a cost reducer, and keeps opponents from establishing a winning board. Once [Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api) is safe, it reserves the first instant and first sorcery of a turn for the winning sequence.

[Ruby Medallion](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/295/ruby-medallion?utm_source=api), [Sapphire Medallion](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/296/sapphire-medallion?utm_source=api), [Goblin Electromancer](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/99/goblin-electromancer?utm_source=api), [Stormcatch Mentor](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/331/stormcatch-mentor?utm_source=api), [Mizzix of the Izmagnus](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/348/mizzix-of-the-izmagnus?utm_source=api), [Vadrik, Astral Archmage](https://scryfall.com/card/mid/248/vadrik-astral-archmage?utm_source=api), [Mindsplice Apparatus](https://scryfall.com/card/one/63/mindsplice-apparatus?utm_source=api), [Primal Amulet](https://scryfall.com/card/xln/243/primal-amulet-primal-wellspring?utm_source=api), and [Thunderclap Drake](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/204/thunderclap-drake?utm_source=api) reduce the large amount of generic mana in the copy package and X-spell finishers.

Twenty-one cards are interaction or protection. Many are deliberately flexible: [Invert Polarity](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/190/invert-polarity?utm_source=api) can steal a spell, [Reinterpret](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/73/reinterpret?utm_source=api) can turn a counter into tempo, [Artistic Refusal](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/46/artistic-refusal?utm_source=api) can be convoked, [Three Steps Ahead](https://scryfall.com/card/otj/75/three-steps-ahead?utm_source=api) scales across three modes, and [Sublime Epiphany](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/167/sublime-epiphany?utm_source=api) can counter, bounce, copy, and draw in one resolution.

## The six-copy lines

### [Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api) plus [Repeated Reverberation](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/879/repeated-reverberation?utm_source=api)

Required: [Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api) on the battlefield, [Repeated Reverberation](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/879/repeated-reverberation?utm_source=api) and a sorcery finisher in hand, no instant or sorcery cast yet this turn, and seven mana for the Tasha line before reductions.

1. Cast [Repeated Reverberation](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/879/repeated-reverberation?utm_source=api) as the first instant of the turn.
2. Resolve [Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api)'s trigger, let an opponent draw, and copy [Repeated Reverberation](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/879/repeated-reverberation?utm_source=api).
3. Resolve both Reverberations. Each creates a separate delayed trigger that will copy the next qualifying spell twice.
4. Cast [Tasha's Hideous Laughter](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/78/tashas-hideous-laughter?utm_source=api) as the first sorcery of the turn.
5. [Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api) copies Tasha once, and the two delayed triggers copy it twice each.

The stack contains the original plus five copies: six Tasha resolutions affecting every opponent.

The same setup wins deterministically with either damage spell:

- [Crackle with Power](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/95/crackle-with-power?utm_source=api) at X=2 deals 10 damage to each of two targets per resolution. Six resolutions provide twelve target slots; assign four to each opponent for 40 damage each.
- [Jaya's Immolating Inferno](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/415/jayas-immolating-inferno?utm_source=api) at X=7 deals 7 to all three opponents per resolution, totaling 42 each. [Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api) satisfies the legendary-sorcery requirement.

### [Storm King's Thunder](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/196/storm-kings-thunder?utm_source=api)

Cast [Storm King's Thunder](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/196/storm-kings-thunder?utm_source=api) with X=5, then cast a finisher. The next spell is copied five times. Mizzix, Vadrik, [Mindsplice Apparatus](https://scryfall.com/card/one/63/mindsplice-apparatus?utm_source=api), and the other reducers make the Thunder and damage X-spells substantially cheaper, but they never reduce colored mana requirements.

### [Thousand-Year Storm](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/248/thousand-year-storm?utm_source=api)

With [Thousand-Year Storm](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/248/thousand-year-storm?utm_source=api) and [Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api) in play, cast four instants while preserving Tasha as the first sorcery. [Thousand-Year Storm](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/248/thousand-year-storm?utm_source=api) supplies four copies and [Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api) supplies the fifth. Without [Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api)'s copy, five earlier instants or sorceries are required.

### The turn-four line

This is the deck's fastest realistic sequence. It needs nine specific cards and does not win on the spot, but it removes most of every opponent's library before anyone has six mana.

| Turn | Play | Mana |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Land, [Sol Ring](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/211/sol-ring?utm_source=api), [Ruby Medallion](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/295/ruby-medallion?utm_source=api) | 1 land + 2 from [Sol Ring](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/211/sol-ring?utm_source=api) |
| 2 | Land, [Sapphire Medallion](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/296/sapphire-medallion?utm_source=api) | 4 available, 2 spent |
| 3 | Land, [Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api) | 5 available, all spent |
| 4 | Land, [Repeated Reverberation](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/879/repeated-reverberation?utm_source=api), [Tasha's Hideous Laughter](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/78/tashas-hideous-laughter?utm_source=api) | 6 available, 5 spent |

On turn four:

1. [Repeated Reverberation](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/879/repeated-reverberation?utm_source=api) costs `{1}{R}{R}` after [Ruby Medallion](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/295/ruby-medallion?utm_source=api). It is the first instant of the turn, so [Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api) triggers; an opponent draws and the spell is copied.
2. Both Reverberations resolve, creating two independent delayed triggers.
3. [Tasha's Hideous Laughter](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/78/tashas-hideous-laughter?utm_source=api) costs `{U}{U}` after [Sapphire Medallion](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/296/sapphire-medallion?utm_source=api). It is the first sorcery, so [Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api) copies it once.
4. Each delayed trigger copies it twice.

That is the original plus five copies. [Goblin Electromancer](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/99/goblin-electromancer?utm_source=api) or another reducer instead of one Medallion produces the same result with a mana to spare. The colored requirements are the real constraint: [Sol Ring](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/211/sol-ring?utm_source=api) cannot pay for `{R}{R}` or `{U}{U}`, so the four lands must cover two red and two blue. [Cascade Bluffs](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/364/cascade-bluffs?utm_source=api) is the single land that most often fixes that turn, since it converts a spare mana into either double-colored pair.

### [Bonus Round](https://scryfall.com/card/bbd/56/bonus-round?utm_source=api)

[Bonus Round](https://scryfall.com/card/bbd/56/bonus-round?utm_source=api) is the explosive but dangerous option. It copies every player's instants and sorceries for the turn, including opposing interaction. Use it only when the additional copies clearly overwhelm the table or when protection is already available.

## Win conditions

[Tasha's Hideous Laughter](https://scryfall.com/card/afr/78/tashas-hideous-laughter?utm_source=api) is the deck's only mill finisher, and six resolutions do not empty a library. Each copy exiles until it reaches 20 mana value, so the number of cards removed depends on the target's curve. Simulated against the three decks stored in this repository, six copies exile roughly 53 cards from a 2.43-average-mana-value deck and 67 from a 1.91-average one, always leaving the opponent alive.

| Copies | Cards exiled (avg MV 1.91) | Cards exiled (avg MV 2.43) |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 6 | 67 | 53 |
| 8 | 89 | 71 |
| 10 | 99 (library emptied) | 89 |

Six copies is therefore a setup turn, not a kill: the opponents draw from a gutted library and lose to the next resolution or to their own draw step. To end the game in one turn, add more copies through a second multiplier, or switch to the damage finishers. [Jaya's Immolating Inferno](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/415/jayas-immolating-inferno?utm_source=api) at X=7 deals 42 to all three opponents across six resolutions, and [Crackle with Power](https://scryfall.com/card/stx/95/crackle-with-power?utm_source=api) at X=2 supplies twelve 10-damage target slots. Both are deterministic where Tasha is not.

The deck intentionally avoids incidental mill engines and unrelated creature-combat plans. Every win condition benefits directly from the same copy infrastructure.

## Game plan

### Early game

Keep a hand with three lands or two lands plus inexpensive ramp, along with either selection or interaction. Deploy mana rocks and reducers before [Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api) when possible. Use [Serum Visions](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/112/serum-visions?utm_source=api), [Lat-Nam's Legacy](https://scryfall.com/card/cst/30b/lat-nams-legacy?utm_source=api), [Strategic Planning](https://scryfall.com/card/khm/77/strategic-planning?utm_source=api), [Peer Through Depths](https://scryfall.com/card/dds/5/peer-through-depths?utm_source=api), and [Pieces of the Puzzle](https://scryfall.com/card/soi/78/pieces-of-the-puzzle?utm_source=api) to assemble one multiplier and one payoff.

### Midgame

Cast [Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api) when protection or interaction remains available. Control only the threats that race the combo or remove key setup pieces. The unusual counterspells reward patience: [Reinterpret](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/73/reinterpret?utm_source=api), [Overwhelming Denial](https://scryfall.com/card/ogw/61/overwhelming-denial?utm_source=api), [Confounding Riddle](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/50/confounding-riddle?utm_source=api), and [Artistic Refusal](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/46/artistic-refusal?utm_source=api) are stronger when opponents tap low.

### Closing turn

Count colored mana separately from generic reductions. Preserve the first instant for [Repeated Reverberation](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/879/repeated-reverberation?utm_source=api) and the first sorcery for the finisher. Choose the [Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api) draw recipients least able to exploit the extra cards, then retain priority only when another copy spell must be added to the stack.

## Mulligan guide

Prioritize:

- three mana sources, or two lands plus a two-mana rock;
- one source of card selection;
- one reducer or ramp spell;
- one interactive spell.

Do not keep a hand containing only finishers and copy effects. The deck needs setup mana more than an early Tasha.

## Sequencing and rules notes

- [Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api) checks the first instant and first sorcery separately. Earlier spells of either type can disable the corresponding copy trigger for that turn.
- [Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api) copies only if the chosen opponent actually draws the card.
- Copies are not cast. They do not create additional [Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api), [Thousand-Year Storm](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/248/thousand-year-storm?utm_source=api), or magecraft-style cast triggers.
- [Repeated Reverberation](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/879/repeated-reverberation?utm_source=api) must resolve before the finisher is cast. Its delayed effect lasts only for the current turn.
- [Display of Power](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/119/display-of-power?utm_source=api) cannot copy itself, but it can copy several other instant or sorcery spells already on the stack.
- Activate [Mistrise Village](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/261/mistrise-village?utm_source=api) before casting the protected spell; the activation costs mana rather than producing it.
- Channeling [Otawara, Soaring City](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/271/otawara-soaring-city?utm_source=api) discards it from hand rather than casting a spell, so it answers a blocker or a hate permanent without spending a land drop or breaking the first-instant reservation.
- [Bonus Round](https://scryfall.com/card/bbd/56/bonus-round?utm_source=api) is symmetrical.

## Weaknesses and what to protect

[Alania](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/204/alania-divergent-storm?utm_source=api) costs five mana and the cleanest line depends on her surviving until the closing turn. [Release to the Wind](https://scryfall.com/card/rix/46/release-to-the-wind?utm_source=api), [Change of Plans](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/24/change-of-plans?utm_source=api), [March of Swirling Mist](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/61/march-of-swirling-mist?utm_source=api), counterspells, and redirection effects protect her. Graveyard exile weakens [Mizzix's Mastery](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/175/mizzixs-mastery?utm_source=api), [Invoke Calamity](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/147/invoke-calamity?utm_source=api), [Increasing Vengeance](https://scryfall.com/card/c19/147/increasing-vengeance?utm_source=api), and [Shreds of Sanity](https://scryfall.com/card/emn/141/shreds-of-sanity?utm_source=api), but does not stop a win assembled from hand.

The deck is also vulnerable to effects that prevent copying, limit spells per turn, or tax noncreature spells. Remove those before committing a multiplier.
