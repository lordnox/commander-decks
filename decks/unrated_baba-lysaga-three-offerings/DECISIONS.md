# Decisions

## How to use

This file is the deck's memory. The primer is how to play the deck. This file is why the list, the primer, and the assessment look the way they do.

- **Cards in** — why each unique card is here and what job a replacement must keep doing.
- **Cards out** — why a card left and what replaced it. Read this before bringing a card back.
- **Primer** — why a line, section, or win-turn claim is written that way.
- **Rules** — Oracle readings and stack order that were checked.
- **Talks** — dated session notes so an argument does not live only in chat.

Every unique card needs a Cards in line. The other headings appear when there is something to record.

## Goals

- **Offerings** — Mixed-type sacrifice (tool, animal, earth) is the repeating action, not generic Golgari creatures-only aristocrats.
- **Grave goods** — Cards should read as Mesolithic kit or later excavation: antler/elk, turtle, feathers, ochre-earth, flint artifacts, and things that return from the pit.

## Rules

- Baba Lysaga counts **card types among the sacrificed permanents**, not the number of permanents. One Darkmoss Bridge is artifact and land (two types). Tree of Tales plus Scryb Sprites is artifact, land, and creature (three types, two permanents). Tokens have the types printed on them; a Food is an artifact, a Saproling is a creature.
- Sacrificing is a cost. If the table kills Baba in response, the permanents are still gone and the life/cards do not happen.
- Nature's Chosen's `{0}` untap is once each turn, only on your turn. The tap-to-untap-something-else clause requires a **white** enchanted creature and does not apply to Baba.
- Six's retrace applies to nonland permanent cards in your graveyard during your turn. Discarding a land is an additional cost, not a Baba activation.
- Constant Mists buyback is "sacrifice a land" in addition to `{1}{G}`. The land is not one of Baba's three permanents unless you also activate her.
- Meandering Towershell: when it attacks it is exiled, then returned tapped and attacking at the beginning of the next combat. That is a delay, not a loop.
- Living Death is not infinite. Each player exiles their grave, sacs creatures, then puts the exiled cards onto the battlefield.
- Disciple of the Vault triggers per artifact going to the graveyard, including artifact lands and Wellspring. It is optional and targets an opponent.

## Talks

### 2026-09-11 — Autobrew: Schamanin von Bad Dürrenberg

The user asked for an auto-deck named **Schamanin von Bas Dürrenberg**. No Oracle card matches. The lock is the Mesolithic shaman of Bad Dürrenberg (grave, antler headdress, mixed grave goods, later pilgrim offerings, turtle, feathers, ochre). No stored or parked list covers that.

**Game plan:** Each turn, assemble a mixed burial offering (tool, animal, or earth) and feed it to a rite that draws, gains life, and drains the table; leftover spirits and returning land convert that into a telegraphed combat or Jarad dump. Backup: land recursion into Titania / World Shaper if the shaman is removed. Experience: kitchen-table archaeology, not generic Golgari value.

**Target:** Incremental Core (Bracket 2). Expected normal win turn 9. Earliest credible high roll turn 7 if Baba lives and the rite fires from turn 4. Commander on turn 3–4. Turn-five milestone: Baba in play and either one successful three-type rite or the three types on board ready, with a land still in play. Interaction buckets: board protection on Baba, targeted edicts and old kill, one or two carve-out wipes, Constant Mists as a land-sac fog, Furnace/Bog as light proactive grave hate. Boundaries: 0 Game Changers, no two-card infinites, no extra turns, no mass land denial (we sacrifice our own lands). No permanent fast mana. Buried Alive is the on-theme burial tutor, not Survival / Worldly Tutor.

Winner: **Baba Lysaga**. The archaeological signature is a mixed assemblage, not a keyword voltron or an enchantment shrine. Kathril loses without white and without enabling the offering. Anikthea loses the flint and ochre. Archelos is the turtle and not the grave. Karador is the wrong gender and too expensive.

**Packages:** (1) Mixed-type fodder — cheap artifacts, artifact lands, enchantment ramp, fetch-sac lands, small creatures. Density: at least two types in play by the first Baba tap. Failure: all creatures, no flint. (2) Land recursion — Loam, Ramunap, Drownyard, Splendid Reclamation, World Shaper, Titania, Six retrace. Failure: no earth comes back. (3) Artifact loop — Wellspring, Baubles, Myr Retriever, Scrap Trawler, Buried Ruin, Disciple. (4) Conversion — Baba drain, Titania elementals, Jarad, Living Death. (5) Interaction — edicts, Shriekmaw, Haywire Mite, Creeping Mold, Deed, Mutilate, Vines, Wrap, Malakir, Hollow, Constant Mists.

**Mana budget before v1:** 36 true lands plus 2 MDFC land-backs (38 playable). No other modal backs. One-mana dorks: Fyndhorn, Elves of Deep Shadow. Enchantment ramp: Wild Growth, Utopia Sprawl. Two-plus ramp: Lore, Visits, Elder, Springbloom, Harrow, Wayfarer's Bauble, Burnished Hart, Sextant. No Sol Ring. Coloured sources: duals plus 5 Forest and 2 Swamp; T3 needs B and G for Baba. Tapped-land tolerance: one in the opener if a dork or two untapped sources remain. Curve: many 0–2 offerings, commander 3, finishers 4–5. P(≥3 lands in 7 | 38/99) ≈ 55%; P(≥2) ≈ 83%. Keep 2-land hands that have a dork or fetch and both colors in reach.

Rejected: Antler Skulkin (persist only for white), Giant Albatross (blue), Blood Pet (one-shot fast mana in Bracket 2), Salvaging Station (six mana), Pit of Offerings (name fit, exile-our-grave and tapped), Glissa the Traitor (triggers on opponent deaths), Poison-Tip Archer (generic drain).
