## Deck summary

The deck first establishes cheap Squirrel or Rat tokens, then lets Hazel turn those tokens into mana and two extra copies of the best Squirrel token each end step. Chatterfang makes every token spell contribute additional Squirrels, while [Maskwood Nexus](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/865/maskwood-nexus?utm_source=api) and [Springleaf Parade](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/19/springleaf-parade?utm_source=api) let the mixed swarm count for [Valley Rotcaller](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/119/valley-rotcaller?utm_source=api).

The full card-by-card rationale is recorded in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).

## The central decision

Every developed turn asks how the Squirrels should be used:

| Choice | Reward | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Tap tokens with Hazel | A large burst of flexible mana | Those tokens cannot attack or activate the Odd Acorn Gang this turn |
| Tap Squirrels to the Gang | Each tapped Squirrel gives another Squirrel +2/+2 and trample | Fewer attackers and less Hazel mana |
| Attack wide | Combat damage draws from the Gang and Toski | The board is exposed and fewer bodies remain available |
| Hold the board back | Fog, removal, and protection remain credible | No combat pressure or draw-on-hit payoff |

[Seedborn Muse](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/268/seedborn-muse?utm_source=api) untaps the board on opponents' turns, but it does not untap the team between Hazel's activation and combat on our own turn. The main-turn allocation still matters.

## How the deck works

### 1. Seed the board

Use [Chatter of the Squirrel](https://scryfall.com/card/2xm/157/chatter-of-the-squirrel?utm_source=api), [Squirrel Sanctuary](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/174/squirrel-sanctuary?utm_source=api), [Squirrel Nest](https://scryfall.com/card/dmr/177/squirrel-nest?utm_source=api), [Scurry Oak](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/172/scurry-oak?utm_source=api), Lord Skitter, and [Chittering Witch](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/175/chittering-witch?utm_source=api) to put multiple bodies into play. [Tireless Provisioner](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/313/tireless-provisioner?utm_source=api), Tippy-Toe, and [Academy Manufactor](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/251/academy-manufactor?utm_source=api) create noncreature tokens that Hazel can tap for mana and Chatterfang can convert into additional Squirrels.

The Bat and Lizard count is intentionally light. [Mirkwood Bats](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/95/mirkwood-bats?utm_source=api) supplies token-based drain, while [Iridescent Vinelasher](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/99/iridescent-vinelasher?utm_source=api) and [Agate-Blade Assassin](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/82/agate-blade-assassin?utm_source=api) add useful Lizard bodies. Rats are the secondary creature type because Lord Skitter, [Piper of the Swarm](https://scryfall.com/card/afc/106/piper-of-the-swarm?utm_source=api), [Chittering Witch](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/175/chittering-witch?utm_source=api), [Experimental Confectioner](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/314/experimental-confectioner?utm_source=api), [Revenge of the Rats](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/67/revenge-of-the-rats?utm_source=api), and [Plague of Vermin](https://scryfall.com/card/shm/73/plague-of-vermin?utm_source=api) can produce them at scale.

### 2. Compound tokens

Hazel should normally copy a Squirrel because that creates two copies rather than one. The strongest targets are token copies of useful nontoken creatures, but an ordinary 1/1 remains worthwhile.

[Second Harvest](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/213/second-harvest?utm_source=api) copies every token already controlled. [For the Common Good](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/172/for-the-common-good?utm_source=api) concentrates mana into copies of the best token and protects the whole token board until the next turn. [Rootcast Apprenticeship](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/32/rootcast-apprenticeship?utm_source=api) can choose its copy mode three times, while [Esika's Chariot](https://scryfall.com/card/khm/169/esikas-chariot?utm_source=api) adds another copy trigger for attacking.

[The Unbeatable Squirrel Girl](https://scryfall.com/card/msh/193/the-unbeatable-squirrel-girl?utm_source=api) converts Hazel's mana back into a larger Squirrel army. Her activated ability counts the Squirrels already controlled, so establish the board before committing four mana to it.

### 3. Decide how combat resolves

[Valley Rotcaller](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/119/valley-rotcaller?utm_source=api) drains on declaration of attack, before blockers or combat damage. [The Odd Acorn Gang](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/7/the-odd-acorn-gang?utm_source=api)'s pump abilities must be activated as sorceries, so their allocation is decided before combat. The Gang then draws once for each player damaged by one or more Squirrels; Toski draws for each creature that connects.

After attackers are declared, read the blocks and responses:

- let combat happen when menace, [Champion of Lambholt](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/293/champion-of-lambholt?utm_source=api), or the Gang's trample can push damage through;
- cast [Arachnogenesis](https://scryfall.com/card/dsc/169/arachnogenesis?utm_source=api), [Tangle](https://scryfall.com/card/mar/38/tangle?utm_source=api), or [Constant Mists](https://scryfall.com/card/sth/104/constant-mists?utm_source=api) when combat becomes unfavorable;
- use removal before blocks when one key creature is stopping the attack;
- preserve [Heroic Intervention](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/295/heroic-intervention?utm_source=api) or [For the Common Good](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/172/for-the-common-good?utm_source=api) when a sweeper is more dangerous than combat.

A full fog prevents our combat damage too. It preserves [Valley Rotcaller](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/119/valley-rotcaller?utm_source=api)'s attack drain but gives up the Gang and Toski draw triggers for that combat.

## Win conditions

1. **Distributed combat:** build a wide board, use the Odd Acorn Gang, Camellia, [Drey Keeper](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/194/drey-keeper?utm_source=api), [Champion of Lambholt](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/293/champion-of-lambholt?utm_source=api), and [Squirrel Sovereign](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/175/squirrel-sovereign?utm_source=api) to force damage through, then refill from the Gang and Toski.
2. **Rotcaller drain:** attack with [Valley Rotcaller](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/119/valley-rotcaller?utm_source=api) while a large mixed or Maskwood-enabled board is present. Combat damage is optional for this line.
3. **Token attrition:** [Mirkwood Bats](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/95/mirkwood-bats?utm_source=api) and [Nadier's Nightblade](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/275/nadiers-nightblade?utm_source=api) turn token creation, sacrifice, and disappearance into life loss.
4. **Triumph turn:** [Triumph of the Hordes](https://scryfall.com/card/nph/123/triumph-of-the-hordes?utm_source=api) gives the wide board trample and infect. It is the direct version-one finisher when ordinary combat cannot close the table.

## Mulligan guide

Keep three lands, or two lands plus [Nature's Lore](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/177/natures-lore?utm_source=api), [Three Visits](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/181/three-visits?utm_source=api), or [Arcane Signet](https://scryfall.com/card/msc/192/arcane-signet?utm_source=api). The hand should also contain a cheap token seed. Hazel without tokens is only a four-mana 3/5, so ship hands whose first productive token arrives after the commander.

Strong openers contain:

- green and black mana;
- one early token source;
- either card draw, a copying effect, or one interaction spell;
- a reason to cast Hazel on turn three or four.

Do not keep a hand made only of token multipliers and finishers. [Parallel Lives](https://scryfall.com/card/isd/199/parallel-lives?utm_source=api), [Second Harvest](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/213/second-harvest?utm_source=api), and [Triumph of the Hordes](https://scryfall.com/card/nph/123/triumph-of-the-hordes?utm_source=api) need an existing battlefield.

## Sequencing notes

- Hazel's mana ability taps Hazel and X other untapped tokens. Hazel is not included in X.
- Hazel can tap Food, Treasure, Clue, and other noncreature tokens for mana.
- Choose targets for Hazel's end-step trigger when the trigger goes on the stack. Removing the target in response stops the copy.
- [The Odd Acorn Gang](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/7/the-odd-acorn-gang?utm_source=api) grants the tap ability to every Squirrel, including itself, but each ability can target another Squirrel or the same attacker.
- Activate Gang pumps before combat; they cannot be held until blockers.
- [Valley Rotcaller](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/119/valley-rotcaller?utm_source=api) counts other Squirrels, Bats, Lizards, and Rats, not itself.
- A changeling is all four listed types but is still counted only once by [Valley Rotcaller](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/119/valley-rotcaller?utm_source=api).

## Weaknesses

The deck is highly exposed to sweepers, token hosers, and effects that prevent life gain or triggered abilities. It also needs a live battlefield before its copy spells and finishers matter. Protect Hazel only when her mana or end-step trigger is essential; Chatterfang, [Champion of Lambholt](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/293/champion-of-lambholt?utm_source=api), the Odd Acorn Gang, and [Valley Rotcaller](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/119/valley-rotcaller?utm_source=api) are often the more dangerous targets.
